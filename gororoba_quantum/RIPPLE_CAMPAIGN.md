## Meaning and scientific return

A reality ripple means an operationally defined change in a measured
distribution caused by a controlled intervention, relative to a named
prediction or reference. The metaphor connects mathematical interference to
recorded events. It makes no claim about macroscopic spacetime deformation.
Exact algebra supplies structure, circuits implement a chosen encoding, the
device supplies outcomes, and uncertainty bounds limit what those outcomes
establish. A visually striking residual can expose an inadequate model even
when the corresponding geometry is already known mathematically.

The completed folded ladder already contributes knowledge: coherent
implementations outperform the archived dynamic implementation in all four
tested labels; omitting feedback damages the two odd-label cases; a narrow
common appended binary-channel explanation fails its retained test. Routing,
terminal writers, instrument effects and drift limit causal attribution.
The two jobs charge 29 and 11 seconds. Their complete retained results are
reused; collecting or visualizing them never requires repeated QPU acquisition.

The next narrow question is whether replacing dependence on a recorded first
bit with a supplied odd label improves unconditional terminal correctness.
Both arms retain measurement, reset and dispatch. A successful fixed-sample
decision applies to the supplied s1/s3 witnesses, with gains exceeding one
percentage point for both labels under four simultaneous directional bounds.
The decision does not certify unknown-eigenstate QPE or general Shor.

0.5889893 percentage points for s1 and s3, both below the one-percentage-point

## Proof and research dependencies

The research chats retain the following obligations without enlarging the
fixed-sample pilot by default:

1. Prove finite F/U ideal-law equivalence for known odd labels and formalize the
   policy implication from four valid directional bounds. A Rocq numerical
   certificate needs rational outward bounds and certified binomial tails;
   float64 beta quantiles implement exact-binomial inference without certified
   numerical rounding. Coverage retains explicit sampling assumptions.
2. Separate stochastic-channel contraction tests from replacement-channel
   explanations and quantum-instrument identifiability. Compare predicted joint
   outcomes with prospective observations. Instrument tomography needs its own
   informationally complete preparations, measurements, model assumptions and
   resource case. Adaptive stopping needs a time-uniform inference contract.
3. Resolve the missing chain-contraction data before treating fold averaging as
   a topology-preserving reduction. Prove chain maps, side conditions and a
   homotopy identity, or retain folding as a different bounded construction.
4. Address Koebisu's rank-stratum question through a bounded lower-nullity slice,
   coverage, boundary and actual kernel-bundle transitions before global
   attachments. Finite noisy norm measurements cannot prove exact global rank
   geometry or topology.
5. Prove the finite dimension-32 absolute-Walsh classes and multiplicities before
   claiming a doubling recurrence. Magnitude multisets, labeled distributions
   and full phase equivalence are distinct relations.
6. Compare identical output tasks, including preparation, synthesis, workspace,
   shots, decoding and reuse, against the relevant classical computation. The
   existing sign recursion already supplies an efficient construction; claims
   of advantage need a complete resource comparison.
