## Main claims

[P] Measurement-only quantum computation rests on projections that leak no information about the state, which means equiangular projections, and the largest continuous family of them comes from the octonions and gives a universal scheme.

- [P] No-leakage equals equiangularity (Sec. 2): subspaces P and Q are equiangular when the minimum angle arccos |(r_P, r_Q)| is independent of the chosen unit vector (Definition 2.1). Theorem 2.3 gives P Q P = alpha^2 P and Q P Q = alpha^2 Q with alpha = cos(theta_{P,Q}); Corollary 2.4 gives the block form; Theorem 2.8 states that a pair for which P Q P acts as a scaled unitary on the image of P (a no-leakage pair, Definition 2.7) is an equiangular pair. Remark 2.9 reduces any pair of projections to two-dimensional invariant blocks.
- [P] Strong equiangularity (Sec. 3, Definition 3.1): P, Q and their complements are equiangular. Theorem 3.2: this holds exactly when, after a unitary change of basis, Q = diag(I_d, 0) and P = [[alpha^2 I, B], [B^dagger, (1 - alpha^2) I]] with B a unitary up to scale alpha^2 - alpha^4; then both projections have rank d = n/2. Corollary 3.7: P_0 P_{i_k} ... P_{i_1} P_0 acts on the image of P_0 as a product of scaled unitary blocks V_11.
- [P] Classification of continuous families (Sec. 4): an n-parameter family of equiangular n-planes in R^(2n) exists only for n in {1, 2, 4, 8} (Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2, by a Hopf-invariant-one argument with Adams' theorem); Theorem 4.4 extends this to n-1 and, for n > 3, n-2 parameters; Remarks 4.5-4.7 and Figs. 1-2 chart which (n, k) are known to exist or not.
- [P] Lines from division algebras (Sec. 4.2-4.3, Lemma 4.8): the graphs of Ax and Bx for A, B unitary up to scale are strongly equiangular exactly when I + A^dagger B is unitary up to scale. The complex numbers give SO(2) and the quaternions give SU(2) (Theorem 4.10).
- [P] Octonions (Sec. 5.1, Eq. 27, Theorems 5.1-5.2): the 8 x 8 matrix A(a) of octonion multiplication by a on the coordinates of x satisfies A A^dagger = ||a||^2 I, is not an algebra homomorphism because the octonions are non-associative, and generates SO(8), of dimension 28, not SU(4). The matrices E_i for the units satisfy E_i^dagger = -E_i for i not 0, and the 28 products E_i E_j (i not equal to j) are linearly independent and span so(8) (Theorem 5.1, proved partly by computer). Products P_0 P_{a_k} ... P_{a_1} P_0 with a_j = t_j e_{k_j} generate SO(8) (Theorem 5.2).
- [P] Forced measurement (Sec. 5.2, Lemma 5.3): a failed projection can be undone by retrying, with success probability 1/2 for the pairs P_{e_i}, P_{c e_j} (dihedral angle pi/4) and a constant for sequences that touch P_0 after halving the rotation angle (tan(t/2) in place of tan t); the failure probability is exponentially suppressed in polynomially many steps.
- [P] Universality model (Sec. 5.3, Eq. 33, Sec. 6): any SU(4) gate on adjacent qubits embeds in SO(8) through [[A_1, -A_2], [A_2, A_1]]; the model assumes all-zero initialization and one destructive single-qubit measurement. Sec. 6 abstracts the local forced measurement model (Definitions 6.1-6.10) and shows that cluster-state and anyonic measurement-only computation fit it.
- [P] Related topics (Sec. 7): adiabatic computation as a limit of near-equiangular projections; a strongly equiangular IC-POVM would need alpha^2 = (n^2 - 2) / (2 (n^2 - 1)) (Eq. 43, Question 7.2); Shor-Sloane Grassmannian packings for m = 4 generate only a finite subgroup of O(2) and the general case is open (Question 7.3); Sec. 8 speculates on chemical protection with small molecules.
- [P] The paper contains no hardware, simulation, gate-count, or associator computation.

- [I] Diagonality of the associator operator. Repository claim: DESIGN_NOTES.md, Design A -- W = L_{e_i e_j}^-1 L_{e_i} L_{e_j} is diagonal with eigenvalue +/-1, and across the 21 unordered pairs these are the 7 nonidentity 3-qubit Z-strings, three pairs each. Source item: the proof of Theorem 5.1 (pp. 17-18): E_i E_j has nonzero entries exactly where E_k does, where e_i e_j is proportional to e_k, and differs from E_k only in signs. Model map: two signed permutation matrices with the same support differ by a diagonal matrix of signs, so E_k^-1 E_i E_j is diagonal with entries +/-1; with E_k = L_{e_k} this is the repository's W up to a global sign. Label: "known theorem reproduced". Not established by the source: the diagonal's values, the Fano-line structure, the plus-one subspace {0, i, j, i XOR j}, the reading of the sign as the associator of (e_i, e_j, e_k), the Z-string form, or any readout. The "distinguished entry" statement in the proof concerns the relative signs among the four upper-triangular entries of E_i E_j and is not a statement about W.
- [I] Non-homomorphism as a resource. - [I] Universality versus Clifford structure. Background; no label.
- [I] Dimension limit and the Albert observable.
