## Main claims

[P] The map from each Fano line {p, r, t} to the Z-string Q{p,r,t} that appears in the Clifford triple product of an earlier manuscript [5] equals the projective orthogonal complement of the line in F_2^3, and that Z-string indexes the Z-stabilizer of the Steane [[7,1,3]] code whose support is the line's complement.

- [P] Setting (Sections 1 and 2.3). A map rho-tilde from the seven imaginary octonion units to Pauli strings on three qubits is built in [5] from the left-multiplication operators L_{e_k} of the octonions. Each L_{e_k} expands as (-i/2) times a sum of four Pauli strings with equal-magnitude coefficients; rho-tilde selects one string per generator, with exactly eight selections making the seven images pairwise anticommute. For every Fano line the triple product rho-tilde(e_p) rho-tilde(e_r) rho-tilde(e_t) equals phi times Q{p,r,t}, with phi in {+i, -i} and Q a non-identity string in {I, Z}^(tensor 3), identical across the eight selections (imported from [5], Remark 6.2).
- [P] Definition 1.1. For a line L the dual string Z_L is Z^(tensor v) with v the unique non-zero vector of F_2^3 orthogonal to bin(p), bin(r), bin(t), where bin is the MSB-first binary representation of 1..7. The map L to v is a bijection from the seven lines to the seven non-zero vectors (Remark 1.2).
- [P] Theorem 3.1 (n = 4). (i) Q{p,r,t} = Z_L. (ii) With M the bit-reversal matrix, the Steane Z-stabilizer labeled M v_L (generator-combination labeling of S_Z, with generator supports {1,3,5,7}, {2,3,6,7}, {4,5,6,7}) has support equal to {1..7} minus {p, r, t}. (iii) Q_{g(L)} = Z^(tensor g^{-T} v_L) for every g in GL(3, F_2).
- [P] Evidence offered for Theorem 3.1. Part (i) rests on inspection of Table 1 (seven rows), which takes the Q column from [5]. Part (ii) rests on the same table, "exhaustive verification over all seven Fano lines". Part (iii) rests on the identity (gW)^perp = g^{-T}(W^perp) plus a computer check on three generators of GL(3, F_2).
- [P] Remark 4.3. The matrix M is a convention artifact of mixing MSB-first bin with a least-significant-first parity-check ordering; with MSB-first generator labeling, M = I_3.
- [P] Corollary 4.1. The sign-parity constraint pi_A pi_B pi_C = -1 (Theorem 7.18 of [1]) is read three ways: sign parity of the three splits of a 2-flat; the global phase -1 of a product of three balanced half-operators ([4], Theorem 4.2); and the projective relation of part (c), "Z_L records the geometric obstruction to associativity".
- [P] Corollary 4.2. The 28 non-collinear triples out of C(7,3) = 35 are the triples with bin(p) + bin(r) + bin(t) nonzero; the cocycle condition for coct fails exactly there, "the algebraic shadow of the absence of a projective dual".
- [P] Theorem 5.1 (n = 5). In PG(3, F_2) the 15 Fano planes W biject with the non-zero normal vectors v_W; the Z-stabilizer labeled v_W of the [[15,7,3]] code has support equal to {1..15} minus W, with M = I_4 (Table 2, 15 rows). Proposition 5.2 states the same identity for every n >= 2, with M = I_{n-1}, under the MSB-first generator convention.
- [P] Remark 5.4. The Clifford triple-product formula does not extend to n >= 5 because A_4 (the sedenions) is not alternative. The remark states that for n = 4 left alternativity gives L_p L_q = epsilon(p, q) L_{p xor q} and hence {L_p, L_q} = 0; for n = 5 it reports 63 index pairs with zero anticommutator ("ZD-index pairs") and 42 pairs, called "non-ZD, alternativity-breaking", with a nonzero anticommutator, out of C(15, 2) = 105.
- [P] Section 6.2. The residue of the ZD zeta function at x = 1/2 is -7 = -|PG(2, F_2)|, read as 7 Fano lines, each a Z-stabilizer of the Steane code.
- [P] Open problems (Section 7). (1) A natural map sigma from the zero-divisor set ZD*(n) into Cliff(0, 2^(n-1) - 1) for n >= 5. (2) A twisted 2-cocycle interpretation of coct. (3) A fault-tolerant-gate or transversal-operation reading of the bridge; this item calls Q "a Z-type logical operator in the Steane code".
- [P] The manuscript contains no QPU data, no circuit, and no octonion associator computation; its experiments are exhaustive finite checks reported only as table entries.

Using cd_core._lmat(8, m): for all 21 pairs, W is diagonal with entries sg * (-1)^popcount(v & k), and v equals the orthogonal vector v_L of the pair's Fano line (3 pairs per line, 7 distinct strings). The real triple product L_{e_p} L_{e_q} L_{e_{p xor q}} along each of the seven lines is likewise a diagonal of +1 and -1 entries equal to sg * (-1)^popcount(v_L & k); the sign sg depends on the ordering of the three factors (of the six orderings, three give each sign on lines {1,2,3} and {1,4,5}) and is real, where the manuscript's phase phi for Pauli selections is +i or -i. The two constructions therefore land on the same seven Z-strings, matched as bit masks on the basis index.
- [I] The statement about eigenvalues also reads off the manuscript's string: Z^(tensor v_L) is +1 exactly on basis indices in {0} union L, the associating subspace the repository names. The manuscript does not state this action on basis states.
- [I] A three-qubit Z-string and a seven-qubit Steane stabilizer are different operators. Z^(tensor v) acts on a Hilbert space of dimension 8 as the diagonal operator k to (-1)^<v,k> with weight 1 to 3. The Steane stabilizer labeled w is Z on the physical qubits k with <k, w> = 1, weight 4, on a space of dimension 128. Both are images of the same F_2-linear functional on F_2^3, read once as a function on the 8 basis indices and once as a subset of the 7 qubits; the manuscript joins them only through the generator-combination labeling of the group (Z/2)^3, which is a group isomorphism and carries no operator content. Recomputed from the manuscript's generator supports: the label M v_L reproduces the line's complement as the support for all seven lines (Table 1, last two columns), and the same identity in the form S_Z(w) = {k : <k, w> = 1} holds for every vector w, so Theorem 3.1(ii), Theorem 5.1(ii), and Proposition 5.2 follow from the definition of the generator labeling and Remark 5.3's "confirmation of the prediction" adds no independent evidence.
- [I] The correspondence that would make the two operators one claim is an isometry from the three-qubit register to the Steane syndrome register, |x> to X_x |code> for x in 1..7 (X on physical qubit x) and |0> to |code>. Z^(tensor w) on the first register matches the stabilizer labeled w on the second, because Z^(S_Z(w)) fixes |code> and anticommutes with X_x exactly when <x, w> = 1. The eight patterns of at most one X error are the eight syndromes of the perfect Hamming code. No isometry into the code space intertwines the two operators, since every stabilizer acts as the identity there while Z^(tensor v) does not. The manuscript does not state this embedding; it is a textbook syndrome statement derived here from the definitions, and Open Problem (3)'s phrase "Z-type logical operator" contradicts Theorem 3.1(ii), which calls the same object a stabilizer.
