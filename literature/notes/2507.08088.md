## Main claims

[P] A 144-qubit digital simulation of the (2+1)-D Z2 gauge-Higgs model on IBM Heron processors resolves string dynamics once several error-handling layers are stacked.

- [P] Model: Z2 Higgs model with matter on vertices and gauge fields on links of a heavy-hex-embedded hexagonal lattice, H = -m sum tau^z - g sum sigma^z - lambda sum tau^x sigma^x tau^x. The Gauss-law generators G_n commute with H. The plaquette term is omitted to avoid six-body interactions.
- [P] Hardware: ibm_kingston and ibm_marrakesh, both Heron r2 with 156 fixed-frequency transmons on heavy-hex. Reported CLOPS 250K (kingston) and 195K (marrakesh); error per layered gate (EPLG) 9.93e-4 and 3.98e-3.
- [P] Scale: up to 144 qubits and 7872 two-qubit gates (7 x 3 lattice, dt = 0.25); 2280 two-qubit gates on the 2 x 2 lattice (dt = 0.15) and 4286 on the 3 x 3 lattice (dt = 0.125).
- [P] Depth: the abstract states up to 192 two-qubit layers; the supplement sets a CNOT-depth cap of 204 with depth 6 per Trotter layer, so at most 34 layers.
- [P] Shots: 300,000 per circuit and 600,000 per time step (simulation plus calibration circuit). After decoding, at least 30,000 shots with the fewest detected flips are retained.
- [P] Execution: Qiskit Runtime Batch mode with SamplerV2; one batch is 68 circuits (34 time instants x 2 for calibration) in 136 jobs and runs 1 h 10 min +/- 5 min.
- [I] That is 20.4 million shots in about 4200 s, an effective 4.9 thousand shots/s, so shot time is about 0.2 ms at this shot count per circuit.
- [P] Physics results: three dynamical regimes (confined, deconfined, Higgs) at (m, g, lambda) = (5, 2, 1), (5, 0.01, 1), (0.3, 0.5, 1). In the confined phase the yo-yo mode has frequency 2g, period 1.57/lambda at g = 2, matching hardware and tensor-network results. The bending mode has frequency lambda^2/g - lambda^2/(2m + g), period 15.08/lambda, unresolved on hardware and resolved to more than half a period by the tensor-network runs.
- [P] The fully glassy point m = g = 0 on the 144-qubit lattice follows the analytic curve a(t) = 1 - 2 lambda^2/(lambda^2 + g^2) sin^2(t sqrt(lambda^2 + g^2)) as mitigation layers are added (Fig. 4a).
- [P] A 3-string quench on the 2 x 2 lattice shows string fragmentation and recombination through four-body string correlators.
- [P] Trotter error: the commutator bound of Eq. A10 exceeds 1 in three of four cases (50.28, 60.50, 4.31, and 0 for the 7 x 3 case). Runs at different dt agree up to dt = 0.2, and the authors judge dt <= 0.175/lambda sufficient.
- [P] Classical comparison: matrix-product-state simulation with the basis update and Galerkin integrator, bond dimension 64 to 512; ground-state DMRG up to 54 super-sites with bond dimension 256 to 1024.

## Methods

[P] Twirling, gauge dynamical decoupling, syndrome decoding and calibration-based rescaling are stacked on one fixed-layout Trotter circuit.

- [P] Second-order Trotter step U1(dt/2) U3(dt) U1(dt/2). U3 is one Pauli gadget per lattice edge; matter qubits are the CNOT targets and gauge qubits the controls. Two matter-qubit sets alternate so that all CNOTs of a layer commute and merge into a dense block of CNOT depth 6.
- [P] Pauli twirling through Qiskit Runtime converts coherent errors into stochastic Pauli errors. It needs no extra quantum resources beyond inserted single-qubit gates.
- [P] Gauge dynamical decoupling (GDD) inserts the Gauss-law generators with random phases (constraint: phases sum to zero per qubit) at no depth cost. Physical-sector states are unchanged and unphysical coherences average out, which pushes the noise toward a Pauli channel.
- [P] Gauss sector correction (GSC): the G_n form a stabilizer code with bit-flip distance 3. A final syndrome is read from the end-of-circuit Z measurements and decoded with PyMatching2 minimum-weight matching. Samples are sorted by detected flips rather than post-selected strictly. Measurement errors are the dominant detected flips; mid-circuit syndrome rounds are rejected because the code cannot detect all error types.
- [P] Operator decoherence renormalization (ODR): for a Pauli-product observable under Pauli noise, <O>_f = (1 - p) <O>_u (Eq. 18), where p is estimated from a calibration circuit with a known ideal output. The method doubles the shot count.
- [P] ODR calibration circuits: mirror circuits (cut the symmetric Trotter circuit in half and invert the second half) outperform Cliffordized circuits at long times, because Cliffordized circuits change the effective couplings (Fig. 4e-f, on a (1+1)-D chain of 41 qubits).
- [P] Layout: Qiskit preset pass manager at optimization level 3. The layout of the first circuit is fixed for the whole batch. For more than 40 qubits, the authors hand-select several layouts from device characteristics and average over them to spread the influence of any faulty qubit.
- [P] Error bars come from bootstrapping the shots.

## Relevance

(a) Calibration-aware or noise-adaptive compilation on IBM hardware: high. [I]
- [I] The paper is a complete Heron workflow: fixed layouts chosen from calibration data, multi-layout averaging against faulty qubits, SamplerV2 with twirling inside Batch mode, mirror-circuit ODR, and a per-run calibration table. Each step transfers to the project's 3-qubit and 5-qubit witnesses, though at 1/30 the qubit count.
- [P] Fig. 7e shows grey data points that depart from the tensor-network curve because one qubit of the chosen layout was faulty; neighboring sites were unaffected.

(b) Cayley-Dickson, octonion, zero-divisor, nonassociative structure: none. [I]
- [P] No occurrence of the terms in the text (grep).
- [I] GSC works because the circuit has known conserved parities (the G_n). Designs A and B expose no such redundancy as built, so GSC has no direct analog unless a conserved check is added to the circuit.

(c) Shor's algorithm and factoring resource estimates: low. [I]
- [I] The paper gives hardware-scale reference points against the 2505.15917 assumptions: median CZ error 1.95e-3 to 3.37e-3 against an assumed uniform 1e-3, EPLG 9.93e-4 on kingston and 3.98e-3 on marrakesh, 156 qubits against 897,864, and degree-3 heavy-hex connectivity against a square grid. It contains no factoring content and no feedforward-latency figure.

## In-paper inconsistencies and gaps

- [P] The 2 x 2 lattice is quoted as 34 qubits (Sec. III A 1), 35 qubits (Fig. 2a and Fig. 4c captions) and 36 qubits (Table I); the 7 x 3 lattice as 144 qubits (abstract, Fig. 4) and 146 (Table I).
- [P] The Fig. 1 caption gives two-qubit depth D = 6 N_e L, while the supplement gives a depth of 6 per Trotter layer independent of the number of edges (204 = 6 x 34).
- [P] The number of Pauli-twirl randomizations is not stated.
