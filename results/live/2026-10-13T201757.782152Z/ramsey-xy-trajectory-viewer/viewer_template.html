Interpretation, time semantics, and provenance

Time is an experimental setting. τ is the returned-schedule idle exposure for separately prepared circuits. It is not elapsed browser time or a timestamp tracking one continuously observed quantum state. X and Y come from distinct circuits with matched exposure. Playback is slowed for inspection.


Aliasing remains unresolved. Echo and window points separated by 2 μs admit 500 kHz detuning aliases. Connector paths do not unwrap phase or select a physical trajectory between samples. Plain Ramsey has a near-zero-idle reference; the other conditions start at 3.604 μs.
