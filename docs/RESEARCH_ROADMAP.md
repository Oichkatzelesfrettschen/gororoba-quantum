# Coordinated mathematical research and QPU optimization

The project develops source-grounded mathematical certificates and quantum
experiments with explicit scientific endpoints, reproducible preparation,
measured accuracy, and charged-time accounting. The cumulative program combines
the claim audit, continuous rank geometry, proof engineering, native Qiskit
optimization, retained hardware evidence, and reviewed repository integration.
The immediate hardware priority is model discrimination followed by a measurable
optimizer decision before broad atlas expenditure. Exact topology research
progresses through its own mathematical dependencies.

The source library and [prior-work ledger](notes/prior_work_synthesis.md)
separate published results, reproduced results, new implementations or
measurements, and candidate new results. Agent handoffs contribute evidence and
proposals; their claims enter the program after source and executable checks.
A surprising finite observation needs its domain, falsifier, and prior-work
comparison before it receives a novelty claim.

homology is free of ranks `1,23,40,8,16,8` in degrees

integral cohomology classes to vanish. Those statements leave homotopy type
open and make no publication-priority claim.

The [execution roadmap](../gororoba_quantum/QPU_EXECUTION_ROADMAP.md) owns the
detailed timing, official-manual references, candidate precision tiers, and
runtime limitations. The [decision synthesis](notes/model_discrimination_optimizer.md)
owns the link from literature and observations to the next intervention.

1. Reuse the retained raw records, exact QPY, source identities, calibration
   windows, and final job usage. Reconcile scientific endpoints across the
   historical static case and the ripple/rank-sector proposals before spending
   on overlapping measurements.
2. Compare identifiable model hypotheses against the existing labeled data.
   Preserve the distinction between an added binary report channel, replacement
   readout model, continuing-state disturbance, coherent error, and temporal
   dependence. Fit and validate against separate observations.
3. Complete execution admission for the prepared known-label correction pilot owned by
   [PR #36](https://github.com/Oichkatzelesfrettschen/gororoba-quantum/pull/36).
   Labels `s1,s3` each receive measured feedback `F` and unconditional correction
   `U`: four PUBs at the selected 8192 shots. Both arms retain measurement,
   reset, and both output bits. Routed prefix/suffix, terminal writer, and
   schedule matching remain evidence gates. The [coordination ledger](../results/live/qpu-coordination.json)
   and [current campaign preparation](../results/ripple/CAMPAIGN_PREPARATION.md)
   own source-bound readiness and timing. The
   [exact correction case](../results/live/2026-10-12T170742.727534Z/UNAPPROVED_CASE.md)
   passes independent planning checks at source `f1b9830`. Clean current-head
   Copilot review, passing checks, merge and fresh live bindings remain gates
   under the user's conditional authority. Returned execution schedules qualify
   the matched inference after acquisition.
4. Apply the declared optimizer decision: both labels must satisfy
   `lower_CP(p_U)-upper_CP(p_F)>0.01`, with four one-sided tails of `0.0125`.
   Binomial assumptions support at least 95% simultaneous coverage. Independent
   accuracy validation and equal-or-lower charged time, or an explicitly
   justified tradeoff, precede policy adoption. Known labels cannot supply
   unavailable phase information to an unknown-phase algorithm.
5. Retain the fresh source-bound forecast and refresh its bindings before
   execution. The four-PUB correction case estimates 11.519211264 nominal
   seconds and 14.474571264 stress seconds under a 30-second ceiling. The
   current campaign totals 106.290103040 nominal seconds and 118.445463040
   stress seconds, with five job ceilings totaling 170 seconds. The earlier
   11.2425-second conditional estimate remains historical. The executed folded
   case attributes most nominal cost to repetition delay;
   precision selection, qualified initialization delay, and compatible endpoint
   packing therefore receive cost attention alongside circuit fidelity.
6. Qualify native mechanisms separately: the N21 shared-predicate challenger,
   measurement/reset-aware scheduling, explicit idle protection, mixed-job
   policy, and compatible static mitigation. The retained N21 challenger reduces
   unrouted CX from 243 to 204 per controlled multiplier; routed cost and noise
   admission determine its value. Two phase bits test a distribution change
   and provide zero verified order recovery.
7. Design full tomography or adaptive stopping only against an identified
   question. Four scalar probes of a real four-state sector do not identify a
   general ten-parameter symmetric Gram matrix. Instrument tomography requires
   continuing-state probes and its own identifiability assumptions. Sequential
   stopping requires valid time-uniform inference or a specified spending rule,
   with job-granularity costs. Repeated fixed-sample intervals lose their stated
   coverage under unrestricted stopping.
8. Admit a broad atlas only after its additional estimand, controls, effect or
   equivalence margins, multiplicity coverage, and incremental information value
   justify the expenditure. The prospective
   [practical accuracy protocol](../results/ripple/STATIC_PACKING_ACCURACY.md)
   supplies the current static criterion; its under-resolved weighted control
   prevents a blanket equivalence claim at the frozen shot allocation. The planning-qualified
   [ripple case](../results/live/2026-10-12T163354.310138Z/UNAPPROVED_CASE.md) and
   [rank case](../results/live/2026-10-12T163354.210407Z/UNAPPROVED_CASE.md)
   preserve their controls and joint shot vectors; independent execution
   admission follows the correction decision and remaining campaign gates.

connects Discussion Q4 to Biss-Dugger-Isaksen annihilator theory, Zhilina's

The mathematical chain has the following acceptance gates.

1. Preserve the exhaustive rank predicates and the published maximal base:
   dimension-32 nullity 16 has two copies of `V2(R7)`. Retain sparse and
   numerical censuses as fixtures and falsifiers for the continuous analysis.
2. Use the [certified four-coordinate geometry](notes/four_support_incidence_geometry.md)
   as a complete restricted attachment model. Its global frame and inverse
   reduce the partner incidence to five coordinate-block masks; its
   608 strict closure incidences retain the exact kernel inclusions.
   The [finite CW certificate](notes/four_support_cellular_homology.md)
   supplies embedded base disks, compatible fiber joins, oriented product
   boundaries, and 552 integral unit cancellations. Extend those mechanisms
   to other supports only after their own frame and attachment proofs pass.
   Use the [production skew-Schur charts](notes/dimension32_skew_schur_charts.md)
   to retain nonzero residuals and complete kernel lifts on explicit invertible
   pivot domains. Their same-tangent path fixture distinguishes first-order
   bounds from actual rank behavior. The [principal atlas](notes/principal_skew_atlas.md)
   replaces center selection with 15 explicit pivot domains and verifies
   residual-factor overlap, inverse, cocycle and metric identities. Its
   principal refinements give kernel bundles on each fixed-rank locus.
   The [acceleration certificates](notes/schur_acceleration_certificates.md)
   test `D(a)+2 B(h)^T A0^(-1) B(h)=0` with the fixed-norm condition.
   A separating cokernel functional also excludes a direction from the
   same-nullity tangent cone. Use those quadratic equations as necessary
   outer bounds, then resolve their real zero sets and higher-order
   compatibility. The [selected-center certificate](notes/nullity_twelve_local_geometry.md)
   resolves the complete real quadratic locus at `e1+e18` as `A15 union B17`,
   with a 13-dimensional intersection. Exact slice curves and automorphism
   flows realize `A15`; the published D-locus bundle realizes `B17`. The
   two inclusions prove the selected-center tangent cone and its nonlinearity.
   Actual local branch equations and attachments remain obligations.
   Resolve the other real reduced equations, their dependencies
   and real radicals before claiming smoothness or codimension.
3. Classify the connected components of nullities 4, 8, and 12 with certified
   coverage of all real coefficients. Subdivide the rank loci into appropriate
   smooth strata. Certify dimensions, normal links, closure incidences, and
   overlap coverage. Construct the actual
   kernel-bundle transition data, characteristic information, and equivalences.
4. Determine limiting kernel inclusions along every frontier. Different approach
   directions can produce different subspaces of an exceptional kernel; one
   polynomial path and a frontier adjacency matrix omit those maps.
5. Construct a global finite model with a proved relation to the complete pair
   space. Coste's triangulation, Hardt, and conic theorems establish existence
   frameworks; computed partitions, gluing maps, and coverage remain work.
6. Compute integral boundary maps, homology and torsion, cup products, and
   explicitly chosen homotopy invariants from that certified model. A matching
   Betti table alone establishes neither the model nor its homotopy type.
7. Prove a covering structural theorem for arbitrary dimension. Ordinary
   embedding doubles annihilators and the complete five-block frame for the
   specified restricted family at every `n>=5`. The same 1,200-cell chain
   matrices persist; a preserved top-factor grading shifts their degrees.
   Published fixed-deficit stability has its own hypotheses. The principal
   atlas covers all nonzero doubly-pure coefficients by `2^(n-1)-1` explicit
   domains for every `n>=2`. That all-stage gluing construction supplies
   equations and maps; unrestricted classification still requires solving
   their geometry and attachments.
8. Formalize chosen lemmas with explicit dependency and assumption manifests.
   Preserve full average/detail coordinates or all XOR sectors when information
   matters; average-only multiplication fails. Certify the proposed complex
   `16 x 16` skew representation before using Pfaffian equations as an
   implemented replacement. General support compilation also remains proposed.
   Kernel transport, algebra homomorphisms, and
   homotopy equivalences each require their own maps and identities.

## Ownership, approval, review, and cleanup

The [coordination ledger](../results/live/qpu-coordination.json) records family
ownership, accepted jobs, shared reservations, and observation times. The ledger
is cooperative coordination rather than an account-wide atomic lock. The
execution owner skips the unavailable mock backend and refreshes live
calibration on `ibm_fez`, `ibm_kingston`, and `ibm_marrakesh` immediately before
run preparation. The complete matched family competes across admitted systems
for the best accuracy/charge decision. Fresh usage, pending-job reconciliation,
compilation, and source/QPY identities determine the proposed case. Every changed physical
case requires its exact written approval; prior consumed approvals authorize
only their recorded execution.

The source integration workstream owns the source-library migration, registry
and provenance, and the retained source-audit/replay bundles. The experiment owner owns PR #36 and the prospective pilot;
that PR remains open under its separate instruction. The proof-planning
workstream owns the new dependency/tomography plan and average/detail
certificate. Each branch reconciles shared AGENTS gates when it integrates.
Concurrent ledger edits, retained results, and the paused CUDA branch keep their
owners. Scope changes update these dependencies before implementation.

Every integration uses a focused branch, local gates, current-head review,
resolved reachable findings, and clean GitHub mergeability. Copilot supplies the
requested substitute review when hosted Codex review quota is exhausted,
under the current-head admission rules in `AGENTS.md`. Canonical main then
fast-forwards while preserving concurrent edits. Verified merged Git worktrees
and branches receive removal/pruning; active work stays intact. A source-path
migration verifies hashes and references before replacing historical paths.
Source notes retain citations when a publisher notice requires permission for
public PDF redistribution.

The completion claims follow evidence: source integration, finite replay,
formal proof, software qualification, physical measurement, and global
classification remain separately named deliverables. The complete dimension-32
topology and unrestricted all-dimensional classification remain open research
obligations.
