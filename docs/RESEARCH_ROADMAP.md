# Coordinated mathematical research and QPU optimization

The project develops source-grounded mathematical certificates and quantum
experiments with explicit scientific endpoints, reproducible preparation,
measured accuracy, and charged-time accounting. The cumulative program combines
the claim audit, continuous rank geometry, proof engineering, native Qiskit
optimization, retained hardware evidence, and reviewed repository integration.
The immediate hardware priority is model discrimination followed by a measurable
optimizer decision before broad atlas expenditure. Exact topology research
progresses through its own mathematical dependencies.

The source library and [prior-work ledger](notes/prior_work_synthesis.md)
separate published results, reproduced results, new implementations or
measurements, and candidate new results. Agent handoffs contribute evidence and
proposals; their claims enter the program after source and executable checks.
A surprising finite observation needs its domain, falsifier, and prior-work
comparison before it receives a novelty claim.

For the restricted ordinary embeddings, set `h=4*2^(n-5)`. Integral
homology is free of ranks `1,23,40,8,16,8` in degrees
`0,2,h+1,2h,3h,4h-1`, respectively, and vanishes in other degrees.
The characteristic maps identify the actual space with the finite CW model;
unimodular cancellations establish the integral groups and zero torsion.
The resulting degree support forces every product of two positive-degree
integral cohomology classes to vanish. Those statements leave homotopy type
open and make no publication-priority claim.
