# Understanding Platforms

On your dashboard (see [Product Tour](https://cloud.quandela.com/webide/guide/user-guide/01-product-tour)) - you can see the list of available platforms you have been granted access too.

For each platform - you have a status card giving you name, availability of the platform, and pending jobs waiting for computing:

![image-20221117-141815.png](https://cloud.quandela.com/webide/docs-assets/97976331.png)

When clicking on `See detail` you get access to the complete information sheet of the platform.

In particular, on the right side of the sheet, the following panel gives valuable information:

![image-20221117-142928.png](https://cloud.quandela.com/webide/docs-assets/98271266.png)

- **Available Commands** gives the native command supported by the platform
- **Parameters** gives the parameters that you can use to configure the platform
- **Characterization** are dynamic information reported dynamically by the platform
- **Constraints** are limitation of use to the platform that are native to the platform or coming from your subscription plan.
