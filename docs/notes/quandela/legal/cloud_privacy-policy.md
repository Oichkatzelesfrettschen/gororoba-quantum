### A. Tracking information and Use of Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser to a website during your visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows the Service or a third-party to recognize you and make your next visit easier and the Service more useful to you.

When you use and access our website or our services we may place a number of cookies files in your web browser. We use cookies for the following purposes: to enable certain functions of the Service, to provide analytics, to store your preferences.

The only information QUANDELA automatically collects is the tracking information related to the use of its Services. This information is gathered through Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. (Google) Google Analytics uses cookies, which are text files placed on visitors’ computers to help QUANDELA analyze how users use our websites. The information generated by the cookie about how visitors use the website is transmitted and stored by Google on their servers in the United States. Google will use this information to evaluate the website, to compile reports on its activity, and to provide QUANDELA with other services related to the activity of the website and the use of the Internet.

You can find detailed information about how Google uses your data in Google’s privacy statement: [www.google.com/policies/privacy/](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)

The legal basis for using Google Analytics is your consent. You can disable Google Analytics at any time by downloading and installing a browser module available at: [http://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

User event data associated with cookies on Google Analytics is deleted after thirteen (13) months.

## 9. Your rights in relation to your Personal Data

You have the following rights in relation to your Personal Data:

- **A right to be informed: **you are entitled to receive information from QUANDELA (please contact contact@quandela.com) regarding the processing of your personal Data. The information will be provided in a concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible document, written in clear and plain language, and free of charge;
- **A right to access **to your Personal Data, without constraint at reasonable intervals and without excessive delay. **A right to rectify:** you are entitled to have your Personal Data rectified if inaccurate or incomplete and QUANDELA will respond to a rectification request within one (1) month if not deemed complex;
- **The right to erasure: **if you request QUANDELA to erase your personal data, we must comply easily and securely, as long as there is no compelling reason for possession and continued processing.
- **A right to object**, on legitimate grounds, and to withdraw your consent, to the processing of your Personal Data. You also have the right to object, on request and free of charge, to the processing of Personal Data that QUANDELA anticipates being processed for the purposes of direct marketing.
- A right to cancel or limit the processing of your Personal Data, which entails that in certain cases you can request us to temporally suspend the processing of the data or that we keep them longer than necessary when you may need it;
- A right to provide instructions regarding the storage, deletion or disclosure of your personal data after your death;
- A right to request the portability of your personal data: you are entitled to receive the personal data that you made available to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-legible format, to be able to transmit them to another entity directly.

You may exercise your rights free of charge by sending to us an e-mail (to a single e-mail address <dataprotection@quandela.com>), simply informing us of the reason of your request and the right that you wish to exercise. If we consider necessary to identify you, we may request you to provide us with a copy of a document evidencing your identity.

Finally, we inform you that you have the right to file a claim before the responsible data protection regulatory authority, in particular, before the French Data Protection Authority (Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés).
