# Bell states

The Bell states are specific quantum states of two qubits that represent the simplest (and maximal) examples of quantum entanglement.

$`\vert\Phi^{\pm}\rangle =\frac{\vert 00 \rangle \pm \vert 11 \rangle}{\sqrt{2}}`$

$`\vert\Psi^{\pm}\rangle =\frac{\vert 01 \rangle \pm \vert 10 \rangle}{\sqrt{2}}`$

For more information see the wikipedia page on [Bell states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_state).
