# Restrictive measures against Russia

As per [Council Decision (CFSP) 2022/327](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L:2022:048:TOC) and [Council Regulation 2022/328](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R0328), the EU Commission amended the restrictive measures against Russia (“Russia sanctions”). Among other things, these new rules prohibit the sale, supply, transfer or export (direct and indirect) of the following to any person/entity of Russia or for use in Russia:

- goods and technology suited for use in oil refining;
- goods and technology suited for use in the aviation and space industry;
- dual-use goods and technology listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 (“the EU Dual-Use List”); and
- goods and technology which might contribute to Russia’s military and technological enhancement, or the development of the defence and security sector. This also includes certain quantum-related technology, including i.e.:

1.  Cat. X.A.IV.006 “Superconductive” electromagnetic sensors, components manufactured from “superconductive” materials: 1. Designed for operation at temperatures below the “critical temperature” of at least one of their “superconductive” constituents (including Josephson effect devices or “superconductive” quantum interference devices (SQUIDS));
2.  Cat. X.E.IV.001 “Technology” for the “development”, “production” or “use” of equipment controlled by X.A.IV.001, X.A.IV.006, X.A.IV.007, X.A.IV.008 or X.A.IV.009.c.

In view of the current situation and QuTech policy in this matter we will screen against (inter)national sanctions- and export controls regulations. This could limit or prevent access to our systems for certain entities.

## Technical Constraints

The computing resources (quantum computers and quantum computer simulators) are shared by all users. Requests for execution may not be executed immediately but will be placed on a queue. Each backend imposes a limit on the number of qubits that can be executed upon or simulated. The number of shots for each execution request is also limited. In addition, a limit on the amount of time an execution may run will be enforced by aborting running executions that exceed this limit. Code has to be written on the website or submitted via the API (either by using an SDK or by initiating requests to the API directly). Limits apply to the amount of data that can be stored in the result database, and depending on the amount of inspection instructions placed by the user in the algorithm, result data may be truncated.
