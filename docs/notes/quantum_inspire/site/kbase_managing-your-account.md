# Managing your account

## Account types

Unlike Quantum Inspire 1.0, which offered three different types of account, we now only have one account. With this ***free*** account you can access our hardware backends, emulator backends and have storage for your jobs and results!

## Creating an account

Creating an account can be done by clicking on the `MyQI` button which brings you to our [quantum compute domain](https://compute.quantum-inspire.com) where you can sign up for an account using your email address or using one of the selected authentication providers.

## Log in to your account

Simply click on `My QI` to log in to your account. The login screen also provides a link to reset your password if you have lost it. When using the [QI tool](https://www.quantum-inspire.com/kbase/managing-your-account/kbase/software-development-kit/), you can login through the Command Line Interface (CLI).

## Managing newsletters

As a Quantum Inspire registered user, you may sometime receive newsletters or other important information from us. You can always opt out for our newsletters using the link provided in the newsletter.

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account can be done from your [account page](https://compute.quantum-inspire.com/auth/account/).
