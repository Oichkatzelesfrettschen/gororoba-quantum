## Connection to the proposed pair-sector experiment

[I] The source supports the prior existence of boson-to-qubit optical
simulation and the DCE proposal. A precise attribution is:

> Sabin derived digital beam-splitter and two-mode-squeezing constructions,
> reported a small beam-splitter experiment on IBM Q5 Tenerife, and proposed
> digital DCE simulation as an application.

[I] The source does not establish the proposal's `V|n>=|n,n>` reduction,
four-level binary Pauli coefficients, cutoff-error table, pump-phase contrast,
Heron gate cost, or paid execution performance. Those are repository-specific
claims requiring separately retained evidence. Optical phase and evolution
sign conventions must be fixed before importing any displayed Pauli formula.

[I] For the proposed pair Hamiltonian, verify `H_full V=V H_red` and each
observable's compression `O_red=V^dagger O_full V`. Equal number occupation
then follows from the representation; an independent full encoding is needed
to observe leakage out of that sector. Mode-local loss or interventions can
leave the sector and require an enlarged representation.

[P] The cutoff discussion on p. 7 uses mean occupation and a qualitative
low-multiple-pair argument. [I] A low mean occupation alone is insufficient
as a bound on finite-Hamiltonian evolution or a chosen correlator. Retain
cutoff-specific operator dynamics, top-level occupation, and convergence of
each preregistered observable. A source's proposed cutoff is a starting
comparison, not an inherited error certificate.
