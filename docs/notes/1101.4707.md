## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Decoherence follows the Bloch-Redfield rates Gamma_1 and Gamma_2 = Gamma_1/2 + Gamma_phi; dephasing, unlike energy relaxation, is in principle reversible by dynamical refocusing.
- [P] Dynamical decoupling sequences act as filters on the noise spectrum, which permits noise spectroscopy from the coherence decay under each sequence.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Plain Ramsey visibility against echoed visibility at matched durations tests whether the window loss is refocusable low-frequency noise.
