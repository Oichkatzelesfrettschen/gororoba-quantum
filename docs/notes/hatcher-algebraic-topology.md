[P] Collapsing a contractible subspace with the homotopy extension property
preserves homotopy type. Homotopic attaching maps for a CW pair give
homotopy-equivalent adjunction spaces. The pair/cofibration hypotheses matter.

[I] The repository uses these results after constructing its restricted
mapping-cylinder incidence model and explicit endpoint nullhomotopies.
The propositions justify that reduction. They supply neither a construction
of the complete dimension-32 base nor its missing attachment maps.
Relevance: topology method high; hardware compilation and factoring none.
