## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Five qubits are read through one 1.2 GHz wide channel with 80 ns pulses, resonator population below 250 ns and an average correct-assignment probability of 97 percent.
- [P] The individual readout errors and those found when measuring the qubits simultaneously differ by under 1 percent (abstract).
- [P] Individual Purcell filters per resonator suppress off-resonant driving; the paper characterizes the dephasing imposed on unintentionally measured qubits.

## Limits

- [P/I] ETH Zurich lab, not QuTech; the device architecture differs from Quantum Inspire's.
- [P/I] Crosstalk is quantified for fresh readout, not conditioned on a prior CZ ladder; the under-1-percent bound applies to that device.
