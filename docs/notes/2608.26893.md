## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] The ambient field has characteristic different from two. A doubly
  alternative element `(a,b)` has both components alternative in the previous
  algebra. Strong alternation of `a,b` means both associators `[a,a,b]` and
  `[b,b,a]` vanish (Definitions 2.17 and 4.18).
- [P] Lemma 4.19 describes left and right annihilators of a doubly alternative
  element when `n(a)` is nonzero. The formulas determine the second component
  from the first, subject to a remaining associator relation. The component
  hypothesis matters in dimension 32 because sedenion components can fail
  alternativity.
- [P] Under anisotropic norm, Lemma 5.1 gives symmetry of zero products and
  double purity of every nonzero zero divisor. In particular, left and right
  annihilators agree with the algebraic orthogonalizer. Here orthogonalizer
  means simultaneous products `ab=ba=0`, rather than merely zero inner product.
- [P] Section 6 extends Biss-Dugger-Isaksen's divisibility argument to
  anisotropic Cayley-Dickson algebras over the stated fields. Theorem 6.11
  gives annihilator dimension divisible by four for `n>=2`; zero has the full
  algebra as annihilator. Examples 6.3-6.5 show how the divisibility or parity
  conclusions can fail with isotropic norm. Their hypotheses differ from the
  repository's positive-definite real algebra.
- [P] Lemmas 6.6-6.9 construct a quadratic field `K`, a `K`-Hermitian form,
  and a conjugate-linear skew-Hermitian multiplication map for doubly pure
  elements. Lemma 6.10 writes that map as `L(z)=conj(A*z)` and proves
  `H*A=-(H*A)^T`, where `H` is the Hermitian-form matrix. The skew-symmetric
  matrix has even rank; the field degree supplies the second factor of two.

## Connection to dimension-32 rank geometry

[I] The source supplies a concrete reduction beyond sparse graph enumeration.
For the real positive-definite dimension-32 algebra, `K=C` and an orthonormal
complex coordinate system has 16 coordinates. A certified coordinate map
therefore expresses multiplication by doubly pure `x` using a 16-by-16 complex
skew-symmetric matrix. Its complex rank is half the real rank of `L_x`.
This statement concerns the operator's linear-algebra structure; a coordinate
implementation must prove the intertwining identity and its convention.

[I] Once that identity is certified, rank `32-4*k` corresponds to complex rank
`16-2*k`. Skew rank predicates use vanishing Pfaffians of order `18-2*k` and
a nonzero Pfaffian of order `16-2*k`. Real and imaginary parts give polynomial
conditions on the real coefficients. A smaller invertible skew pivot then
supplies a kernel chart and overlap transformations. These are standard
skew-matrix deductions, rather than a new theorem attributed to the paper.

[I] Pfaffian equations offer another presentation of the same rank locus as
[the trace/Newton predicates](../../gororoba_quantum/rank_polynomials.py).
The restriction of a determinantal variety to the Cayley-Dickson coefficient
space can still be singular. Independent equations, tangent-rank checks,
smooth subdivision, closure incidences, and attachment data remain required.
The paper supplies neither the full dimension-32 pair-space topology nor a
proof that its doubly alternative families cover all real coefficient vectors.

[I] The useful next checks are exact: certify the complex coordinate map,
compare real and complex ranks on arbitrary rational inputs, and verify each
new polynomial identity symbolically. The comparison with
[BCDI eigentheory](0905.2987.md) separates the all-eigenspace multiplicity
result from the zero-eigenspace argument developed here. QPU observations
probe a compiled operator; they cannot certify these universal algebraic
identities or global topology.

## Implemented coordinate certificate

The [complex skew certificate](complex_skew_rank_geometry.md) implements
`Phi(c,d)=c+i*conjugate(d)` and `K=L_a-i*R_b`. The identity is
`Phi(L_x y)=K*conjugate(Phi(y))`; the source convention
`L(z)=conjugate(A*z)` corresponds to `A=conjugate(K)`. The proof note
retains conjugation in the kernel transport, constructs the coefficient-pair
cover and supplies exact Pfaffian/kernel controls. These implementation results
resolve the proposed coordinate check above and preserve the source priority
and open geometric boundaries.
