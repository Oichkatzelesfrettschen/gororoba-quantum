The paper uses the repository's Cayley-Dickson multiplication convention.
Its `B_x=L_(conj(x)) L_x` equals `L_x^T L_x` over the reals. The polynomial
`N=sum x_i^2` is the squared Euclidean input norm; it differs from the LCU
normalization `alpha=sum |x_i|` used by a block-encoding implementation.

## Statements used

| Locator | Source statement | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definitions 3.1 and 3.3 | `q_j=tr(B_x^j)/4-N^j`; Newton recursion constructs `E_0,...,E_7`; `D14=E7` | The construction supplies rational polynomial expressions without expanding every monomial |
| Proposition 4.3 | `E7` is the product of seven nonnegative residual fourfold spectral blocks after removing one block `N` | Positivity applies to real coefficient vectors |
| Theorem 5.3 | `det L_x=N^2 E7^2` | A determinant identity does not identify kernel bundles or attachments |
| Corollary 7.1 | For `x != 0`, zero-divisor membership is equivalent to `E7=0` | The zero input needs separate treatment |
| Theorem 9.1 | For `n>=2`, use `m=2^(n-2)-1` and obtain `det L_x=N^2 E_m^2` | An all-dimensional identity differs from an all-dimensional topological classification |
| Discussion question 4 | Describe the zero-divisor strata according to kernel dimension; dimension 32 has maximum nullity 16 | The paper explicitly leaves the geometric rank-stratum problem open |

The source derives fourfold multiplicity and the distinguished norm block
from Biss-Christensen-Dugger-Isaksen's eigentheory. The source ledger records
that provenance. Remark 4.2 suggests that globally continuous eigenvalue
labels may fail; ordered eigenvalues of real symmetric matrices do admit
continuous labeling. The symmetric Newton construction requires no such
labeling assumption and survives that wording correction.

The complexification certificate in Proposition 8.3 uses the bilinear
transpose and `N=sum z_i^2`, rather than a Hermitian norm. Its conclusion
that `N` does not divide `E7` does not transfer real-domain spectral
positivity to complex coefficients.

## Repository use and outstanding geometry

[rank_polynomials.py](../../gororoba_quantum/rank_polynomials.py) evaluates
the trace/Newton coefficients exactly at rational inputs and checks the
characteristic identity against independent matrix computation. The written
nonnegative-spectrum argument gives the all-real predicate
`nullity(L_x)=4k` exactly when `E_(7-k)>0` and `E_(8-k)=0`, for nonzero
dimension-32 inputs and `k=1,...,4`. This is a local deduction from the
source's spectral construction, not a claim that the paper classifies all
rank components.

[rank_charts.py](../../gororoba_quantum/rank_charts.py) supplies fixed-minor
kernel charts and overlap identities. The certified path in
[rank_attachments.py](../../gororoba_quantum/rank_attachments.py) and its
[topological model](../../gororoba_quantum/rank_path_topology.py) classify
the incidence over a specified one-parameter family. Their scope excludes
arbitrary bases outside that family.

The [research decomposition](koebisu_rank_stratum_research.md) explains how
local skew Schur reductions, continuous-rank stratification, and limiting
kernel maps can advance Discussion Q4. Its proposed extensions separate
mathematical proof obligations from hardware model discrimination. A QPU
norm measurement tests a selected noisy operator implementation; the
measurement supplies neither an all-real rank proof nor global topology.
