## Main claims and evidence

- [P] T1 of a transmon fluctuates unpredictably by up to an order of magnitude in time and in frequency; the paper resolves T1 spectrally and temporally and attributes the largest fluctuations to individual two-level-system (TLS) defects whose transition frequencies diffuse (abstract).
- [P] A spectroscopic T1 trace over 400 MHz at 1 MHz resolution takes about 15 minutes; fluctuations between extrema occur abruptly on 15-minute timescales and across 5 MHz.
- [P] TLS in the telegraphic regime switch between discrete frequencies; those in the diffusive regime wander with a diffusivity of 2.5 +/- 0.1 MHz per square-root hour.
- [P] Weak periodic features in the T1 spectrum are assigned to modes of the qubit control lines, and a sharp resonance to carrier bleedthrough; these are periodic in qubit frequency, not in time.

## Limits

- [P/I] Timescales are minutes to hours; a periodic modulation with a 50-shot period (tens of milliseconds at plausible shot rates) lies far above anything this paper measures.
- [P/I] TLS physics modulates T1 and frequency; it predicts no sharp periodic line, and the paper reports none.
- [P/I] Google Xmon-style devices with fixed couplers; the CZ-gate conditional phase is not studied.
