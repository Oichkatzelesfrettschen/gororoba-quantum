## Relevance

[P] The paper introduces the 42 assessor planes (diagonal axis-pair systems),
seven box-kites, 28 triangular sails, and signed zero-product relationships
among sedenion zero divisors. [I] These are the combinatorial structures
underlying the repository's 42-assessor census, the seven kite partition,
and the sail/frame plane distinction: the F-D-B sail and A-F strut
constructions used in the measurement analysis originate here. [P] The
abstract describes normed zero divisors via G2 and a breakdown of
multiplication-table equivalence classes. [I] The paper's physical and
philosophical extrapolations (singularities, networks) are hypotheses, not
established results, and are treated as such.
