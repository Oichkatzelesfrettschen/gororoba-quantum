## Implementation consequences

[I] The source is a precedent for qubit-based calculation of a specified,
regularized field energy. It is not evidence that a gate-model computer
measured an unknown absolute vacuum offset, mechanical force, or cosmological
vacuum energy. The physical interpretation enters through the supplied
Hamiltonian, units, boundary conditions, and subtraction convention.

[I] Reconstruct the selected finite Hamiltonian independently before adopting
the numerical tables as an oracle. The paper's printed frequency/index
notation and its descriptions of register size require clarification for an
exact reproduction. A complete local artifact should state mode count,
lattice spacing/size, occupation cutoff, boundary conditions, matrix shape,
tensor order, fermion parity convention, Pauli coefficients, and the energy
reference. The historical Qiskit Aqua implementation does not define the
current Runtime interface.

[I] For a modeled separation `d` in a fixed basis,
`H(d)=c_I(d)I+sum_j c_j(d)P_j` gives the force operator
`F_hat(d)=-c_I'(d)I-sum_j c_j'(d)P_j`. This is a repository design inference;
the paper computes energies and does not supply this force-estimator
implementation. Retaining the derivative of the identity coefficient is
essential when that coefficient depends on `d`.

[I] The equality `-dE0/dd=<F_hat>` requires an exact differentiable eigenstate
under the usual Hellmann-Feynman assumptions. Approximate VQE states still
define force-operator expectations, but their values need not equal numerical
derivatives of incompletely optimized energy curves. Distance-dependent
bases require their derivative terms. A force in newtons additionally needs
a declared physical length and energy scale; changing a dimensionless
parameter alone gives a generalized force in energy units.

[I] A useful repository gate compares the direct force-operator expectation,
the derivative of the independently solved finite-model energy, and a
controlled finite-difference sequence. Repeat the comparison while varying
occupation cutoff and spatial regulator separately. Ordinary cloud execution
then measures model Pauli expectations; a physical force claim needs a
calibrated physical transducer and its observation chain.

[P] The conclusion proposes parallel computation on smaller partitions.
[I] That proposal supplies a prior idea, rather than measured packing savings
or a proof that an interacting Hamiltonian separates. Establish additivity
or a valid decomposition, preserve covariance and measurement accounting,
and retain actual compiled and charged cost before applying the idea to
simultaneous repository experiments.
