## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A millimeter-wave dielectric haloscope of dielectric disks and a mirror searched for randomly polarized dark photon dark matter and found none.
- [P] The kinetic-mixing constraint over 387.72 to 391.03 ueV improves earlier limits by two orders of magnitude.

## Mapping to this repository
