## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Betting-based nonnegative martingales give confidence intervals and time-uniform confidence sequences for the mean of bounded observations, generalizing the Chernoff method.
- [P] The bounds adapt to the unknown variance and empirically outperform Hoeffding and empirical Bernstein bounds; the construction extends to sampling without replacement.
