## Mathematical content used

[P] PDF pages 2-3 describe 84 diagonal zero-divisor lines in 42 assessor
planes. An assessor uses an octonion index `o` in `1..7` and a sedenion index
`S` greater than 8, with `S != o XOR 8`. The diagonals `e_o +/- e_S` carry
the primitive zero divisors. The statement concerns those lines, rather than
every vector in an assessor plane or the complete continuous zero-divisor
locus.

[P] Page 3 organizes the assessors into seven octahedral box kites. Positive
edge signs indicate cancellation between equally oriented diagonals; negative
edge signs indicate cancellation between oppositely oriented diagonals.
The strut constant `s` labels the omitted octonion index. An assessor's upper
index is its lower index XOR `(8+s)`. Opposite vertices have lower indices
whose XOR is `s`. The paper presents the strut constant rule without proof
at that location.

[P] The construction gives two opposite triangles negative edge signs and
the six remaining edges positive signs.

[I] Every triangular face therefore has negative sign product. The raw
counts of positive and negative edges depend on the chosen diagonal
orientations; cycle products survive vertex sign switching.
