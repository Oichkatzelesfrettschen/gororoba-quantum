## Relevance

[P] The paper observes that any pair of zero divisors in the sedenions
produces a double hexagon in the orthogonality graph, and classifies
connected components of the zero-product relation graph. [I] This is the
formal graph-theoretic counterpart to the historical box-kite cycles: an
independent mathematical check on the 16D assessor census structure. [I]
The graph vertices, de Marrais's assessor planes, and the 21-dimensional
observable space are not interchangeable without an explicit map between
vertex sets and relations.
