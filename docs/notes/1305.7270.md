## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Weak measurement of a microwave cavity that embeds a transmon, amplified by a near-quantum-limited Josephson parametric amplifier, tracks individual quantum trajectories conditioned on the measurement record.
- [P] The amplified quadrature selects the backaction: trajectories confine to the equator or a meridian of the Bloch sphere.
- [P] Quantum state tomography at discrete times along the trajectory verifies that the inferred state was tracked faithfully.
