## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Y6 3x2pt gives S_8 = 0.789 +- 0.012 and Omega_m = 0.333 (+0.023/-0.028) in LambdaCDM, a 1.8 sigma full-space parameter difference from combined CMB data (2.6 sigma projected into S_8).
- [P] In wCDM, w = -1.12 (+0.26/-0.20); combining all DES dark-energy probes gives a 2.8 sigma LambdaCDM parameter difference from the CMB, and 3x2pt with other low-redshift data 2.3 sigma; the joint fit gives w = -0.981 (+0.021/-0.022).
