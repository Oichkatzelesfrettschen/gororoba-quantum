## Main claims and evidence

- [P] The pathfinder run's dark-photon exclusion bound is refined through improved modeling of frequency instability in high-quality resonators.
- [P] The constraint is about an order of magnitude stronger than the earlier report, a signal-to-noise ratio four orders of magnitude larger.
- [P] It is the leading constraint on non-dark-matter dark photons below 6 ueV and gives a laboratory photon-mass limit m_gamma < 2.9e-48 g.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Frequency jitter enters a resonant search as a fitted nuisance; a residual from an unmodeled instability is not a new field. The repository's drift and calibration nuisances ([1907.13608](1907.13608.md)) play the same role for QPU residuals.
- [I] Any dark-photon proposal in [vacuum_field_run_opportunities.md](vacuum_field_run_opportunities.md) compares its parameter region with this exclusion before claiming sensitivity.
