## Model and observable boundaries

- [P] Section I.A, p. 6, Eq. (2) gives the zero-temperature ideal-metal
  plane-plane energy per area `-pi^2*hbar*c/(720*a^3)` and pressure
  `-pi^2*hbar*c/(240*a^4)`. The derivation compares configurations; ideal
  reflectivity is part of its assumptions.
- [P] Section II.A, pp. 16-18, Eqs. (3)-(9) expresses free energy per area
  and pressure in terms of material reflection coefficients at Matsubara
  frequencies. The zero-frequency term has half weight. Eq. (8) is
  `P(a,T)=-partial_a F(a,T)` for free energy per unit area.
- [P] Thermal equilibrium is explicit in the Lifshitz setup. Frequency-
  dependent permittivity describes the response; the displayed simple
  Fresnel coefficients assume homogeneous nonmagnetic media with temporal
  dispersion. Layering, finite thickness, and anisotropy require modified
  reflection coefficients.
- [P] Section III.A.1, pp. 36-37 connects the imaginary-frequency response
  with optical data and a dispersion relation whose applicability depends
  on the analytic structure of the response.

[I] The ideal formula can serve as a benchmark. A prediction for a real
specimen requires the declared geometry, material response, temperature,
and applicable approximations. A finite scalar-ring model or an oscillator
matrix constructed from Cayley-Dickson singular values carries its own
physical assumptions; it is not automatically the electromagnetic interaction
of the material plates in a Lifshitz calculation.

## The physical force measurement chain

[P] Section IV.A, pp. 55-58 describes a metal-coated sphere and plate.
The interaction bends a cantilever. A laser and photodiodes produce a
deflection signal `S_def`. Electrostatic calibration determines conversion
coefficients and residual potential; piezo motion is calibrated by an
interferometer. Eq. (83) writes the actual gap as
`a=a0+a_piezo+S_def*m`. Hooke's law gives the measured force as
`S_def*k*m`, before the stated separation-dependent background treatment.
Conductivity and roughness enter comparisons with theory.

[I] The transferable lesson is a complete observation chain:
physical interaction, transducer response, readout signal, calibration,
units, and nuisance corrections. A cloud qubit's bit counts, frequency
shift, or relaxation rate lack a force conversion until a specified physical
coupling and calibration supplies it. A QPU expectation of an encoded
`-partial_d H` is a model-force estimate; it is a different observation
chain from the cantilever experiment.
