## Relevance

[P] The paper gives systematic production rules for box-kites, strut
constants, and the combinatorics connecting assessor diagonals. [I] These
rules underlie the strut-constant indexing (e.g., S=3 for the sail/frame
planes) used in the measurement analysis. [P] The paper proposes that
zero divisors derived by the Cayley-Dickson process can represent
singularities and, in the infinite limit, fractals and scale-free networks.
[I] Those are hypotheses, not established results; the combinatorial
production rules are the verifiable contribution relevant here.
