# Methods of the A_5 fiber-cone program and the sources that justify them

The A_5 fiber-cone program ([ledger](../../evidence/a5-exceptional-fibers/ledger.tsv),
[findings](a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)) studies, at a point of a deeper nullity stratum of the 32-dimensional
Cayley-Dickson algebra, the limits of kernels along a shallower stratum, as the special fiber of a blow-up. This note
maps each method the ledger uses to the registered source and the numbered statement that justifies it, and names the
gap where the printed statement is narrower than the use. Each source has a note under `docs/notes/papers/` with the
pages read; a statement is quoted here only by its number and page.

Evidence status. Every theorem number and page below is taken from the linked source note, which records the page
image it was read from. The "Gap" entries are repository readings, tagged [I] in those notes. No computation ran for
this note.

| The Cohen-Macaulay test of `F(I)` by the numerator of its Hilbert series (row A5-245; rows A5-305, A5-312 and A5-423 record Hilbert numerators) | [D'Cruz, Raghavan and Verma](../papers/math-0212119.md), Theorem 2.1 (p. 2); Bruns and Herzog, Section 4.1 (Hilbert series of a homogeneous ring, pp. 147-155) | Theorem 2.1 is stated with a minimal reduction, so with the infinite residue field of the previous row |

## What no registered source supplies

- A comparison of a standard basis over `F_p` with one over `Q(i)` for an ideal that is not homogeneous in any positive
  grading: Arnold, Pfister 2007 and Boku et al. cover the homogeneous and the local cases.
- A dimension bound for a fiber cone from the rank of jets of arc families.
- The Rees algebra, fiber cone or Hilbert function of the ideal of 4-Pfaffians of a specialized `9 x 9` alternating
  matrix.
- An identification of a variety of limiting annihilators of a Cayley-Dickson algebra with a named variety: the search
  recorded in [cd-annihilator-varieties-prior-art.md](cd-annihilator-varieties-prior-art.md) found none.
