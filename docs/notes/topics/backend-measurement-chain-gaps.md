# Measurement-chain gaps of Tuna-17, QX, Ry, Ascella and Belenos

Scope: the measurement chain of each backend (state preparation, gates or linear optics, noise channels, readout and
assignment, sampling, batching and queueing, drift) against the code, cases and retained records of gororoba-quantum
(main at `3004b42e8`, and branch `qi-one-cz-null-twin-rule-v2` of pull request 881 for the rule-v2 files and the
bracketed successor case `results/quantum_inspire/cases/pathion_one_cz_null_bracketed_tuna_17/`, named "the successor
CASE.md" below). Paths
are repository-relative. Each candidate was tested against the source line, record or measurement that could cover it;
only survivors enter the table. Evidence class per row: measured (computed here from retained records), documented
(stated by a repository or platform source and left untested), computed (arithmetic or a local Perceval run), or
hypothesis. A row whose falsifier is a computation on retained records that this sweep did not run says
"falsification not attempted". Novelty claims are bounded to the files named in each row. No provider job, MCP tool or
credential was used.

Local computations of this sweep (scratch scripts, read-only on the records):

- Bracket offset per batch, c = (e10 - e01)/2 of the selector q8, over the 8 retained batches that hold both readouts
  (batches 00, 03, 04, 07, 08, 11, 12, 15 of `results/quantum_inspire/runs/2026-11-07T202323Z-pathion_one_cz_null_tuna_17-*`):
  chi-square 61.56 on 7 dof about the weighted mean, range -0.00136 to +0.00319; lambda chi-square 39.55 on 7 dof;
  correlation of e01 and e10 across those batches -0.05.
- Step model with segments batches 00-02, 03-07 and 08-15, breakpoints chosen after looking at e01: selector e01
  5.68 on 9 dof within segments (84.41 on 11 dof for one mean); e10 29.96 on 9 dof (57.96 on 11); c 12.74 on 5 dof
  (61.56 on 7). The step model fits e01 and does not fit e10.
- Within-job stationarity of the selector reading from `raw/*.json` (per-shot lists, 16 consecutive blocks per job, 48
  template, twin and readout jobs): sum chi-square 716.4 on 720 dof. This reads the list order as acquisition order,
  which no platform source states, and tests inside a job only, not across the jobs of a batch.
- Queue wait (submission to first execution, from `submission.json` `submitted_utc` and result `created_on` minus
  `execution_time_in_seconds`): 22 to 70 min, mean 41.8 min over 19 batches; the series spans 16.50 h.
- Base contrasts from `results/quantum_inspire/pathion_one_cz_null_tuna_17_analysis.v3.json` `batch_contrasts`:
  b0 0.01624 then 0.03929 (se 0.00276 each); k4 -0.00440, -0.00571; k8 -0.00238, -0.00224; k12 0.00017 then -0.01452
  (se 0.0055 each, z 1.88). CZ count per template file: b0 21, k4, k8 and k12 6 each.
- Perceval 1.3.1 two-photon HOM on a 50:50 beam splitter (`pcvl.Processor("SLOS", pcvl.BS(), noise=NoiseModel(...))`,
  input |1,1>, no photon filter, photon-number-resolved coincidences, V = 1 - P(coinc)/0.5): at g2 = 0 the visibility
  equals `indistinguishability` exactly; (M 0.8728, g2 0.020) gives V 0.8537; (M 0.911, g2 0.020) 0.8915;
  (M 0.825, g2 0.009) 0.8167; (M 0.842, g2 0.009) 0.8336; (M 0.9438, g2 0.00732) 0.9366; (M 0.9296, g2 0.00732) 0.9225.
- Photon-number check over `results/quandela/runs/*/results.json`: 154 of 166 files carry output-state keys; 3504 keys
  (2413 QPU, 799 simulator, 292 other), none with more than one count in a mode.

## (b) Refuted candidates

- FAQ "3 jobs" ambiguity: the runner satisfies both readings with 3 circuits per batch and one queued batch (`scripts/qi_platform.py:925-927`; `usage.md:55-90`).
- Four-circuit bracketed pair `[r0, c0, c1, r1]`: refused by the same rule, as the successor CASE.md states ("Derived coefficients" item 1); a constraint, whose consequence row 4 carries.
- Compile stage `none` against the decomposer passes for the one-CZ series: every circuit uses only CZ, Rz, X90 and X (`results/quantum_inspire/cases/pathion_one_cz_null_tuna_17/batch-01/*.cq`), and the SDK's own run flow also uploads at NONE (quantuminspire 4.2.0, `quantuminspire/managers/resource_manager.py:613`, `compile_stage=CompileStage.NONE`).
- Rotation quantization: all 17 distinct Rz angles of the case lie on the pi/36 grid (computed).
- Register width for the one-CZ series: all 19 retained batches declare 17 qubits.
- Mid-circuit measurement for a classical coin: The successor CASE.md ("Designs rejected") rejects it because a measurement on the coin edge adds the coin-conditioned readout channel under test; that reason stands. Its added clause "unmeasured on Tuna-17" is too broad: retained mid-circuit readout runs exist (`results/quantum_inspire/runs/2026-10-17T032034Z-readout_ringdown_tuna_17_P-batch-*`), though not with a coin in this circuit context.
- Drift inside one job: within-job stationarity 716.4 on 720 dof; drift across the jobs of one batch is untested.
- 300 s job limit: the longest retained jobs (131072 shots) took 71.5-75.8 s.
- Tuna initialization 500 or 200 us: at most 0.58 ms per shot at 131072 shots favors 500 us; 200 us would leave about 0.35 ms per shot unexplained (computed).
- Bracket readout context (readout-1 excites the five region qubits): a context offset common to both twins cancels in the twin contrast (successor CASE.md, "Derived coefficients" item 5).
- Twin drift scale: forward-versus-mirror chi-square 14.77 on 8 dof implies an excess of about 0.0025 per batch, within `OC_DRIFT_SD` 0.003 (computed).
- QX acceptance statistic undefined: defined as TVD within 2 x the expected TVD (`gororoba_quantum/pathion_coset_analysis.py:41-43`, :542-562), and `results/quantum_inspire/pathion_one_cz_null_qx_analysis.json` passes; seeding survives as row 15.
- PNR against threshold detection: 3504 retained output keys in 154 files, none with a mode count above 1 (computed).
- Client constraint checker bypassed (inventory rows 23-24): `scripts/qd_platform.py:1283` calls `validate_single` inside plan, which runs `check_experiment` and `ConstraintChecker.verify_experiment` (Perceval `runtime/remote_computer.py:165-187`).
- Postselection filter unset: the same `validate_single` path enforces `min_detected_photons_filter` (`remote_computer.py:180-183`).
- Simulator tariff: cases budget 1.0 credit per job (`docs/notes/quandela/README.md:257-259`), and the QPU's 0.963 per job sits below the rule's 1 + 1e-6 x shots, so the rule over-budgets.
- `charge_used` against `charged` (inventory row 30): `job_billing`'s docstring (`scripts/qd_platform.py:1469-1480`) states that the charge lies at or below `charge_used` for an iterated job at its cap; intended.
- Forecasts omit loss (inventory row 46): `belenos_suppression_case.py:428-440` applies per-mode detection efficiency and `ascella_cz_tomography_case.py:447` uses uniform loss T/(2 - T); dark rates are screened (`belenos_suppression_case.py:74`, :227) and the CZ case names a "source, loss and dark counts" coverage variant (:136), whose arithmetic this sweep did not check.
- Compilation seed to served mesh: the served mesh is recorded by sha256 and compared (`strategy.md:486-489`), so a departure is detected.
- Heater phase error: the mesh screening carries its own sigmas (`belenos_best_mesh_first_principles.py:333-334`).
- Quandela step drift untested: `scripts/bdsc_stationarity.py` (blocks one hour apart), `scripts/bdsc_drift_series.py` and `scripts/ascella_target_slot_drift.py` test drift between acquisition windows.
- Simulator noiselessness for one-photon observables: measured exact on `sim:belenos` (`docs/notes/quandela/README.md:500-505`); narrowed to row 13.
