## Main claims and evidence

[P] Crosstalk errors violate locality of operations or independence of local
operations. A logically disjoint circuit can therefore produce dependent
regional outcomes on imperfect hardware (Secs. 2-3).

- [P] The operational description partitions the processor into disjoint regions. Each region has a setting variable, representing its local circuit, and a result variable, representing its measured bit string (Sec. 6.1).
- [P] Definition 2 requires a region's outcome distribution, conditioned on its own setting, to be independent of other settings and outcomes. Conditional tests are preferable to marginal tests when settings themselves can be correlated (Eq. 9).
- [P] A lightweight design repeats local subcircuits in multiple contexts and varies settings in the other regions. Context variation is a structural requirement of the construction (Sec. 6.3.1).
- [P] The design samples fixed-depth subcircuits from regional bags and deliberately increases the chance of all-idle companion circuits, which can reveal effects obscured by noisier active circuits (pp. 14-15; Appendix C).
- [P] Circuit repetitions should be spread across the experimental period by rastering or randomization. Executing each context in a single time block can confound time drift with a setting-to-outcome relationship (pp. 15-16).
- [P] Regional subcircuits can be drawn from application motifs instead of generic random gates. The paper cautions that isolated motifs may miss errors appearing only in the assembled application (Sec. 6.3.2).
- [P] The data-analysis proposal uses conditional-dependence tests and a graph-discovery algorithm to identify relationships between regional settings and outcomes. Such a graph localizes operational dependence; it does not by itself identify the underlying physical interaction (Secs. 6.4 and 8).
- [P] Detection depends on the partition, sampled layers, inference procedure, and statistical power. The paper describes high-weight effects and unfaithful distributions that its efficient procedure can miss (Sec. 6.5).
- [P] The overall distinct-experiment count scales as `O(n^3 log n)` in the stated worst case and `O(n^2 log n)` for local connectivity under the construction's restrictions. These are experiment-count statements, not a charged-time or total-shot guarantee (p. 16).
- [P] Demonstrations use simulated two-qubit and six-qubit processors. The six-qubit example uses local and context-dependent depolarizing noise (abstract, Sec. 7 opening, p. 24). This source reports simulation evidence for the protocol, rather than an IBM hardware implementation.

## Mapping to this repository

[I] [packing.py](../../gororoba_quantum/packing.py) provides disjoint circuit
composition and separate registers. [adjudicate.py](../../gororoba_quantum/adjudicate.py)
retains per-stream outcomes and computes shot-wise correlation. A complete
conditional-dependence design would additionally bind every outcome to the
companion settings, regional circuit identities, and collection context.

[I] Fixed companions across diagnostic arms can stabilize one experimental
context while collecting additional endpoints. Fixed companions cannot measure
how that context changes the target. Varied companions and an idle reference
supply the extra comparisons needed for that separate question.

[I] This distinction creates two candidate workload sets: an arm comparison
with companions held fixed, and a characterization set with companions varied.
Each set needs its own controls and budget. Repeating the same local circuit
across varied contexts follows the paper; rotating the diagnostic arms across
physical patches is an additional repository proposal.

[I] Label: known experiment-design method available for adaptation. A new result
would be an endpoint-specific explanation or intervention on repository
hardware, supported by actual schedules and outcomes. The paper supplies
neither a general proof of independent replicas nor a Heron-specific error map.

## Relevance

- [I] Calibration-aware compilation on IBM hardware: high methodological relevance, with simulation-only demonstrations in this source. Admission can use measured context sensitivity in addition to isolated gate calibration.
- [I] Cayley-Dickson structure: none. The source develops operational crosstalk definitions and tests rather than algebraic structure.
- [I] Factoring resource estimates: low. Context sensitivity affects execution reliability; the paper supplies neither a factoring resource advantage nor a full cost model.

[I] Diagnostic applicability to small QPE experiments is high. The approach can
test context sensitivity of QPE motifs, while the assembled dynamic circuit
still needs its own validation. Local Walsh circuits can become targets and
controlled neighboring contexts; their algebraic degree supplies neither a
crosstalk guarantee nor a hardness proof.

## Action items and acceptance gates

1. Decide whether a proposed pack estimates arm effects under fixed companions
   or dependence on companion settings. Acceptance: every comparison has an
   explicit estimand and varied factors; the plan separates the two objectives.
2. Build regional manifests with target digest, companion setting, physical
   qubits, register map, and schedule. Acceptance: every retained shot maps to
   exactly one intended context and stream without cross-stream reordering.
3. Include an idle reference and varied contexts for a dependence study.
   Acceptance: each tested target subcircuit appears in enough common contexts
   to estimate the intended conditional contrasts.
4. Audit service execution order before claiming rasterization or randomization.
   Acceptance: retained metadata supports the actual collection design; API
   submission order alone supplies insufficient evidence of execution order.
5. Validate inference on context-dependent, context-independent, drifting,
   correlated-setting, and higher-order-dependence fixtures. Acceptance: the
   report identifies missed effects and multiple-testing behavior explicitly.
6. Require an application-level confirmation after motif characterization.
   Acceptance: the proposed intervention predicts a concrete target-endpoint
   change under a separately approved hardware case, with charged usage retained.

## Boundaries

[I] A dependence detector's missed effect remains possible within its power and
model limits. Filling spare qubits is therefore an experimental intervention
whose scientific value depends on controlled comparisons. [Rudinger et al.](1810.05651.md),
[Proctor et al.](1907.13608.md), and the
