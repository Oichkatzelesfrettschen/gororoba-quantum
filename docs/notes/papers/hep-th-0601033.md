## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] Setting (pp. 2-3): `A_n` with `x y = (x_1 y_1 - conj(y_2) x_2, y_2 x_1 + x_2 conj(y_1))` (Eq. 1), flexible for all `n`; the
  reciprocal transformation is `(x, y) -> (-y, x)`; the identities `<x, y z> = <x conj(z), y> = <conj(y) x, z>` (Eq. 2) and
  `conj(x)(x y) = (y conj(x)) x` (Eq. 3); Table 1 lists commutativity (`n <= 1`), associativity (`n <= 2`), alternativity
  (`n <= 3`) and flexibility (all `n`).
- [P] Operators (pp. 3-4): `y R_x = y x`, `y L_x = x y`; for `x = (x_1, x_2)`, `R_x = [[R_(x_1), L_(x_2)], [-L_(conj x_2), R_(conj x_1)]]` (Eq. 4)
  with the transposes `R_x^t = R_(conj x)` and `L_x^t = L_(conj x)` (Eq. 5). With `N_x = R_x R_(conj x)` and `Nbar_x = L_x L_(conj x)`,
  `N_x = N_(conj x) = Nbar_x = Nbar_(conj x)` (Eq. 6), `N_x = [[A, -B], [B, A]]` with `A = N_(x_1) + N_(x_2)` and `B = [R_(x_1), L_(x_2)]` (Eq. 7), and
  `|N_x - nu I| = |C - nu I|^2` for `C = A + iB` (Eq. 8). A unit `x` has `R_x` in `SO(2^n)` exactly when every eigenvalue
  `nu` of `N_x` equals 1.
- [P] Section 2.1 (p. 5): the sets `S_n` of eigenvalues of `C' = C - (|x|^2 + |y|^2) I` for `x, y` in `A_n`: `S_0 = {0}`, `S_1 = {0, 0}`,
  `S_2 = {0, 0, 0, 0}`, `S_3 = {0, 0, 0, 0, +-2|x cross y|, +-2|x cross y|}` with `|x cross y|^2 = |x|^2 |y|^2 - <x, y>^2` for the imaginary parts;
  `S_(n-1)` is in general not contained in `S_n` for `n >= 4`.
- [P] Section 2.2 (pp. 6-10): Lemma 6: for `n >= 3`, the imaginary parts of `x` and `y` are linearly dependent iff `B_(x,y) = 0`; Eq. 9: `x = (x_1, x_2)`
  is alternative iff `x_1, x_2` are alternative with `B_(x_1, x_2) = 0`. Lemma 8: for alternative `x, y` with independent imaginary parts,
  `V(x, y) = span{e_0, x, y, xy}` has dimension 4, is annihilated by `B_(x,y)`, and `B_(x,y)` equals `-[R_x, R_y]` on `V(x, y)^perp`. Proposition 13: for alternative
  entries `S_0 subset S_1 subset S_2 subset ...`, and `A_n` splits into subspaces `V_1, ..., V_(n-1)` of dimensions 4, 4, 8, ..., `2^(n-1)` with
  eigenvalue sets `S_2, S_3 minus S_2, ..., S_n minus S_(n-1)` (Table 2, p. 10).
- [P] Section 3, reciprocity (pp. 11-13): requiring invariance under `(x, y) -> (-y, x)` gives `<x(k), y(k)> = 0` and
  `|x(k-1)|^2 |y(k-1)|^2 = (a(k)^2 + b(k)^2) |x(k)|^2 |y(k)|^2` (Eq. 20), with `a(k) = kappa_2(k) - kappa_1(k)`, `b(i) = 1 + kappa_1(i) kappa_2(i)`
  and `sin theta_k = a(k)/sqrt(a(k)^2 + b(k)^2)`, `cos theta_k = b(k)/sqrt(...)` (Eq. 21); the eigenvalues are then
  `Delta cos(+-theta_1 +- ... +- theta_k)` with `Delta = 2|x cross y|`, each element quadruply degenerate.
- [P] Sections 4 and 5 (pp. 12-14): an identification of the eigenspaces `V_1 + V_2 + V_3` of `R^16` with the photon, `Z` and `W` fields, from which the paper
  concludes `cos theta_1 = (m_W/m_Z)^a`; the Yang-Baxter `R` matrix written with `Delta_(+...+)` for `theta_1 + 2 theta_2 + theta_3 = 0`; and a
  remark that the right multiplication `R_(x,y)` restricted to alternative entries is the exponential of a derivation-built isometry.

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Product. Eq. 1 is `cd_core`'s doubling `(a,b)(c,d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))`. The paper's operators act on row
vectors (`y R_x = y x`); in `cd_core` columns, `N_z` is the operator `v -> (v z) conj(z)`.

[I] Check results: no mismatch with the algebra. A scratch script (ignored `docs/local/`, run with `-I`) built `N_z` from `cd_core`
and compared its spectrum with the paper in double precision:

| Paper statement | Result under `cd_core` |
| --- | --- |
| `S_3`: for octonion `x, y` and `z = (x, y)` in `A_4`, `N_z` has eigenvalues `\|x\|^2 + \|y\|^2` plus `0` (8 times) and `+-2\|x cross y\|` (4 times each) | equal on 2 random pairs to `1e-13`; for `z = e1 + e10` the spectrum is `0` (4), `2` (8), `4` (4) |
| `N_z = Nbar_z` (Eq. 6) | the spectra of `R_z R_(conj z)` and `L_z L_(conj z)` agree to `2.8e-14` on random `z` at `D = 16` |
| Section 3: for alternative `x = (x_1, kappa_1 x_1)`, `y = (y_1, kappa_2 y_1)` in `A_4` with orthogonal pure `x_1, y_1`, the spectrum of `N_z` in `A_5` is `\|x\|^2 + \|y\|^2 + {0, +-Delta, +-Delta cos theta_1}` | exact agreement of all values for `(kappa_1, kappa_2) = (0,0), (0,1), (0.5,-0.3), (2,0.7)`; multiplicities 8 (value `\|x\|^2 + \|y\|^2`), 4 and 4 (`+-Delta`), 8 and 8 (`+-Delta cos theta_1`), merging to 12/8/12 when `cos theta_1 = 1` |

[I] The check covers the first two levels of the recursion (`n = 3, 4`) and the two-level reciprocity formula; the general
`k`-fold formula `Delta cos(+-theta_1 ... +-theta_k)` was not tested beyond `k = 1`. The physical identification of
Section 4 is interpretation and no check is possible.

## Repository bearing

[I] Zero divisors and annihilator dimensions. For a zero divisor `z = (x, y)` of the sedenions (`x` orthogonal to `y`, equal norms), the
spectrum of `N_z` is `|z|^2 + {-Delta, 0, +Delta}` with multiplicities 4, 8, 4 and the smallest eigenvalue zero: the kernel of
`N_z` is the 4-dimensional annihilator, and the 4/8/4 sectors are the sedenion Gram sectors recorded in the claim
`algebra-device-operational-falsifiers`. The paper gives this spectrum for all alternative pairs, which includes the
Moreno and Biss et al. zero divisors of dimension 16 and, at `n = 4`, the doubly alternative elements of dimension 32 whose
Gram spectrum has the five clusters above (the paper's `Delta` and `Delta cos theta_1`). Claims `a5-stiefel-universal` and
`dimension32-global-topology`: none directly; the spectrum concerns alternative entries, while the repository's rank
strata include non-alternative zero divisors.

[I] Eigentheory. The paper's `N_x` is the Gram operator `L_x^T L_x` of [Biss, Christensen, Dugger and Isaksen](../0905.2987.md); its Section 2 and
Table 2 recover the sector structure `4/4/8/...` by an independent route and add the reciprocity condition. The physics
application has no bearing on the repository. Dimensions 64 and above: Proposition 13 and Table 2 state the sector dimensions
`4, 4, 8, ..., 2^(n-1)` for every `n`; they are replayed to dimension 32 only. Moreno's Stiefel question (`math/0512517` Section 5):
none.
