## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper explores connections among Monstrous Moonshine, orbifolds, the Kitaev chain and topological modular forms. Symmetric orbifolds of the Monster conformal field theory, together with further orbifolds by subgroups of the Monster, satisfy the divisibility property that was recently used to rule out extremal holomorphic conformal field theories; for orbifolds by cyclic subgroups of the Monster, divisibility properties involving the full McKay-Thompson series arise; orbifolds by non-abelian subgroups are considered with the data of Generalized Moonshine.
