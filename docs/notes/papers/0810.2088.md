## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the first five of the axioms formulated for spectral triples suffice, in a slightly stronger form, to characterize the spectral triples of smooth compact manifolds; the algebra, assumed commutative, is isomorphic to the algebra of all smooth functions on a unique smooth oriented compact manifold, the operator is of Dirac type and the metric is Riemannian.
- [P] Theorem 1.1 (p. 3, restated as Theorem 11.3): let `(A, H, D)` be a spectral triple with `A` commutative fulfilling the first five conditions of the earlier paper (cited as [11]) in a slightly stronger form, that is, assuming that regularity holds for all `A`-endomorphisms of `intersection of Dom D^m` and that the Hochschild cycle `c` is antisymmetric. Then there exists a compact oriented smooth manifold `X` such that `A` is the algebra `C^infinity(X)` of smooth functions on `X`; every compact oriented smooth manifold appears in this way.
- [P] Theorem 1.2 (p. 3, restated as Theorem 11.5): let `(A, H, D)` be a spectral triple with `A` commutative fulfilling the first five conditions with `c` antisymmetric, and assume that the multiplicity of the action of `A''` in `H` is `2^(p/2)`. Then there exists a smooth oriented compact (spin^c) manifold `X` such that `A = C^infinity(X)`. The text calls the multiplicity hypothesis "a weak form of the Poincare duality condition 6" of the earlier paper and states that `D` is then a Dirac operator, that the reality condition selects spin manifolds among spin^c, and that the spectral action selects the Levi-Civita connection.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The reconstruction of a manifold from a spectral triple uses the first five conditions in the stronger form of Theorem 1.1 (regularity for all `A`-endomorphisms and an antisymmetric Hochschild cycle); Theorem 1.1 carries no Poincare duality hypothesis, and Theorem 1.2 uses a weak form of it as the multiplicity hypothesis for the spin^c case. The paper has no Cayley-Dickson content; the repository carries no spectral triple computation, so the paper serves as the citation against which such statements are checked.
