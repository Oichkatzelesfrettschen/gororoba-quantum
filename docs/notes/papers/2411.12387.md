## What the experiment did

1. [P] Two independent GaAs quantum dots (droplet-etched), each in a circular
   Bragg resonator ("bullseye cavity"), driven by two-photon resonant
   excitation, emit photon pairs through the biexciton-exciton cascade. The
   exciton photon is `X`, the biexciton photon `XX`. The polarization qubit
   is prepared on the `X1` photon from quantum dot 1 (Node 1) and the
   `XX2`-`X2` entangled pair comes from quantum dot 2 (Node 2). A Bell-state
   measurement of `X1` and `X2` at Node 2 teleports the state onto `XX2`
   (pp. 2-3, Fig. 1).
2. [P] Tuning, with no frequency conversion: quantum dot 2 sits on a
   micro-machined PMN-PT piezoelectric actuator whose multi-axial strain
   cancels the fine-structure splitting, reaching `0.3 +- 0.2` micro-eV
   (Fig. 2b, p. 4). Quantum dot 1 sits in a magneto-cryostat; a field of
   `B = 0.9` T shifts one Zeeman-split `X1` transition by Zeeman splitting and
   diamagnetic shift into resonance with `X2` (Fig. 2e, pp. 4-5). The photons
   still differ in lifetime and linewidth, which limits their
   indistinguishability (p. 5).
3. [P] Entangled pair: fidelity `F = 0.94 +- 0.01` to the Bell state
   `|phi+>` and concurrence `C = 0.89 +- 0.01`, from tomography without time
   filtering (Fig. 2c, p. 4).
4. [P] Hong-Ou-Mandel interference between the two dissimilar dots reaches a
   maximum visibility `V_HOM = 0.598 +- 0.025` for a 20 ps coincidence
   window (Fig. 2f), and `0.57 +- 0.02` for 30 ps (Fig. 3 caption, p. 5). The
   limit is the time resolution of the superconducting nanowire single-photon
   detectors with a 19 ps (FWHM) time-to-digital converter (p. 5).
5. [P] Bell-state measurement: linear optics on a symmetric beam splitter
   with polarization projection samples two of the four Bell states,
   `|psi+>` and `|psi->` (p. 5). The model of Fig. 3a assumes a 50 percent
   Bell-state measurement (caption, p. 5). The joint state after
   interference is
   `|Psi> = (1/2)(|phi+> I + |psi+> sigma_X - i|psi-> sigma_Y + |phi-> sigma_Z)|phi>`
   (p. 6). [I] Read from that equation, the `|psi+>` outcome leaves a Pauli
   X to correct and the `|psi->` outcome a Pauli Y.
6. [P] Network and routing: Node 1 and Node 2 in one building are joined by a
   15 m single-mode fiber. The `XX2` photon then crosses a 270 m free-space
   link to Node 3 in a second building. [I] Fixed fiber and free-space links
   carry the photons; the paper describes no optical switch or reconfigurable
   routing. [P] GPS-disciplined oscillators synchronize the nodes, and slow and
   fast steering mirrors stabilize the link against turbulence (pp. 3, 7).
   Almost 90 percent of the signal is lost in the free-space channel (p. 7).

## Measured fidelities and the classical benchmark

[P] The classical limit for the average teleportation fidelity is `2/3`,
cited to Massar and Popescu (pp. 6-7, reference 46). The fidelities below are
post-selected on the Bell-state coincidence window `Delta tau`.

| Quantity | Value | Condition | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model prediction, average fidelity | `0.827 +- 0.006` | Bell fidelity 0.94, `V_HOM` 0.57 | Fig. 3 caption, p. 5 |
| `|psi->` projection | `0.82 +- 0.01` | fiber network, 30 ps | p. 6 |
| `|psi+>` projection | `0.77 +- 0.01` | fiber network, 30 ps | p. 6 |
| Average of both projections | `79 +- 1 %` | about 11 percent of events retained, 30 ps | p. 7 |
| Abstract headline | `82 +- 1 %` | [I] equals the `|psi->` value | p. 1 |
| Hybrid network | `0.80 +- 0.04` | 270 m free-space link, 30 ps | p. 7 |
| Rate at the classical limit | more than 70 percent of events, about 30 Hz | fiber, `Delta tau` about 250 ps | pp. 6-7 |
| Rate at the best fidelity | about 11 percent of the maximum rate, about 4 Hz | fiber, 30 ps | p. 7 |

[P] Both projections lie above `2/3` by more than 10 standard deviations in
the fiber network (p. 6); the hybrid value lies more than 3 standard
deviations above (p. 7). The paper attributes the `|psi+>` shortfall against
the model to polarization-compensation imperfection (about 2 percent) and
background (p. 6). The paper also states that the classical limit is exceeded
with a HOM visibility below 20 percent when the Bell-state fidelity exceeds 90
percent (p. 6).

## Repository use

[I] Scope of the demonstration. The experiment teleports a polarization
qubit between photons from two solid-state emitters, with the fidelity
measured by process tomography on three-fold coincidences after temporal
post-selection. It uses no frequency conversion, no routing, and no photonic
integrated processor, so it supplies no evidence about linking two cloud
processors such as the Ascella or Belenos QPUs. It supplies a worked case in
which a 50 percent linear-optics Bell-state measurement ([quant-ph-0007058](quant-ph-0007058.md)),
a measured entangled-pair fidelity, and a measured HOM visibility predict the
average teleportation fidelity, compared against the benchmark
`2/3` ([quant-ph-9807091](quant-ph-9807091.md)).

[I] Post-selection. Each fidelity above conditions on a narrow coincidence
window, and the rate falls with the window (table). A comparison of this
number with an unconditioned fidelity needs the retained-event fraction as
well.
