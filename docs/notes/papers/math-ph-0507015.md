## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the quantum Calogero-Sutherland model for the Lie algebra `E_7` and the coupling constant `kappa = 1` is re-expressed with the fundamental irreducible characters of the algebra as dynamical variables, which requires a systematic procedure to obtain the Clebsch-Gordan series; the resulting quantum Hamiltonian operator can be used to compute more characters and Clebsch-Gordan series for `E_7`.
- [P] Equation (5) (p. 5): the trigonometric Calogero-Sutherland Hamiltonian of a root system `R` of rank `r` is `H = (1/2) sum_(j=1)^r p_j^2 + sum_(alpha in R^+) kappa_alpha (kappa_alpha - 1) sin^-2 (alpha, q)`, with `p_j = -i d/dq_j`, `R^+` the positive roots and `kappa_alpha = kappa_beta` if `|alpha| = |beta|`; for simply-laced root systems (the `E`-series) one coupling constant `kappa` remains. The configuration space is the Weyl alcove `{q : 0 < (alpha, q) < pi}`.
- [P] Equations (6) to (8) (p. 5): the ground state energy and wave function are `E_0(kappa) = 2 rho^2 kappa^2` and `Psi_0^kappa(q) = prod_(alpha in R^+) sin^kappa (alpha, q)`; the excited states are indexed by the dominant weights `mu = sum m_i lambda_i` with `E_m(kappa) = 2(mu + kappa rho, mu + kappa rho)`; the wave functions have the form `Psi_m = Psi_0 Phi_m`, where `Phi_m` solves the eigenvalue problem (8) for the operator `Delta^kappa` of equation (9).
- [P] Equations (10) to (13) (pp. 5-6): the energies above the ground level are `epsilon_m(kappa) = 2(mu, mu + 2 kappa rho)` (equation (10)); for `kappa = 0` the wave functions are the monomial symmetric functions `M_lambda(q) = sum_(w in W) e^(2i (w lambda, q))` (equation (12)), and for `kappa = 1` they are the characters of the irreducible representations, `chi_lambda(q) = sum_w (det w) e^(2i (w (lambda + rho), q)) / sum_w (det w) e^(2i (w rho, q))` (equation (13)).
- [P] Equations (14) and (15) (p. 6): in the variables `z_k = chi_(lambda_k)(q)` the operator is `Delta^kappa = sum a_jk(z) d_(z_j) d_(z_k) + sum_j [b_j^0(z) + kappa b_j^1(z)] d_(z_j)`, and the coefficients are `b_j(z) = 2(A^-1_jj + 2 sum_k A^-1_kj) z_j`, with the sum over all `k`, determined by the Cartan matrix `A`. The quadratic Clebsch-Gordan series (16) determine the coefficients `a_jk`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is the reference for the `E`-series Calogero-Sutherland Hamiltonian in the root system form (5) with the ground state `Psi_0 = prod sin^kappa (alpha, q)` and energy `2 rho^2 kappa^2`, and for the statement that at `kappa = 1` the eigenfunctions are Weyl characters (equation (13)). The form of (5) is a sum over the positive roots of `E_7`; the `A`-type particle form `sum_(i<j) kappa(kappa - 1)/sin^2(x_i - x_j)` is a different root system. The paper has no octonionic or Cayley-Dickson content on the pages read, although the exceptional algebra `E_7` has octonionic constructions in the [adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md).
