## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Dispersive readout can induce qubit state transitions when the resonator photon number is high; the transitions show resonant behavior as a function of photon number and can push the qubit out of the two-level subspace (abstract).
- [P] The theory rests on level crossings in the Jaynes-Cummings ladder mediated by terms beyond the rotating-wave approximation, the most important arising from a broken symmetry in the qubit potential.

## Limits

- [P/I] Transitions occur at specific photon-number resonances set by readout power and qubit-resonator detuning; the source says nothing about an assignment error that depends on which other qubits were excited.
- [P/I] Google Xmon devices; Quantum Inspire's readout power and detuning are not published, so applicability to Tuna is open.
