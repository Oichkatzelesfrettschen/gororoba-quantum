## Relevance

[P] The dissertation gives structural criteria for zero products in higher
real Cayley-Dickson algebras. [I] This is the earliest foundational
algebraic certification of specific zero-divisor pairs in the literature
chain used here, preceding Moreno's quaternion-subalgebra constructions and
de Marrais's assessor combinatorics. [I] Its criteria bear on which
signed zero products used in the measurement analysis are theorems of the
algebra rather than combinatorial observations.
