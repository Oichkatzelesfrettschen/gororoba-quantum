## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper investigates holomorphic functions, in the sense of the kernel of the Dirac operator, on domains of the real Cayley-Dickson algebras; it studies the multiplication tables for certain elements of the basis and gives an algorithm for constructing examples of that class of functions. Keywords: Cayley-Dickson and generalized Cayley-Dickson algebras, Dirac operator, holomorphic functions.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the text traces the theory of quaternionic differentiable functions to a three-dimensional analogue of the Cauchy-Riemann conditions, the four-dimensional case, and the octonionic case, and states that an earlier paper generalized the Cauchy-Riemann conditions to all Cayley-Dickson algebras; the present paper considers another class of differentiable functions, using the Dirac operator, and gives an example.
