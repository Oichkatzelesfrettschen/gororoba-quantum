## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a survey of quantum linear-system algorithms from HHL through the post-HHL solvers that reach the optimal bounds in error tolerance and condition number, with a proposed taxonomy and applications to differential equations, quantum machine learning and many-body physics.
- [P] Section IV (p. 9): the taxonomy of fault-tolerant QLSP solvers with demonstrable speedups; Section III (p. 4) states the QLSP and its input models.
