## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Coherent and correlated errors arise from the spatial and temporal configuration of instructions executing together.
- [P] Dynamical decoupling or error compensation succeeds when the compiler accounts for layer context: connectivity, crosstalk terms, and concurrent gates or idles.
- [P] Context-aware suppression raised the layer fidelity of a 10-qubit candidate layer by 18.5 percent over context-unaware suppression.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Simultaneous X on both work qubits leaves their mutual ZZ term unrefocused, (X_A X_B)(Z_A Z_B)(X_A X_B) = Z_A Z_B; the single-work-qubit references separate that context effect.
