## Physical model and measured quantity

- [P] Section II, p. 2 models a superconducting transmission line terminated
  by a SQUID. An externally modulated Josephson energy changes an effective
  boundary length. Eqs. (8)-(13) assume weak modulation and a pair of modes
  close to half the pump frequency, giving the perturbative map
  `b_+/- = -a_+/- - i*f*a_dagger_-/+`, with
  `f = delta_L_eff*sqrt(omega_+*omega_-)/v`, approximately
  `delta_L_eff*omega_d/(2*v)`.
- [P] Section IV, p. 4, Eqs. (27)-(32) starts with a small thermal occupation
  in both modes, constructs the output covariance matrix, and approximates
  the two thermal occupations by a common `n_th` under small detuning.
- [P] Section V.C, p. 5 defines coherence relative to a fixed orthonormal
  basis. Its Gaussian expression, Eq. (41), uses the state entropy and
  the entropies of reduced thermal reference states. Eq. (43) gives a
  perturbative formula in `f` and `n_th`; its expression includes
  `log(n_th)`.
- [P] Figure 1, p. 6 illustrates a nonzero coherence response for nonzero
  pump amplitude in the stated thermal regime, with `f<0.08` and small
  detuning. Section VI calls the work a theoretical study and proposes
  experimental use of the discriminator.

## Scope of the no-threshold statement

[P] Section V compares coherence with leading-order formulas for logarithmic
negativity and Gaussian discord. On p. 5, the paragraph immediately before
Section V.C explicitly states that higher-order or nonperturbative treatment
could make discord nonzero for every nonzero squeezing and that other
discord measures can behave differently.

[I] The source therefore supports a qualified statement: its chosen
coherence measure remains nonzero across the investigated perturbative
parameter regime where the displayed leading-order comparators can vanish.
It does not establish a universal threshold difference for every discord
measure, all temperatures, or arbitrary squeezing. Substituting `n_th=0`
directly into Eq. (43) is outside that expansion's usable logarithmic form.
A vacuum reference needs the exact model or an independently justified limit.

[I] A mathematically nonzero expectation also differs from experimentally
resolvable evidence. Shot uncertainty, readout, phase calibration, and drift
set the attainable contrast. The source supplies motivation, not the
repository's detection power or precision guarantee.
