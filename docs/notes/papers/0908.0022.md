# Wocjan et al.: Efficient quantum processing of ideals in finite rings

[P] Pawel M. Wocjan, Stephen P. Jordan, Hamed Ahmadi and Joseph P. Brennan;
arXiv:0908.0022, 2009; [version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.0022v2),
5 July 2023. [Exact PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.0022v2).

## Result and scope

[P] Sections 1–4 assume a finite associative ring with identity, injective
element labels, quantum arithmetic access and generators. They construct an
additive basis representation in time polynomial in log ring size and use it
for ideal membership and expressions in the original generators. Section 5
reduces membership to classical integer linear algebra once a ring basis
representation and input coordinates are available.

[I] For an explicit finite quotient of dimension D over F_p, log2|R| = D log2 p.
Constructing that quotient and its arithmetic remains part of the comparison;
this result does not establish a speedup for the repository's explicit
coordinates. A5 itself is nonassociative. See the
[algorithm-fit audit](../topics/quantum-linear-algebra-fit-a5.md).

[I] Relevance: Cayley–Dickson research, high for the access-model boundary;
IBM calibration-aware compilation and factoring resource estimates, none.
