## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are PDF page indices, and Eqs. 12, 13 and 16 were read from the
rendered pages 5 and 7.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a "clock" (a particle with evolving internal degrees of freedom) in a
  Mach-Zehnder interferometer "will not only display a phase shift, but also reduce the visibility of the
  interference pattern", because proper time flows at different rates along the two paths and
  complementarity makes the visibility drop to the extent that the path information can be read from the
  clock.
- [P] Eqs. 1-2 (p. 1): without internal structure the detection probability is
  `P_(+-) = (1/2)(1 +- cos(Delta Phi + phi))` with unit visibility, a phase shift that an
  Aharonov-Bohm-type potential also explains.
- [P] Eqs. 3-5 (pp. 2-3): with internal states `|tau_i>` on the two paths the state is entangled and
  `P_(+-) = (1/2)(1 +- |<tau_1|tau_2>| cos(Delta Phi + alpha + phi))`, so the visibility is
  `V = |<tau_1|tau_2>|`.
- [P] Eq. 12 (p. 5): for a clock with two internal levels `E_0`, `E_1`, initial state
  `(|0> + |1>)/sqrt(2)` and paths at constant heights separated by `Delta h`, the detection probability is
  `P_(+-) = 1/2 +- (1/2) cos(Delta E Delta V Delta T / 2 hbar c^2) cos((m c^2 + <H> + E_corr) Delta V Delta T / hbar c^2 + phi)`,
  with `Delta V = g Delta h` the gravitational potential difference, `Delta T` the laboratory time spent
  in superposition and `Delta E = E_1 - E_0`.
- [P] Eq. 13 (p. 5), the visibility: `V = |cos(Delta E Delta V Delta T / 2 hbar c^2)|`.
- [P] Eqs. 14-16 (p. 7): the orthogonalization time is `t_perp = pi hbar / Delta E`, the proper-time
  difference between the paths is `Delta tau = Delta V Delta T / c^2`, and for a general clock
  `V = |cos((Delta tau / t_perp)(pi / 2))|`. The visibility vanishes when `Delta tau = t_perp`.
- [P] Table I (p. 8), outcomes of the proposed experiment by measured visibility `V_m` against the
  prediction `V_QM` of Eq. 13: `V_m = 0` means a sharply defined quantum proper time, "disproved" by
  existing experiments; `0 < V_m < V_QM` means a quantum proper time with uncertainty `sigma_tau`,
  consistent with current data for `sigma_tau > |Delta tau| / sqrt(-8 ln(1 - Delta V))` (`Delta V` the
  visibility error); `V_m = V_QM` means proper time is not a quantum degree of freedom or has a very
  broad uncertainty, consistent with current data; `V_m > V_QM` means interferometric complementarity
  fails when general-relativistic effects become relevant, "not tested".

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The loss of visibility is `omega Delta tau / 2` with `omega = Delta E / hbar` (the same Eq. 13
  rewritten). For a 5 GHz transmon transition in a hypothetical path superposition 1 m apart for 100
  microseconds, `Delta tau = 1.1 x 10^-20` s and `omega Delta tau / 2 = 1.7 x 10^-10` rad, a visibility
  deficit of `1.5 x 10^-20`. Neither Heron r2, Tuna-17, Belenos nor Ascella prepares a path superposition
  of an internal-state clock, and their instabilities are order-one fractions
  ([tuna-17.md](../quantum_inspire/tuna-17.md), [platform-specifications.md](../quandela/platform-specifications.md)).
- [I] Signature: the effect scales with `Delta V Delta T` and so with the height difference of the
  two paths; it reverses nothing under a sidereal or orientation change and appears only in an
  interferometer whose arms sit at different potentials.
