## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): an explicit formula for the bracket of the exceptional simple Lie algebra `E_8` based on triality and oct-octonions, following the Barton-Sudbery description of `E_8`; descriptions of the subalgebras `E_6` and `E_7`; and an explicit formula for the 26-dimensional irreducible representation of `F_4`.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the 248-dimensional `e_8` is the highest dimensional exceptional Lie algebra and its lowest dimensional non-trivial representation is 248-dimensional, so a matrix representation is not a good option, in contrast to `g_2` with its 7-dimensional faithful representation. The text recalls that a cited article calls `E_8` the "most exceptional Lie group" and argues that `E_8` could be called "the Monster of Lie theory". It describes constructions that start from a subalgebra `h` of `g = e_8` with `g = h + V` and define the bracket on `V x V`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is a construction reference for `e_8` through octonionic data (triality and oct-octonions), the same family of constructions that the repository's [adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md) lists for the Albert algebra and the exceptional Lie algebras. The page read states that the lowest non-trivial representation of `e_8` is the 248-dimensional adjoint one, so a finite-dimensional matrix representation exists and is the adjoint representation, which the author calls an inconvenient choice for computation. The page contains no Cayley-Dickson doubling content beyond the octonions.
