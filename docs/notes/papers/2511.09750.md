## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A planar annular source mass inside a 10 m atom-interferometer vacuum chamber probes screened fifth forces (chameleon and symmetron).
- [P] The Q-flip protocol alternates interferometry sequences to make the signal time-dependent and detrend transient noise; an in situ procedure calibrates the plate's Newtonian gravity.
- [P] The projected reach advances existing screened-scalar bounds by 1 to 1.5 orders of magnitude.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Signal modulation by a controlled protocol switch plus independent calibration of the known background is the same identification pattern the repository's matched-control designs use; a scalar-field proposal needs both.
