## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Long-term interleaved measurement of T1, Ramsey dephasing and the qubit frequency on a fixed-frequency transmon shows telegraphic (burst) noise in coherence times and qubit frequency (Fig. 1 shows a continuous 19-hour measurement).
- [P] Correlations between the three parameters identify a small number of microscopic TLS near the film edges as the origin; the power spectral density of the frequency fluctuations supports this model, with the 1/f background attributed to a bath of more weakly coupled TLS.
- [P] The paper connects noise at millihertz frequencies to qubit dephasing times.

## Limits

- [P/I] Millihertz spectral content from repeated characterization sweeps, not a time-ordered shot record at kilohertz sampling.
- [P/I] The model is single telegraph fluctuators plus a 1/f background; a narrow line with a harmonic series is outside it.
- [P/I] Non-tunable transmon without flux pulses.
