## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): ordinary quantum TDA estimates Betti numbers as a fraction of the Laplacian dimension, which is non-vanishing and efficiently estimable only when Betti numbers are exponentially large; Mayer homology replaces d^2 = 0 by d^N = 0 and the paper gives an efficient quantum estimator for Mayer Betti numbers and their persistent counterparts.
- [P] Eq. 2 (p. 3): the Mayer differential is d[v_0 ... v_d] = sum_k xi^k [v_0 ... omit v_k ... v_d] with xi = exp(2 pi i / N), N >= 2, and d^N = 0; kernels Z_{d,p} = ker(d^p) and images B_{d,p} = im(d^(N-p)) give p-th Mayer Betti numbers beta_{d,p} = dim(Z_{d,p} / B_{d,p}).
- [P] Abstract: for high-order simplices the Mayer Betti numbers are often exponentially large in the dense regime, the dequantization approaches lose their guarantees in the Mayer setting under the paper's assumptions, and the stated resource estimate is roughly a few hundred qubits and sixty million Toffoli gates.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper estimates kernel dimensions of boundary-type operators through block-encoded Laplacians; its advantage claim lives in the regime where the nullity is a constant fraction of an exponentially large dimension. The fiber program's kernels are exact F_p ranks of matrices with 10^4 to 10^5 columns, outside that regime and outside real-amplitude output.
- [I] Fault-tolerant scale (sixty million Toffoli gates) is far outside Heron r2; no experiment follows.
