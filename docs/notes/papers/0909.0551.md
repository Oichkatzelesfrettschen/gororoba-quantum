## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): supersymmetry is related to division algebras. Nonabelian Yang-Mills fields minimally coupled to massless spinors are supersymmetric if and only if the dimension of spacetime is 3, 4, 6 or 10, and the same holds for the Green-Schwarz superstring. In both cases supersymmetry relies on the vanishing of a certain trilinear expression involving a spinor field, which in turn follows from the existence of normed division algebras in dimensions two less, namely 1, 2, 4 and 8: the real numbers, complex numbers, quaternions and octonions. The paper gives a self-contained account.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the goal is a self-contained proof of the "if" part of the statement on the Yang-Mills Lagrangian `L = -(1/4)<F, F> + (1/2)<psi, D_A psi>`; the text names earlier work by Brink, Schwarz and Sherk, Evans (supersymmetry in dimension `n + 2` implies a normed division algebra of dimension `n`), Kugo and Townsend, and Sudbery.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper cites the four normed division algebras `R, C, H, O` (dimensions 1, 2, 4, 8) as the origin of supersymmetry in dimensions 3, 4, 6 and 10. This is the Hurwitz boundary of the Cayley-Dickson tower: the sedenions (dimension 16), the first level with zero divisors, lie outside it, and no statement on the page read extends the supersymmetry argument to them ([octonion structure checks](../topics/octonion_structure_checks.md)).
