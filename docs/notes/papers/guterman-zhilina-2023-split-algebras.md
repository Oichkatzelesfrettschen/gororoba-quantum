## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] The paper studies doubly alternative zero divisors of the real
  Cayley-Dickson split-algebras, algebras with isotropic norm.
- [P] It describes their annihilators and orthogonalizers and relates
  centralizers to orthogonalizers for such elements.
- [P] For the split-octonions it obtains an analogue of the real Jordan
  normal form.
- [P] It describes the commutativity, orthogonality and zero-divisor graphs
  of the split-complex numbers, the split-quaternions and the
  split-octonions through their diameters and cliques.

## Repository bearing

[I] The repository's algebra is the positive-definite real main sequence,
where the norm is anisotropic; a split-algebra has zero divisors from
dimension 2. The paper's objects therefore sit outside the repository's
sedenion census, and the abstract supplies no statement transferable to it.
The companion results for anisotropic algebras are in
[2608.26893](../2608.26893.md) and [2608.26890](2608.26890.md).
