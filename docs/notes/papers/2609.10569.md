## Transcribed statements

Index and sign convention [P]: sedenion basis `s_0 = 1, s_1, ..., s_15` with `s_i^2 = -1` and `s_i s_j = -s_j s_i`
for `i != j` (Eq. 47, p. 15); `s_i s_j = -delta_ij + C_ijk s_k` with totally antisymmetric structure constants
(Eq. 48); `s_1, ..., s_7` span the octonion subalgebra of `S = O + O s_8`; the basis element `s_(i+8)` is the
product `s_i s_8` (inferred from Eqs. 58 and 63, pp. 16-17). The doubling (Eqs. 38 to 41, p. 14) is
`(a, b)(c, d) = (ac - conj(d) b, da + b conj(c))` with `conj((a, b)) = (conj(a), -b)` and `l_n^2 = -1`
(bars read from the page image; the text layer drops them).

- [P] Eq. 53 and 54 (p. 16): "Brown showed that the automorphism group of the standard Cayley-Dickson sedenions
  is `Aut(S) = G2 x S3`", attributed to Brown [66]; for the doublings beyond `S` "the continuous derivation
  algebra remains `g2`, while further doublings introduce additional discrete `S3` factors [66,67]".
- [P] Eqs. 55 to 57 (p. 16): `O_3 cap O_5 cap O_7 = span{1, s_4, s_8, s_12} = H` and
  `O_1 cap O_2 cap O_3 = span{1, s_1} = C`; the sedenion basis holds seven further copies of `O` besides the
  distinguished `O_1 = span{1, s_1, ..., s_7}`.
- [P] Eqs. 58 to 62 (pp. 16-17): writing a sedenion as `A + B s_8` with `A, B` octonions, the maps
  `phi : A + B s_8 -> phi(A) + phi(B) s_8` for `phi in G2`, `epsilon : A + B s_8 -> A - B s_8` and
  `psi : A + B s_8 -> (1/4)[A + 3 A* + sqrt(3)(B - B*)] + (1/4)[B + 3 B* - sqrt(3)(A - A*)] s_8`
  (`A*` the octonion conjugate) satisfy `epsilon^2 = Id`, `psi^3 = Id`, `epsilon psi = psi^2 epsilon` and
  generate `S3`; the `G2` action commutes with `epsilon` and `psi`. Eq. 63 gives the action on units:
  `psi(s_i) = -(1/2) s_i - (sqrt(3)/2) s_(i+8)` and `psi(s_(i+8)) = -(1/2) s_(i+8) + (sqrt(3)/2) s_i` for
  `i = 1, ..., 7` ("schematically", with the `~` sign printed).
- [P] Eqs. 108 to 111 (p. 22): with `e_i := L_(s_i)` (left multiplication), `i = 1, ..., 8`,
  `e_i^2 = -1_16` and `{e_i, e_j} = -2 delta_ij 1_16`, the defining relations of `Cl(0, 8)`; over `C` these generate
  `Mat(16, C) = Cl(8)` (Eq. 112, p. 23). The left actions `g_i := L_(s_(i+8))` are multivectors of this `Cl(8)`
  (Eq. 113 onward, p. 23).
- [P] Section 3.2 (p. 15): `N(xy) != N(x) N(y)` for sedenions in general; `S` is power-associative and flexible,
  contains zero divisors, and has "no nontrivial idempotents [65]"; Baez is cited for the relation of the unit
  zero divisors to `G2`.
- [P] Appendix A, Table 5 (p. 54): the sedenion product table in `s_i`, "the entry in the row labelled `s_i` and
  the column labelled `s_j` gives the product `s_i s_j`" (p. 53).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Index map. The paper's `s_i` is the `cd_core` basis element of index `i` (units `s_1, s_2, s_4, s_8` at
  the power-of-two indices, `s_i s_j = +-s_(i XOR j)`), and Eq. 40 is the doubling that `cd_core.cd_sign`
  implements.
- [I] Check results, from scratch scripts under the ignored `docs/local/`:
  - Table 5, parsed from the text layer and confirmed against the page image: all 225 result indices equal
    `i XOR j`; 214 of 225 signs equal `cd_core.cd_sign(16, i, j)`; the opposite reading (column times row)
    disagrees on 199 cells. The 11 disagreeing cells are the printed entries
    `s_9 s_6, s_9 s_7, s_9 s_8, s_9 s_14, s_9 s_15` and `s_10 s_8, ..., s_15 s_8`; each of them breaks the paper's
    own Eq. 47 (`s_i s_j = -s_j s_i`) against the cell with the indices exchanged, and flipping those 11 signs
    recovers `cd_core` exactly. Result: map found (identity index map, row times column) with 11 printed-table
    defects, none a convention difference.
  - Eq. 61, 60 and 62, exact arithmetic over `Q(sqrt 3)` with `cd_core.cd_multiply_exact`: the maps `epsilon`
    (a signed permutation) and `psi` satisfy `T(e_p e_q) = T(e_p) T(e_q)` on all 256 basis pairs, `psi^3 = Id`,
    `epsilon^2 = Id` and `epsilon psi = psi^2 epsilon`, and Eq. 63 holds (`s_(i+8) = s_i s_8` with sign
    `cd_sign(16, i, 8) = +1`). `psi` is not a signed permutation; its certificate is the exact `Q(sqrt 3)`
    product comparison. Eq. 111 holds for the real left-multiplication matrices of `cd_core` on `i, j = 1, ..., 8`
    (zero violating entries).
- [I] Certificate status: the paper gives `epsilon` and `psi` explicitly and does not itself check the
  basis-pair identity; the repository check above supplies it for the printed form of Eq. 61 and the two
  maps generate an `S3` inside the automorphism group of the `cd_core` sedenions. The check does not cover the
  `G2` factor, its commutation with `S3`, or the completeness of `G2 x S3` in `Aut(S)`, which the paper takes
  from Brown [66] without proof in this review.
- [I] Disagreement with a second source. [Wilmot](2512.07210.md) disputes Brown's equality for `Aut(S)` (his
  Theorem 7 and p. 24); the review cites Brown and does not cite that paper. The `epsilon` and `psi` certificate holds
  independently of either claim.
- [I] The sedenion `S3` automorphisms act on `s_i` and `s_(i+8)` within the pairs `{i, i + 8}` for
  `i = 1, ..., 7`; the star-lattice group `G_16` of [cd-star-lattice-tower](../topics/cd-star-lattice-tower.md)
  also preserves the eight doubling pairs. The repository has not compared `psi` with `G_16`.
