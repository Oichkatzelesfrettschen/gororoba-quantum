## Setting and conventions

- [P] Section 1 (p. 1): for `m, n` positive integers, the rational map `Lambda_2 : P(Hom(C^m, C^n)) --> P(Hom(wedge^2 C^m,
  wedge^2 C^n))` sends a matrix to the tuple of its 2 x 2 minors; `X_(m,n)` is the closure of its image, `S = Sym(V_1 tensor V_2)`
  with `dim V_1 = m`, `dim V_2 = n`, `W = wedge^2 V_1 tensor wedge^2 V_2`, `R = Sym(W)`, `Psi : R -> S` is induced by the inclusion
  `W subset S`, `A = image(Psi)` is the homogeneous coordinate ring of `X_(m,n)` and `I(X_(m,n)) = ker(Psi)`. For `m = 2` the
  variety `X_(2,n)` is the Grassmannian `Gr_2(C^n)`, cut out by the Plucker quadrics.
- [P] Page 3: `A` and its permanent analogue are the special fiber rings of the maps (1.1) and (1.2), and natural quotients of
  the Rees algebras of the ideals of 2 x 2 minors (`I_2`) and of 2 x 2 permanents. The Rees algebras are the bigraded coordinate
  rings of the graphs of those maps, and computing their presentation is stated as an open problem in general.
- [P] Section 7 (pp. 19-20): `Rees(I_2) = sum_d I_2^d` is isomorphic to `S[It] subset S[t]`; `J` is the kernel of the map
  `Pi : S tensor R -> S[t]` and `I(X_(m,n)) = ker(Psi)`; the identities `J_(0,2d) = I(X_(m,n))_d` and `J_(d,2) = Syz(I_2)_(d+2)`
  hold. The ideal `I_2` is of fiber type when `J` is generated by `J_(0,2d)` and `J_(d,2)`.

## Theorem statements

- [P] Theorem 1.1 (p. 2, the case `t = 2` of the Bruns-Conca-Varbaro conjecture): `Tor_1^R(A, C)_j = 0` for `j` not in {2, 3},
  `Tor_1^R(A, C)_2 = S_(1,1,1,1) V_1 tensor S_(2,2) V_2 + S_(2,2) V_1 tensor S_(1,1,1,1) V_2`, and `Tor_1^R(A, C)_3 =
  S_(3,1,1,1) V_1 tensor S_(2,2,2) V_2 + S_(2,2,2) V_1 tensor S_(3,1,1,1) V_2`, as `GL`-representations. Since `Tor_1` encodes the
  minimal generators of `I(X_(m,n))`, the ideal is generated by quadrics and cubics, with the listed representations.
- [P] Theorem 1.2 (p. 2): the same vanishing and the isomorphisms `Tor_1(A-bar, C)_2 = S_4 V_1 tensor S_(2,2) V_2 + S_(2,2) V_1
  tensor S_4 V_2` and `Tor_1(A-bar, C)_3 = S_(4,1,1) V_1 tensor S_(3,3) V_2 + S_(3,3) V_1 tensor S_(4,1,1) V_2` for the ring `A-bar`
  generated by the 2 x 2 permanents; the paper states that Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 are equivalent through transpose duality.
- [P] Proof strategy (pp. 3-4): the vanishing `Tor_1(A-bar, C)_j = 0` for `j > 4` is the main task; it combines a Koszul
  homology computation relative to the space of 2 x 2 minors, a filtration of the second Veronese subring `S^(2)` with finite-length
  quotients `M_r / M_(r-1)`, the bound `Tor_1(A-bar, C)_j = 0` for `j > floor(n/2) + 2`, a subspace variety for the induction on
  `(m, n)`, and the Kempf-Weyman technique.
- [P] Section 6.2 (p. 18): the assumption `n <= 5` gives `floor(n/2) + 2 <= 4`, so the vanishing for `j > 4` follows from
  Corollary 4.2 (stated on a page not opened). Section 6.3 (pp. 18-19): the inductive step for `m >= n >= 6` ends with
  `Tor_1^R(A-bar, C)_j = 0` for `j > 4`.
- [P] Question 7.1 (p. 20): is `I_2` of fiber type for a generic `m x n` matrix? Proposition 7.2 (p. 20): if `I_2` is of fiber type
  for a generic `(n + 2) x n` matrix, then it is of fiber type for every generic `m x n` matrix.
- [P] Corollary 7.3 (p. 20): for a generic `m x 3` matrix, `I_2` is of fiber type. The justification (p. 20) is a Macaulay2
  verification that the generic `5 x 3` case is of fiber type, followed by Proposition 7.2 with `n = 3`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The A_5 program computes special fibers (fiber cones) of Rees algebras of Pfaffian ideals, and its measured Hilbert
  functions equal the quadric values through the computed degrees ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)).
  This paper gives the matching statement for the determinantal case: the special fiber ring of the 2 x 2 minors of a generic
  matrix has minimal relations in degrees 2 and 3 only (Theorem 1.1), so a fiber cut out by quadrics alone is a special fact
  against that expectation. The source concerns minors of generic matrices, whereas the repository's fibers come from the
  kernel-coordinate ideals of the A_5 strata and from Pfaffian ideals; no identification of any repository fiber with `X_(m,n)`
  is established.
- [I] Consequence of Theorem 1.1 with the standard fact that `S_lambda V` vanishes when `lambda` has more rows than `dim V`:
  the cubic summands need `m >= 4` and `n >= 3` or the transposed condition, so a cubic minimal relation of the special fiber of
  the 2 x 2 minors appears only for matrices of at least that size. This bound is a repository derivation, not a statement of the paper.
- [I] Corollary 7.3 gives a worked instance of the fiber-type property (the Rees ideal generated by the fiber relations
  together with the first syzygies), the same structural question that arises for the Rees algebras of Pfaffian ideals; the
  paper decides it only for generic matrices with three columns.
