## Statements read

- [P] Section III and Eqs. (2) and (3) (p. 2): a passive linear-optical evolution with unitary matrix U maps a^(l)dagger|0> to sum_m U_ml a^(m)dagger|0>, and for every unitary U a sequence of phase shifters and beam splitters implements it up to a global phase.
- [P] Section IV (p. 2): a qubit is encoded as one boson in two modes (|0>_L -> |0>_a|1>_b, |1>_L -> |1>_a|0>_b); single-qubit rotations are passive linear optics, and the text states that linear optics is insufficient for the two-qubit c-not and c-sign gates, and that a direct bosonic implementation would need strong optical nonlinearities.
- [P] Abstract: without feedback from the detectors, non-deterministic quantum computation is possible.
- [I] Relevant to Ascella because the platform exposes passive linear optics, source-connected single photons and threshold detection without feed-forward.
