## Setting and conventions

- [P] All work is over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero (organization paragraph, p. 2). `V` is a vector space of dimension `n + 1` and `P^(N_-)`, `N_- = C(n + 1, 2) - 1`, parametrizes non-zero skew-symmetric maps `V -> V` up to scalar (p. 2).
- [P] A complete skew-form on `V` is a finite sequence of non-zero 2-forms `omega_i` with `omega_1` a 2-form on `V`, `omega_(i+1)` a 2-form on the kernel of `omega_i`, and the last form either non-degenerate or of kernel dimension one (introduction, p. 1). The space of complete skew-forms is the closure of the graph of `Z -> (wedge^2 Z, ..., wedge^n Z)` (equation (2.2), p. 3).
- [P] A point of `P^(N_-)` is an `(n + 1) x (n + 1)` skew matrix `Z`; the Grassmannian `G(1, n)` is the locus of rank two matrices, `Z` lies in the `h`-secant variety `Sec_h(G(1, n))` exactly when `rank Z <= 2h`, and the ideal of `Sec_h(G(1, n))` is generated by the `(2h + 2) x (2h + 2)` sub-Pfaffians of `Z` (p. 3, citing Landsberg and Ottaviani).

## Theorem statements

- [P] Construction 2.3 (p. 3): `A(n)_1` is the blow-up of `A(n)_0 := P^(N_-)` along `G(1, n)`, and `A(n)_i` is the blow-up of `A(n)_(i-1)` along the strict transform of `Sec_i(G(1, n))`, up to `i = floor((n - 1)/2)`. Each center is smooth, the exceptional divisors `E_1 + ... + E_i` form a simple normal crossing divisor, and `A(n)` is isomorphic to the space of complete skew-forms (by Thaddeus, Theorem 6.3 as cited on p. 3).
- [P] Remark 2.4 (p. 3, citing Catalisano, Geramita and Gimigliano): `dim Sec_h(G(1, n)) = 2(n - 1)h + h - 1 - 2h(h - 1)` for `h < floor((n + 1)/2)`.
- [P] Theorem 1.1 (p. 2): with `D_(2j+2)` the strict transform of the hypersurface of vanishing `(2j + 2) x (2j + 2)` sub-Pfaffians and `E_j` the exceptional divisors, `Eff(A(n))` is generated by `E_1, ..., E_(floor((n-1)/2))` and `D_(n+1)` (`n` odd) or `D_n` (`n` even), `Nef(A(n))` is generated by the `D_(2j+2)`, and the canonical sections of the `D_(2j+2)` and `E_j` are a minimal generating set of `Cox(A(n))`. The Cox rings of `A(4)` and `A(5)` are isomorphic to the homogeneous coordinate rings of the 10-dimensional Spinor variety `S_5` in `P^15` and the 15-dimensional Spinor variety `S_6` in `P^31`.
- [P] Theorem 1.2 (p. 2): the Mori chamber and stable base locus decompositions of `Eff(A(4))` and `Eff(A(5))` coincide and have 2 and 3 chambers; for `A(6)` the Mori chamber decomposition has 5 chambers and the stable base locus decomposition 4; for `A(7)` they have 9 and 8; for `A(8)` the Mori chamber decomposition has 15 chambers and the stable base locus decomposition 9.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The repository's kernel map of a skew matrix `L_a` lands in a Grassmannian of 8-planes in a 32-dimensional space, while the paper's complete-forms tower resolves the corank-one kernel-line map and its secant strata. The tower is the frame for the full container of limiting kernels (a flag together with non-degenerate skew forms on the graded pieces); it supplies no equation of the fiber cone of a restricted ideal ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)).
