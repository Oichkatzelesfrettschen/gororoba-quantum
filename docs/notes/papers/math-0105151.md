## Setting and conventions

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): three determinantal expressions for the Hilbert series and the Hilbert function of a Pfaffian ring, a closed product formula for its multiplicity, and an appendix on degeneracy loci with applications to multiplicity formulas.
- [P] Pfaffian ideals are the ideals generated by the Pfaffians of a fixed size in a generic skew-symmetric matrix; the corresponding quotients are Pfaffian rings, which are Cohen-Macaulay normal domains and are Gorenstein (introduction, p. 1, with citations). In the notation of pp. 11-12, `X` is a generic skew `n x n` matrix over a field `K`, `I_(r+1)(X)` is generated by the `(2r + 2) x (2r + 2)` Pfaffian minors and `R_(r+1)(X) = K[X]/I_(r+1)(X)`.

## Theorem statements

- [P] Equation (9) (p. 11): for `r = 1`, the ideal `I_2(X)` of `4 x 4` Pfaffians is the ideal of Plucker relations of the Grassmannian of 2-planes in `n`-space, and by Hodge's formula `dim_K R_2(X)_l = C(l + n - 2, l)^2 - C(l + n - 2, l - 1) C(l + n - 2, l + 1)`. The text on p. 11 above it notes that the Hilbert function of `R_(r+1)(X)` is a polynomial in `l` for all `l`, so the ideal is Hilbertian in Abhyankar's sense.
- [P] Equation (10) (p. 11): for `r = 1` the paper's Corollary 1 formula (6) reduces to a sum that the paper proves equal to (9) by Pfaff-Saalschutz summation (pp. 11-12).
- [P] Theorem 2 (p. 12): the dimension of the Pfaffian ring `R_(r+1)(X)` is `r(2n - 2r - 1)` and its multiplicity is `product over 1 <= i <= j <= n - 2r - 1 of (2r + i + j)/(i + j)` (equation (11)). The proof (p. 12) takes the Hilbert series as `Q(z)/(1 - z)^d`, reads dimension `d` and multiplicity `Q(1)`, and evaluates the numerator at `z = 1` as the determinant (12).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] For `n = 6` and `r = 1`, the cone over the Grassmannian `G(2, 6)` that the first-order images of the repository's kernel strata live in, equation (9) gives the Hilbert function 1, 15, 105, 490, 1764, 5292, Theorem 2 gives dimension 9 and multiplicity 14, and multiplying the Hilbert series by `(1 - z)^9` gives the h-vector 1, 6, 6, 1 (a symmetric, Gorenstein h-vector). The arithmetic was checked by exact integer computation on the printed formulas.
- [I] A linear section of the generic ring carries these invariants only when the section ring equals `R_2(X)/(l_1, ..., l_s)` and the `l_i` form a regular sequence, that is, when the section has the expected dimension `9 - s`. The repository's nullity-12 and nullity-16 strata give excess intersections under the skew family `a -> L_a`, so the section hypothesis fails there and the formulas serve only as an oracle for sections of proper dimension ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)).
- [I] The pages opened contain no statement about fiber cones of Pfaffian ideals, and the characteristic of the ground field is not restricted in the statements of Theorem 2 and equation (9) as printed (the text on p. 11 says "over `K`"); the repository's finite-field computations over `F_32009` need the multiplicity and Hilbert-function formulas only as a cross-check.
