## Mathematical content used

[P] The abstract states the sedenion zero-divisor system is based on 7
octahedral lattices ("Box-Kites") whose 6 vertices collect and partition
the "42 Assessors" (pairs of diagonals in planes spanned by pure
imaginaries, one a pure octonion of subscript < 8, the other a sedenion of
subscript > 8 and not the XOR with 8 of the chosen octonion).

[P] In the 32-ions ("Pathions"), all non-Midden-collected zero-divisor
diagonals belong to 15 "emanation tables" ("sand mandalas"), each with 14
assessors and 168 fillable cells (the order of PSL(2,7), governing octonion
multiplication). 7 of these tables, whose axis-pairs' "inner XOR" exceeds
24, fold 1-to-1 onto the 7 sedenion Box-Kites.

[P] Those same 7 tables display DMZ (dyads-making-zero) sparsity: 72 of the
168 available cells are filled.

[I] The 72 filled cells per sparse table correspond, in the repository's
32-dimensional assessor-plane census, to 72 directed adjacency entries per
sparse component: the seven sparse components are each the join of two
nonadjacent hub vertices with three disjoint 4-cycles (I2 join 3C4), with 36
undirected edges = 72 directed entries. The audit's independent enumeration
(verify.py graph_32_all_pairs) reproduces this count from the repository's
sign table without consulting the paper.

[I] The paper's speculative links (curvature tensor, pair creation,
cellular-automata readings of the sparsity sequencing) are the author's and
establish no experimental claim for this repository. The repository uses
only the combinatorial correspondence: sparse emanation-table fill counts
versus computed adjacency counts.
