## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] Abstract and Section 3 (p. 3): the loop `T_L = +-{e_0, ..., e_31}` of order 64 generated by the 32 basis
  elements of the trigintaduonion algebra `T` is a non-associative finite invertible loop (NAFIL) with 373
  non-trivial subloops, of orders 32 (31 loops), 16 (155), 8 (155), 4 (31) and 2 (1). All 373 are normal, so `T_L` is
  Hamiltonian, which gives a modular subloop lattice by the cited result of Lausch.
- [P] Section 2.2 (p. 2): `e_i * e_j = +-e_k` for basis elements, `(+-e_i)^2 = -1` for `i >= 1`, and
  `(-e_i) * e_j = e_i * (-e_j) = -(e_i * e_j)`. The text does not print the doubling formula, and Table 1 (p. 3)
  is an image.
- [P] Section 3.1 (p. 4), Tables 2 to 5 (pp. 4-5): the 31 order-32 ("sedenion-type") subloops fall into four
  isomorphism classes, with representatives named by their index sets: the standard sedenion loop `SL`
  (`{0,...,15}`, class of 16 subloops), `SL_alpha` (`{0,1,2,3,8,9,10,11,20,21,22,23,28,29,30,31}`, 7), `SL_beta`
  (`{0,1,2,3,12,13,14,15,20,21,22,23,24,25,26,27}`, 7) and `SL_gamma` (`{0,...,7,24,...,31}`, 1).
- [P] Section 3.2 (p. 6): the 155 order-16 subloops form two isomorphism classes, 50 octonion loops `O_L` and 105
  quasi-octonion loops `O~_L`; `O_L` satisfies the Moufang identity and `O~_L` does not (Summary, p. 8).
- [P] Section 3.3 (p. 6): the 155 order-8 subloops are quaternion groups `Q_8`, the 31 order-4 subloops are cyclic
  `C_4` and the one order-2 subloop is `C_2`.
- [P] Section 3.5 (p. 6) and Table 6 (p. 7): every sedenion-type subloop has one of three maximal-subloop
  compositions: `[8 O_L + 7 O~_L]` (17 subloops), `[2 O_L + 13 O~_L]` (7) and `[0 O_L + 15 O~_L]` (7). Hence every
  sedenion-type loop contains at least 7 quasi-octonion subloops. `SL` and `SL_gamma` share the composition
  `[8 + 7]` and are stated to be non-isomorphic.
- [P] Section 3.5 (p. 7) and Summary (p. 9): the sedenion-type loops generate 16-dimensional real subalgebras of `T`
  with zero divisors, because each contains `O~_L`; all 16-dimensional subalgebras of `T` have zero divisors, and
  only `S` among them had been identified before.
- [P] Section 3.6, Table 7 (p. 7): `T_L` and its subloops satisfy the inverse, alternative, flexible, C-loop,
  power-associative, weak-inverse and anti-automorphic-inverse properties; the algebra `T` satisfies only the
  flexible and power-associative identities.
- [P] Section 3.7 and Figure 1 (pp. 7-8): the lattice of the 9 isomorphism classes of subloops is drawn in block
  form; the complete subloop lattice of `T_L` is stated as undetermined.
- [P] Remark 2 (p. 9): Catto and Chesley named the quasi-octonions "twisted octonions"; de Marrais studies the same
  algebras by box-kites ([math/0011260](math-0011260.md) is the registered paper cited).

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Index map. The paper's `e_0, ..., e_31` carry the XOR index of `cd_core.cd_sign(32, p, q)`, which every subloop
index set printed on p. 4 respects: each of the four sets is an `F_2`-subspace of `{0,...,31}`, and a subloop of
`T_L` is exactly the set `+-{e_a : a in V}` for a subspace `V`. The subloop counts therefore equal the Gaussian
binomial coefficients of `F_2^5`: `1, 31, 155, 155, 31` for subspace dimensions 0 to 4, and `1 + 31 + 155 + 155 + 31
= 373`.

[I] Check result: no mismatch. A scratch script (ignored `docs/local/`, run with `-I`) built the order-64 loop from
`cd_sign(32, p, q)` and compared with the paper:

| Paper statement | Result under `cd_core` |
| --- | --- |
| 373 non-trivial subloops, orders 32/16/8/4/2 in the counts 31/155/155/31/1 | 373 = 31 + 155 + 155 + 31 + 1 subspaces |
| all 373 subloops normal | 373 of 373 satisfy `xV = Vx` for every loop element `x` |
| 50 octonion (Moufang) and 105 quasi-octonion order-16 subloops | 50 Moufang and 105 non-Moufang; all 155 are alternative |
| compositions `[8+7]`, `[2+13]`, `[0+15]` with 17, 7, 7 subloops | Moufang 8-dimensional subspaces per 16-dimensional subspace: 8 (17 times), 2 (7 times), 0 (7 times) |
| `SL`, `SL_alpha`, `SL_beta`, `SL_gamma` compositions 8, 2, 0, 8 octonion subloops | 8, 2, 0, 8 |
| loop alternative property holds on `T_L` | 0 violations over all 64 x 64 pairs |

[I] The loop-level invariants do not pin the doubling variant: the table above holds for `cd_core`'s doubling
`(a,b)(c,d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))`, and the paper does not state which variant its program used. The
non-isomorphism of `SL` and `SL_gamma` is not decided here: the associating-triple count (14,784), the commuting-pair
count (184) and the nucleus size (2) agree for the two loops, so those invariants do not separate them.

## Repository bearing

[I] Sedenion-type subalgebras and zero divisors. The 105 non-Moufang 8-dimensional subspaces of `F_2^5` are the
quasi-octonion subloops, and every 4-dimensional subspace contains 7, 13 or 15 of them (the compositions above), so the
paper's claim that all 31 sixteen-dimensional coordinate subalgebras carry zero divisors holds under `cd_core`. The
two-term zero divisors `e_i + s e_j` of dimension 32 number 588 (annihilator dimensions 4, 8 and 12 in the counts 168,
168 and 252 of the [sparse census](../topics/cayley_dickson_sparse_census.md)); a scratch mutual-zero-division graph over
all 930 two-term elements found exactly those 588 vertices, so every two-term zero divisor has a two-term partner.
This note does not map the 31 subalgebras onto the 588 elements.

[I] Claims in `docs/state/research_state.json`: `cd-associator-census` states that every 3-dimensional index plane carries 168 or 72
non-associating ordered basis triples and that the algebra of dimension `D` has `(D - 2)(D - 4)(D + 6)/2` of them. The paper's
split of the 155 order-16 subloops into 50 octonion and 105 quasi-octonion loops (replayed above) reproduces the dimension-32
count exactly; a direct count of non-associating ordered basis triples over the planes gives 168 for each of the 50 octonion planes
and 72 for each of the 105 others: `50 * 168 + 105 * 72 = 15,960 = 30 * 28 * 38 / 2`. The same identity holds in dimension 16 with the 8 octonion
and 7 quasi-octonion planes of the sedenion loop: `8 * 168 + 7 * 72 = 1,848 = 14 * 12 * 22 / 2`. Every non-associating
basis triple spans one 3-dimensional plane, so the census equals the sum over planes; the paper's counts therefore tie
directly to the claim, although the paper states no associator count.
`sedenion-g2-bundle` (the zero-product pair space of the sedenions) and `dimension32-global-topology` (rank strata of
dimension 32): none, because the paper has no statement about annihilator dimensions or continuous families.
Moreno's Stiefel question (`math/0512517` Section 5): none. Dimensions 64 and above: none; the paper stops at `T`.

[I] Companion sources. The sedenion case is the 2004 paper of Cawagas, registered on the unmerged branch
`sources/sedenion-graph-and-structure-papers` as `docs/notes/papers/cawagas-2004-sedenion-structure.md`; this paper
cites it as reference [2] with a 2005 co-author. The box-kite description of the same algebras is
[de Marrais](../math-0403113.md).
