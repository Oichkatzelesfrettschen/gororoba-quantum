## Transcribed statements

Index and sign convention [P]: Cayley-Dickson basis elements `o_alpha` carry `alpha` in the graded order
`{}, 1, 2, 12, 3, 13, 23, 123, 4, 14, ...` with `o_i^2 = -1` and unity `o_{}` (p. 2); the grading exposes the XOR
rule `alpha xor beta` for products, with sign and ordering fixed by the doubling rule (p. 2). Clifford basis
elements `e_alpha` of positive definite signature square to `+1` (p. 2). Eq. 10 (p. 9) maps `e_1, ..., e_F`
(hexadecimal subscripts) onto `o_1, o_2, o_12, o_3, o_13, o_23, o_123, o_4, ..., o_1234`.

- [P] Introduction (p. 1): `Aut(O) = G2` (Engel); Schafer found `Aut(S) = G2` and Brown `Aut(S) = G2 x S3`, each
  printed with a question mark above the isomorphism sign. The paper states that it "extends the work of
  Schafer and Brown for sedenions to find that `G2 x S3` is contained in `Aut(S)` and the full automorphism
  group is much more complicated", and that this "disagrees with Brown's full result that
  `Aut(A_N) = Aut(A_{N-1}) x S3` for N in (4, 5, 6)".
- [P] Eq. 2 (p. 3): the first pure 7-dimensional associative calibration
  `theta_1 = e123 + e145 - e167 + e246 + e257 + e347 - e356`; 30 pure calibrations `theta_i`; Eq. 1 sends
  `e123 : (e1, e2, e3) -> (o1, o2, o12)` and `e321 : (e1, e2, e3) -> (o12, o2, o1)`.
- [P] Table 1 (p. 5): the octonions `O` and six quasi-octonion algebras `P4, P8, P10, P12, P14, P16`, tabulated by
  the counts of associator Types A, B, C and X among the 28 non-associative triads; each `P_k` has 12 zero
  divisor pairs; only `P4, P12, P14` occur in the Cayley-Dickson series.
- [P] Eq. 6 (p. 7): the eight octonion triads `phi_O` and seven `P4` triads `phi_P` of the sedenions, after Cawagas:
  "eight octonions and seven P4 rings are embedded in the sedenions", with `252 = 8 x 28 + 7 x 4` ordered
  non-associative triads.
- [P] Eq. 9 (p. 9): the 15-dimensional associative calibration `Theta` of 35 three-form terms
  (`e123 + e145 - e167 + e189 - e1AB - e1CD + e1EF + ...`, each with a coefficient `1/3` in front of the
  sum), which "specif[ies] the sedenion multiplication table shown in Table 2".
- [P] Table 2 (p. 10): the 15 x 15 sedenion multiplication table in graded notation, `o_alpha` rows against
  `o_beta` columns; the text does not state whether a cell is row times column or column times row.
- [P] Theorem 1 (p. 11): `Phi = Phi_A + Phi_O + Phi_P` (Eqs. 7 and 8) is a non-associative calibration with
  invertible form `psi = (7 e_{1..F} - Phi)/8`, `psi^2 = -1`, and generates the sedenions under the map of Eq. 10.
- [P] Theorem 2 (p. 12), as read with the superscripts restored: a Cayley-Dickson algebra of `N` generators has
  `H_N = (2^N - 1)(2^N - 2)/6` quaternion rings and `2^N - 1` embedded subalgebras of `N - 1` generators, each
  carrying `H_{N-1}` quaternion rings; the number of Fano-plane subalgebras is
  `T_N = (2^N - 1)(2^N - 2)(2^N - 4)/168`. At `N = 4` these give 35 quaternion rings and 15 Fano planes.
- [P] Definitions of strong and weak automorphisms (p. 15): a transformation from `Cl(n)` that keeps the simplex
  labels in the same relative order, so that the associative calibration is invariant, is a strong automorphism;
  one that maps the calibration to another calibration is weak, either internal (sign changes of the same
  primary) or external (labels change to those of another primary). These are the paper's own definitions and
  rest on invariance of a calibration under Clifford conjugation `R_beta x R_{-beta}`.
- [P] Theorem 3 (pp. 17-18): the invariants of `Phi` under 90-degree rotations are given by a cyclic map (Eq. 22)
  and a mixed map (Eq. 23); 105 first-sign-variation invariants, quadrupled by paired negations to 420 elements
  of `Sharp(4)` that keep `Harp(4)` invariant.
- [P] Theorem 5 (p. 22): 91 elements of `Phi_S` form a basis of the 2912 calibrations of `Harp(4)`; the proof counts
  `92 x 32 = 2912`.
- [P] Theorem 6 (p. 22): `Aut(Harp(4))` equals the group generated by `Phi_{O+P}^{C(1+2+3+4)}` under the strong
  definition and that group times `C` under the weak internal definition.
- [P] Section 5 (p. 23): Brown's transformations for `a, b in A_{N-1}` and `sigma in Aut(A_{N-1})`
  (Eq. 24): `sigma' : a + b o_N -> a^sigma + (b^sigma) o_N`, `epsilon : a + b o_N -> a - b o_N` and an order-3
  map `psi` built from `beta = sqrt(3) e_{1..F}`; the paper recovers `psi sigma' = sigma' psi`,
  `epsilon sigma' = sigma' epsilon`, `epsilon^2 = 1`, `psi^2 = 1` (as printed) and `epsilon psi = psi^2 epsilon`,
  "which defines the S3 group", hence `Aut(A_{N-1}) x S3` is contained in `Aut(A_N)`. It adds that Brown's
  claim of equality "ignores the Sharp automorphisms derived from `Phi_P` and the other two sign variations".
- [P] Theorem 7 (p. 24): `Aut(S) = Phi_O^{C(1)}` under the strong definition and `Phi_O^{C(1)} x C` under the
  weak internal definition. The proof (p. 25) states that the 29 other primaries "do not satisfy the composition
  property" and concludes "the automorphisms of sedenions have the same structure as for O". The paper states no
  finite group order for `Aut(S)`; `G2` is a 14-dimensional Lie group.
- [P] p. 25: rotating `Phi` or `Theta` repeatedly by the 105 two-forms of `Cl(15)` yields 64,864,800 unique
  primary representations, attributed to the paper's reference [20].
- [P] Summary (p. 26): the paper reports that it re-analyzed Schafer and Brown "which
  provided a clarification of these automorphisms"; it conjectures analogous calibrations for all
  Cayley-Dickson levels.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Index map. The graded label `o_alpha` corresponds to the `cd_core` index `p = sum_{i in alpha} 2^(i-1)`:
  `o_1 = 1, o_2 = 2, o_12 = 3, o_3 = 4, ..., o_1234 = 15`, so Eq. 10 sends `e_k` to index `k`, and the
  paper's XOR rule is the `p XOR q` of `cd_core.cd_sign`.
- [I] Check result: map found, zero mismatches. A scratch script under the ignored `docs/local/` parsed all 225
  cells of Table 2 from the PDF text and compared them with `cd_core.cd_sign(16, p, q)`: 225 of 225 result
  indices equal `p XOR q` and 225 of 225 signs agree when a cell is read as row times column. The opposite
  reading (column times row) disagrees on 210 cells, the anticommuting pairs. The agreement also validates the
  "9" glyph as a minus sign. The same script, applied to the 35 three-form terms of Eq. 9 under the map of
  Eq. 10, finds 35 of 35 sign agreements: a term `+e_{ijk}` (`-e_{ijk}`) occurs exactly when
  `cd_sign(16, i, j) = +1` (`-1`) for `i XOR j = k`. Table 2 and Eq. 9 therefore describe the algebra of
  `cd_core` with its doubling `(a, b)(c, d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))`.
- [I] Certificate status. The paper supplies no signed-permutation or matrix map `T` and no check of
  `T(e_i e_j) = T(e_i) T(e_j)` on basis pairs: its automorphisms are conjugations `R_beta x R_{-beta}` in
  `Cl(15)` (Eqs. 15 to 18), and its group statements rest on the counting arguments of Theorems 3, 5 and 6.
  The repository therefore holds no certificate from this paper that a stated automorphism is a basis-pair
  automorphism of `cd_core`; the certificate available for `epsilon` and `psi` is the one recorded in
  [2609.10569](2609.10569.md), computed from Gresnigt's Eq. 61, not from Wilmot's Eq. 24.
- [I] The paper's statements about `Aut(S)` do not agree with each other. The introduction and abstract
  assert `G2 x S3` inside `Aut(S)` and a much larger full group, while Theorem 7 gives `G2` (strong) and
  `G2 x C` (weak internal) and concludes the structure equals that of `O`. The proof on p. 25 classifies Brown's
  `epsilon` as an internal weak automorphism that fails the composition property of Eq. 15, a test the paper
  defines through Clifford conjugation. Both `epsilon` and Gresnigt's `psi` satisfy the ordinary identity
  `T(xy) = T(x) T(y)` on all 256 basis pairs of `cd_core` (see
  [2609.10569](2609.10569.md#mapping-to-this-repository)), so Theorem 7 bounds `Aut(S)` only under the
  paper's own definitions. For algebra automorphisms (linear bijections with `T(xy) = T(x) T(y)`) the exact
  computation of [sedenion-automorphisms](../../evidence/sedenion-automorphisms/README.md#wilmots-two-statements)
  decides both statements: `Aut(S) = G2 x S3`, Brown's value. The containment of the introduction holds, the
  claim that the full group is "much more complicated" fails, and Theorem 7's "same structure as for O" fails,
  since `Aut(S)` has six components and `Aut(O)` one.
- [I] Subalgebra counts. Table 1 and Eq. 6 give 8 octonion and 7 `P4` subalgebras of the sedenions. The
  repository has not compared them with `cd_core.sedenion_zero_divisor_corpus` or with the box-kite census of
  [de Marrais](math-0207003.md).
- [I] Overlap with [Structure of the Cayley-Dickson algebras](2505.11747.md): the cited reference [3] supplies the
  triad classification (Types A to X) that Table 1 and Eq. 6 reuse; this paper adds the Clifford calibration
  and the automorphism claims.
