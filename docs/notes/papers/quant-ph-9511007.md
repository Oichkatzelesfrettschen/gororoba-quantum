## Main claims

[P] The quantum Fourier transform that precedes the final measurement in Shor's algorithms can be carried out with one-bit gates controlled by classical signals from earlier measurements, with no two-bit gates.

- [P] Setting (p. 2, Eq. 1): the state |psi> is a superposition of |a>, a in [0, q-1], q = 2^(s+1). The transform is F|a> = q^(-1/2) sum_c exp(2 pi i a c / q) |c>, followed by a measurement of all s+1 bits of c.
- [P] Reference network (Fig. 1, Eqs. 4-9): one one-bit gate per bit (Eq. 7) and controlled-phase gates with phase exp(2 pi i / 2^m) (Eq. 8). The output is the product state of |p(phi_j)> with phi_j = sum_k a_k 2^(j+k-s-1) (Eqs. 4-6). The paper credits the network to Coppersmith and to Deutsch through Shor's second paper (ref. 18).
- [P] Semiclassical network (Fig. 2, Eqs. 10-11): each box applies |0> -> (|0> + |1>)/sqrt2 and |1> -> exp(2 pi i phi)(|0> - |1>)/sqrt2, measures the bit to obtain c, and passes phi' = phi/2 + c/4 to the next box. The first box uses phi = 0. Every two-bit gate of Fig. 1 is absent.
- [P] Argument for equivalence (p. 4, Eq. 12): the projector on c0 = 1 commutes with the controlled-phase gates that precede the measurement, so the measurement result moves back to point B. The paper offers the alternative of verifying by traditional means that Fig. 2 and Fig. 1 give the same probability for c, and states that the check must cover an arbitrary linear combination of the |a> states. The paper does not carry that check out.
- [P] Limitation (p. 4): a bit must be measured and converted into a classical signal before the next bit is measured. The paper names this a disadvantage when the physical carriers decohere within that time, and suggests arranging the earlier computation so that the more significant bits of a are produced first.
- [P] Scope: the figures cover s = 3 (four bits). The paper gives no gate counts, no noise analysis, no modular-exponentiation circuit, and no experiment.
- [P] The paper cites Bennett et al. 1993 (ref. 16) for an earlier use of a measurement result to set a later unitary, in teleportation.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Semiclassical inverse QFT in the order-finding circuit. Repository claim: HYBRID_SHOR.md, "Exact order-finding decomposition" -- a measurement, reset, and classically conditioned phase implement each remaining inverse-QFT step (order_finding.py, `factor_order_finding`). Source item: Fig. 2 with Eqs. (10)-(11), reduced from Fig. 1 through Eqs. (4)-(9). Model map: box j of Fig. 2 is round j of the repository circuit. The repository's Hadamard on the recycled control creates the a-register bit, the controlled multiplier entangles it with the work register, and the conditional phase, second Hadamard, and measurement form the box. Round 0 carries the largest exponent 2^(m-1), the most significant a-bit, which is the bit that enters the first box of Fig. 2 and produces c0, the least significant output bit. The repository applies negative angles (-pi / 2^k) and the paper applies positive phases, so the repository realizes the conjugate transform of Eq. (1); the Aer phase-support and factor-recovery checks in `_verify_factor_path` are the authority for the sign the decoder needs. Label: "known theorem reproduced". Not established by the source: modular exponentiation as the preceding stage (the paper takes |psi> as given), reuse of a physical qubit, any noise or hardware behavior, and the equivalence check for an arbitrary |psi> (the paper only states that it is required).
- [I] Feed-forward representation. Repository claim: each round applies one `if_test` per prior active round (28 branches for eight unpruned rounds, 45 for ten; HYBRID_SHOR.md pruning table and MODULAR_SYNTHESIS.md). Source item: Eq. (11), one accumulated phase phi' = phi/2 + c/4 per box. Model map: the accumulated angle is the sum of the per-bit angles -pi/2^(round - prior), and diagonal phase gates on one qubit commute and add, so the two forms are the same unitary; they differ in the count of conditional operations, n(n-1)/2 against n-1 classically updated angles. Label: "known theorem reproduced". Not established by the source: whether an IBM dynamic circuit can condition a gate on a classically computed angle, or the latency of either form.
- [I] Recycled control qubit. Repository claim: one control qubit, measured then reset, serves all rounds (5 qubits for N = 15). Source item: none; the paper measures each of the s+1 bits on its own device (p. 2, Stern-Gerlach picture) and never resets a measured carrier. Martin-Lopez et al. (arXiv 1111.4147, main text and refs. 10-12) attribute the single recycled control qubit to Parker and Plenio and to Mosca and Ekert, and describe the result as closely related to the semiclassical transform. Model map: the repository combines the feed-forward of this paper with the recycling credited in those two references. Label: "known theorem reproduced". Not established by the source: recycling, reset, or any statement that one qubit suffices. Neither Parker-Plenio nor Mosca-Ekert has been read for this note.
- [I] Exact circuit domain. Repository claim: HYBRID_SHOR.md -- the multiplier acts as a permutation of the whole register (identity on invalid basis states), every bounded permutation is checked exhaustively, scratch workspace is restored, and the host decodes by continued fractions and validates the order. Source item: none; the paper stops at the transform and treats the earlier stage as given. Model map: the repository supplies the circuit that prepares the superposition of |a> in Eq. (1) through controlled modular multiplication. Label: "new implementation or measurement". Not established by the source: any arithmetic circuit, any classical post-processing, any resource count.
- [I] Hardware mapping, controls, and measured performance. Repository claim: HYBRID_SHOR.md, "Evidence levels" -- on a Heron device the N = 15, a = 2 circuit reads factor yield 0.4971 [0.4726, 0.5216] against the exact uniform null 0.2969 and an identity-null yield 0.0042, and the paragraph concludes that hardware conformance of the semiclassical inverse QFT is established at the level of the decoder's factor yield. Source item: Fig. 2. Model map: the ideal output of the two executed rounds is the uniform distribution on {0, 64, 128, 192}, and that distribution does not depend on the feed-forward phase. The order of 2 modulo 15 is 4 = 2^2, so the control after the second controlled multiplier is entangled with orthogonal work states (|1> +/- |4> against |2> +/- |8>) and its measurement is uniform whatever phase is applied. An exact two-round state-vector calculation by the note author returns {0: 0.25, 1: 0.25, 2: 0.25, 3: 0.25} with the conditional -pi/2 phase and the same four values without it (not part of the repository; plain numpy on the two executed rounds). The factor-yield endpoint therefore cannot register a missing or wrong feed-forward phase, and the 0.4971 against 0.2969 admission does not establish the semiclassical inverse QFT. At most it establishes that the controlled arithmetic randomizes the control, which the identity-multiplier null does not. Only the folded-QPE test, whose eigenstates with odd labels need the phase (0.715 measured against 0.899 modeled), exercises the feed-forward. Label: "new implementation or measurement". Not established by the source: any hardware number, and any claim about the N = 15 yield; the measured yield stands as a measurement and the inference to inverse-QFT conformance does not follow from it.
- [I] Measurement-to-signal latency. Repository claim: HYBRID_SHOR.md, Roadmap item 9 -- the scheduler tables give a classical round trip of 533 dt (two streams) to 577 dt (nine streams) plus 7 dt to receive, a control segment of 1,299 to 1,463 dt from first capture to the end of reset, and work-qubit idle of 1,380 to 2,380 dt; HARDWARE_PLAN.md, "Risks" attributes about a quarter of the folded-QPE gap to idle T1 and T2. Source item: p. 4, the sentence on carriers that decohere within the measurement-to-signal time. Model map: the paper's concern is the work-register idle window during which the control is measured, reset, and the signal returns. Label: "new implementation or measurement". Not established by the source: any duration, any decoherence number, or any mitigation other than producing the more significant bits first.
