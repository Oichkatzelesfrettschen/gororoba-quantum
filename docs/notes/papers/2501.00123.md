## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] Loops with involution (pp. 3-9): an involution is an anti-automorphism of order 2. Central involutions satisfy
  `a* = mu(a) a` with `mu(a)` central; normal involutions have `x* x` central (Section 3); central-by-abelian loops
  (`L/Z(L)` abelian) carry the commutator and associator identities of Section 2.
- [P] Doubling (pp. 9-11, Eqs. 4.1 to 4.3): for a loop `L` with involution `*` and central `gamma`, `D(L, *, gamma) = L + L j` with
  `a (b j) = (b a) j`, `(a j) b = (a b*) j`, `(a j)(b j) = gamma b* a`, so `j b = b* j` and `j^2 = gamma`. `D(L, *, gamma)` has inverses
  exactly when `L` does and `gamma* = gamma` (Corollary 4.3). Proposition 4.13 (p. 12): `Aut(M; L, Zj)` embeds in
  `Aut(L, *) x| Z(L)` via `sigma -> (sigma|_L, sigma(j) j^(-1))` with `sigma(gamma) gamma^(-1) = p* p`.
- [P] Centers and central-by-abelian doubles (pp. 13-20): Proposition 5.1: `D(L, *, gamma)` is commutative iff `*` is the identity
  (and `Z(D) = D(Z(L), gamma)` then, Proposition 5.7); Corollary 5.6: `Z(D(L, *)) = Z(L, *)` when `*` is not the identity.
  **Theorem 6.8 (p. 19):** for `L` central-by-abelian, `D(L, *, gamma)` is central-by-abelian iff `[L, L, L]` lies in `Z(L, *)` and `*` is
  super-central on `L`; Corollary 6.9: the variety of central-by-abelian loops with symmetric associators and super-central
  involutions is closed under doubling with symmetric `gamma`.
- [P] Sections 7 to 9 (pp. 20-26): the derivative `V'` of a variety `V` is the variety of loops whose double lies in `V`; perfect
  varieties satisfy `V' = V`; homogeneous varieties remove the dependence on the doubling parameters.
- [P] **Moufang doubles (pp. 26-29):** Theorem 10.1: `D(L, *, gamma)` is Moufang iff `L` is Moufang, `[a, c c*] = 1`, `[c, c*] = 1`,
  `[a, c, c*] = 1` and every `c c* c` is in the nucleus. Corollary 10.4: `D^3(L, *; gamma_1, gamma_2, gamma_3)` is Moufang for some (equivalently
  every) `gamma_i` iff `L` is a commutative Moufang loop with the identity involution; Corollary 10.6: for associative `L`, `D(L, *, gamma)` is
  Moufang iff `*` is normal; Corollary 10.7: for `L` with a normal involution, `D(L, *, gamma)` is Moufang iff `L` is associative;
  Corollary 10.8: `D^3` is Moufang with a normal involution iff `L` is an abelian group with the identity involution.
- [P] Sections 11 to 13 (pp. 29-32): in `ZA cap E^2` (central-by-abelian, every `x^2` central) central and normal involutions
  coincide; `ZA cap E^2 cap S* cap D` (with diassociativity) is perfect (Corollary 12.3); octonion loops are the Moufang
  3-dimensional loops, and the associator of any three generators of an octonion loop is a fixed central element of order at most 2
  (Lemma 13.4).
- [P] Section 14 (pp. 32-35): conditions under which the doubling generator is fixed by every automorphism; Corollary 14.7: for
  an admissible `T` that is not an abelian group, every automorphism of `D^2(T)` stabilizes `j'` modulo the center; Example 14.8:
  this applies to `T = Q_2` and shows every automorphism of `Q_4 = <Q_3, j'>` stabilizes `j'` modulo `{+-1}`, while the
  automorphism group of `Q_3` acts transitively on `Q_3 / Z(Q_3) = Z_2^3`.
- [P] **Section 15 (pp. 35-38):** Theorem 15.1: for `T` admissible, anti-commutative and not an abelian group, `D(T)` is a characteristic
  subloop of `D(D(T))`. Example 15.2: `Q_n = D(Q_(n-1), *, -1, -1)` with `Q_0 = {+-1}` is the loop of standard basis elements of the classical
  Cayley-Dickson algebra. Remark 15.3 (p. 36): Lemma 36 of [Kirshtein](1102.5151.md) claims that if `<x, y, z>` in `Q_(n-1)` is an octonion
  subloop (`n >= 4`) then `<xj, yj, zj>` is a non-associative octonion subloop; in that loop `[xz, yz, zj] = 1`, but the associators of
  bases of a non-associative octonion loop all equal `-1`, so the claim is incorrect. Theorem 15.6 and Corollary 15.7: for
  `n >= 3`, `Aut(Q_n) = Aut(Q_3, *) x {+-1}^(n-3)`; Corollary 15.8 and Theorem 15.9 extend the statement to the loops
  `(gamma_0, ..., gamma_(n-1))` over a field `F` fixing `F`.
- [P] Statements about `Aut(Q_3)` (abstract, pp. 1-2): the abstract and the introduction state that `Aut(Q_n)` is
  `GL_3(F_2) x {+-1}^(n-3)` for `n > 3`, and that "one can verify by direct computations that `Aut(Q_3) = GL_3(F_2)`"; the introduction
  attributes `Aut(Q_n) = Aut(Q_(n-1)) x {+-1}` for `n > 3` to Kirshtein, with a proof that this paper corrects.

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Product. `Q_n` is the sign loop `+-e_m` of `cd_core.cd_sign(2^n, p, q)`, of order `2^(n+1)`. The doubling of Eqs. 4.1 to 4.3
is the loop form of `(a,b)(c,d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))` for `*` the inverse and `gamma = -1`; the paper notes in Remark 4.1
that its scheme places `j` on the right and equals the usual scheme on the opposite loop.

[I] The abstract's identification of `Aut(Q_n)` with `GL_3(F_2) x {+-1}^(n-3)` is therefore short by the factor 8 of the
sign-change automorphisms at every `n >= 3`. The body of the paper states `Aut(Q_n) = Aut(Q_3, *) x {+-1}^(n-3)` (Corollary 15.7),
which is correct with `|Aut(Q_3, *)| = 1344`, so the discrepancy is in the displayed description of `Aut(Q_3)` as `GL_3(F_2)`,
which is the image of `Aut(Q_3)` acting on `Q_3 / Z(Q_3)`. The check covers `n <= 4`; `|Aut(Q_5)|` was not computed.

## Repository bearing

[I] Automorphisms of the sign loop (monomial automorphisms of the algebra): `|Aut(Q_n)| = 1344 * 2^(n-3)` for `n = 3, 4` by
exact count and, by Corollary 15.7, for every `n >= 3`. This agrees with [Kirshtein](1102.5151.md) Theorem 41 and
complements the exact linear result `Aut(S) = G2 x S3` for the sedenions
([evidence](../../evidence/sedenion-automorphisms/README.md)). Claim `sedenion-g2-bundle` and the associator claims
`cd-associator-census`, `cd-associator-phase-degree`: none directly; Lemma 13.4 (the associator of every basis of an octonion loop
is the same central element) is the sign fact behind the octonion subalgebra counts of [Cawagas et al.](0907.2047.md).

[I] Dimensions 32 and above. Theorems 6.8, 10.1 and 15.6 hold for every `n`; the paper's automorphism description reaches all
`Q_n`, and Corollary 10.4 explains why `Q_4`, `Q_5`, ... are not Moufang. Zero divisors, annihilator dimensions and Stiefel
elements: none. Moreno's Stiefel question (`math/0512517` Section 5): none.
