## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Hyper-complex quantum theories predict that phases need not commute; single photons pass through a Sagnac interferometer holding two materials with refractive indices of opposite sign, one a metamaterial.
- [P] The phases commute to high precision, which limits that particular prediction of hyper-complex quantum theories.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The experiment bounds a deviation from complex quantum theory; it reports no octonionic or quaternionic amplitudes. Its scope is disputed by [Adler](1604.04950.md) and defended in [the authors' comment](1607.01648.md). The repository's sedenion circuits are ordinary complex quantum mechanics encoding an algebra, which this experiment neither supports nor constrains.
