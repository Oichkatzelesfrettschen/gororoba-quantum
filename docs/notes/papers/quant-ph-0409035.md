## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a quantum algorithm verifies a product of two n x n matrices over any integral domain with bounded error in worst-case time O(n^(5/3)) and expected time O(n^(5/3) / min(w, sqrt n)^(1/3)), with `w` the number of wrong entries, against the classical O(n^2) of Freivalds.
- [P] Theorem 6 (p. 6): Product Verification always accepts a correct product, rejects a wrong one with probability at least 2/3, and uses O(n) space; Theorem 1 (p. 5) is Szegedy's quantum-walk search on which it rests.
