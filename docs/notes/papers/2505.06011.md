## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] Method (pp. 1-4): the Clifford algebra `G_n = Cl(n, 0)`; a primary 3-form `Phi_i` is a set of seven triples `e_ijk` covering all 21 edges of the 6-simplex once
  (30 of them, Table 1, p. 7), a signed form `Phi_(i,j)` flips signs of terms (128 per primary), and a positive term `e_ijk` imposes the 3-cycle
  `e_i e_j = e_k`, `e_j e_k = e_i`, `e_k e_i = e_j` with negative squares. `Phi_1 = e123 + e145 + e167 + e246 + e257 + e347 + e356` (p. 4).
- [P] Section 2 (pp. 4-10): the dual `Phi*` is the 4-form `-e1234567 Phi`; Lemma 1 (p. 5) states that the terms of `{1 + Phi*_i}` and `{1 + Phi_i + Phi*_i + e1234567}` form
  commuting subalgebras of `Spin(7)` and `Pin(7)`. **Theorem 1 (Classification, p. 6):** an invertible multivector `rho_(i,j) = (3 e1234567 - (-1)^sigma Phi_(i,j))/4` has
  `rho^2 = -1` exactly for the 16 octonion representations in each primary (`O`); otherwise `Phi_(i,j)` generates a non-associative algebra with 4, 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16
  non-associative triples, `P_4, P_8, P_10, P_12, P_14, P_16`; the octonions have 28 non-associative and 7 associative triples. The proof uses the isomorphisms of Tables 2 to 5
  (pp. 8-11) and a uniqueness count of the 128 sign patterns; the primaries `Phi_11` and `Phi_20` carry no remainder and generate only octonions among their
  single-sign variants. The count of octonion representations is 30 x 16 = 480.
- [P] Section 4 (pp. 12-14): the pseudo-octonion algebras `P_k` are power-associative and have 12 zero divisors each
  of the form `(a + b)(c + d) = 0` with `a, b, c, d` distinct pure basis elements (the list for `P_4` is on p. 13); octonions have none. The sedenions have 252
  non-associative triples and 84 zero divisors [citing Cawagas 2004]. The 15-dimensional primary `Phi` of 35 triples (the lines of the 14-simplex, printed with hexadecimal
  indices, p. 13) with each term negated in turn yields the printed counts of non-associative triples `210, 210, ..., 252, 228, ..., 204` (36 numbers, p. 13); the
  21st term `e48C`, negated, gives 252 and "is isomorphic to sedenions"; 105 further rotations generate 100 distinct "pseudo-sedenion" algebras.
- [P] Section 5 (pp. 14-18): Theorem 2 (Automorphism, p. 14): the 4-form terms `e_jklm` of `Phi*_i` define rotations `R_jklm` that fix `Phi_(i,O)`, which gives the
  automorphisms of the octonions (`G2`) in the Clifford setting; Tables 7 to 10 give the `G2` Lie table and a construction of `G2` from Table 8.
- [P] Section 6 (pp. 18-19): the series of algebras `S = P_252, P_3612, P_36204, P_319788, P_2678508` for the sedenions and the next four doublings, all power-associative; the
  pseudo-octonion algebras are called forerunners of the sedenions; "there is no `G2` equivalent for `S`"; a Python calculator (github.com/GPWilmot/geoalg) verifies the results.

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Product. Wilmot's algebras are defined by oriented triples, not by a doubling, so no pair-product convention applies. His 35 triples of the 15-dimensional `Phi`
are exactly the 35 lines `{i, j, i xor j}` of the XOR index group (checked: every printed product has index `i xor j`), so the index labeling agrees with `cd_core`'s
and the difference between his algebras and `cd_core` is a choice of orientation `s` of each line (`cd_core` makes all 35 lines cyclic: `e_a e_b = s e_c`, `e_b e_c = s e_a`,
`e_c e_a = s e_b`, checked at `D = 8, 16, 32`, 7, 35 and 155 lines).

[I] Check results. Scratch scripts (ignored `docs/local/`, run with `-I`) built the algebras of this paper from the printed triples and compared them with the printed
counts and with `cd_core`:

## Repository bearing

[I] Orientation structure. The sedenion product of `cd_core` is one of the `2^35` orientations of the 35 lines; this paper enumerates oriented-line algebras but
reaches `cd_core` only by the counting statement above. The algebraic-degree claims `cd-sign-cocycle-degree`, `cd-sign-row-degree` and `cd-right-row-degree` concern `cd_core`'s
orientation and are not touched. The claim `cd-associator-census` (non-associative triples of the sedenions): the count 252 in canonical order and the equal all-orders
count agree with the repository's census at `D = 16`; the paper's counts at `D >= 32` differ from the repository's definitions, so none of them is imported.

[I] Zero divisors and Stiefel. The zero divisors of the paper are the two-term elements of the pseudo-octonion algebras at dimension 8, a different object from the
sedenion zero divisors: claims `a5-stiefel-universal`, `a6-stiefel-witness`, `dimension32-global-topology`, `sedenion-g2-bundle`: none. The statement that the sedenions have
no `G2` analog is consistent with the repository's `Aut(S) = G2 x S_3` ([evidence](../../evidence/sedenion-automorphisms/README.md)), which contains `G2`. Dimensions 64 and above:
Section 6 names the `P_k` series to dimension 256, not verified here. Moreno's Stiefel question (`math/0512517` Section 5): none.
