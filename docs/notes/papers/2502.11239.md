## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1) and Section I (p. 3): excluding memory and data transfer, an HHL advantage over the classical conjugate-gradient method could emerge at matrix size N of about 2^33 to 2^48 or lower, requiring O(10^5) physical qubits, O(10^12 to 10^13) J and about 10 days (O(10^6) s) under surface-code fault tolerance with 15-1, 116-12 and 225-1 magic-state distillation, at kappa, s of O(10 to 100), epsilon of about 0.01 and physical error 10^-5.
