## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): structures related to octonions are said to be ubiquitous in M-theory; all the exceptional Lie groups and the projective Cayley line and plane appear in M-theory; exceptional `G_2`-holonomy manifolds show up as compactifying spaces and are related to the M2 brane and 3-form; the paper reviews the evidence, stresses the relations among these objects when extant and understood, and argues for a better understanding of the role of the octonions themselves, in particular non-associativity, in M-theory.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the five exceptional simple Lie groups `G_2, F_4, E_6, E_7, E_8` are related to the octonions; the Cayley plane and the octonionic projective line, and the geometries of the Magic Square, are named as fundamental octonionic geometries; the four coincidences `A_1 = B_1 = C_1`, `B_2 = C_2`, `A_3 = D_3`, `D_2 = A_1 x A_1` among simple Lie groups are related to some representations of the exceptional groups.
