## Statements

- [P] Abstract and Contributions (pp. 1-2): computation over the quaternions H is polynomially equivalent to the standard complex circuit model (same class BQP up to polynomial reductions), through the multiplicative embedding iota : H -> M_2(C) (Theorem 2 and Proposition 7 as cited on p. 2; verify the statements in Section 3).
- [P] Abstract and p. 3: over the octonions, non-associativity gives path-dependent evolution, order-sensitive adjoints and inner products, no canonical multipartite tensor product, and possible failure of energy conservation outside associative sectors; the paper lists four mitigation routes: confinement to associative subalgebras, G_2-invariant coding, dynamical decoupling of associator terms, and a seven-factor algebraic synthesis in associative sectors.
- [P] Page 2: it reviews the octonionic measurement-only scheme of Freedman, Shokrian-Zini and Wang (arXiv 1811.08580) and describes its own octonionic results as obstruction and mitigation analyses, not a gate-based octonionic model.
- [P] Abstract: routes to implementation "via pulse-level synthesis on current hardware" are discussed for the quaternionic model.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Supports the repo's stance (`docs/notes/algebra_circuit_rules.md`) that a Cayley-Dickson product enters circuits only as a linear encoding a -> L_a on a complex Hilbert space, never as a native non-associative qubit model: the obstructions listed here (no associative tensor product, ambiguous adjoints) are the reason. The paper stops at the octonions; it carries nothing for dimension 16, 32 or zero divisors.
