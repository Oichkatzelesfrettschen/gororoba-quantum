## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a rigorous discrete adiabatic theorem bounds the error by the spectral gap of an intrinsically discrete time evolution, and yields a quantum linear-system algorithm with complexity strictly linear in the condition number `kappa`, matching a known lower bound, with combined scaling O(kappa log(1/epsilon)); constant factors are determined for gate-count estimates.
- [P] Introduction (p. 2): the quantum linear-system problem (QLSP) asks for a state `|x>` proportional to the solution of `A x = b` for an N-by-N matrix `A`; HHL scales quadratically in `kappa` and linearly in `1/epsilon`, and Ambainis's variable-time amplitude amplification improved the `kappa` scaling.
- [P] Table I (p. 3) lists the history of the lowest-scaling QLSP algorithms; Section I (p. 3) states the result O(kappa log(1/epsilon)).
