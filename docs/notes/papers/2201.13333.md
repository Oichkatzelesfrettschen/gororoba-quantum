## Statements read

- [P] Abstract and Section II.B (pp. 1-2): a two-layer cyclic interferometer of N = 2n balanced beam splitters on 2n modes turns n indistinguishable photons into an interference fringe whose visibility is the genuine n-photon indistinguishability; an 8-mode femtosecond-laser-written chip with four photons from a quantum-dot source gave up to 0.81 +- 0.03.
- [P] Section II.B (p. 2): Fock states are written |s> = prod_i a_{s_i}^dagger |0> and the output law P_{g,h} is the probability of Fock state h from input g.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the authors state that the indistinguishability of the full set of photons is not characterized by the HOM interference of all pairs, and that only lower and upper bounds on it have been defined.
- [I] A three-photon or heralded Ascella encoding therefore cannot take the platform two-photon HOM figure as its interference visibility; this reading rests on the abstract and introduction only.
