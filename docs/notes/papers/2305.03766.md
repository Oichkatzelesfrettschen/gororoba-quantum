## Main claims and evidence

- [P] An adaptive circuit on Quantinuum's H2 trapped-ion processor prepares the ground state of D4 topological order on a 27-qubit kagome lattice with fidelity per site above 98.4 percent.
- [P] Creating and moving anyons along Borromean rings in spacetime, anyon interferometry detects an intrinsically non-Abelian braiding process.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Operational realization here means specified many-body states, fusion rules, braiding operations and matched measurements, not a relabeling of basis states. Non-Abelian operations are noncommutative and associative, so the work supports engineered realization and gives no evidence about a matrix-associativity obstruction.
