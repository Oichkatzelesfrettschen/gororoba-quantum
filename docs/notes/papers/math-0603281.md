## Relevance

[P] The paper develops XOR-based digitizations (binary-string
representations) of Cayley-Dickson zero-divisor structures and proposes
connections with singularity theory, nonlinear systems, networks, and
computation. [I] The XOR-indexing convention (basis-pair XOR signatures
grouping the 21 bivector coordinates into seven triples) used in the
norm-defect measurement analysis follows the digitization approach
introduced here. [I] The proposed connections with physical singularities
and computation are hypotheses requiring independent models, not
established results.
