## Transcribed statements

- [P] Abstract: the paper "details the mathematics of the two finite simple groups" with two- and three-dimensional
  irreducible representations, the icosahedral group `A_5`, a subgroup of `SO(3)`, and `PSL_2(7)`, a subgroup of
  `SU(3)`.
- [P] Section 3 (p. 6): `PSL_2(7)` is the projective special linear group of `2 x 2` matrices over `F_7`, contains
  168 elements, has one complex three-dimensional irreducible representation and its conjugate, "is isomorphic to
  `GL_3(2)`, the group of non-singular `3 x 3` matrices with entries in `F_2`", and is the only simple group on
  the list of finite subgroups of `SU(3)` that the paper cites (labeled `Sigma(168)` there).
- [P] Eq. 10 to 12 (p. 7): the presentation `< A, B | A^2 = B^3 = (AB)^7 = [A, B]^4 = 1 >` with
  `[A, B] = A^(-1) B^(-1) A B`, realized over `F_7` by `A = [[0, -1], [1, 0]]` and `B = [[0, -1], [1, 1]]`, so that
  `AB = [[1, 1], [0, 1]]` and `[A, B] = [[1, 1], [1, 2]]`.
- [P] p. 7: six conjugacy classes, with representatives and sizes `1 (1)`, `A (21)`, `B (56)`, `[A, B] (42)`,
  `AB (24)` and `AB^2 (24)`; six irreducible representations of dimensions `1, 3, 3-bar, 6, 7, 8`, with the
  character table printed there (entries `b_7 = (-1 + i sqrt(7))/2` on the order-7 classes of the triplet,
  Eq. 13, p. 8).
- [P] p. 8: the Kronecker products, among them `3 (x) 3 = 3-bar_a + 6_s`, `3 (x) 3-bar = 1 + 8`,
  `6 (x) 6 = (1 + 6 + 6 + 8)_s + (7 + 8)_a` and `7 (x) 7 = (1 + 6 + 6 + 7 + 8)_s + (3 + 3-bar + 7 + 8)_a`; and
  (pp. 8-9) the branching of `SU(3)` representations to `PSL_2(7)`: `3 = 3`, `6 = 6`, `8 = 8`, `10 = 3 + 7`, `15 = 7 + 8`. The
  sextet is real for `PSL_2(7)` and complex for `SU(3)`; `PSL_2(7)` has two cubic invariants built from three
  sextets where `SU(3)` has one, and `SU(3)` has no seven-dimensional representation.
- [P] Eq. 22 and 23 (p. 11): the triplet generators `A^[3]` and `B^[3]` as `3 x 3` matrices over `Q(eta)` with
  `eta` a primitive seventh root of unity and prefactor `i/sqrt(7)`; `A` is real symmetric, "reproducing Klein's
  involution", and the antitriplet generators are the complex conjugates (Eq. 24, p. 12).
- [P] Section 5 (pp. 19-20): `PSL_2(7)` has two maximal subgroups, `S_4` of order 24 with the presentation
  `< a, b | a^4 = b^2 = (ba)^3 = 1 >`, generated over `F_7` by `a = [[1, 1], [1, 2]]` and `b = [[4, 4], [1, 3]]`
  (Eq. 60) and expressed as `a = [A, B]`, `b = A (AB^2)^3 (AB)^3` (Eq. 61), and the Frobenius group `Z_7 x| Z_3` of
  order 21 with the presentation `< c, d | c^7 = d^3 = 1, d^(-1) c d = c^4 >`.
- [P] Eqs. 77 to 79 (p. 26): with one triplet there is a unique quartic invariant,
  `I^[4] = phi_1^3 phi_3 + phi_3^3 phi_2 + phi_2^3 phi_1`, the Klein quartic; "`PSL_2(7)` acts as the group of
  conformal transformations of the quartic into itself".

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Check results, from a scratch script under the ignored `docs/local/`: the matrices of Eq. 11 satisfy
  `A^2 = B^3 = (AB)^7 = [A, B]^4 = 1` in `PSL_2(F_7)` (matrices identified with their negatives), `AB` and
  `[A, B]` equal the paper's values up to sign (`AB = -[[1, 1], [0, 1]]` for the representative the script
  forms), and the group generated by `A` and `B` has order 168 with 1, 21, 56, 42 and 48 elements of order
  1, 2, 3, 4 and 7, which agrees with the class sizes on p. 7 (the two order-7 classes of 24 elements each give
  48). The `F_2` and `GL_3(2)` isomorphism and the character table were not recomputed.
- [I] Link to the star-lattice note
  ([cd-star-lattice-tower](../topics/cd-star-lattice-tower.md#the-boundary-as-a-paley-matrix)): the octonion boundary
  group `G_8 = SL(2, 7)` acts on the projective line over `F_7` through `PSL(2, 7)` of order 168, and the
  stabilizer of `e_0` of order 21 "being 7:3" is the Frobenius group of this paper's Section 5. Both are the unique
  non-abelian group of order 21, so the identification holds as groups; the repository has not matched the
  paper's generators `c, d` with the stabilizer's elements. The paper makes no statement about the octonions, the
  Fano plane, the Cayley-Dickson algebras or Paley matrices.
- [I] Convention: the generators are projective `2 x 2` matrices over `F_7` and complex `3 x 3` matrices; no
  signed permutation of `cd_core` basis elements, no sign rule and no multiplication table occurs, so there is no
  `cd_core` convention to compare ("no table given"), and the paper supplies no `T(e_i e_j) = T(e_i) T(e_j)`
  certificate.
