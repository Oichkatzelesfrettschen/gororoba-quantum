## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): two-photon indistinguishability of 88 +- 1 % between photons from two distant quantum-dot cavity sources, stated to reach the upper bound set by the intrinsic indistinguishability of successive photons from each source; the sources are InGaAs quantum dots in electrically connected micropillar cavities with two tuning mechanisms.
