## Statements

- [P] Definition 2.1 (p. 4): max-LINSAT over F_p asks for x in F_p^n maximizing the number of constraints sum_j B_ij x_j in F_i; DQI uses the quantum Fourier transform to reduce it to decoding the code defined by B, and the expected number of satisfied constraints follows from the number of errors the decoder corrects (p. 4).
- [P] Definition 2.2 (p. 5): Optimal Polynomial Intersection (OPI) asks for Q in F_p[y] of degree at most n - 1 maximizing the number of y with Q(y) in F_y; on OPI the paper reports a superpolynomial speedup over known classical algorithms (abstract, p. 1).
