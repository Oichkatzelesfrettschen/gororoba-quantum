## Statements read

- [P] Abstract and Fig. 1 (p. 1): active temporal-to-spatial demultiplexing routes n photons of one temporal stream into n spatial modes, using electro-optically reconfigurable directional couplers on a lithium niobate chip; the abstract states the scaling is quasi-polynomial in photon number and that the measured performance can enable a six-photon rate three orders of magnitude above the equivalent heralded SPDC source.
- [P] Eqs. (1) and (2) (p. 2): the n-photon count rate is R (eta_SD eta_det)^n S_DM(n) with S_DM(n) = (1/n) [eta_DM^n + (n - 1) ((1 - eta_DM)/(n - 1))^n] for the active scheme, against the 1/n^n factor of a passive beam-splitter chain.
- [P] Table I (p. 2): measured splitting ratios of the three switches, on 0.87 +- 0.06, 0.94 +- 0.05 and 0.90 +- 0.06, off 0.06 +- 0.02, 0.13 +- 0.03 and 0.13 +- 0.05.
- [I] The paper concerns the lineage of the demultiplexed source (Senellart and co-authors) behind the Ascella source-connected modes; it carries no statement about Quandela's own demultiplexer design, which is unverified here.
