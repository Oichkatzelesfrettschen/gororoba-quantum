## Statements read

- [P] Abstract and p. 2: a quantum-dot micropillar source emits a pulse train that a passive temporal-to-spatial demultiplexer (a chain of n - 1 beam splitters with tuned transmittances, with delay lines of 12.5 ns) routes into 2, 3 and 4 spatial modes; single-photon purity 1 - g2(0) = 0.990 +- 0.001 at 1.2 P0; the paper reports source brightness up to 0.14 into a single-mode fiber and 3.6 MHz detected at 32 % detector efficiency.
- [P] Fig. 2 (p. 3): n-photon probability per trial of the demultiplexed source falls steeply with n (the passive scheme scales as 1/n^n, per the text on p. 3).
- [P] Eq. (1) (p. 3): for two photons of pairwise indistinguishability I entering inputs i, j of a network with submatrix C and leaving on outputs k1, k2, the coincidence probability is p(2) = ((1 + I)/2) |per C|^2 + ((1 - I)/2) |det C|^2, which reduces to |per C|^2 at I = 1.
- [I] Eq. (1) equals the linear mixture P_dist + I (P_ind - P_dist) of the distinguishable and indistinguishable laws, because (|per|^2 + |det|^2)/2 is the distinguishable law; a local Perceval check of that mixture is in the Ascella folding analysis (session report, not retained).
