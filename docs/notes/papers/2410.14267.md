## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): Hsiang algebras are a class of nonassociative algebra defined in terms of a relation quartic in the elements; they arise in the construction of real algebraic minimal cones and, through work of Nadirashvili and Vladut, of singular viscosity solutions of nonlinear uniformly elliptic partial differential equations. Their classification is hard and rests on a Peirce decomposition like that used for Jordan algebras. The paper introduces two tools: a class of algebras called quasicomposition, which generalize the Hurwitz algebras (the reals, complexes, quaternions and octonions) and cross-product algebras, and a tripling construction that associates to an algebra one of three times its dimension and "can be thought of as a kind of analogue of the Cayley-Dickson doubling process". One main result states that the triple is an exceptional Hsiang algebra if and only if the original algebra is quasicomposition.
- [P] Contents (p. 1): Hsiang eigencubics (Section 2), metrized commutative algebras (3), from Hsiang eigencubics to Hsiang algebras (4), quasicomposition algebras (5), the tripling construction and Hsiang algebras (6), realizability of exceptional Hsiang algebras as triples (7).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The tripling construction is an analogue of Cayley-Dickson doubling that triples the dimension; it applies to quasicomposition algebras, which contain the Hurwitz algebras. The abstract's statement is that the triple is an exceptional Hsiang algebra exactly when the original algebra is quasicomposition.
