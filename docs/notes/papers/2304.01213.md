## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the exceptional Jordan algebra is the algebra of `3 x 3` Hermitian matrices with octonionic entries, the only algebra of Jordan's formulation of quantum mechanics not equivalent to the conventional formulation. The paper claims that it explains values of the fundamental constants of the standard model and their relation to gravitation, unravels a connection between general relativity and the weak force, and predicts a new `U(1)` gravitational interaction that modifies general relativity and provides a basis for Modified Newtonian Dynamics.
