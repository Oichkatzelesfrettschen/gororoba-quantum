## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a unified treatment of the `2 x 2` analog of the Freudenthal-Tits magic square of Lie groups, with an explicit representation in terms of matrix groups over composition algebras. Keywords: division algebras, magic squares, orthogonal groups, Clifford algebras.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the Freudenthal-Tits magic square is a `4 x 4` array of semisimple Lie algebras whose rows and columns are labeled by composition algebras, and "in the row or column labeled by the octonions or the split octonions" it produces four of the five exceptional Lie algebras `f_4, e_6, e_7, e_8`. The paper is part of an effort to give a concise construction of the exceptional Lie groups through a magic square of Lie groups.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is a reference for the statement that the exceptional Lie algebras arise from the octonions and the split octonions through the magic square, which the [adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md) already uses for the Albert algebra. The `2 x 2` case concerns orthogonal groups and Clifford algebras, with no Cayley-Dickson doubling beyond the composition algebras and no zero-divisor content on the page read.
