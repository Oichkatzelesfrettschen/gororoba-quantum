## Statements

- [P] Table 1 (p. 2): for GF(2)^(m x n), m >= n, this construction uses O(n^2 m) Toffoli gates, Toffoli depth O(m log2 n + n (log2 m)^4), m - 1 temporary qubits and no supplemental (garbage) qubits; the classical arithmetic complexity of Gaussian elimination is O(n^2 m).
- [P] Definition 1 (p. 3): the pseudo row echelon form of a full-rank matrix keeps the upper-triangular entries of the row echelon form and stores in its lower part the information that makes the map reversible; footnote 1: handling a rank-deficient matrix is doable, but substantially complicates the construction.
- [P] Proposition 3 and Table 3 (p. 6): over GF(p^l), with alpha = l ceil(log2 p), Algorithm 1 uses O(nm(log2^3 m + alpha log2^2 alpha + alpha n)) Toffoli gates at depth O(n log2^4 m + alpha n log2 m), O(n^2 m) multiplications and additions, O(nm) multiplicative inverses and O(n^2 m) additive inverses.
- [P] Section 2.4 (p. 4): earlier GF(2) elimination circuits served information-set decoding under Grover search (Esser et al., Perriello et al.) and the offline Simon algorithm (Bonnetain and Jaques).
- [P] Proposition 6 (p. 11): RowReduction at iteration j' costs at most 4(m-j')(n-j'-1) multiplications at depth 4 ceil((n-j'-1)/(j'+1)), 2(m-j') multiplicative inverses at depth 2 and 4(m-j'-1)(n-j'-1) controlled additions.

## Mapping to this repository

[I] The construction is exact arithmetic over F_p with the same field-operation count as classical elimination, so it serves as a subroutine of a search rather than a faster rank. Its data register is l ceil(log2 p) m n qubits, 15 per F_32009 entry; one character block of the W8 truncated rank at N = 12 (5,152 columns, about 24,570 product rows) would need about 1.9 x 10^9 logical qubits, and the A_5 ranks are the unknown that its full-rank construction assumes away ([quantum linear algebra against the A_5 fiber computations](../topics/quantum-linear-algebra-fit-a5.md)).
