## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper decomposes the Lie algebra `e_8(-24)` into representations of `e_7(-25) + sl(2, R)` using the authors' description of `e_8` in terms of (generalized) `3 x 3` matrices over pairs of division algebras; Freudenthal's description of both `e_7` and its minimal representation is realized explicitly within `e_8`, with the action given by the generalized matrix commutator and a natural parameterization by division algebras; and the standard operations on the Albert algebra (trace of the Jordan product, the Freudenthal product and the determinant) are implemented using commutators in `e_8`.
- [P] Table I (p. 2): the "half-split" real form of the Freudenthal-Tits magic square, a `4 x 4` array of real Lie algebras with rows `R', C', H', O'` and columns `R, C, H, O`. Its last row reads `f_4(4), e_6(2), e_7(-5), e_8(-24)`, and its last column reads `f_4 = su(3, O)`, `e_6(-26) = sl(3, O)`, `e_7(-25) = sp(6, O)`, `e_8(-24)`.
- [P] Section II (p. 2): the 248 elements of `e_8(-24)` are `3 x 8 x 8 + 2 x 14 = 192 + 28 = 220` tracefree `3 x 3` anti-Hermitian matrices over `O' tensor O`, together with the `14 + 14 = 28` elements of `g_2(2) + g_2`. The generators are the matrices `X_p`, `Y_p`, `Z_p` and the diagonal matrices `D_q`, `E_q`, `F_q` of equations (1) to (3), with `p` in `O' or O` and `q` imaginary; nested generators such as `D_(p,q) = (1/2)[D_p, D_q]` are written as generalized matrices with the composition `(p o q) r = p (q r)` (equations (4) to (6)).
- [P] Section III (p. 3): the Albert algebra is `H_3(O)`, the `3 x 3` Hermitian matrices over the octonions `O`, with Jordan product `X o Y = (1/2)(XY + YX)` (equation (15)) and Freudenthal product (16). The generators of `e_6 = e_6(-26)` are 26 boosts, 14 derivations of `O` (elements of `g_2`) and the remaining rotations, acting on the Albert algebra by `X -> phi X + X phi^dagger` (equation (17)). Freudenthal's description of `e_7 = e_7(-25)` has elements `Theta = (phi, rho, A, B)` with `phi` in `e_6`, `rho` real and `A, B` in `H_3(O)` (equation (19)): the 78 elements of `e_6`, the dilation, and 27 + 27 = 54 translations, 133 in all. Its minimal representation consists of `P = (X, Y, p, q)` with `X, Y` in `H_3(O)` and `p, q` real (equation (20)), of dimension `27 + 27 + 1 + 1 = 56`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is a reference for three octonionic statements about exceptional Lie algebras: the Albert algebra is `H_3(O)` over the (non-split) octonions, with the Jordan product (15); the cubic form belongs to the Albert algebra, while the 56-dimensional Freudenthal module carries the structure of equation (20), not the Albert determinant; and `e_8(-24)` splits as 220 matrix generators plus the derivations `g_2(2) + g_2` of `O' tensor O`. The repository records the Albert algebra and the exceptional Lie algebras built from the octonions in the [adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md); the paper adds the commutator realization of the Jordan and Freudenthal operations inside `e_8`.
- [I] The pages opened use the octonions and split octonions only; no Cayley-Dickson doubling beyond dimension 8 and no zero-divisor statement occurs there.
