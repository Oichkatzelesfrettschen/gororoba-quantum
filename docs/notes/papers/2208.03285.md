## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): end-to-end analyses of data from high-energy physics experiments with machine and deep learning go directly from low-level detector information to high-level quantities that classify the interactions; the most popular class of algorithms is convolutional neural networks operating on experimental data formatted as images; such analyses skip stages of the traditional workflow, including the reconstruction of particles, and so avoid efficiency losses and sources of inaccuracy; in many cases deep learning end-to-end analyses have been shown to have significantly increased performance compared to previous state-of-the-art methods.
