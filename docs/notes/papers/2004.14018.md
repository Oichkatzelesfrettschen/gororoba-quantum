## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are the printed page numbers, which equal the PDF page
indices.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper develops "a framework for characterising non-Markovian dynamics in
  quantum systems" and tests it "on multi-qubit superconducting quantum devices"; "our reconstruction
  predicts the behaviour of the devices with an infidelity of 10^-3".
- [P] Setting (p. 2): non-Markovian dynamics "constitute any interaction between a system and its
  environment which then affects the system at a later time; the environment need not even be
  coherent". The process tensor is "a multi-linear map on the control operations", so a controlled
  process is described by the final states rho_k(A_{k-1:0}) = T_{k:0}[A_{k-1:0}] for sequences of
  control operations (Eq. 1) and is determined by its response to a basis of control sequences
  (Eq. 2). The paper states that this framework "gives rise to a clear necessary and sufficient
  definition of quantum non-Markovianity" and cites ref. [25] for it.
- [P] Restricted tensor (p. 3): on the IBM devices only unitary control is available, so the paper
  reconstructs a "restricted" process tensor on the span of unitary maps, of dimension
  d_S^4 - 2 d_S^2 + 2 for system dimension d_S; the experiments reconstruct a four-time restricted
  tensor for one qubit from 28 random unitaries and test its predictions by the reconstruction
  fidelity (Eq. 3). The devices were ibmq_johannesburg, ibmq_boeblingen, ibmq_poughkeepsie and
  ibmq_valencia.
- [P] Memory bound (p. 4): "to fully account for the non-Markovianity in a system requires in-situ
  measurements, which break all correlations between the system and its environment, and represent a
  clean barrier to any past-future dependence"; without access to them "a restricted process tensor
  can only infer aspects of the non-Markovianity", so the paper "extract[s] a lower bound on
  non-Markovianity". The barrier is the maximally depolarising channel R[rho] = I/d for all rho
  (Eq. 4), which lies in the span of the unitary operations.
- [P] Intervention design (pp. 4-5, Methods p. 10): "a nonzero mutual information between the input
  operation and final measurement suggests information has travelled into the environment and
  returned after R has been applied". The paper numerically searches for an encoding set E (two
  unitary operations applied with equal probability) and a decoding set D (two orthogonal
  measurement effects), maximizing the conditional mutual information of Eqs. 5 to 7, with R in the
  first position, the second, or both, and with I(E:D) the Shannon mutual information of Eq. 8 over
  the joint distribution p(E,D).
- [P] Result (p. 5, Fig. 3b): the maximum conditional mutual information is "a conservative lower
  bound for non-Markovian memory"; it is non-zero "in almost every case", and larger when
  neighbouring qubits are prepared in a coherent state (|+>); the extended ibmq_johannesburg
  experiment "is the only case for which CMI overlaps zero in all three tests", which the paper
  reads as a finite memory lifetime that this experiment loosely upper-bounds.
- [P] Comparison with gate set tomography (pp. 5-6, Fig. 4): on ibmq_valencia, over 64 circuits, the
  median reconstruction fidelity of a Markov-model prediction from GST matches the process tensor
  with neighbours in |0>, and with neighbours in |+> GST "suffers from a fidelity drop of about
  1.2%". The paper states that the comparison "is not framed competitively" because the techniques
  estimate different objects.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Drift series ([bdsc_drift_series.py](../../../scripts/bdsc_drift_series.py)). The paper defines
  memory by intervention: choose the past operation, insert a barrier that erases the system, and
  test whether the final statistic still depends on the past choice. A repeat series of one frozen
  request, in which the preceding job cannot be set or erased by the experimenter, tests the weaker
  statement that the repeats share more variance than counting statistics allow. The drift series'
  excess variance is therefore a variance statement; a claim of memory in the sense of this paper
  needs an intervention that the cloud interface does not expose between jobs.
- [I] The reconstruction-fidelity metric (Eq. 3) and the 1.2 % GST gap bound how much of a
  device-level deviation a Markov model hides on the 2020 IBM devices; the Heron r2 devices of
  [ibm_heron_platform.md](../ibm_heron_platform.md) were not measured by this paper.
