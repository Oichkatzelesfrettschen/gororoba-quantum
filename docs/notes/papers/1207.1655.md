## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Sequential Monte Carlo with Bayesian experimental design infers Hamiltonian parameters online, with tunable computational and experimental cost.
- [P] The method learns parameters that drift between experiments and in the presence of unknown noise, and estimates its own Cramer-Rao bound.
