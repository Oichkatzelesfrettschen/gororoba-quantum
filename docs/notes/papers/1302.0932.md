## Results needed by the project

1. [P] Common-state assumption (p. 1): tomography assigns one density matrix
   to a sequence of systems. The justification rests on permutation
   invariance (Renner): if the order of the measured systems is randomized
   and a subset is traced out, the remaining systems are approximately
   independently and identically distributed. Measuring each observable of
   the quorum in one long block, the standard practice, voids that argument.
2. [P] Drift example (p. 2): with a source that drifts across three
   sequential groups, `rho_bar_1 = |+><+|`, `rho_bar_2 = |+i><+i|` and
   `rho_bar_3 = |0><0|` give outcome `+1` in every measurement of `sigma_x`,
   `sigma_y` and `sigma_z`. Linear inversion returns a non-positive matrix
   (Eq. 1), and maximum likelihood returns a projector; neither predicts
   future outcomes.
3. [P] Cause (p. 2): the failures share one mechanism, sample-apparatus
   correlation. Experimental drift is the simple case, in which the sample
   state and the apparatus setting both vary with time. Randomizing the order
   of measurements removes the drift case; other correlations stay.
4. [P] Detection (pp. 2-3, 5): the Akaike information criterion
   `Omega_k = ln L_k - K_k` ranks a single-density-matrix model against an
   alternative with one matrix per time block or per measurement setting. For
   an overcomplete measurement set, repeating each observable in two blocks
   lets the criterion compare the two estimates of each average; in the
   simulation of a random-walk angle over 3000 measurements in six blocks of
   500 (Fig. 1), the single-matrix model ranks `Omega_s - Omega_a = -5.07`,
   significantly worse, and tomography fails. A smaller drift leaves the
   single-matrix model ranked best (p. 5).
5. [P] Consequence (p. 7): what fails under strong evidence of drift is the
   independent and identically distributed assumption, so the joint state
   lacks de Finetti form. A relaxed form with a distribution over several
   states (Eq. 17, p. 8) needs its own validation against a richer model.

## Repository use

[I] Any estimate of an entangled-state fidelity, teleportation fidelity or
HOM visibility that pools runs taken at different times assumes the common
state of item 1. Acquisition that repeats each preparation or measurement
setting in at least two separated blocks gives the data that item 4 compares;
an acquisition that measures each setting once in one block has no internal
drift test. Randomize the order of settings where the experiment permits it.
