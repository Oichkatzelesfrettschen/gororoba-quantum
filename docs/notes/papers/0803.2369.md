## Setting and conventions

- [P] Section 0 (pp. 4-11): for an ideal `I` of a ring `A`, `nu_I(x) = sup{ n : x in I^n }` is an integer-valued order function; Definition 0.2.3 (p. 8)
  sets `nu-bar_I(x) = lim_(k -> infinity) nu_I(x^k)/k` and `nu-bar_I(J) = lim nu_I(J^k)/k`; Lemma 0.2.1 (p. 7) shows the limit exists in `[0, infinity]`;
  Proposition 0.2.5 (p. 8) gives `nu-bar_I(J) = inf_i nu-bar_I(x_i)` over generators of `J`; Corollary 0.2.6 (p. 9) shows `nu-bar_I` is an order function;
  Example 0.2.4 (p. 8) shows the limit need not be rational for a non-noetherian monoid algebra.
- [P] Remarks 0.2.12 (p. 11): the graded ring `gr-bar_I A` of the filtration by `nu-bar_I` is reduced, with a canonical surjection `gr_I A -> gr-bar_I A`
  whose kernel is the nilradical of `gr_I A`.
- [P] Definition 1.1 (p. 12): `f` in `A` is integral over `I` when it satisfies `f^k + a_1 f^(k-1) + ... + a_k = 0` with `a_i` in `I^i`. Lemma 1.3 (p. 12): `f` is
  integral over `I` if and only if `fT` is integral over the Rees algebra `P(I) = sum I^n T^n` in `A[T]`. Corollary-Definition 1.4 (p. 13): the set of such `f` is an
  ideal, the integral closure `I-bar`. Lemma 1.7 (p. 13): the integral closure of `P(I)` in `A[T]` is `sum I-bar^n T^n` (with `I-bar^n` the integral closure of `I^n`).
- [P] Setting of Sections 2 to 5: `X` is a reduced complex analytic space, `Y` a nowhere dense closed analytic subspace defined by a coherent ideal `I` (the statements
  carry the hypotheses "reduced" and "rare" explicitly).

## Theorem statements

- [P] Proposition 1.9 (p. 14): `f` is integral over `I` if and only if there is a finitely generated `A`-module `M` with `fM` contained in `IM` such that, whenever `aM = 0`, some power
  satisfies `a f^k = 0`; Remark 1.10 replaces the second condition by faithfulness of `M` when `I` is finitely generated and contains a nonzerodivisor.
  Proposition 1.15 (p. 17): an element integral over `I` has `nu-bar_I(f) >= 1`. Proposition 1.18 and Remark 1.19 (pp. 17-18): two ideals primary to the maximal ideal with the
  same integral closure have the same multiplicity, with Rees's converse for equidimensional analytic local algebras.
- [P] Proposition 1.14 (p. 16): for a reduced excellent ring and an ideal containing a nonzerodivisor, `I . (I^n)-bar = (I^(n+1))-bar` for `n` large (finiteness of the
  integral closure filtration).
- [P] Theorem 2.1 (p. 19): for `X` reduced complex analytic, `Y` rare with ideal `I`, `J` a coherent ideal and germs at `x`, the following are equivalent: (i) `J` is
  contained in `I-bar`; (ii) `nu-bar_I(J) >= 1`; (iii) for every germ of morphism `h : (D, 0) -> (X, x)` from the unit disc, `h^* J . O_(D,0)` lies in
  `h^* I . O_(D,0)` (the arc criterion); (iv) for every proper surjective morphism `pi : X' -> X` with `X'` a normal analytic space and `I . O_X'` invertible, `J . O_X'` lies
  in `I . O_X'` near `x` (the normalized blow-up criterion); (v) the same inclusion on the blow-up of a coherent ideal `K` with nowhere dense support; (vi) the inequality
  `|f(y)| <= C sup_i |g_i(y)|` for sections `f` of `J` and generators `g_i` of `I`. Corollary 2.2 (p. 23): `J` lies in `(I^k)-bar` if and only if `nu-bar_I(J) >= k`.
- [P] Theorem 2.6 (p. 24) and Proposition 2.8 (p. 25): the sheaf `I-bar` is a coherent ideal with germ `{ f : nu-bar_(I_y)(f) >= 1 }` at every point, and `sum (I^n)-bar` is a
  finitely presented `O_X`-algebra and a finite module over `sum I^n`.
- [P] Proposition 3.2 (p. 28): the normalized blow-up of `Y` is isomorphic over `X` to `Projan` of `sum (J^n)-bar` over the normalization `X-bar` composed with `X-bar -> X`; Proposition 3.3
  (p. 28) gives the formula `Projan sum (I^n)-bar` when the support of `Y` contains the non-normal points of `X`; Proposition 3.4 (p. 29), under the hypotheses of Proposition 3.3: the blow-up of `Y` is a normal space if and
  only if locally on `X` there is `N` with `(I^n)-bar = I^n` for `n >= N`, and the cusp example on p. 29 shows `Specan sum (I^n)-bar` can fail to be normal.
- [P] Theorem 4.1.6 (p. 32): with `pi : X' -> X` the normalized blow-up of `I` and `D_alpha` the irreducible components of the exceptional divisor, `nu-bar_I^K(f)` equals the
  minimum over `alpha` of `v_alpha(f)/v_alpha(I)`, where `v_alpha` is the order of vanishing along `D_alpha`. Corollary 4.1.7 (pp. 32-33): a universal denominator `q` exists, so `nu-bar_I(f)` lies in
  `(1/q) N` or is infinite, in particular rational.
- [P] Proposition 4.3.3 (p. 38): for an analytic algebra `O`, an ideal `I`, a non-invertible `f` and `d > 0`, `nu-bar_I(f) >= d` if and only if there is an integral dependence relation
  `f^k + a_1 f^(k-1) + ... + a_k = 0` with `nu_I(a_i) >= i d`; the appendix to Section 4 (pp. 41-45) proves it in general through anisotropic normal cones of an installation, with
  Corollary A.3.3 (p. 44) `nu-bar_I(f) = infinity` if and only if a power of `f` vanishes, and Corollary A.4.1 (p. 45) rationality of `nu-bar_I(f)`. Proposition 4.4.1 and Corollary 4.4.2 (p. 41): the
  graded algebra `gr-bar_I O_X` is locally of finite presentation over `O_X/I`.
- [P] Theorem 5.2 (p. 46), the arc formula: for `I` a coherent ideal on a complex analytic space `X`, `x` in `X` and `f` a section of `O_X`, `nu-bar_I^x(f) = inf over h in A_(X,x) of v(f o h)/v(I o h)`, where
  `A_(X,x)` is the set of nonconstant analytic arcs `h : (D, 0) -> (X, x)` (Definition 5.1, p. 46) and `v` is the order in `t`. Section 5.3 (p. 47) describes arcs transverse to a divisor component `D_alpha`
  that realize the minimum `m_alpha/e_alpha`; Proposition 5.4 (p. 48): the points `x` at which some arc realizes the infimum contain an everywhere dense analytic open subset of the support of `O_X/I`;
  Proposition 5.5 (p. 48): for a proper surjective `p : X_1 -> X`, `nu-bar_I^x(f)` is the minimum of `nu-bar` over the points of the fiber.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The A_5 program needs a source for the description of integral dependence by arcs and by the normalized blow-up. Theorem 2.1 (iii) and (iv) (p. 19) and Theorem 5.2 (p. 46)
  give them in the complex analytic setting: containment in the integral closure is tested on arcs, and `nu-bar` is an infimum over arcs of an order ratio. The arc rank and jet computations
  of the program ([arc rank check](../../evidence/a5-exceptional-fibers/arc-rank-check.json)) consider arcs through a point of a smaller orbit; the source relates such arc data to the
  integral closure of the kernel-coordinate ideal, with the hypotheses that the space be reduced and complex analytic.
- [I] The computations of the program run over `Q(i)` and finite fields, not over `C`; Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 5.2 are statements for complex analytic germs, so a transfer of an arc
  certificate to the algebraic setting needs a separate argument that this paper does not supply.
- [I] Proposition 3.4 (p. 29) ties normality of the blow-up of `Y` to the stabilization `(I^n)-bar = I^n`; for a fiber cone `F(I)` this is the condition that integral closure and powers agree
  in high degree, which the repository has not established for any of its ideals.
