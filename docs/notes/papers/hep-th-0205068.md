## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a natural generalisation of the class of hyperbolic Kac-Moody algebras; a detailed description of the conditions under which these algebras are Lorentzian; the construction of their fundamental weights; an analysis of whether they possess a real principal `so(1,2)` subalgebra. The class includes the Lorentzian Kac-Moody algebras recently proposed as symmetries of M-theory and the closed bosonic string.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper concerns Kac-Moody root systems of Lorentzian signature, with no octonionic, Cayley-Dickson or zero-divisor content on the page read.
