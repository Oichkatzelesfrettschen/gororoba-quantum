## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are PDF page indices. Eqs. 1 and 2 were read from the rendered
page 2.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a robust radiofrequency composite pulse sequence in the `^2F_(7/2)` manifold of an
  Yb+ ion extends the coherence time from 200 microseconds to more than 1 second, matches the
  stability of the previous best Lorentz-symmetry test nearly an order of magnitude faster and improves
  the constraints on the symmetry-breaking coefficients to the 10^-21 level.
- [P] Section 2.1 (p. 2): the combined electron-photon symmetry-breaking tensor is
  `c'_(mu nu) = c_(mu nu) + k_(mu nu)/2`, taken traceless and symmetric; a unique definition `c_(MN)`
  exists in the Sun-centered celestial equatorial frame (SCCEF).
- [P] Eq. 1 (p. 2): the Lorentz-violating energy shift of a bound electron is
  `delta H = -(1 / 6 m_e) C_0^(2) T_0^(2)`, with `T_0^(2) = p^2 - 3 p_z^2` and `C_0^(2)` built from the
  `c_(MN)` tensor. Eq. 2 gives `<J, m_j | T_0^(2) | J, m_j> = [-J(J+1) + 3 m_j^2] / sqrt((2J+3)(J+1)(2J+1)J(2J-1)) x <J||T^(2)||J>`.
  The shift modulates with Earth's rotation, and the reduced matrix element is particularly large for
  the `^2F_(7/2)` manifold of Yb+.
- [P] Section 2.2 (p. 4): the sidereal-day frequency is `omega_Earth / 2 pi = 1/(23.934 h)`; a Lorentz
  violation appears as oscillation of the retrieved fraction `P_f` at that frequency and its second
  harmonic, 11.967 h.
- [P] Table 1 (p. 6), correlated components of `c_(MN)` fitted in this work, with 1 sigma
  uncertainties: `c_(X-Y) = (-5.2 +- 7.8) x 10^-21`, `c_(XY) = (4.4 +- 3.9) x 10^-21`,
  `c_(XZ) = (-5.0 +- 9.3) x 10^-21`, `c_(YZ) = (6.3 +- 8.9) x 10^-21`. The same table gives the 171Yb+
  limits (`c_(XY) = (-7.0 +- 8.1) x 10^-21`) and the 40Ca+ limits (`c_(XY) = (2.4 +- 4.8) x 10^-19`).
- [P] Table 2 (p. 6): the uncorrelated combinations include `(0.0 +- 1.0) x 10^-20`,
  `(-13.8 +- 9.6) x 10^-21`, `(3.0 +- 7.2) x 10^-21` and `(-0.4 +- 3.5) x 10^-21`.
- [P] Sections 2.4 and 2.5 (pp. 5-6): only `c_(XY)` deviates from zero, by 1.1 sigma, and the
  Lomb-Scargle periodogram shows no significant component at twice the sidereal frequency, so the paper
  finds no evidence of Lorentz violation. The tightest constraint, `3.9 x 10^-21`, is on `c_(XY)`,
  because the geometry favors signals at `2 omega_Earth`. The data average down as
  `sigma_kappa = 372(9) mrad/s tau^-1/2`, 29(3) % above quantum projection noise.
- [P] Supplementary transformation (pp. 8-9, Eqs. 7-8): the rotation from the SCCEF to the laboratory
  uses `omega_Earth` and the colatitude; the orbital boost is `beta = 1 x 10^-4` and the boost from
  Earth's rotation, `beta_L = 1.5 x 10^-6`, is neglected as two orders of magnitude smaller.
  Table S1 lists the contributing harmonics `omega_Earth`, `2 omega_Earth` and their sidebands at the
  orbital frequency `Omega_Sun`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Size: the bound is on the dimensionless coefficients, `|c_(MN)|` of 10^-20 to 10^-21, so the
  fractional shift of an electronic transition is of that order times a state-dependent matrix-element
  factor; the paper gives that factor only for the `^2F_(7/2)` manifold of Yb+. The repository's
  instabilities are order-one fractions of readout probability (Tuna-17 q9 idle `P(1|0)` of 0.026 to
  0.27, [tuna-17.md](../quantum_inspire/tuna-17.md)) and the calibration moves of IBM Heron r2
  ([ibm_heron_platform.md](../ibm_heron_platform.md)), so a coupling at this bound sits roughly 19
  orders of magnitude below any retained record.
- [I] Signature: a periodogram of the retained shot records must show power at 1/(23.934 h) and
  1/(11.967 h) with the phase set by the lab's colatitude and the quantization axis, plus orbital
  sidebands at one cycle per year; a peak at 24 h or at the hourly IBM calibration cadence
  ([ibm_heron_platform.md](../ibm_heron_platform.md)) indicates a terrestrial source.
