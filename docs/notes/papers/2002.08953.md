## What the theorem supplies

[P] The procedure repeatedly prepares a fixed unknown state, randomly chooses
a measurement basis, and records both the basis and its bit-string outcome.
A known invertible measurement channel defines an unbiased classical
snapshot. Independent snapshots support median-of-means estimates of selected
linear observables; an individual snapshot need not be a physical state.

[P] Supplementary Theorem 1, equations (S13)-(S14), uses `K` groups of `N`
independent snapshots, with `K=2*log(2*M/delta)` and
`N=(34/epsilon^2)*max_i ||O_i-tr(O_i)*I/2^n||_shadow^2`, with appropriate
integer ceilings in an implementation. The total is `N*K` snapshots, and
all `M` declared expectations have additive error at most `epsilon` with
probability at least `1-delta`. The norm depends on the measurement ensemble;
the logarithm in `M` alone is not the resource bound.

[P] Equations (S16)-(S17), p. 13, compare global Clifford and independent
single-qubit Pauli measurements. Global Clifford sampling has a bound in the
Hilbert-Schmidt norm but requires entangling measurement circuits. Local
Pauli sampling has a bound `4^k*||O||_infinity^2` for an observable supported
on `k` qubits, and Proposition 2 explains independence from spectator qubit
count. Page 4's non-example shows that a global Pauli string can require
exponentially many generic-shadow samples while direct measurement of that
one string needs order `1/epsilon^2` copies.

## Repository comparison and admission gates

[I] Shadows are relevant when the proposed output is a large declared set of
local observables, correlations or candidate witnesses. They do not turn one
Walsh sample into a complete distribution, a reconstructed phase function,
or a classification theorem. They also do not characterize a continuing
measurement instrument merely by estimating its terminal output state.
For that question retain the input and outcome-resolved design in
[QILGST](2103.03008.md) and the [GST identifiability conditions](2009.07301.md).

[I] Compare direct measurements, a deterministic local-Pauli design and
shadows on the same observable list, accuracy and failure probability.
Include basis synthesis, layout, measurement calibration, shot count,
classical storage/decoding and [per-job overhead](2201.13438.md). A small
endpoint family may favor direct measurements; reusing a large shadow for
many observables can favor randomized acquisition. A reused snapshot does
not constitute an independent sample for each queried observable.

The admission artifact should name the fixed state-preparation procedure,
measurement ensemble and inverse channel, basis seed and shot mapping,
observable family, shadow-norm bounds, grouping rule and integer sample
counts. Validate unbiasedness and joint failure rates in independent local
fixtures, then inject SPAM, drift and correlated samples to show which
assumptions fail. The theorem's ideal known-measurement model supplies no
automatic correction for real basis errors or readout error. Calibrating or
modifying that inverse needs a separate justified noise model and uncertainty
budget. A data-selected observable family also needs selection-aware error
control beyond directly applying the fixed-family theorem.
