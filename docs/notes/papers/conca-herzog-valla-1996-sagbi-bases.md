## What the introduction states

- [P] Aim and method (p. 113): the paper studies blow-up algebras of certain rational normal scrolls by Sagbi basis theory,
  the subalgebra analog of Groebner bases (introduced by Robbiano and Sweedler and independently by Kapur and Madlener):
  for a `K`-subalgebra `A` of a polynomial ring and a term order, the initial algebra `in(A)` is generated by the initial
  monomials of the elements of `A`, and properties of `A` are inherited from `in(A)` when it is finitely generated.
- [P] Section 1 (p. 113): Theorem 1.2 shows that `in(A)`, if finitely generated, is the associated graded ring of a suitable
  filtration of `A`, hence the special fiber of a flat one-parameter family whose general fiber is `A`. If `in(A)` is
  normal, Cohen-Macaulay, has rational singularities or is F-rational, so is `A` (stated as 2.3 in the text).
- [P] Section 3 (p. 114): the Rees algebra and the special fibre of the ideal `I(c, d)` generated by the 2-minors of the
  `2 x c` matrix with first row `x_1, ..., x_c` and second row `x_(1+d), ..., x_(c+d)`, which defines certain rational
  normal scrolls. Theorem 3.3 is stated to show that the natural generators of the Rees algebra and the special fibre form
  a Sagbi basis, and Theorem 3.8 that these algebras are Cohen-Macaulay normal domains with rational singularities.
  Example 3.9 shows that the canonical generators of the ideal of a general scroll are not a Sagbi basis.
- [P] Section 4 (p. 114): the initial ideal of the defining ideal of the special fibre is generated by square-free monomials
  with a shellable simplicial complex (stated as 4.6), which gives the `h`-vector (Theorem 4.7), the multiplicity (4.5),
  the `a`-invariant (4.8) and the Gorenstein cases (4.10).
- [P] Section 5 (p. 114): in characteristic zero, the analytic spread of the defining ideal of every rational normal scroll;
  the paper answers a question of an earlier reference on rational normal curves in `P^n` (analytic spread `n + 1` for `n >= 4`).
