## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a new construction of the Lie algebra of type `E_8` in terms of `3 x 3` matrices such that the Lie bracket has a natural description as the matrix commutator, which gives a new interpretation of the Freudenthal-Tits magic square of Lie algebras acting on themselves by commutation.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the Chevalley construction of `E_8` produces the split real form as `248 x 248` real matrices; the other real forms can be written as `248 x 248` complex matrices, but writing them as real matrices of that size is more of a challenge. By Vinberg's interpretation of the magic square the compact real form is the algebra of traceless anti-Hermitian `3 x 3` matrices over `K tensor L`, with `K = L = O` the real octonions, plus the derivations of `K` and `L`; with `L = O'` the split octonions the semi-split real form arises, and with `K = L = O'` the split real form. The paper combines Vinberg's matrix picture with the Barton-Sudbery triality construction.
