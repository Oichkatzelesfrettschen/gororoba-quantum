## Main claims

- [P] The paper studies twisted group algebras over (Z_2)^n with cubic twisting
  functions. The octonions carry f(x, y) = x1 x2 y3 + x1 y2 x3 + y1 x2 x3 +
  sum_(1 <= i <= j <= 3) x_i y_j, of degree 3, and the series O_n takes the same form
  for every n (Eq. 1.3); M_n keeps only the cubic part (Eq. 1.4).
- [P] A generating function alpha : (Z_2)^n -> Z_2 encodes an algebra when one exists;
  Proposition 4.1 states that alpha is the generating function of some twisted group
  algebra exactly when alpha is a polynomial of degree at most 3. The introduction and
  Section 3 use the existence of a generating function to separate O_n, M_n, the Clifford
  algebras and the octonions from the Cayley-Dickson algebras, and Section 4 notes that
  the Cayley-Dickson algebras do not have a symmetric associator phi = delta f, citing
  Albuquerque and Majid.
- [P] No degree is stated for the Cayley-Dickson twisting function beyond the octonions.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The octonion twisting function above is term for term the Corollary 4.3 cochain of
  Albuquerque and Majid at n = 3 that `am_octonion` in
  [CdTowerBasisSign.v](../../../proofs/CdTowerBasisSign.v) proves, with paper index i on
  index bit i - 1: the cubic terms y1 x2 x3, x1 y2 x3 and x1 x2 y3 and the bilinear part
  sum_(i <= j) x_i y_j. So F = (-1)^f is F_AM(x, y) = cd_sign(8, y, x), and the paper is a
  second printed source for the cubic term x1 x2 y3 that corrects the x2 x2 y3 of
  Albuquerque-Majid Proposition 4.4(iii) ([math-9802116.md](../math-9802116.md)). The
  bilinear part is the one
  [cd_phase_degree.md](../cd_phase_degree.md#leading-coefficients-right-rows-and-the-bilinear-layer)
  proves for every Cayley-Dickson level; from n = 4 on, O_n keeps the cubic form and leaves
  the Cayley-Dickson series.
- [I] The paper places the Cayley-Dickson algebras outside its cubic class and states no
  degree for them. The joint degree n of the Cayley-Dickson cochain on 2n variables
  (measured for n = 2 to 5, [math-9802116.md](../math-9802116.md)) and the fixed-argument
  degrees of [cd_phase_degree.md](../cd_phase_degree.md) quantify how far above degree 3
  the series runs from the sedenions on.
