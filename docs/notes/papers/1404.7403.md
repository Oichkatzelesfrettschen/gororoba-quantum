## Statements

- [P] Problem (abstract, p. 1): with `m` independent estimators, the Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) procedure classifies the
  signs of the parameters with the directional false discovery rate controlled at `q`; the paper constructs
  sign-determining confidence intervals instead, with the false coverage-statement rate (FCR), the expected proportion
  of non-covering intervals among those constructed, controlled.
- [P] Definition 1, p. 8 (the Benjamini-Yekutieli FCR-adjusted selective-CI procedure): apply a selection criterion
  `S` to obtain `S(Y)`; for each selected `i`, let `R_min(Y^(i)) = min_y { |S(Y^(i), Y_i = y)| : i in S(Y^(i), Y_i = y) }`
  (Eq. 4), where `Y^(i)` omits `Y_i`; construct the marginal interval at level `R_min q / m`. For BH-type step-up
  selection `R_min = R`, so the adjustment multiplies `q` by the number selected and divides by `m`.
- [P] Definition 2, p. 9 (the BY-adjusted selective sign-determining CI procedure): order the estimates by absolute
  value; let `R` be the largest `r` such that the level-`r q / m` interval of the `r`-th largest lies entirely in
  `(-inf, 0]` or in `(0, inf)`; select every `i` with `|Y_i| >= |Y_(R)|` and construct each selected interval at level
  `R q / m`.
- [P] Theorem 1, p. 10: for independent `Y_i ~ f(y_i - theta_i)` and a marginal interval family satisfying the
  monotonicity requirements (MON 1) and (MON 2), the procedure of Definition 2 has FCR at most `q`.
- [P] Theorem 2, p. 18: for independent normal estimators with known variance, Definition 2 with the modified
  quasi-conventional (MQC) interval, `0 < psi < 0.9`, has FCR at most `q`.
- [P] Definition 3, p. 19, and Theorem 3, p. 20: when the selection uses an independent second component `Y_i2` and the
  intervals use `Y_i1`, the level-`(q1, q2)` adjusted sets have FCR `q1 + (1 - q1) FCR(CI_2; S_2; q2)`: selection on
  independent data costs the coverage only through the second stage.
