## Statements

- [P] Theorem 1.1 (p. 7): a QLS algorithm whose runtime depends on the sparsity of the augmented matrix H = [A, -b], the precision, ||A^+ b|| and the parameter ET = sum_i p_i^2 d_i with p = (A A^T)^+ b and d_i the squared row norms of H.
- [P] Theorem 1.2 (p. 7): for a quadratic system over C with a unique Boolean solution of Hamming weight `h`, Algorithm 2 recovers it with probability 1 - delta in a stated time; Theorem 1.3 (p. 7) gives polynomial time for a planted maximum-independent-set family under a condition on the independent-set counts.
