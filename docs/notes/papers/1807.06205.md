## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; the arXiv v2 pagination prints page 5 on PDF page 6, so page labels give PDF
page indices. Only the solar-system dipole relative to the cosmic microwave background (CMB) is
transcribed.

- [P] Section 2.1 (PDF p. 6): the "Solar dipole" is the dipole induced by the motion of the Solar System
  barycenter with respect to the CMB rest frame. The paper separates it from the "orbital dipole", the
  modulation induced by the satellite's orbit around the barycenter.
- [P] Table 2 (PDF p. 6), Planck 2018 (HFI): amplitude `3362.08 +- 0.99` microkelvin (CMB
  thermodynamic units), Galactic longitude `l = 264.021 +- 0.011` degrees, latitude
  `b = 48.253 +- 0.005` degrees. Footnote e gives the same direction as `RA = 167.942 +- 0.007` degrees,
  `Dec = -6.944 +- 0.007` degrees (J2000). The uncertainties are the linear sum of the statistical and
  systematic terms and exclude the 0.02 % uncertainty of the CMB monopole temperature. The text
  recommends the HFI determination as the most accurate available.
- [P] Section 2.1 text (PDF p. 6): the amplitude corresponds to `beta = v / c = (1.23357 +- 0.00036) x 10^-3`
  or `v = (369.82 +- 0.11)` km/s, with the systematic uncertainties added linearly. The amplitude is
  known to about 0.025 %.
- [P] Table 3 (PDF p. 7), Sun to CMB: `369.82 +- 0.11` km/s toward `l = 264.021 +- 0.011` degrees,
  `b = 48.253 +- 0.005` degrees. The same table lists the Local Group relative to the CMB as
  `620 +- 15` km/s toward `l = 271.9 +- 2.0` degrees, `b = 29.6 +- 1.4` degrees.
- [P] Section 2.1 (PDF p. 6): the dipole direction lies in the constellation Crater, near the border
  with Leo.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The dipole sets the laboratory's velocity relative to the CMB rest frame, the reference
  direction of ether-drift and Lorentz-violation searches: `beta = 1.234 x 10^-3`, a second-order
  time-dilation factor `beta^2 / 2 = 7.6 x 10^-7`, and a speed modulation of `beta_L = 1.5 x 10^-6`
  from Earth's rotation (the value [2206.00570](2206.00570.md) uses). A pure boost shifts no Lorentz-invariant
  observable, so the dipole matters only through a symmetry-breaking coefficient, which
  [2206.00570](2206.00570.md) bounds at the 10^-20 to 10^-21 level.
- [I] Signature a real coupling to the dipole needs: a modulation at the sidereal period 23.934 h and
  its second harmonic 11.967 h, with phase fixed to the dipole's right ascension 167.94 degrees. A
  24 h solar period, a fixed-clock-time drift or a calibration-cycle period identifies a thermal or
  control origin.
