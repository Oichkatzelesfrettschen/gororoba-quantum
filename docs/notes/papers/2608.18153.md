## Statements read

- [P] Abstract and Fig. 1 (pp. 1, 4): two logical qubits on `qpu:belenos`, each a two-dimensional subspace of the seven-dimensional zero-sum sector of an eight-mode single-photon register; the registers sit on circuit modes 0 to 7 and 8 to 15 (16 of 24 modes), one photon is injected into circuit modes 0 and 8, and a postselected linear-optical controlled-Z of the Ralph-Langford-Bell-White type couples them with ideal success probability 1/9.
- [P] Section II (pp. 2-3): the coupling uses beam splitters of transmissivity T = 1/3 between modes 2 and 10 and between modes 3 and 11, plus matching T = 1/3 couplings of rail 0 to local dump modes; the two-photon interference supplies the sign flip T - R = -1/3 on the one-photon-per-block branch, and the 8/9 failure branch is discarded.
- [P] Section II (p. 3): the Perceval circuit is built as U_target = (U_read^A (+) U_read^B) U_CZ (U_prep^A (+) U_prep^B), verified unitary to a residual below 1e-10, and the provider's compiled physical mapping is not returned.
- [P] Section III and Tables I and II (p. 5): `min_detected_photons` 2 with 5,000 returned samples per job; acceptance is exactly one click in the two logical ports of each block; Perceval 1.2.4 with perceval-quandela; vendor-dashboard HOM 92.0 % and 91.5 %, g2 0.011, clock 4.94 MHz, transmittance about 5 %; detectors treated with threshold semantics.
- [P] Abstract: eight same-day passes each gave a raw score above 2, session means 2.40 and 2.58; the authors state the data support an operational reference acquisition and no entanglement-witness claim.
- [I] The two-register layout is a published instance of the tensor-product Fock encoding (one photon per register, postselected exchange coupling) analyzed in the Ascella folding analysis (session report, not retained); the 1/9 success factor is the ideal success of this one coupling, not a bound on every sign-entangling coupling.
