## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the Leech lattice is the unique densest lattice in `R^24`; the proof combines human reasoning with computer verification of properties of certain explicit polynomials; no sphere packing in `R^24` can exceed the Leech lattice's density by a factor of more than `1 + 1.65 x 10^-30`; and the paper gives a new proof that `E_8` is the unique densest lattice in `R^8`.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the density of the packing from a lattice `Lambda` with minimal vector length `r` and covolume `|Lambda|` is `(pi^(n/2) / (n/2)!) (r/2)^n / |Lambda|`; the greatest density known in `R^25` is achieved by at least 23 distinct lattices; the densest known packing in `R^10`, the Best packing `P_10c`, is not a lattice packing.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is the citation for the optimality of `E_8` in dimension 8 and of the Leech lattice in dimension 24 among lattices. The repository's Cayley-Dickson work reaches dimensions 8, 16, 32 and above as algebras, not as lattices, and no statement on the page read connects the sedenion or pathion zero-divisor strata to the Leech lattice; the integral octonion order whose lattice is `E_8` appears in the [Corradetti note](2605.09333.md).
