## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper presents a unified sedenion algebra model that, "without the need for an ad hoc Standard Model's hypothesis", explains why there are three generations of charged and neutral leptons, why neutrinos have a vanishingly small mass, and why flavor mixing and mass oscillations occur. It states that the sedenion algebra contains three types of non-associative octonion algebra, each corresponding to a generation of leptons, and that a generalized higher dimensional Dirac equation with extra degrees of freedom accounts for the internal structural dynamics. Keywords: Standard Model, neutrino flavor mixing, mass oscillations, sedenions, octonions, Clifford geometric algebra.
- [P] The title page prints the real title: "A unified sedenion model for the origins of three generations of charged and neutral leptons, flavor mixing, mass oscillations and small masses of neutrinos" (JHEP template, two authors).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The abstract's algebraic claim, that the sedenions contain three types of non-associative octonion algebra, is checkable against `cd_core` by enumerating the 8-dimensional subalgebras of the sedenions; the [Chan and Dokovic note](chan-dokovic-2006-sedenion-subalgebras.md) records the literature on sedenion subalgebras. That enumeration was not run for this note, and the physical identification of the three types with lepton generations has no algebraic content to test.
