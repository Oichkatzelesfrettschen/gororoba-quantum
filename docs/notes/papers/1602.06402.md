# Hinuma, Pizzi, Kumagai, Oba and Tanaka -- Band structure diagram paths based on crystallography (arXiv 1602.06402)

[P] marks a statement of the source, cited by section, table or page of the
arXiv v4 PDF. [I] marks a repository application or deduction. The paper
defines the HPKOT convention that the seekpath package implements
([seekpath manual](../quantum-tools/seekpath.md)); the repository uses the
package only as an independent three-dimensional symmetry classifier
([lattice cross-check](../../evidence/seekpath-lattice-crosscheck/README.md)),
and computes no band structure.

## Relation to Setyawan-Curtarolo

[P] Section 1 credits Setyawan and Curtarolo (SC; Comp. Mater. Sci. 49, 299
(2010)) with a collectively exhaustive list of basis vectors of a standard
primitive cell, labels and suggested paths, used by aflowlib and
the Materials Project, and names three shortcomings that oblige a new
scheme: the SC standard cell differs from the crystallographic conventional
cell "in quite a few situations"; SC labels differ in many cases from those
of Cracknell, Davies, Miller and Love (CDML) and the Bilbao
Crystallographic Server; and SC suggest one path per Bravais lattice and
axial ratio, while a path that reflects the crystal's symmetry depends on
the reciprocal space group G*, one of 73 symmorphic types.

[I] HPKOT is a successor convention that departs from SC on cells, labels
and paths. SeeK-path implements HPKOT, so it does not automate the SC/AFLOW
standard; the two conventions disagree on labels for many lattices. The
AFLOW crystallographic prototype encyclopedia page that the repository cites
([aflow-a_cf4-225-a.md](aflow-a_cf4-225-a.md)) is a third, separate AFLOW
product: it names structure prototypes and supplies no k-path.

## Conventions

[P] Section 3.1: direct-space basis vectors are columns, reciprocal ones
rows; a k-vector is the row of coefficients times the reciprocal basis. The
crystallographic conventional cell follows the first setting of ITB Table
A1.4.2.7; cubic, tetragonal and hexagonal conventional cells coincide with
SC's (3.1.1), while rhombohedral ones differ: hexagonal axes here, SC's
primitive rhombohedral cell there (3.1.2). The crystallographic primitive
cell is the conventional cell times the matrix P of Table 3, derived from
Table 2 of Aroyo et al.; for oA and mC the P of Table 3 differs from
spglib's (section 5).

[P] Section 4.3 rules for the recommended path, stated for G*_high, the
highest-symmetry reciprocal space group of the Bravais lattice:

1. representatives of all special k-vector points and lines are on the path;
2. a zone-face-center special point not joined to Gamma by a special line
   gets a segment to Gamma;
3. a zone-edge-center special point that ends only one special line gets,
   if possible, a segment to a zone vertex or face-center special point;
4. all special k-vector points and lines of the crystal's own G* are on the
   path.

[P] Labels follow CDML and the Bilbao server where defined; added labels
carry even indices, with X1 = (1/2, 0, 0) of cP kept from the Bilbao KVEC
figure (section 1).

parameters (Table 94). The cases this repository's checks reach:

## Without time reversal (section 4.4)

[P] When neither time reversal nor inversion holds, the path adds the
irreducible wedge inverted through Gamma, with primed labels (for mP:
Gamma-Z'-D'-B'-Gamma-A'-E'-Z'-C2'-Y2'-Gamma). Table 95 lists the point
groups with inversion by space-group range.

[I] Table 95 of v4 prints 4/m at 81 <= N <= 82 (page 135, read from the page image); the 4/m groups are 83-88,
and 81-82 are P-4 and I-4. seekpath reads inversion from spglib's
point-group index (`pointgroup_has_inversion`), so the misprint does not
reach the code.

## Implementation (section 5)

[P] `seekpath.get_path(structure, with_time_reversal, recipe, threshold)`
takes spglib's `(cell, positions, numbers)` tuple; spglib supplies the space
group and the standardized conventional cell, whose definition matches the
paper's; a lookup table over the 230 space groups gives the Bravais symbol
and inversion; a lattice-parameter comparison within `threshold` raises
`seekpath.EdgeCaseWarning` and still picks a case; aP uses spglib's Niggli
reduction; coefficients come from `band_path_data/<symbol>/` files, with
fewer than 40 distinct coefficient expressions mapped to Python functions
instead of parsed; the primitive cell is the paper's P applied to the
conventional cell, with overlapping images merged and their count checked
against det(P^-1). `get_explicit_k_path` adds `reference_distance` in 1/A.
The online visualizer computes the zone from SciPy's Voronoi and Delaunay
routines.

[I] Differences in the 2.2.2 package: `symprec`, `angle_tolerance` and the
`*_orig_cell` functions exist, and the `aiidawrappers` helpers of section 5
are gone ([manual](../quantum-tools/seekpath.md#api-entry-points)).
