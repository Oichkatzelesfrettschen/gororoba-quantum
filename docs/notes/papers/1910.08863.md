## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): fifteen deterministically fabricated quantum-dot micropillar sources show average indistinguishability 90.6 +- 2.8 %, single-photon purity 95.4 +- 1.5 % and average first-lens brightness 13.6 +- 4.4 %; the authors include affiliations with Quandela SAS.
- [I] The scatter of indistinguishability across sources bounds how far a platform HOM figure (88.44 % on the live Ascella record) can be read as a property of every source in a multi-photon job; the paper is a source-fabrication study and states nothing about the Ascella processor.
