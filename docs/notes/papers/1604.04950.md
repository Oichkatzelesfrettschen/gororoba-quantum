## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Under the standard axioms of quaternionic quantum theory and a spatially localized scattering interaction, the S-matrix is complex, so the index of refraction is complex.
- [P] The optical experiment of [Procopio et al.](1602.01624.md) therefore does not test hyper-complex effects within the standard Hilbert-space framework; such a test needs near-zone fields.
