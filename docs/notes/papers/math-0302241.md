## Setting and conventions

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): for a Noetherian local ring `(R, m)` and an ideal `I` with Rees algebra `R`, the paper seeks conditions for the special fiber ring `F = R/mR` to be Cohen-Macaulay, with the key idea of requiring "good" intersection properties and "few" homogeneous generating relations in low degrees; for a strongly Cohen-Macaulay ideal with `G_l` and the expected reduction number, `F` is Cohen-Macaulay; the paper also characterizes Cohen-Macaulayness of `R/KR` for an `m`-primary ideal `K`, recovering a criterion of Valabrega and Valla when `K = I`.
- [P] Section 2 (p. 5): `I` is minimally generated by `f_1, ..., f_n`, the Rees algebra is `R = R[T_1, ..., T_n]/A`, and for an ideal `K`, `R/KR = (R/K)[T_1, ..., T_n]/Q`; "homogeneous generating relations" of `R/KR` are elements of a homogeneous minimal generating set of `Q`.

## Theorem statements

- [P] Theorem 2.2 (p. 5): let `(R, m)` be a Noetherian local ring with infinite residue field, `I` an ideal with analytic spread `l`, `n` minimal generators and reduction number `r`, `t >= r` a positive integer, `a_1, ..., a_l` general elements of `I` with `a_i = (a_1, ..., a_i)`, and `K` an `m`-primary ideal. Assume (a) `(a_i I^(t-1) : a_(i+1)) cap I^t = a_i I^(t-1)` for `0 <= i <= l - 1`; and (b) (i) if `K = m` and `n >= l + 2`, then `R/KR = F` has at most two homogeneous generating relations in degrees at most `t`; (ii) if `K = m` and `n = l + 1`, at most one; (iii) if `K != m`, none. Then `R/KR` is Cohen-Macaulay.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The binding constraint of condition (b) is the count of low-degree relations of the fiber cone. The repository fibers carry no linear relations (`h_1 = mu(I)`) and, from the tracked Hilbert functions, `C(mu + 1, 2) - h_2` quadratic relations: 69 (pair X, `mu = 15`), 68 (Iso, 17), 148 (E8-bar to N, 21), 144 (W8, 24) and 21 (E8-bar to VB, 10) ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)). Condition (b)(i) allows at most two relations in degrees at most `t >= r`, so Theorem 2.2 cannot apply to a fiber with reduction number `r >= 2`; for `t = r = 1` condition (b) is empty and only condition (a) and `r(I) <= 1` remain, which is the case of the E8-bar to VB candidate (Hilbert series `(1 + 6t)/(1 - t)^4`, see the D'Cruz-Raghavan-Verma note).
- [I] The theorem requires an infinite residue field; base change `R -> R[X]` localized at `m R[X]` restores it without changing `F(I)` up to field extension.
- [I] The theorem concludes Cohen-Macaulayness from relations in low degrees and gives no degree beyond which `F` has no further relations, so it supplies no stopping bound for the fiber-cone computations.
