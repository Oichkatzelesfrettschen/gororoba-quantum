## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): improves the dependence on precision of HHL from poly(1/epsilon) to poly(log(1/epsilon)) for a sparse, well-conditioned A, keeping the other dependences essentially the same, by implementing the inverse through a Fourier or Chebyshev series representation and bypassing phase estimation.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the quantum linear systems problem is to prepare a state proportional to the solution of A x = b given procedures for the entries of A and for |b|; it differs from the classical problem of solving the system.
