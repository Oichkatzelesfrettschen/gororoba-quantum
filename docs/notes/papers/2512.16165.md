## Setting and conventions

- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the generic Hankel matrix `H_(l,m)` has entries `x_(i+j-1)` over an infinite field `K` of characteristic 0; the Hankel determinantal ideal `I_t(H)` is generated by its `t`-minors in the standard graded polynomial ring. The paper studies coordinate sections of these ideals obtained by setting the last `r` entries to zero, in particular sub-maximal minors (p. 1).
- [P] Introduction (p. 2): the two extremal degenerations are `H[n-1]`, the most degenerate case (the largest number of variables set to zero without making the sub-maximal minors vanish), and `H[1]`, the least degenerate case with one zero in the bottom-right corner.

## Theorem statements

- [P] Main Theorem (1) (p. 2): for `I = I_n(H[n-1])`, the ideal of sub-maximal minors, the special fiber ring `F(I)` is Cohen-Macaulay and its defining ideal is generated by the Plucker relations and the Laplace relations (their equation (9)); these relations are all quadratic and form a Groebner basis of the defining ideal, so the special fiber is Koszul. For the coordinate sections `J[n-1]` of the gradient ideal of `det H`, the associated rational map is birational onto its image, which gives the multiplicity of the special fiber of `J[n-1]`.
- [P] Main Theorem (2) (p. 2): for `I = I_n(H[1])`, the special fiber `F(I)` is a Gorenstein ring whose defining ideal is minimally generated by the Plucker relations and exactly one extra relation of degree `n`, arising from the vanishing of the determinant of a square matrix of size `n^2` (their equation (16)); the Rees algebra `R(I)` is of fiber type, and `I` has linear powers although `R(I)` is not Koszul.
- [P] Introduction (p. 2): in the general case the special fiber is not minimally generated by quadrics and cubics, as conjectured by earlier work (Cunha, Mostafazadehfard, Ramos and Simis); the degrees of the minimal generators depend on the number of zeros and the matrix size. All ideals considered have codimension 3 and are not Gorenstein. In proving Main Theorem (1), the special fiber of `I_n(H[n-1])` is shown isomorphic to that of the ideal of 2-minors of the `2 x (n + 2)` matrix with rows `x_1, ..., x_(n+2)` and `x_2, ..., x_(n+2), 0`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper's hypotheses (a generic Hankel matrix over a field of characteristic 0, homogeneous sub-maximal minors of expected codimension 3 with an Eagon-Northcott-type resolution) do not hold for the repository's kernel-coordinate ideals, which live on a localized orbit-closure ring, are inhomogeneous and are computed over `F_32009` ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)). No theorem of the pages opened applies to a repository fiber.
- [I] The method is reusable: the paper identifies a special fiber with that of an easier ideal (2-minors) and then borrows a quadratic Groebner basis and Koszulness. The repository would need an explicit matrix presentation of a kernel-coordinate ideal for the same move.
