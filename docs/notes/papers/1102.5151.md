## Statements and hypotheses

- [P] Doubling (p. 1, Eq. 1): `(a, b)(c, d) = (ac - d* b, d a + b c*)`, `(a, b)* = (a*, -b)`; the results hold over any field of
  characteristic other than 2. The Cayley-Dickson loop `Q_n` (p. 2, Eq. 4) is the multiplicative closure of the basic elements
  `+-(x_1, ..., x_n)` with `x_j in {0, 1}` of the algebra after `n` doublings, of order `2^(n+1)`: `Q_1 = C_4`, `Q_2 = H_8`, `Q_3 = O_16`
  (Moufang), `Q_4 = S_32` (not Moufang), `Q_5 = T_64`. Eqs. 5 to 8 give the rules `(x,0)(y,0) = (xy,0)`, `(x,0)(y,1) = (yx,1)`,
  `(x,1)(y,0) = (xy*,1)`, `(x,1)(y,1) = (-y*x,0)`.
- [P] Theorem 5 (p. 3, after Culbert): any two elements of a Cayley-Dickson loop generate a subgroup of the quaternion group;
  Corollary 7: every Cayley-Dickson loop is diassociative; Corollary 17: it has the inverse property. Lemma 14 (p. 3): for a
  subloop `S`, `Z(S) = {1, -1}` when `|S| > 4`, the associator subloop is `Z(S)` when `|S| > 8`, and `S' = Z(S)` when `|S| > 4`.
  Proposition 15 (p. 4): `x* = -x` for `x` not `+-1`, all such `x` have order 4, `|Q_n| = 2^(n+1)`, `Q_k` embeds in `Q_n`.
- [P] Lemma 21 (p. 5): commutators `[x, y]` and associators `[x, y, z]` of elements of `Q_n` are `+-1`; `[x, y] = -1` exactly when
  `<x, y>` is `H_8`; `[x, y, z] = -1` when `<x, y, z>` is `O_16`. Remark 22: `Q_n / {1, -1}` is `(Z_2)^n`. Lemma 23 (p. 5): the loop
  generated by `n` elements has order `2^k` with `k <= n + 1`.
- [P] Theorem 25 (p. 6): `Q_n` is Hamiltonian (every subloop is normal), by Lemmas 21 and 14; Norton's Theorem 26 gives an
  alternative route.
- [P] Section 4 (pp. 6-7): `|Aut(C_4)| = 2`, `|Aut(H_8)| = 24`, `|Aut(O_16)| = 1344 = 14 * 12 * 8`, `|Aut(S_32)| = 2688`,
  `|Aut(T_64)| = 5376`, the last two computed with the GAP package LOOPS. **Theorem 32 (p. 7):** for `n >= 4` an automorphism
  `phi` of `Q_n` fixes `1` and `-1`, sends `e = (1, 1)` to `+-e`, restricts to `psi` in `Aut(Q_(n-1))`, and satisfies
  `phi((x, 1)) = psi(x) phi(e)`. **Theorem 41 (p. 13):** `Aut(Q_n) = Aut(Q_(n-1)) x Z_2` for `n >= 4`, the `Z_2` generated by
  `(x, x_(n+1)) -> ((-1)^(x_(n+1)) x, x_(n+1))`.
- [P] Lemma 33 (p. 8) and Table 1 (p. 9): three elements generating a subloop of order 16 generate `O_16` or the
  quasi-octonion loop `O~_16`, according to the three associators `[x, y, z]`, `[x, z, y]`, `[x, y, xz]` (all `-1` gives `O_16`).
  Lemma 34 (p. 9) lists the associators of `Q_n` in terms of those of `Q_(n-1)`. Lemma 35 (p. 10): `<x, y, e>` is `O_16` whenever `e`
  is not in `<x, y>`, so `e` is special.
- [P] Lemma 36 (p. 11): for `x, y, z` in `Q_(n-1)` with `<x, y, z>` equal to `O_16` (`n >= 4`), `<(x,0),(y,0),(z,0)>` and
  `<(x,1),(y,1),(z,1)>` are `O_16`, and `<(x,0),(y,0),(z,1)>` and `<(x,0),(y,1),(z,1)>` are `O~_16`. Definition 37 and Lemma 38
  (pp. 11-12): index-2 subloops of `Q_n` have three types; Lemma 39 (p. 12): a subloop of the third type is not a
  Cayley-Dickson loop; Lemma 40 (p. 13): every element other than `+-1, +-e` lies in a copy of `O~_16`.

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Product. Eq. 1 is `cd_core`'s doubling `(a,b)(c,d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))`, and `Q_n` is the set `+-e_m` of the
XOR-indexed basis under `cd_sign(2^n, p, q)`; Eqs. 5 to 8 agree with the recursive definition of `cd_sign`.

[I] Check results. A scratch script (ignored `docs/local/`, run with `-I`) computed automorphism counts and the lifts of
Lemma 36 from `cd_core`:

| Paper statement | Result under `cd_core` |
| --- | --- |
| `\|Aut(C_4)\| = 2`, `\|Aut(H_8)\| = 24`, `\|Aut(O_16)\| = 1344`, `\|Aut(S_32)\| = 2688` (p. 7) | exhaustive homomorphism search over generator images: 2, 24, 1344, 2688 |
| Theorem 32(2), `n = 4`: `phi(e) = +-e` | the images of the newest generator `i_4` over all 2,688 automorphisms are `+e8` (1,344 times) and `-e8` (1,344 times) |
| Theorem 32(3), Theorem 41, `n = 4` | the 2,688 automorphisms have 1,344 distinct restrictions to `Q_3`, equal to `Aut(O_16)`, each with two lifts |
| Lemma 36, `<(x,0),(y,0),(z,1)>` and `<(x,0),(y,1),(z,1)>` are `O~_16` | both lifts are non-Moufang: 1 of 1 triple at `D = 16`, 8 of 8 octonion subloops at `D = 32` |
| Lemma 36, `<(x,1),(y,1),(z,1)>` is `O_16` | **mismatch:** the lift is non-Moufang in 1 of 1 case at `D = 16` and in 8 of 8 cases at `D = 32`, so it is `O~_16`, not `O_16` |

[I] The Lemma 36 mismatch matches Remark 15.3 of [Chapman, Levin, Vishne and Zaninelli](2501.00123.md), which states that the
associators of the lift `{xj, yj, zj}` contain a `+1`, so the lift is not a non-associative octonion loop. The incorrect
third claim is not used downstream: the proofs of Lemmas 39(2) and 40 invoke only Equations (21) and (22) (p. 12-13), which
concern the lifts `<(x,0),(y,0),(z,1)>` that the check confirms, and the conclusions of Theorem 32 (2) and (3) and of
Theorem 41 hold at `n = 4` by the exhaustive counts above (images of `i_4` confined to `+-e8`; restrictions exactly
`Aut(Q_3)`). The count `|Aut(T_64)| = 5376` and the cases `n >= 5` were not recomputed
here.

## Repository bearing

[I] Automorphisms. Theorem 41 gives the order of the automorphism group of the sign loop as `1344 * 2^(n-3)` for `n >= 3`, a
group of loop automorphisms of the basis (sign-and-permutation maps). The repository's exact algebra automorphism result
`Aut(S) = G2 x S3` for the sedenions ([evidence](../../evidence/sedenion-automorphisms/README.md)) concerns linear
automorphisms of the algebra, a larger group in general; the loop automorphisms are the monomial ones, and the two
results do not contradict each other. Claim `sedenion-g2-bundle` (`SU(2) -> G2 -> V_2(R^7)` with the pair space of normalized
zero products) and the associator claims `cd-associator-census` and `cd-associator-phase-degree`: none directly; Lemma 21's
statement that every associator of basis elements is `+-1` is the sign-cocycle fact on which the repository's associator
phase analysis stands.

[I] Dimensions 32 and above. The paper treats all `n`: `Aut(T_64)` (dimension 32) is stated as 5,376 from GAP; Lemma 40 gives, for
every `n >= 4` and every element other than `+-1, +-e`, a quasi-octonion subloop containing it. Moreno's Stiefel question
(`math/0512517` Section 5): none; annihilators and zero divisors do not appear.

## Boundaries

[I] The GAP counts for `T_64` and the structural lemmas for `n >= 5` were not recomputed; only `n <= 4` was searched
exhaustively. The reproduced statements are about the loop `Q_n`, not about the linear automorphism group of the algebra.
The Hamiltonian property was checked for dimension 32 through the 373 normal subloops counted in
[Cawagas et al.](0907.2047.md), not for larger `n`.
