## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; the page numbers are PDF page indices, and Eqs. 15 and 16 were read from the
rendered page 2. Natural units, `c = 1`.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): "The reduced density matrix for [the spin of a single free spin-1/2 particle]
  is not covariant under Lorentz transformations. The spin entropy is not a relativistic scalar and has
  no invariant meaning."
- [P] Eqs. 1-7 (p. 1): the state is a two-component momentum spinor `psi(p) = (a_1(p), a_2(p))`. The
  reduced spin density matrix `tau = (1/2)[[1 + n_z, n_x - i n_y], [n_x + i n_y, 1 - n_z]]` has Bloch
  vector `n_z = integral (|a_1(p)|^2 - |a_2(p)|^2) dp` and `n_x - i n_y = integral a_1(p) a_2(p)^* dp`. The
  entropy is `S = -tr(tau ln tau) = -sum lambda_j ln lambda_j` with `lambda_j = (1 +- |n|)/2`.
- [P] Eq. 9 (p. 2): under a boost `Lambda` the momentum amplitudes transform as
  `b_r(p) = [(Lambda^-1 p)_0 / p_0]^(1/2) sum_s D_rs[Lambda, (Lambda^-1 p)] a_s(Lambda^-1 p)`, with
  `D_rs` the Wigner rotation matrix, whose direction and magnitude depend on the momentum.
- [P] Eqs. 10-14 (p. 2): for a particle with spin along z, a Gaussian momentum amplitude
  `a_1(p) = (2 pi)^(-3/4) w^(3/2) exp(-p^2 / 2 w^2)` and a boost with velocity `beta` along x
  (`gamma = cosh alpha`, `C = cosh(alpha/2)`, `S = sinh(alpha/2)`), the boosted amplitudes are
  `b_1 = K [C (q_0 + m) + S (q_x + i q_y)] a_1(q)` and `b_2 = K S q_z a_1(q)`.
- [P] Eq. 15 (p. 2), to leading order in `w/m << 1`: `n'_x = n'_y = 0` and
  `n'_z = 1 - (w tanh(alpha/2) / 2m)^2`.
- [P] Eq. 16 (p. 2): the entropy in the boosted frame is positive, `S = t (1 - ln t)` with
  `t = w^2 tanh^2(alpha/2) / 8 m^2`; for a sharp momentum `w` and `t` vanish and so does the entropy.
- [P] Discussion (p. 2): `tau` has no covariant transformation law except for sharp momenta; a boost
  does not always raise the entropy (an observer boosted the other way sees zero); an invariant
  entropy could be the minimum over Lorentz frames or the entropy in the frame with `<p> = 0`; the
  notion "spin state of a particle" is meaningless without the complete state including momentum.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Size: for a laboratory boost `beta` the correction in Eq. 15 is `(w/m)^2 (beta/4)^2` to leading
  order (`tanh(alpha/2) = beta/2`), which is `9.5 x 10^-8 (w/m)^2` at the CMB-frame speed
  `beta = 1.234 x 10^-3` ([1807.06205](1807.06205.md)) and `5 x 10^-15 (w/m)^2` at the Earth-rotation
  boost `beta_L = 1.5 x 10^-6`, with `w/m << 1` by the paper's assumption. The photons of Belenos and
  Ascella have `m = 0` and fall outside the Gaussian-momentum calculation, and the Heron r2 and Tuna-17
  qubits are not free particles with a momentum degree of freedom, so the effect is far below any
  repository observable.
- [I] Signature: an entropy or purity shift from this mechanism would depend on the observer's boost
  and so on the sidereal and annual motion of the laboratory; a purity drift at 24 h or at the
  calibration cadence has a terrestrial origin.
