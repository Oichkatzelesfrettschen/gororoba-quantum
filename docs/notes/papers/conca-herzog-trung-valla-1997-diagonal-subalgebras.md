## What the opening pages state

- [P] Abstract (p. 859): for a closed subscheme `V` of `P^(n-1)` defined by a homogeneous ideal `I` in `A = K[X_1, ..., X_n]`
  and `X` the blow-up of `P^(n-1)` along `V`, the paper studies the ring-theoretic properties of the subalgebra `K[(I^e)_c]` of `A`,
  through a theory of the local cohomology of certain modules over generalized Segre products of bigraded algebras. The results
  apply to the Cohen-Macaulay property of the homogeneous coordinate ring of the blow-up of projective space along a complete
  intersection, and the paper also studies the Koszul property of diagonal subalgebras of bigraded standard `k`-algebras.
- [P] Introduction (p. 859): the main tool is a relationship between `K[(I^e)_c]` and the Rees algebra `A[It]`; the authors are
  inspired by work of Geramita and others on the blow-up of `P^(n-1)` at a set of points.
- [P] Definition (p. 860): the `(c, e)`-diagonal of `Z^2` is `Delta = {(cs, es) : s in Z}`, and the diagonal subalgebra of a
  `Z^2`-graded algebra `S` along `Delta` is `S_Delta = sum_(s in Z) S_(cs, es)`. For an ideal `I` with `I^e` generated in degree at
  most `c`, `K[(I^e)_c]` is isomorphic to `A[It]_Delta` (p. 860).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Kumar and Venugopal attribute to Conca, Herzog, Trung and Valla (p. 2 of their paper) the solution of an open problem on the pairs `(c, e)` for which the diagonal of the Rees algebra of a complete intersection is Cohen-Macaulay, and the Koszul property of `R_Delta` for `c, e >> 0`; their bibliography entry was not opened to confirm that it is this article ([Kumar and Venugopal note](2302.07486.md)). The repository ideals are not complete intersections of that type, so the opening pages carry no statement about the A_5 kernel fibers ([A_5 exceptional fiber findings](../topics/a5-exceptional-fiber-findings.md)). The paper supplies the vocabulary (diagonal subalgebra, `(c, e)`-diagonal) for reading that literature.
