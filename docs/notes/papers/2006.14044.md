## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Two post-processing mitigation schemes use a tensor-product noise model (2n single-qubit rates 0->1 and 1->0) and a correlated model based on continuous-time Markov processes (CTMP) that captures readout cross-talk.
- [P] The introduction names cross-talk during readout from frequency multiplexing as a source of correlated errors the tensor-product model omits.
- [P] Error rates are extracted from calibration data by a simple formula; mitigation applies the inverse noise matrix to the outcome probability vector; the overhead is exponential in n times the maximum error rate.
- [P] Experiments on IBM devices cover graph states up to 12 qubits and 20-qubit Clifford-circuit states.

## Limits

- [P/I] Both models assume the readout noise acts as a fixed stochastic matrix measured in calibration circuits; a state-dependent change between calibration and the target circuit (preparation error in the calibration, leakage, drift) enters as unmodeled bias.
- [P/I] IBM devices with different readout hardware; no flux-pulsed CZ context.
