## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): if the zeros of the zeta function of a finite-field equation are eigenvalues of an efficiently implementable unitary, eigenvalue estimation approximately counts its solutions; Section 5.2 (pp. 3, 9) constructs such circuits for Fermat (diagonal) hypersurfaces through quantum Gauss-sum estimation, and the extension to general hypersurfaces is left open.
