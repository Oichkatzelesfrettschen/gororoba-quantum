## Equations and numbers

- [P] Dynamic fusors, [0016]-[0018] (PDF p. 5): counter-spinning conical structures "made from an alloy of Tungsten
  with high capacitance (such as Tungsten Nitride)"; "An alloy of Tungsten ... in order to hold an electric
  charge of a least one Coulomb, is the material of choice" ([0018]).
- [P] Eq. 1, [0019] (PDF p. 5): the Lawson criterion `n T tau_E >= 3 x 10^21 keV s/m^3`, with `n` the plasma
  density, `T` the temperature and `tau_E` the energy confinement time ([0020]).
- [P] Eq. 2 and Eq. 3, [0021]-[0022] (PDF pp. 5-6): `Energy Gain ~ B^3` and `Fusion Power Density ~ P^2 ~ B^4`,
  "given the condition that the ratio of plasma pressure and magnetic field pressure is on the order of unity".
- [P] [0025] (PDF p. 6): fusion rests on fields "possibly exceeding 30 Tesla, which not even high temperature
  Rare Earth Barium Copper Oxide (REBCO) superconducting magnets can readily generate"; the patent proposes that
  "extremely high B-fields can be generated by controlled motion of electrically charged matter, via accelerated
  spin and/or accelerated vibration, subjected to rapid acceleration transients".
- [P] Eq. 4 and Eq. 5, [0030] (PDF p. 6): `2H + 2H -> 3H (1.01 MeV) + p (3.02 MeV) [50%]` and
  `2H + 2H -> 3He (0.82 MeV) + n (2.45 MeV) [50%]`; deuterium is the preferred fuel ([0027]-[0029]).
- [P] Eq. 6 and Eq. 7, [0033]-[0034] (PDF p. 7): `S_max = f_G (sigma^2/eps0) [(R_v nu^2) t_op]` and, for a
  non-zero time differential of acceleration, `S_max = f_G (sigma^2/eps0) [(R_v nu^3) t_op^2]`.
- [P] Eq. 8 and Eq. 9, [0036]-[0037] (PDF p. 7): `E_T = m R_v^2 Omega^2 [exp(2 Omega t)]` for a vibrating mass with
  an "energy/momentum-pumping (negative damping) term", and `S_max = (Q^2/eps0)(R_v^2/R_s^5) Omega [exp(2 Omega t)]`
  for a spherical geometry of radius `R_s` carrying charge `Q`.
- [P] Eq. 10, [0038] (PDF p. 7): `B/R_v ~ curl B = A_v B`, a "B-field amplification factor which mathematically
  can go to infinity" for a force-free vortex in the plasma core.
- [P] Eq. 11, [0039]-[0041] (PDF p. 7): `B_MAX = mu0 sigma R_omega omega^3 t_op^2`, so that with
  `mu0 sigma R_omega t_op^2 ~ O(1)` the maximum field scales as `omega^3`. [0041]: for `omega` of order 1e4 rad/s
  (discs of 10 cm diameter at 10,000 rad/s, "100,000 RPM"), "a value for B_MAX on the order of 10^6 Tesla is
  achievable".
- [P] [0042] (PDF p. 7): "Taking into consideration Equation 2, the energy gain of the fusion reaction is on the
  order of 10^18", "gigawatt to terawatt range" output for "kilowatt to megawatt" input, and a self-sustained
  burn "without need for external input power".
- [P] The description reports no measurement. [0041] cites Technical Paper AIAA 2017-5343 and SAE 2017-01-2040 by
  the inventor for the high-field claim.

## Claim 1 (PDF p. 8)

[P] "A plasma compression fusion device comprising: a hollow linear-duct having a vacuum chamber disposed within the
hollow linear-duct; one pair of opposing, smoothly curved-headed, counter-spinning conical structures disposed within
the hollow linear-duct, each counter-spinning conical structure having a plurality of orifices and an outer surface
which is electrically charged, and in combination the pair create a concentrated magnetic energy flux and
electromagnetic radiation within the vacuum chamber, whereby the concentrated magnetic energy flux compresses a
mixture of gases that are injected through the orifices to the vacuum chamber such that a plasma core is created,
and the electromagnetic radiation heats the plasma core, while produced magnetic fields confine the plasma core
between the counter-spinning conical structures, such that when an additional mixture of gases is introduced into
the plasma core through the orifices, an energy gain is created." Claim 2 is the cross-duct form with at least two
pairs, claim 4 the conical-frustum form, and claims 3 and 5 add hollow shafts and toroidal coils.
