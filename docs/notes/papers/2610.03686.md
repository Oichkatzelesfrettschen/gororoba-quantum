## Statements

- [P] Abstract and Introduction (pp. 1-2): a structured Hamiltonian H = sum_{j,b} alpha_{j,b} Pi_j^{-1} P_T^{(b)} Pi_j is block-encoded by a PREPARE that writes a support address j and an operator label b, an address-controlled permutation Pi_j that brings the selected support to a fixed target register T, a shared local circuit on T, and the inverse permutation (Fig. 1).
- [P] For Pauli terms the operator adds only an XZ mask on T, at a cost independent of the system size; SELECT costs 8 S N + o(N) T gates with O(log N) permutation work qubits, S = 1 for nearest-neighbor supports and S = 3/2 for all-to-all pairs.
- [P] For two-site supports the paper states it establishes and attains the minimum CSWAP count within address-controlled networks of site transpositions (proof in Appendix A, not read).
- [P] A bridged CSWAP keeps the temporary AND across the target action and halves the T count of a matched forward-inverse pair when the repair is Clifford, at the cost of one retained work qubit.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The kernel-map SELECT applies L_i or L_j, whose index action is the XOR permutation k -> k XOR m with signs; the paper's address-controlled permutation plus XZ mask is the same split between a permutation (where) and a sign pattern (what), but it targets site transpositions on N qubits at fault-tolerant T-count, whereas the repo's XOR permutations are X-gate networks on 5 data qubits and the cost that matters on Heron r2 is two-qubit gate count after routing. The statement transfers as a design pattern, not as a cost number.
- [I] Fault-tolerant T-count is out of scope for the account's hardware (Heron r2, no error correction); cite only for the structure.
