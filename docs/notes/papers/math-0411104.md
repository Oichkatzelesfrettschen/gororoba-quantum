## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): an integral version of the Freudenthal construction relating Jordan algebras and exceptional algebraic groups, related to higher composition laws of Bhargava in number theory; an algorithmic approach to orbit spaces of groups that underlie higher composition laws, which yields two new examples of spaces sharing similar properties and indicates several more.
- [P] Contents (p. 1): Section 2 treats cubic Jordan algebras and associated structures (composition algebras and their integral structures, the Springer construction of cubic Jordan algebras, three main examples, two groups attached to cubic Jordan algebras); Section 3 the Freudenthal construction (the module of the Jordan algebra, the rank of elements, the canonical form over a field); Section 4 the integral version; Section 5 an appendix on Bhargava's cube law.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper is the reference for the Freudenthal construction on a cubic Jordan algebra and for the rank classification of the elements of the associated module (Section 3 of the contents).
