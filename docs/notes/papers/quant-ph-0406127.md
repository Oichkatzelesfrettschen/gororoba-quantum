## Statements read

- [P] Eq. (10) (p. 2): <m_1..m_N| U |n_1..n_N> = (prod_i n_i!)^(-1/2) (prod_j m_j!)^(-1/2) per Lambda[Omega'|Omega], where Lambda is the mode unitary and Omega, Omega' repeat each index as often as its occupation.
- [P] Eq. (13) (pp. 2-3): per Lambda is the amplitude <1|^{(x)N} U |1>^{(x)N}, so |per Lambda| <= 1 for a unitary Lambda.
- [P] Eq. (14) (p. 3): U |n_1..n_N> = (prod_i n_i!)^(-1/2) sum over non-decreasing sequences omega of mu(omega)^(-1/2) per Lambda[omega|Omega] |m(omega)>, the full action on a Fock state as permanents of submatrices of Lambda.
- [P] Section I (p. 1): the author notes that optimizing a linear-optical network for gate success probability is a permanent maximization under constraints.
- [I] Eqs. (10) and (14) are the amplitude law that the Sym^n description of an n-photon interferometer rests on; the Ascella folding analysis (session report, not retained) checks Eq. (10) numerically against Perceval SLOS on a 12-mode unitary for 1, 2 and 3 photons.
