## Mechanism and evidence

- [P] Quantum Crosstalk-aware Parallel workload execution (QuCP) replaces a
  detailed simultaneous-randomized-benchmarking map with a topology-dependent
  penalty during partition selection (Section III).
- [P] For prospective CNOT pairs one hop from already allocated pairs, QuCP
  multiplies their error estimate by a parameter sigma. Its Estimated Fidelity
  Score combines the mean penalized two-qubit error times two-qubit gate count,
  mean one-qubit error times one-qubit gate count, and readout-error sum.
- [P] Section IV-A tunes sigma against QuMC partitions on IBM Q 27 Toronto.
  Values at least four give the same partitioning in that experiment; the
  reported comparison then uses sigma equal to four. The authors characterize
  Toronto with SRB for the comparison, although QuCP allocation itself replaces
  the detailed map with the heuristic penalty.
- [P] Section IV-B varies an admission threshold for one to six simultaneous
  copies on IBM Q 65 Manhattan. The paper defines hardware throughput as the
  fraction of physical qubits occupied, and reports a fidelity loss beyond
  38 percent utilization for the selected benchmark circuits.
- [P] Section IV-C combines Pauli grouping with simultaneous H2 measurement
  circuits. The two-qubit Hamiltonian has terms `{II, IZ, ZI, ZZ, XX}`, grouped
  into two measurement settings. The experiment evaluates 8, 10, or 12 selected
  common parameter values with 16, 20, or 24 concurrent measurement circuits.
  The text reports utilization up to 73.8 percent with energy error below
  10 percent under the comparisons it defines.
- [P] Section IV-D generates four randomly folded circuits with Mitiq at scale
  factors 1, 1.5, 2, and 2.5. The authors run those circuits simultaneously
  across Manhattan partitions and compare baseline, serial ZNE, and QuCP+ZNE.
- [P] The same section evaluates linear, polynomial, and Richardson
  extrapolations, then reports the method closest to the ideal simulator result.
  The authors explicitly acknowledge that ZNE was designed to scale noise on
  the same occupied qubits, while their parallel version uses different patches.
- [P] Section IV-D reports a threefold runtime/throughput improvement and
  average error reduction by 2x, with a best-case 11x reduction against baseline.
  Those are source-reported benchmark outcomes, not a repository prediction.

[I] Diagnostic applicability is high: the concurrent folded-circuit study informs
phase-estimation controls and patch-dependent mitigation. Co-acquisition can also
serve defined algebraic endpoints while preserving each output task. Those uses
remain separate from direct Cayley-Dickson structure and factoring estimates.

- [I] The topology penalty offers a cheap placement heuristic. Its Toronto
  tuning supplies no calibrated Heron error model; sigma equal to four and the
  utilization threshold require their own justification before reuse.
- [I] The repository's physical-CZ buffer is a hard geometric constraint.
  QuCP uses a soft score penalty. Compare those designs with
  [palloq's buffers](2112.07091.md) and the retained [QuMC analysis](../2102.05321.md)
  before creating another allocator or claiming a new placement principle.
- [I] Concurrent folded circuits already have published preprint precedent.
  A diagnostic that distinguishes fold effect from patch effect requires a
  design beyond assigning one scale to each patch. Rotate arms across patches,
  retain arm-by-patch interactions, and recompute patch-dependent delay controls.
- [I] Choosing an extrapolator after consulting the true answer uses information
  unavailable for an unknown target. Such best-of-results evidence motivates
  exploration but cannot validate an answer-blind mitigation pipeline. Select
  the method and fitting rules before evaluating a held-out diagnostic.
- [I] Fixed companions can be shared across all diagnostic arms; changing the
  companion mix with the arm creates another treatment variable. A crossover
  can measure that variable when its ordering and analysis are explicit. See
  [the joint design synthesis](../quantum_experiment_parallelism.md).
- [I] Occupancy, simultaneous circuit count, useful endpoints, precision, and
  charged seconds measure different resources. The source's occupancy-based
  throughput and quoted runtime factors do not establish current IBM billing.

## Prior art and action gates

1. Credit parallel measurement settings and folded-circuit ZNE as prior art.
   Candidate new results concern controlled diagnostic comparisons, supported
   model-hardware explanations, and measured cost at an explicit accuracy target.
2. Design a treatment-by-patch-by-period table before circuit construction.
   Preserve seed, fold, layout, actual scheduled delays, and companion identities
   for every cell; define the output estimator and exclusion rules in advance.
3. Compare the diagnostic on the same patch under declared contexts, then test
   generalization across patches. Use calibration and schedule records to bound
   attribution, and retain ordered raw outcomes for cross-stream analysis.
4. Produce a submit-free plan, inspect compiled circuits, and demonstrate local
   register/endpoint attribution before proposing a hardware run. The existing
   written approval case remains the gate for any QPU submission.

still depends on its distribution and dependence on the other streams.
