## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are the printed page numbers, which equal the PDF page
indices.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): previous crosstalk models treated crosstalk "solely as an interaction between
  controlled phase shifters"; the paper introduces "the concept of induced phase shifter, arising from
  crosstalk on bare waveguide sections as predicted by simulations" and a mitigation framework that
  "allows to establish a criterion certifying that a given interferometer has a sufficient number of
  degrees of freedom adequately laid out to fully mitigate crosstalk".
- [P] Eq. 1 (p. 2): the restricted crosstalk model, phi_res = sum over k of C_k(res) V^(.k) + c_0(res)
  over the controlled shifters only; diagonal elements of C_k(res) are self-heating coefficients and
  off-diagonal elements are crosstalk.
- [P] Section II (p. 2): in a finite-element simulation of a 6-mode silicon-nitride chip with trenches,
  "waveguides that do not initially feature a PS heater acquire an unwanted phase shift due to heat
  diffusion"; implementing target unitary matrices while modeling only controlled-shifter crosstalk
  gives an average amplitude fidelity of 99.3 % in simulation, against 99.7 %, the highest
  experimental value then recorded for a 12-mode Clements chip (its ref. [20], which the paper's
  introduction describes as the authors' previous machine-learning work).
- [P] Section I (p. 1): the phase-voltage relation recovered by the authors' earlier characterization
  (its ref. [20]) "exhibits unphysical long-range crosstalk", which "hints towards a mechanism
  overlooked by current crosstalk models"; Section V (p. 10) states that the restricted model
  absorbs induced shifters of a Clements mesh as artificial long-range crosstalk.
- [P] Theorem 2, the crosstalk-robustness criterion (p. 8): a photonic circuit is crosstalk-robust if
  and only if its associated pruned graph is acyclic; the Reck, Clements and Bell-Walmsley universal
  interferometers are crosstalk-robust, specialized interferometers such as those of refs. [33]-[36]
  are not, and "an MZI followed by two beamsplitters is universal on two modes, but not
  crosstalk-robust".
- [P] Section V (pp. 9-10): on the 12-mode Clements chip with 126 phase shifters and 132
  beamsplitters, with about 16 000 training and 4 000 test samples, the restricted model reaches
  TVD_test = 7.5 % after 2 iterations and the extended model 7.6 %; the paper attributes the gap to
  the 2.9 % of the earlier work to its use of "an attenuated laser instead of a true single-photon
  source". The amplitude fidelities of both models agree within error bars, the two ML stages take
  3.9 minutes for the restricted model and 1.5 minutes for the extended model, and the mean
  off-diagonal amplitude of the estimated crosstalk matrix is about 1e-4 (restricted) against
  about 5e-5 (extended).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper identifies the long-range entries that the clear-box fit of
  [Fyrillas et al. 2024](2310.15349.md) returned as the restricted-model image of induced shifters, so
  a fitted crosstalk matrix is a control model and not a physical heater-to-heater map. A
  calibration-dependent crosstalk residual of this kind is one candidate physical source for the
  compilation-dependent leakage that the Belenos acceptance and routing controls of
  [bdsc-qualification.md](../../design/bdsc-qualification.md) measure by resubmitting equivalent
  circuits; no measurement in the repository separates it from other sources.
- [I] The reported 5e-5 to 1e-4 off-diagonal amplitudes set the scale of the crosstalk that the drift
  series of [bdsc_drift_series.py](../../../scripts/bdsc_drift_series.py) would have to see to attribute
  excess variance to crosstalk drift; the survey in [literature.md](../quandela/literature.md)
  (source 17) carries the fuller reading.
