## Statements

- [P] Theorem 1 (p. 2): a quantum algorithm computes the numerator P(t) of the zeta function of a genus-g curve over F_q in time polynomial in g and log q, by producing provably random elements of the class group and recovering the Weil polynomial from enough cyclic resultants.
- [P] Section 3 (p. 6): the curve is smooth, projective and geometrically irreducible; p. 7: the method eventually requires q not too small compared with g.
