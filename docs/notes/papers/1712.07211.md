## Statements

- [P] Theorem 1 (p. 3, summarized from Section 4): for m = n Boolean quadratic equations in n variables (MQ_2), under an algebraic assumption, a Las Vegas quantum algorithm evaluates an expected O(2^(0.462 n)) quantum gates; p. 4: a quantum security level 2^s needs at least 2.16 s variables.
