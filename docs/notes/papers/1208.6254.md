## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): for each element of the Fischer-Griess Monster the paper constructs an infinite dimensional Lie algebra with a projective action of the centralizer of that element, by applying the string-theoretic "add a spacetime torus and quantize" functor to an abelian intertwining algebra formed from a family of twisted modules of the Monster vertex operator algebra. It proves that for all Fricke elements of the Monster the characters of the centralizers acting on the irreducible twisted modules are Hauptmoduln, and from these resolves Norton's Generalized Moonshine Conjecture.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): for the elements satisfying the Fricke property the Lie algebras are Borcherds-Kac-Moody algebras with a real simple root, and the twisted denominator identities prove the conjecture for the class of irreducible twisted `V^natural`-modules. The text recalls that in the late 1970s McKay observed that the `q^1` coefficient of the normalized modular function `J = q^-1 + 196884 q + 21493760 q^2 + ...` is one more than the dimension of the smallest nontrivial complex representation of the Monster.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper concerns the Monster vertex operator algebra and generalized moonshine.
