## Transcribed statements

- [P] Setting (p. 2): for `char F != 2` and `gamma` in `F^x`, the doubling `B = A{gamma} = A + A l` has
  `(q + r l)(s + t l) = q s + gamma sigma(t) r + (t q + r sigma(s)) l`, `sigma(q + r l) = sigma(q) - r l`; the `n`-fold algebra
  `(gamma_1, ..., gamma_n)_F` starts from `F` with the trivial involution. For `n = 3` these are octonion algebras and for `n = 4`
  sedenion algebras. The `n`-fold Cayley-Dickson loop over a subgroup `Z` of `F^x` containing the `gamma_i` is the multiplicative
  subloop generated by `Z` and the generators `l_1, ..., l_n`, of order `2^n |Z|`; `(-1,-1)_Z` is `Q_8` and `(-1,-1,-1)_Z` is the octonion
  loop `O_16` for `Z = {+-1}`. All such loops are di-associative (Culbert, Theorem 5.4).
- [P] Central product (p. 3): `L_1 * L_2` is `L_1 x L_2` modulo `{(x, x^(-1)) : x in Z}`, and the commutators of the loops
  here lie in `{+-1}`. Each `n`-fold loop `D_i` satisfies `D_i / Z = (Z/2)^n`, and `A = D_1 * ... * D_m` satisfies `A / Z = (Z/2)^(mn)`.
- [P] Proposition 1 (p. 4): for `x` in `D_k` outside `Z`, `C_A(x) / Z` has cardinality `2^((m-1) n + 1) = |A/Z| / 2^(n-1)`.
- [P] Theorem 2 (p. 4): for `y` of rank `k` in `A` (a product of `k` elements from distinct factors outside `Z`),
  `|C_A(y) / Z| / 2^(mn) = 1/2 + (1/2) (2p - 1)^k` with `p = 1 / 2^(n-1)`.
- [P] Corollary 3 (p. 5): for `L = D_1 * D_2` with `|Z| < infinity`, the commutativity degree is
  `P_c(L) = 1 - 6/2^n + 22/4^n - 24/8^n + 8/16^n`. Corollary 4: the commutativity degree of these di-associative loops
  tends to 1, whereas a non-abelian group has commutativity degree at most 5/8. Remark 5: for fixed `n >= 2`, `P_c(A_m)` tends to
  1/2 as `m` grows.
- [P] Theorem 6 (p. 5): if `D_1 * ... * D_m` and `E_1 * ... * E_m` are isomorphic central products of `n`-fold Cayley-Dickson loops
  over `Z` with `n >= 3`, then `D_j` is isomorphic to `E_(sigma(j))` for a permutation `sigma`; the proof shows an isomorphism
  preserves rank because `|b_k - 1/2|` is strictly decreasing for `n >= 3`.
- [P] Theorem 7 (p. 6): the associativity degree of `L_n = (-1, ..., -1)_Z` (the probability that three random elements generate
  a group) is `P_A(L_n) = (7 * 4^n - 14 * 2^n + 8) / 8^n`; it equals 1 for `n = 2` and `43/64` for `n = 3`, the bound found for Moufang
  loops by Levin (Comm. Algebra 54 (2026)).

## Convention map and cd_core check

[I] Product. With `gamma = -1` and `sigma` the conjugation, the printed doubling is `cd_core`'s
`(a,b)(c,d) = (ac - conj(d) b, d a + b conj(c))` term by term (`q = a`, `r = b`, `s = c`, `t = d`), so `(-1, ..., -1)_{+-1}` is the
sign loop `Q_n` of `cd_core.cd_sign(2^n, p, q)`, of order `2^(n+1)`.

[I] Check results: no mismatch. A scratch script (ignored `docs/local/`, run with `-I`) counted exactly over the index
group `F_2^n` (a subloop is a subspace, and three random loop elements generate the subloop on the span of their indices):

| Paper statement | Result under `cd_core` |
| --- | --- |
| commutator `[e_a, e_b] = -1` exactly when `a`, `b` are nonzero and distinct (used throughout Section 3) | holds for every pair at `n = 2..7` |
| Theorem 7, `P_A(L_n)` | exact fractions `1, 43/64, 197/512, 841/4096, 3473/32768` for `n = 2..6` (loops of order up to `2^7`; algebra dimension up to 64), each equal to the formula |
| Corollary 3, `P_c(D_1 * D_2)` | the commutativity degree computed from the commutator rule over all `(a_1, a_2, b_1, b_2)` equals the formula for `n = 2..6` (`17/32, 281/512, 5777/8192, 109217/131072, 1911617/2097152`) |

[I] The central product enters the check only through the commutator rule and the index group `F_2^n x F_2^n`; the loop
`D_1 * D_2` was not built as a subloop of a tensor product of two `cd_core` algebras. Theorem 6 (isomorphism rigidity)
and Theorem 2 for `m > 2` were not replayed.

## Repository bearing

[I] Associator and sign statistics. The associativity degree `P_A(L_n)` counts triples of basis elements, with signs, that
generate an associative subloop; for `n = 4, 5, 6` (dimensions 16, 32, 64) it is `197/512`, `841/4096` and `3473/32768`.
The repository's `cd-associator-census` counts the non-associating ordered basis triples `(D - 2)(D - 4)(D + 6)/2` of
dimension `D = 2^n`; the two counts are different statistics (the paper counts triples whose generated subloop is a group,
including repeated indices and the center), and their relation is not derived here. The check also confirms that the
commutator sign rule `[e_a, e_b] = -1` for distinct nonzero indices holds through dimension 128.

[I] Zero divisors, annihilator dimensions, Stiefel elements, automorphisms of the algebra and box-kites: none; the paper
concerns loops (basis signs) only. Moreno's Stiefel question (`math/0512517` Section 5): none. Dimensions 64 and above: the
formulas of Theorems 2 and 7 hold for every `n`, and the exact replay covers `n = 6` (dimension 64).

## Boundaries

[I] The central-product constructions for `m >= 2` and Theorem 6 were not rebuilt from `cd_core`. The relation between the
associativity degree and the repository's associator counts is not derived. Only `Z = {+-1}` was used in the replay; larger
finite `Z` scale the counts and leave the probabilities unchanged, as the paper's formulas indicate.
