## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Frequency-tunable superconducting qubits serve as sub-wavelength haloscope units, so several resonant detectors with different frequencies fit on one chip.
- [P] A three-qubit device sets kinetic-mixing constraints on dark photons simultaneously over 15.632 to 15.638 ueV, 15.838 to 15.845 ueV and 16.463 to 16.468 ueV, much more stringent than the cosmological constraints in those windows.
