## Statements

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): a QLSP algorithm makes Theta(1/sqrt(p)) queries to the state-preparation oracle `O_b`, optimal in the success probability `p`, and O(kappa log(1/p)(log log(1/p) + log(1/epsilon))) queries to the block encoding `O_A`; the tool is a variable-time amplitude amplification with tunable thresholds.
- [P] Theorem 1 (p. 40): solution-norm estimation with optimal initial-state preparation, for `A/alpha_A` block-encoded by `O_A` with `alpha_A >= ||A||`.
- [P] Theorem 2 (p. 42): the QLSP algorithm with optimal initial-state preparation, given a constant-factor estimate of the success amplitude `sqrt(p_succ) = ||A^-1 |b>|| / alpha_(A^-1)`.
- [P] Theorem 3 (p. 44): a lower bound on the initial-state preparation cost; Section 6 (p. 44) introduces block preconditioning.
