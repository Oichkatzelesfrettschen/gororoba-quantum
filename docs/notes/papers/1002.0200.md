## Main claims and evidence

- [P] QET transports energy between subsystems by local operations and classical communication through ground-state entanglement, retaining causality and local energy conservation.
- [P] Two energy-entanglement inequalities are proved for the minimal QET model: two qubits with an Ising interaction in a transverse field, H_A = h sigma_A^z + h^2/sqrt(h^2+k^2), H_B likewise, V = 2k sigma_A^x sigma_B^x + 2k^2/sqrt(h^2+k^2).

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The repository reference uses the same model with -h Z, an equivalent labeling under sigma^z -> -sigma^z that leaves sigma^x unchanged; its closed forms E_A = h^2/r and E_B = sqrt(h^2+4k^2) - (h^2+2k^2)/r rest on (h^2+2k^2)^2 + h^2 k^2 = (h^2+k^2)(h^2+4k^2), the combination that appears in the paper's optimization.
