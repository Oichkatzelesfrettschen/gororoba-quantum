## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are PDF page indices, and Eqs. 2 and 3 were read from the rendered
page 2.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): in low-energy quantum mechanics with gravitational time dilation, the
  coupling between the internal degrees of freedom and the center of mass of a composite particle
  produces decoherence of the center-of-mass superposition.
- [P] Eq. 1 (p. 2): the interaction Hamiltonian is `H_int = Phi(x) H_0 / c^2 = hbar (g x / c^2) (sum_i omega_i n_i)`,
  with internal frequencies `omega_i(x) = omega_i (1 + g x / c^2)`.
- [P] Eqs. 2-3 (p. 2): for a superposition of two heights separated by `Delta x` and `N` internal
  oscillators at temperature `T`, the visibility is `V(t) ~ exp[-(t / tau_dec)^2]` with the decoherence
  time `tau_dec = sqrt(2/N) hbar c^2 / (k_B T g Delta x)`.
- [P] Eq. 4 (p. 3): the general visibility is `V = |<exp(-i H_0 Delta tau / hbar)>|`, with `Delta tau` the
  proper-time difference between the two world lines; it reduces to Eq. 3 for harmonic internal
  states.
- [P] Eq. 5 (p. 3): the master equation carries the term
  `-(Delta E_0 / hbar c^2)^2 integral_0^t ds [Gamma(x, p), e^(-i H_cm s/hbar) [Gamma(x, p), rho_cm(t - s)] e^(i H_cm s/hbar)]`
  with `Gamma = Phi(x) - p^2 / 2 m^2` and `Delta E_0` the internal energy spread.
- [P] Eq. 7 (p. 4): for `N ~ 10^23`, room temperature and `Delta x = 10^-6` m the decoherence time is
  `tau_dec ~ 10^-3` s. The paper states that the effect "cannot be shielded" and occurs whenever there
  is time dilation between superposed amplitudes.
- [P] Related published record: Crossref lists a comment, "Questioning universal decoherence due to
  gravitational time dilation" (Bonder, Okon, Sudarsky, Nature Physics 12(1), 2, 2016, DOI
  10.1038/nphys3573), and the authors' reply (Nature Physics 12(1), 2-3, 2016, DOI 10.1038/nphys3650).
  The note imports neither text.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The decoherence needs a spatial superposition of a composite body with many thermally populated
  internal modes. The repository's devices prepare none: Belenos and Ascella photons are not composite,
  and the transmons of Heron r2 and Tuna-17 sit in one place. For the paper's own example,
  `sqrt(2/N) hbar c^2 / (k_B T g Delta x)` at `N = 10^23`, `T = 300` K and `Delta x = 1 micrometer`
  evaluates to `1.0 x 10^-3` s, and the same formula scales as `1/sqrt(N)`, so a single qubit sits far outside it.
- [I] Signature: decoherence from time dilation scales with the height separation `Delta x` of
  the superposed branches (`tau_dec` falls as `1/(T sqrt(N) g Delta x)`) and enters the visibility as
  `exp[-(t/tau_dec)^2]`; it carries no sidereal or orientation dependence.
