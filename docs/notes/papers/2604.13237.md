## Main claims and evidence

- [P] The worldline effective action for a real scalar field coupled to a spacetime-dependent mass term models a moving medium with imperfect boundary conditions on time-dependent surfaces.
- [P] Expanding in the departure from a planar geometry factorizes the worldline path integral; strong coupling recovers the Dirichlet result with corrections in inverse powers of the coupling.
- [P] The imaginary part of the effective action is computed to arbitrary order, and the method extends to two surfaces.
