## Transcribed statements

Read from the PDF above; page numbers are the printed page numbers, which equal the PDF page
indices.

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): "the comment of Kobrin et al. is consistent with Jafferis et al. on key
  points: i) the microscopic mechanism of the experimentally observed teleportation is size winding
  and ii) the system thermalizes and scrambles at the time of teleportation. These properties are
  consistent with a gravitational interpretation of the teleportation dynamics, as opposed to the
  late-time dynamics. The objections of Kobrin et al. concern counterfactual scenarios outside of the
  experimentally implemented protocol."
- [P] Response 1, thermalization (abstract and Sections I, II, IV, pp. 1-5): the single-sided
  revivals appear at about t = 10, after the teleportation time t of about 2.8; the paper's
  "thermalization" and "scrambling" refer to the timescale of the teleportation, where "the two
  Majorana fermions being teleported each spread onto 8 operators, with the largest operator of size
  5" (p. 2). Adding the non-commuting term H1 = 0.3 psi1 psi2 psi3 psi5 (Eq. 4, p. 4) "forces the
  system to thermalize at long timescales but does not significantly modify the two-point function or
  mutual information dynamics at the timescale t = 2.8", and the coupled left-right Hamiltonian,
  of which the experimental protocol is "a first-order Trotterization" with a single step (p. 2), shows
  no revivals (Fig. 1a).
- [P] Response 2, untrained fermions (Section III, pp. 3-4): "in the learned Hamiltonian, we find that
  all fermions show size winding at times 2 <~ t <~ 5; their claim is an artifact of only analyzing
  size winding at t = 2.8" (p. 2). Fermions psi4 and psi7, which the comment found poor at t = 2.8,
  reach near-linear size-winding phases at t of about 4 and, with the interaction applied at t = 4,
  show a mutual-information peak asymmetry (Fig. 2d). The paper reads slower-thermalizing fermions as
  lighter and later to traverse the wormhole, and adds that "a holographic dual need not have size
  winding across all fermions", so absence on untrained fermions "has no known implication for the
  holographic dual of the teleported fermions".
- [P] Response 3, random Hamiltonians (Section IV, pp. 4-5): there is "a unique 7-fermion 5-term
  commuting Hamiltonian, up to relabeling of fermions", so the comment's two randomization procedures
  "are strictly equivalent". Reproducing the comment's procedure on 1000 random instances at
  t = 2.8, 29 % match the learned model's size winding on the best two fermions and 3 % on all
  fermions (25 % and 2 % by the comment's chi metric); the paper concludes this "contradicts the
  claim of [1]" and adds that "the presence of similar Hamiltonians with similar properties does not
  have any bearing on a gravitational interpretation of the learned Hamiltonian".
- [P] Method (p. 5, footnote 5): size-winding quality is the standard deviation of the difference of
  adjacent values of arg q(l); the time of best size winding is the first local minimum of that
  deviation after t = 0 with the deviation below 0.8.
- [P] Floquet control (p. 5, Fig. 4): evolving under H0 and H1 alternately in intervals of 2.8 keeps
  the mutual-information asymmetry in the interaction sign, so the late-time dynamics are decoupled
  from the dynamics at the time of teleportation; a fermion explores 12 operators under the Floquet
  Hamiltonian against 8 under H0.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Vacuum and quantum-energy-teleportation notes
  ([vacuum_field_run_opportunities.md](../vacuum_field_run_opportunities.md)). The exchange fixes which
  claims are in dispute: the two sides agree that size winding is the mechanism and that the
  single-sided learned Hamiltonian revives after the teleportation time, and they differ on whether
  the properties at the teleportation time and on trained operators suffice for the gravitational
  reading. The reply answers the comment's controls with further simulations (the 1000-instance
  null at t = 2.8, the Floquet case) and offers the non-bulk explanation as compatible with a
  gravitational one, so the exchange leaves the interpretation open; the repository records both
  papers and takes no position.
- [I] A repository case that borrows the teleportation protocol states, before the run, the time at
  which each property is evaluated and the operator set, because the reply's rebuttal of the second
  objection rests on a different evaluation time (t about 4) from the one the comment used (2.8).
