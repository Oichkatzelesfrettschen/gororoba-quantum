## Summary of principles and software architecture

### 1. Modality and representation
- [P] Perceval targets discrete-variable (DV) photonic quantum computing based on Fock states and linear
  optical circuits.
- [P] Physical states are represented as superpositions of Fock states $|n_0, n_1, \dots, n_{m-1}\rangle$,
  where $m$ is the number of spatial/temporal modes and $\sum n_i = n$ is the total photon count.
- [P] Linear optical components are modeled as unitary scattering matrices $U \in U(m)$ acting on mode
  annihilation and creation operators: $a_i^\dagger \to \sum_j U_{ji} a_j^\dagger$.

### 2. Simulation backends
Perceval integrates four complementary simulation backends implemented in C++ (`exqalibur`):
- [P] **SLOS (Strong Linear Optical Simulator)**: Fast exact computation of output state probability
  amplitudes using recursive permanent evaluation and Glynn-type formulas.
- [P] **Clifford & Clifford 2017**: Polynomial-time approximate and exact sampling from linear optical
  circuits based on repeated conditional single-photon state generation (Clifford & Clifford, SODA 2018).
- [P] **Naive**: Brute-force calculation of transition matrix permanents for verification and unit testing.
- [P] **Stepper**: Incremental mode-by-mode state propagation, enabling intermediate non-unitary filtering,
  polarization rotation, and time delays.

### 3. Noise and physical modeling
- [P] **Source imperfections**: Implements emission probability ($p_{\text{em}}$), photon indistinguishability
  ($V_{\text{HOM}}$), multi-photon emission rate ($g^{(2)}(0)$), and spectral/temporal mode mismatches.
- [P] **Circuit imperfections**: Incorporates transmission loss per component, phase noise, and beam splitter
  imbalance.
- [P] **Detector imperfections**: Supports detection efficiency ($\eta$), dark counts ($p_{\text{dark}}$),
  dead time, and threshold vs. photon-number-resolving (PNR) detection modes.

### 4. Transpilation and hardware interfacing
- [P] Transpiles abstract unitaries into canonical physical meshes (Reck et al. triangular mesh, Clements et al.
  rectangular mesh).
- [P] Integrates directly with Quandela Cloud via `QuandelaCommunicationLayer` to execute compiled circuits
  on physical QPUs (`qpu:ascella`, `qpu:belenos`) and remote emulators (`sim:ascella`).
