## Results needed by the project

1. [P] Theorem 2 (p. 7): a state `rho` on `H_1 (x) H_2` is separable if and
   only if `(I (x) Lambda)(rho)` is positive for every positive map
   `Lambda : A_2 -> A_1`, with `A_i` the operators on `H_i`.
2. [P] Theorem 3 (p. 8): a state on `C^2 (x) C^2` or `C^2 (x) C^3` is
   separable if and only if its partial transposition `rho^(T2) = (I (x) T) rho`
   (Eq. (16)) is a positive operator.
3. [P] The proof of Theorem 3 uses the result of Stormer and Woronowicz that
   every positive map between `M_2` and `M_2`, or between `M_3` and `M_2`, has
   the form `Lambda = Lambda_1^CP + Lambda_2^CP T` with completely positive
   `Lambda_i^CP` (Eq. (17), p. 8).
4. [P] The condition `rho^(T2) >= 0` is necessary for separability in every
   dimension (footnote 4, p. 8, credited to Peres) and sufficient only in the
   cases of item 2; the Appendix (p. 11, Eq. (27)) outlines why the cone of
   positive maps equals the cone of maps of the form (17) only for `M_2 -> M_2`
   and `M_2 <-> M_3`, so the condition is in general only necessary (stated on
   p. 8 and in the abstract).
5. [P] A state is separable when it can be approximated in the trace norm by
   convex combinations of product states, Eq. (2), p. 3.

### Corrections to the earlier reading

Three statements changed. Item 3 earlier read "every positive map on
`C^2 (x) C^2` or between `C^3` and `C^2`"; the paper states the form (17) for
positive maps `Lambda : A_1 -> A_2` with `H_1 = H_2 = C^2` [Stormer] or
`H_1 = C^3`, `H_2 = C^2` (equivalently `C^2`, `C^3`) [Woronowicz] on p. 8, and
lists the same three cases as `M_2 -> M_2`, `M_2 -> M_3`, `M_3 -> M_2` in the
Appendix (p. 11). Item 4 earlier cited "p. 8, abstract" for the outline of the
proof; the outline is the Appendix on p. 11, and p. 8 holds the announcement.
Item 1 earlier omitted the direction of `Lambda`. The earlier statement that
the examples were "checked only for the statement that the criterion is not
sufficient" is replaced by the scope above. A printing slip on p. 11: the text
says that the first of the determinants of Eq. (24) is positive and that "for
the second one" `W_1 = -p^2/4`; Eqs. (24) and (26) give `W_1 = -p^2/4` and
`W_2 = p^2/4`, so the formula is right and the ordinal is not.

## Repository use

The repository uses this source in
[the Ascella-Belenos design](../../design/ascella-belenos-entanglement-teleportation.md)
(sections "Decision statistics" and "Gates") and in
[`scripts/qd_network_protocol.py`](../../../scripts/qd_network_protocol.py).

[I] Two-qubit and qubit-qutrit systems. A two-photon polarization or
dual-rail state of two qubits is separable exactly when its partial
transpose has no negative eigenvalue (Theorem 3); a negative eigenvalue of
`rho^(T2)` witnesses entanglement without a separate optimization, which needs
only the necessity direction (item 4, valid in every dimension). A pair of
qubits drawn from a larger system, each addressed through its own subspace,
needs the full state or a criterion valid in that dimension.

[I] Separable witness bound of gate G4 and claim level C2 (also the reference
witness of G6). For target `Phi+`, `tr(rho Phi+) = (1/2) tr(rho^(T2) SWAP)`
because `(Phi+)^(T2) = SWAP/2` and `tr(A B) = tr(A^(T2) B^(T2))`. A separable
state has `rho^(T2) >= 0` with unit trace (the necessity direction, footnote
4, p. 8, which holds in every dimension), and `SWAP <= I`, so
`F = tr(rho Phi+) <= 1/2`. The design's statement that `F > 1/2` certifies
entanglement of a valid two-qubit state, and that the classically correlated
mixture of `|00>` and `|11>` reads exactly 1/2, follows from this step and the
paper's item 4. The step from PPT to `F <= 1/2` is a repository derivation
checked by hand, not a statement of the paper. The statement concerns the
state, not the estimate: the Hoeffding radius that converts finite counts into
a lower bound on `F` is not in this source and not in any of the four network
sources; it follows from [Hoeffding 1963](hoeffding-1963.md) and the design's
independence and selection model.

[I] Choi-PPT benchmark of gate G5 and claim level C3. The design restricts the
accepted operation to entanglement-breaking maps, whose Choi state is
separable, and states the conditions `J >= 0`, `J^(T_in) >= 0` and
`tr_out J <= I/2` for the accepted trace-nonincreasing map. Replacing
"separable" by "PPT" for the Choi state uses the sufficiency direction of
Theorem 3, which holds only for `2 (x) 2` and `2 (x) 3`. The equivalence of an
entanglement-breaking channel and a separable Choi state is a separate result
([quant-ph/0302031](quant-ph-0302031.md)). When accepted multiphoton sectors
or loss take the instrument out of the two-qubit case, the equivalence is not
supplied by this paper and the benchmark has to be derived again, which is the
design's own "derived again" clause.

[I] Dimension limit. Any statement of the form "PPT therefore separable" holds
only for `2 (x) 2` and `2 (x) 3`. For a qubit and a larger system, or for
more parties, `rho^(T2) >= 0` leaves bound entangled states possible
(item 4).

[I] Exact states versus estimates. The theorem classifies a known density
operator. A tomographic or witness estimate of finite counts has no
separability certificate from this paper; the design's separable bound and its
statistical radius carry that step.
