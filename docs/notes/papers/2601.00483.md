## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Casimir forces between a polystyrene surface and a Teflon-coated metal substrate in toluene with magnetite particles produce quantum levitation with repulsion-attraction transitions at measurable distances.
- [P] Material choices of metal and ferrofluid control the trapping; thermal and quantum contributions and the ferrofluid's optical and magnetic properties set its magnitude and range.
