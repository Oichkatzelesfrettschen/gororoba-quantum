## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A lossless N-mode interferometer is an N x N unitary U acting on fields or annihilation operators. T_(m,n)(theta, phi) is a beam splitter of reflectivity cos theta between adjacent channels with a phase phi at input m (Eq. 1), realized as a Mach-Zehnder of two 50:50 couplers after a phase shift.
- [P] U = D prod T_(m,n) with D diagonal of unit-modulus phases (Eq. 2); the decomposition nulls successive diagonals of U, alternating left and right multiplication (Eq. 3), and commutes the inverse factors through D (Eqs. 4 and 5).
- [P] Both the Reck design and this rectangular design use the minimal N(N - 1)/2 beam splitters; the optical depth is N here against 2N - 3 for Reck.
- [P] Any M x N linear transformation embeds within a 2N x 2N (or 2M x 2M) unitary to within a scaling factor.
- [P] Balanced loss, equal on every path, preserves the target interference to within an overall scaling factor; unbalanced loss distorts it, and the symmetric design tolerates it better than Reck's in 500 random unitaries per size.
