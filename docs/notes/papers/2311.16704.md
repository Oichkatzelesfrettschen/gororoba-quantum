## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): over a composition algebra `A`, a polynomial `f(x)` in `A[x]` has a root `alpha` if and only if `f(x) = g(x) (x - alpha)` for some `g(x)` in `A[x]`; the paper examines whether this holds for general Cayley-Dickson algebras and concludes that it does when `f(x)` is linear or monic quadratic and fails in general; it studies the connections between `f` and its companion `C_f(x) = f(x) conj(f(x))` and computes the left eigenvalues of `2 x 2` octonion matrices.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): an algebra is unital and not necessarily commutative or associative; for an algebra `A` with involution `tau` over a field `F` and `gamma` in `F^x`, the Cayley-Dickson double `B = A{gamma}` is `A + A l` with product `(q + r l)(s + t l) = q s + gamma conj(t) r + (t q + r conj(s)) l`; a Cayley-Dickson algebra is obtained by repeating the doubling starting from a quadratic separable extension `K` of `F`, written `K{gamma_2, ..., gamma_m}`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper's doubling `(q + r l)(s + t l) = q s + gamma conj(t) r + (t q + r conj(s)) l` coincides, for `gamma = -1` and `(a, b, c, d) = (q, r, s, t)`, with the repository's doubling `(a, b)(c, d) = (ac - conj(d) b, da + b conj(c))` (checked by comparing the two product formulas term by term), so statements about the algebras `F{-1, ..., -1}` of this paper apply to `cd_core` over the real numbers ([adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md)). The abstract's root criterion for composition algebras (dimension at most 8) and its failure for general Cayley-Dickson algebras mark the same boundary as the zero divisors of the sedenions.
