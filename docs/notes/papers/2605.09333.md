## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): the paper studies the interplay among the Coxeter-Dickson `E_8`-order, the para-octonions and the real Okubo algebra. It explains why a usual rotation presentation of the order-three automorphism, though algebraically correct, does not preserve the classical integral orders; it introduces an integral order-three automorphism and proves that its Petersson isotope is the compact real Okubo algebra; the four octonionic orders with lattices `C_8, A_2^4, D_4^2` and `E_8` are preserved by the new realization of the Okubo product; and the same Coxeter-Dickson `E_8` lattice supports octonionic, para-octonionic and Okubo integral structures whose multiplicative systems are not isomorphic.
- [P] Introduction (p. 1): the culmination of the classical line from Gauss and Hurwitz is the Coxeter-Dickson octonions, the maximal order in the octonion algebra, isometric to the exceptional lattice `E_8`. Among the eight-dimensional real composition algebras (Generalised Hurwitz Theorem) there are three non-isomorphic division cases: the unital octonions, the para-octonions (para-unital) and the non-unital real Okubo algebra, whose automorphism group is `SU(3)` rather than `G_2`.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The paper concerns eight-dimensional integral structures. The repository's `cd_core` algebra at dimension 8 is the octonions with signed basis products `e_p e_q = s(p, q) e_(p xor q)`, whose Z-span of the basis is an integral order; the relation of that order to the Coxeter-Dickson order is not stated on the pages read, and the Okubo and para-octonion products are not Cayley-Dickson doublings ([adjacent-algebras survey](../packages/adjacent-algebras.md)). The octonions are the largest level of the Cayley-Dickson tower that is a division algebra, so the paper's lattice and order statements apply at dimension 8 and below, and not at the sedenions, which carry the zero divisors the repository studies.
