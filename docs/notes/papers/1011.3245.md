## Statements read

- [P] Abstract (p. 1): identical photons sent through a linear-optical network and measured by photon counting define a sampling model whose exact classical simulation would collapse the polynomial hierarchy; the approximate case needs the Permanent-of-Gaussians and Permanent Anti-Concentration conjectures, and the paper develops the theory of noninteracting bosons and its link to the permanent in a self-contained way.
