## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Distinct quantum-superposed photon states are prepared in a superconducting microwave resonator, and their decay is described quantitatively by two channels only, energy relaxation and dephasing.
- [P] Preparing specific initial states lets the experiment follow specific density-matrix elements in time, off-diagonal ones included, and compare each with theory.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The precedent for measuring how selected coherences evolve, rather than terminal populations alone. The gate-level counterpart on IBM hardware is the selected-coherence estimator of PR #79 (`C = (S_XX + S_YY + i(S_XY - S_YX)) / 4` per context), which reads one off-diagonal element of the work pair at matched exposures.
- [I] The resonator and its Wigner tomography have no counterpart in the cloud gate set; the transfer is the measurement principle, not the apparatus.
