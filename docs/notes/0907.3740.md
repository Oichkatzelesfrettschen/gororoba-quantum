## Main claims and evidence

- [P] Empirical Bernstein bounds give data-dependent, variance-sensitive confidence bounds for bounded variables, with improved constants, and extend uniformly over function classes with polynomial growth.
- [P] Sample variance penalization uses those bounds; under stated conditions its excess risk reaches order 1/n against 1/sqrt(n) for empirical risk minimization.
