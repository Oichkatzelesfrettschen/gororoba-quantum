## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A generalized stabilizer code and unitary protocol on a superconducting processor create projective non-Abelian Ising anyons at graph vertices and braid them.
- [P] The experiment verifies the anyons' fusion rules, realizes their braiding statistics, and uses braiding to prepare an entangled state of anyons encoding three logical qubits.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The superconducting counterpart of [2305.03766](2305.03766.md): its evidential standard is a specified state, fusion rule, braid and measurement. Braid operations are noncommutative and associative matrices, so the work distinguishes non-Abelian from nonassociative rather than linking them.
