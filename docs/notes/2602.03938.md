[P] The paper adapts the error-generator formalism to quantum instruments
and separates physically interpretable error components. The abstract names
amplitude damping, readout error and imperfect collapse, examines their
dependence on readout pulse amplitude, and reports a reduced model with a
few parameters. Figure 1 explicitly uses a linearized regime.

[I] Reduced instrument models give a disciplined candidate hierarchy for the
repository's residual: classical outcome error, state disturbance and coherent
components. Fitting extra parameters to the same data does not establish
mechanism or predictive improvement. Hold-out predictions and independently
validated interventions decide whether a model improves compilation.
Small-error/Markovian premises and parameter identifiability require checks;
the paper supplies no Heron-specific attribution or granted pulse access.
