## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A graded view of the Cayley-Dickson process defines associativity in three classes and splits the non-associative parts into four types.
- [P] The graded notation derives the eight octonion and seven power-associative subalgebras of the sedenions; sedenion zero divisors occur in multiples of 84 and reduce to seven primary pairs.
- [P] Table 5 (page 13) lists, from the octonions O through U7 (D = 8 to 1024), the quaternion-like counts H 7, 35, 155, 651, 2667, 10795, 43435, 174251, the octonion counts O 1, 8, 50, 310, 2046, 14478, 107950, 831470, and the quasi-octonion counts P4, P12, P14 with totals 0, 7, 105, 1085, 9765, 82677, 680085, 5516245.
- [P] Theorem 8 states S_{m+1} = 7 (O_m + S_m) for the quasi-octonion total S; its proof observes the pattern in Table 5 and argues by induction from the triad identity C(N_m, 3) = H_m + 28 (O_m + S_m), N_m = 2^(m+3) - 1.
- [E] The closed forms O(D) = (D-2)(D-4)(D+48)/1344, Q(D) = (D-2)(D-4)(D-8)/192 and H(D) = (D-1)(D-2)/6 reproduce every Table 5 column, and Theorem 3 of [the census note](topics/cd_associator_defect_census.md) gives a proof of Theorem 8 from the doubling lifting identity.
