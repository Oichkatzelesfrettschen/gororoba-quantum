## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A graded view of the Cayley-Dickson process defines associativity in three classes and splits the non-associative parts into four types.
- [P] The graded notation derives the eight octonion and seven power-associative subalgebras of the sedenions; sedenion zero divisors occur in multiples of 84 and reduce to seven primary pairs.
