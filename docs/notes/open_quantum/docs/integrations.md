# Integrations

Open Quantum is built to live inside the tools developers already use. Any platform can integrate Open Quantum and give its users free and subsidized access to real QPUs.

## Secure and Simple

Account linking uses standard **OAuth 2.0** authorization. Once an account is linked:

- Your users pick an Open Quantum backend and run circuits directly from your platform.
- Open Quantum handles transpilation, routing, and result formatting.
- Jobs are billed to each user's Open Quantum credits, so you never touch quantum billing. Every Open Quantum account starts with **\$50 of free Spark credits**.
