# Your Account

Manage your profile, credits, and settings from the portal:

- **[Dashboard](https://www.openquantum.com/dashboard)** -- Credit balance, recent jobs, and organization overview
- **[Settings](https://www.openquantum.com/settings)** -- Profile, billing, SDK keys, referral, and organization settings

## Account Settings

Your dashboard and settings pages show:

- **Email** -- The email address associated with your account (managed via your identity provider).
- **Organization ID** -- Your default organization UUID (used when configuring the SDK).
- **Credit balance** -- Your current available credits and credit history (see [Billing](https://www.openquantum.com/settings/billing)).

## Changing Your Password

Password changes are managed through your identity provider. If you've forgotten your password, use the reset link on the sign-in page.

## Referral Program

Each organization is tied to a unique referral link. Share yours from [Settings → Referral](https://www.openquantum.com/settings/referral) to earn free credits when others sign up.

## Deleting Your Account

To delete your account, contact us at <support@openquantum.com>. Account deletion is permanent and removes all associated data including job history and SDK keys.
