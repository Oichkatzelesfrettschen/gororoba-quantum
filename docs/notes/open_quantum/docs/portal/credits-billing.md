# Credits & Billing

Open Quantum uses a credit system for job execution. You can view your balance, purchase credits, and review transaction history from the portal.

## Checking Your Balance

Your current credit balance is visible on the [portal dashboard](https://www.openquantum.com). Each organization has its own credit balance.

## Free Credits

New accounts receive free **Spark credits** — no credit card required. These credits let you run circuits on real QPUs to evaluate the platform.

You can earn additional free credits through:

- **Referrals** -- Share your referral link from the portal. You earn bonus credits each time someone signs up.

## Purchasing Credits

To purchase additional credits:

1.  Go to the credits page in the portal.
2.  Select a credit package.
3.  Complete the purchase.

Credits are added to your organization's balance immediately.

## Transaction History

The portal shows a complete history of credit transactions for your organization:

- **Purchases** -- Credits bought through the portal.
- **Job charges** -- Credits consumed by job submissions.
- **Refunds** -- Automatic refunds for failed or canceled jobs.
- **Bonuses** -- Referral credits and promotional grants.

## How Pricing Works

Job cost depends on:

- **Backend** -- Different QPUs have different per-shot rates.
- **Shot count** -- More shots cost more credits.
- **Execution plan** -- Public Compute (subsidized) vs Private Compute (dedicated).
- **Queue priority** -- Standard or priority queue placement.

The SDK shows a cost quote before submitting. In the portal, the cost is displayed during job creation.

For full pricing details, visit the [Open Quantum pricing page](https://www.openquantum.com/pricing).
