## About Garnet

Garnet is IQM’s 20-qubit superconducting quantum computer built on a square lattice with tunable couplers and 99.51% median CZ fidelity, suited to cloud research and algorithm development. Run circuits on Garnet through the Open Quantum platform with \$50 of free quantum compute on signup.
