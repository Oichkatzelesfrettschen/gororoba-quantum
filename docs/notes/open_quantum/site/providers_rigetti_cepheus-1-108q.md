## About Cepheus-1-108Q

Cepheus-1-108Q is Rigetti’s 108-qubit superconducting quantum computer built on a square lattice with 4-fold nearest-neighbor connectivity and 99.1% CZ fidelity, designed for production workloads and hybrid algorithms. Run circuits on Cepheus-1 through the Open Quantum platform with \$50 of free quantum compute on signup.
