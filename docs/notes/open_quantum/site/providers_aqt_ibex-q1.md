## About IBEX Q1

IBEX Q1 is AQT’s room-temperature, rack-mounted trapped-ion quantum computer with 12 fully-connected qubits, built for HPC integration and enterprise proof-of-concept work. Run circuits on IBEX Q1 through the Open Quantum platform with \$50 of free quantum compute on signup.
