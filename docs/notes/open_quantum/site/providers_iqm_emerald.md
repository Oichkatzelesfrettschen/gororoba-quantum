## About Emerald

Emerald is IQM’s 54-qubit superconducting quantum computer built on a square lattice with tunable couplers and 99.5% median CZ fidelity, aimed at production workloads and error-correction research. Run circuits on Emerald through the Open Quantum platform with \$50 of free quantum compute on signup.
