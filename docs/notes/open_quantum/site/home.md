## Two Ways to Run

Same quantum hardware, same API. Choose the execution path that fits your needs.

Public Compute

Half the price. Same hardware.

Public Compute routes your jobs through qBitTensor Labs and a decentralized network, delivering the same QPU results at half the cost of Private Compute. Start free with \$50 of quantum compute, claimable every 90 days.

- **Real QPUs** — IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, AQT, and more through one API.
- **Free to start** — Claim Spark Credits from your dashboard. No credit card needed.
- **Data is anonymized** — Your circuits are routed through the network with privacy protections.

Private Compute

Full privacy. Direct access.

Private Compute runs your jobs on infrastructure managed by Quantum Rings, with full data privacy and priority access. Built for production workloads and sensitive IP.

- **Fully private** — Your circuits and results stay between you and the hardware.
- **Priority access** — Results are returned faster and queue lengths are shorter.
- **Pay with Full Credits** — Purchase credits as needed, valid for one year.
