## About Forte-1

Forte-1 is IonQ’s trapped-ion quantum computer with 36 algorithmic qubits, 99.6% two-qubit gate fidelity, and full all-to-all connectivity, so any qubit can interact with any other without routing overhead. Run circuits on Forte-1 through the Open Quantum platform with \$50 of free quantum compute on signup.
