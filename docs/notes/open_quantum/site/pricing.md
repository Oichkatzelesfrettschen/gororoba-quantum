## How Pricing Works

Pricing is determined by the QPU you select, the parameters of your job, and whether you choose Public or Private Compute. Public Compute is priced at **half the cost** of Private Compute across all backends.

Costs are calculated using a combination of the QPU's base cost and the number of shots. You can pay to jump the queue on Public Compute. Private Compute runs on shorter, dedicated queues. All prices shown in USD equivalent are for reference; actual deduction uses your Spark or Full Credits.
