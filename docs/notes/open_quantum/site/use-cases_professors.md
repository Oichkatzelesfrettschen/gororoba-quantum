## Why Educators Choose Open Quantum

### Your Whole Class, Funded

If we can verify it is a real course, you are in: \$50 of quantum compute per student, through a single class code. No committees, no waiting on a decision. We just onboard you.

### Minutes, Not Hours

Queue times are regularly under a minute, versus hours on other free tiers, so students iterate on real hardware within a single lab session.

### Your Curriculum Just Works

Native Qiskit and PennyLane plugins, or raw OpenQASM straight from the browser. Whatever you already teach runs unchanged.

### Four QPU Vendors, One Login

QPUs from IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, and AQT through a single account. No vendor accounts, no procurement, no setup scripts.

### \$50 Free, Every 90 Days

Beyond your class grant, every student account refreshes with \$50 of free quantum compute every 90 days. No credit card, ever.

### A Real Developer Platform

Web console, job history, SDK keys, and a REST API. Students learn on the same tools they will use in industry.

## How It Works for Your Class

- Tell us about your course and enrollment in a two-minute form, using your institution email. It is onboarding, not an application.
- We confirm it is a real course from your institution email and public course page, then provision your class code and fund \$50 of quantum compute per student, typically within one business day.
- Students join through your class link and write circuits in Qiskit or PennyLane, or upload OpenQASM files directly through the web.
- Jobs run on real hardware from IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, and AQT through one unified API, with results usually back in under a minute.
- Building a course? Use any content from Quantum 101, our free 14-lesson curriculum, and we will help you design labs around real QPU access.
