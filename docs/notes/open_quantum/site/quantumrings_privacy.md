## 10. Quantum Execution Data

When you execute quantum jobs (i.e., run a quantum circuit and obtain results) through our Services, the handling of your quantum circuits and results depends on the execution method:

- **Local SDK Simulation:** If you use the Quantum Rings SDK for local simulation, your quantum circuits and results remain on your device. We collect only metadata about the job, such as performance metrics, but do not access or store your circuits or results.

&nbsp;

- **Hosted Simulation or Quantum Computer Execution**: When we host simulations or execute circuits on quantum hardware for you, your circuits and results are transmitted through our systems. We process this data only as necessary to provide the Service and ensure its performance.

&nbsp;

- **Public Tier (Open Quantum)**: If you use the public tier of the Open Quantum platform, your circuits and results are broadcast to a decentralized network of operators facilitated by our partner, qBitTensor Labs, through the bittensor network. These operators collaborate to execute your circuit and verify results before returning them to you. **Your circuits and results are not confidential in this tier,** and Quantum Rings retains a copy of this data for unlimited use, including research, analytics, product development, or AI training. Do not submit proprietary or sensitive information via this option.

&nbsp;

- **Private Tier (Open Quantum):** If you use the private tier, your circuits are shared only with trusted quantum hardware providers, including but not limited to, qBraid, AWS, Microsoft, IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, Quantinuum, Infleqtion, QuEra, Pasqal, and others, depending on your selected backend. We process these circuits only as needed to complete the execution and do not retain copies afterward, except for temporary processing purposes.
