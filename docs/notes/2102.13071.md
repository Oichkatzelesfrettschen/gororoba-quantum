## Main claims and evidence

- [P] The device is the 7-transmon Surface-7 processor (Fig. 1); the paper does not use a 17-qubit chip.
- [P] Every transmon has an individual microwave drive line, a flux line for two-qubit gates and a dispersively coupled readout resonator with a Purcell filter; all transmons are flux biased at the maximum-frequency sweetspot between gates.
- [P] Gate times are 60 ns for CZ and parking and 540 ns for ancilla readout; T1 spans 27-102 us and T2 spans 55-117 us.
- [P] The logical qubit is stabilized for up to n = 15 error-detection cycles; the total cycle duration is 840 ns (pipelined scheme) or 1000 ns (parallel scheme), so CZ layers interleaved with ancilla readout run repeatedly on this lab's control stack.

## Limits
