## Main claims

[P] The multiplication group Mlt(Q_n) of the Cayley-Dickson loop and its one-sided analogue Mlt_l(Q_n) are split extensions of elementary abelian 2-groups; the inner mapping groups are elementary abelian.

- [P] Setting (p. 1-2). The doubling is (a,b)(c,d) = (ac - d*b, da + bc*). The loop is Q_0 = {1,-1}, Q_n = {(x,0),(x,1) : x in Q_{n-1}} with the four rules (x,0)(y,0) = (xy,0), (x,0)(y,1) = (yx,1), (x,1)(y,0) = (xy*,1), (x,1)(y,1) = (-y*x,0). |Q_n| = 2^(n+1). The canonical generators are i_1, ..., i_n, and e = i_n is the unit added last. Z(Q_n) = {1,-1} for n >= 2 (Lemma 3, item 3), and Q_n/{1,-1} is (Z_2)^n (Theorem 4).
- [P] Commutator and associator (Lemma 3): [x,y] = -1 exactly when <x,y> is the quaternion group H_8, and [x,y,z] = 1 when <x,y,z> <= H_8 and [x,y,z] = -1 when <x,y,z> is the octonion loop O_16, with both values in {1,-1}; the lemma states these as sufficient conditions and is silent on order-16 subloops other than O_16 (p. 3). Lemma 13: [x,y,z] = [z,y,x].
- [P] Definitions (p. 4): Mlt_l(Q) = <L_x>; Inn_l(Q) is the stabilizer of 1 in Mlt_l(Q); L_{x,y} = L_{yx}^(-1) L_y L_x. Theorem 9 (Pflugfelder): Inn_l(Q) = <L_{x,y} : x,y in Q>. Lemma 10: |Mlt_l(Q)| = |Q| |Inn_l(Q)|.
- [P] Equations (4) to (6), the proof of Lemma 14 (p. 6): T_x(z) = [x,z] z, L_{x,y}(z) = [y,x,z] z (equation (5)), R_{x,y}(z) = [z,x,y] z. Each inner mapping acts on every class {z,-z} as the identity or the swap. Corollary 15: L_{x,y} = R_{x,y}.
- [P] Theorem 16: Inn(Q_n) is elementary abelian of order 2^(2^n - 2), each element a product of disjoint transpositions (z,-z). Lemma 12: elements of Mlt(Q_n) are even permutations. Lemma 17: every element of Mlt(Q_n) has order 1, 2, or 4.
- [P] Lemma 23: K = <g_{1,n}, ..., g_{n,n}> with g_{k,n} = (product of T_x over a prescribed set) L_{i_k} is elementary abelian of order 2^n, with exactly one element above each class of Q_n/{1,-1}. Theorem 24 (n >= 2): Mlt(Q_n) is (Inn(Q_n) x Z(Q_n)) semidirect K.
- [P] Lemma 29: Inn_l(Q_n) = Inn_r(Q_n). Theorems 26 and 27: Mlt_l(Q) and Mlt_r(Q) are isomorphic for any inverse property loop, by L_a -> R_(a^-1).
- [P] Lemma 33 and Theorem 34 (p. 19-20): Inn_l(Q_n) is elementary abelian of order 2^(2^(n-1) - 1). The proof exhibits the elements L_{x,e} L_{i_{n-1},e} = (x,-x)(i_{n-1},-i_{n-1})(xe,-xe)(i_{n-1}e,-i_{n-1}e) and h, the product of (z,-z) over every class z with z in Q_n e. The upper bound rests on equation (9), L_{x,y}(z) = [xbar,ybar] L_{x,y}(ze), together with L_{x,y}(1) = 1.
- [P] Theorem 35 (n >= 2, p. 20): Mlt_l(Q_n) is (Inn_l(Q_n) x Z(Q_n)) semidirect K, so Mlt_l(Q_n) is ((Z_2)^(2^(n-1) - 1) x Z_2) semidirect (Z_2)^n. The normal subgroup N = Inn_l(Q_n) Z(Q_n) is the set of f in Mlt_l with f(1) = +1 or -1 (proof, step 1).
- [P] Theorem 8: Aut(Q_n) is Aut(O_16) x (Z_2)^(n-3) of order 1344 * 2^(n-3) for n >= 3. The author ran GAP with the LOOPS package for experiments and states that the final results do not rely on computational proofs.

## Mapping to this repository

Relevance: (a) none, (b) high, (c) none. [I] The paper has no quantum, circuit, Boolean-function, or Reed-Muller content.

Convention map for every bullet below. [I]
- Units: e_m is the loop element (1, eps_1, ..., eps_n) with eps_j = bit j-1 of m, taken with positive sign; i_j = e_(2^(j-1)); e = i_n = e_(2^(n-1)) is the repository's top coordinate xi (bit n-1).
- Product: the branches of `cd_sign` in gororoba_quantum/cd_core.py are the four rules of p. 2, branch for branch. Both low: (x,0)(y,0) = (xy,0). Low then high: (x,0)(y,1) = (yx,1), the rewrite (p,q) -> (q - half, p). High then low: (x,1)(y,0) = (xy*,1), negation when q != 0. Both high: (x,1)(y,1) = (-y*x,0), the early return -1 when q' = 0 and otherwise (q',p'). A scratch implementation of the p. 2 rules, written independently of `cd_sign`, agreed with it on every pair of indices for dimensions 1 through 128; that script is outside the repository and outside every gate.
- Loop versus matrices: Q_n has the 2^(n+1) points +-e_m, and G_n is a group of signed permutation matrices on R^(2^n). Every element of Mlt_l(Q_n) commutes with negation, so its action on the +- basis and its signed matrix are one group, and L_{-1} is -I.
- Left-only: Kirshtein's Mlt includes the R_x; G_n = <L_{e_m}> is Mlt_l(Q_n) alone. For n >= 3, Inn_l(Q_n) is strictly inside Inn(Q_n): 2^(2^(n-1) - 1) against 2^(2^n - 2).
- Schreier vector against inner mapping: the repository's s_{a,b} is the sign diagonal of L_{e_(a^b)}^(-1) L_{e_a} L_{e_b}. With y = e_a and x = e_b, Kirshtein's L_{x,y} = L_{yx}^(-1) L_y L_x, and yx = psi(a,b) e_(a^b), so the two differ by the scalar sign psi(a,b), that is by a factor (-I)^phi(a,b).

Per-claim mapping. Each bullet carries the source locus, the label, and what the source leaves unestablished.

- [I] Group identification. G_n = Mlt_l(Q_n); H_n, the kernel of the translation map, is N = Inn_l(Q_n) x Z(Q_n) = Inn_l x <-I>; the normalized code C_n = H_n cap ker(ev_0) is Inn_l(Q_n) itself, the f with f(1) = 1; H_n = C_n x Z_2 holds as a direct product of groups. Source: Theorem 35, step 1 of its proof for N, Lemma 10 for the order. The orders agree: |Mlt_l(Q_n)| = |Q_n| |Inn_l(Q_n)| = 2^(n+1) * 2^(2^(n-1) - 1) = 2^(n + 2^(n-1)) = |G_n|, and rank C_n = 2^(n-1) - 1 is the exponent of Theorem 34. Label: known theorem reproduced. Not established by the source: any coordinate description of N as functions on F_2^n, any ANF or code-theoretic statement, and the range of the GAP experiments.
- [I] Schreier generation and the associator code. The repository's statement that V_n is spanned by the Schreier sign vectors s_{a,b} is Theorem 9 (left cosets with transversal {L_x}). The handoff identity [omega_{a,b} = -1] = P(s_{a,b}) is equation (5), L_{x,y}(z) = [y,x,z] z, written in basis-sign coordinates: P removes the scalar sign psi(a,b), and the loop associator of positive basis units is the associator sign. Label: known theorem reproduced. Not established by the source: the reading of P as the projection of H_n onto ker(ev_0) with kernel <1>, and the monomial basis of C_n.
- [I] Explicit coordinates for H_n. Kirshtein's generators on p. 20, restated in repository coordinates, give the function space of the repository theorem. L_{x,e} L_{i_(n-1),e} flips the classes x, i_(n-1), xe, and i_(n-1)e, so it is the sign function g(x') = delta_x + delta_(2^(n-2)) on the low n-1 bits, constant in xi. These span the even-weight g with g(0) = 0, of dimension 2^(n-1) - 2. The element h is the indicator of xi = 1. Together they span C_n = {g(x') + c xi : wt(g) even, g(0) = 0} of dimension 2^(n-1) - 1, and adjoining -I gives H_n. Label: known theorem reproduced, in other coordinates. Not established by the source: the statement as a function space, the monomial basis x^S, and every degree statement.
- [I] The repository proof against the printed upper bound. The repository proves the upper containment through the recursion (R), the parity Lemma 2, and the closed form Lemma 3 of literature/notes/left_mult_sign_rank.md. Kirshtein's upper bound (end of the proof of Theorem 34) states that equation (9) makes the upper-half values a function of the lower-half values, so at most 2^(|Q_(n-1)|/2 - 1) = 2^(2^(n-1) - 1) elements exist. The constant [xbar,ybar] in (9) varies with the pair (x,y), so for a product f of inner mappings f(ze) = c_f f(z) with a constant c_f that depends on f. The element h of Lemma 33 has trivial lower half and nontrivial upper half, so the lower half alone does not determine f. The count 2^(2^(n-1) - 1) lower-half patterns times 2 values of c_f would allow a factor 2 more than the theorem; the repository's parity Lemma 2, condition (ii) of the upper bound, removes it by forcing wt(g) even. Lemma 12 (even permutations) imposes no such constraint, because wt(g) + wt(g + c) is even for every g. The theorem is correct for n = 3 to 11 by exhaustive rank (the order of H_n equals 2^(2^(n-1)) in each row of the repository table), so the gap lies in the printed argument and not in the statement. Label: new proof or formalization. Not established by the source: the parity of the lower half as an independent lemma.
- [I] ANF degree and Reed-Muller form. Every element of H_n has ANF degree at most n-2, attained, and H_n = RM(n-2, n-1) on the low bits (+) <x_(n-1)>. Kirshtein states nothing about ANF, Boolean degree, or Reed-Muller codes. The p. 20 generators give the statement once coordinates are fixed (delta_x + delta_a on n-1 variables has degree at most n-2), so the novelty is the coordinate form. No search of the Boolean-function or coding-theory literature was run. Label: candidate new result. Not established by the source: any degree bound.
- [I] Contrast with the full inner group. Inn(Q_n) is every even-weight sign function on the nonidentity classes (Theorem 16, order 2^(2^n - 2)); delta_x + delta_y on n variables has degree n-1, so the full inner group reaches degree n-1 and the left-only subgroup stops at n-2. Consequence for DESIGN_NOTES, "Phase degree along the ladder": the sign function f_m of L_{e_m} has degree n-1, and L_{e_m} h for h in H_n has sign function f_m + h of the same degree n-1. The degree-3 sign of a sedenion left multiplication is therefore constant on its coset L_{e_m} H_n; no element of G_4 with translation m has a Clifford sign diagonal. Label: candidate new result. Not established by the source: any statement about the sign function f_m or phase synthesis.
- [I] Exponent and commutation lemmas. The repository's Lemma 1 (distinct nonzero basis units anticommute, every other pair commutes) is Lemma 3, item 1; the exponent statement of `verify.check_left_mult_group` (every element has order dividing 4) is Lemma 17. Label: known theorem reproduced. Not established by the source: the closed form C_n(c,y) = N(c) + delta_0(y) + delta_c(y).
- [I] Computation and mutation gates. `scripts/left_mult_sign_rank.py` checks the statement through n = 11 and catches both single-branch mutations of the sign recursion at n = 2. Kirshtein used GAP and LOOPS for small experiments and does not state the dimensions. Label: new implementation or measurement. Not established by the source: any value of n for which the order was computed.
- [I] The n = 2 exception. Q_2 is the group H_8, and the left translations of a group act regularly, so Mlt_l(Q_2) = Q_2 of order 8 and Inn_l(Q_2) = 1 (Lemma 10: |Mlt_l| = 8 |Inn_l|). The repository records G_2 = Q8 of order 2^3 and C_2 = 0. Label: known theorem reproduced. Not established by the source: the case n = 2 of its own theorems, see the next bullet.
- [I] Range of Theorems 34 and 35 as printed. Theorem 34 carries no restriction on n and gives 2^(2^1 - 1) = 2 at n = 2, and Theorem 35 is stated for n >= 2 and gives order 2 * 2 * 4 = 16 there. Both disagree with order 8 and with Inn_l(Q_2) = 1. The failing step in Theorem 35 is "K <= Mlt_l(Q_n)": for n = 2 the element g_{1,2} = T_{i_2} L_{i_1} would equal L_{-i_1} on 1, which forces T_{i_2} = L_{-i_1} L_{i_1}^(-1) = -I, and T_{i_2} is the identity on +-{1, i_2} and -1 elsewhere. Theorem 24 for the full Mlt(Q_2) is consistent (order 32). From n = 3 the printed orders agree with the repository's table (log2 |G_3| = 7 through log2 |G_11| = 1035). Label: candidate new result. Not established by the source: the n = 2 case; the observation has not been compared with a published erratum.

## Gaps and cautions

- [P] Several proofs cite the author's earlier paper [9] (automorphism groups) and thesis [8] (an external semidirect product model of Mlt(Q_n)); neither was read.
- [I] The comparison of cd_sign with the p. 2 rules covers dimensions up to 128. The correspondence of canonical-generator exponents with index bits follows from the recursive definition of Q_n and the standard-basis recursion; it is not separately proved in the paper.
- [I] The gap in the upper bound of Theorem 34 is a reading of the printed argument, falsifiable by an element of Inn_l(Q_n) whose lower half has odd weight; the exhaustive ranks through n = 11 show none exists.
