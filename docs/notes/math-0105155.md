## Main claims and evidence

- [P] The real numbers, complex numbers, quaternions and octonions are exactly the four normed division algebras; the octonions are the largest and are nonassociative.
- [P] The survey relates the octonions to Clifford algebras and spinors, Bott periodicity, projective and Lorentzian geometry, Jordan algebras and the exceptional Lie groups.
