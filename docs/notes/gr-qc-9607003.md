## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A simpler method yields uncertainty-principle-type bounds on the magnitude and duration of negative energy density seen by an inertial observer for the free massless scalar field in four-dimensional Minkowski spacetime.
- [P] Similar quantum-inequality bounds are derived for the electromagnetic field and for the massive scalar field in two and four dimensions.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] Free-field Minkowski bounds do not transfer unchanged to a Casimir cavity: a boundary geometry needs its own difference-type inequality and complete renormalized stress tensor before negative local energy is read as a usable resource.
