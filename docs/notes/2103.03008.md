[P] A quantum instrument consists of outcome-indexed completely positive
maps `Q_i` whose sum is trace preserving. It produces both a classical
outcome and a conditional continuing state:
`p_i=tr Q_i(rho)`, `rho_i=Q_i(rho)/p_i` when `p_i>0`.
QILGST characterizes these maps together with gate, preparation and terminal
measurement models. The abstract reports a dispersive transmon experiment
that varies delay after measurement to investigate residual cavity photons.

[I] A terminal confusion matrix cannot represent the conditional-state
disturbance. The repository therefore retains both bits, delays and physical
writers when testing a folded-QPE model. The source supplies a model class,
not a measurement of residual photons on Heron. A small matched pilot can
discriminate selected predictions without performing complete QILGST.
