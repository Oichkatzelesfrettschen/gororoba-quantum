## Main claims

[P] A 100-qubit adaptive circuit with about 5000 two-qubit gates and 5000 mid-circuit reset operations runs on ibm_fez without error mitigation and still reproduces the universal critical behavior of a chaos-to-control transition.

- [P] Model: the adaptive Bernoulli circuit. At each step, with probability 1 - p a two-body scrambling gate acts on qubits i, i+1 and the site moves right; with probability p a single-qubit control resets qubit i to |0> (measurement, then X if the outcome is 1) and the site moves left. The classical limit is the Bernoulli map a -> 2a mod 1 in competition with a -> a/2.
- [P] Phases: volume-law entanglement for p below about 0.3 (measurement-induced transition at p_ent about 0.3), area-law chaotic phase up to p = 0.5, unentangled controlled phase above 0.5. The Lyapunov exponent is (1 - 2p) log 2, so it changes sign at p = 0.5.
- [P] Hardware: ibm_fez, Heron r2, 156 fixed-frequency transmons. Each run used 100 linearly connected qubits chosen from device parameters to maximize expected circuit fidelity.
- [P] Device figures on the data days (Fig. 5 caption): mean T1 1.4e2 us, mean T2 1.0e2 us; single-qubit gate 24 ns, two-qubit gate 84 ns, readout 1.6e3 ns; mean errors 3.0e-4 (single-qubit), 1.8e-2 (two-qubit), 1.3e-2 (readout).
- [P] Scale: L = 10 to 100 in steps of 10, p = 0.2 to 0.85 in steps of 0.05, 50 circuit realizations per (L, p). The two-qubit gate count peaks near 8000 at p = 0.2, L = 100; resets peak near 5000 at p = 0.5, L = 100, where the circuit also runs longest because reset takes substantially longer than a two-qubit gate. Consecutive resets are merged, which the supplement ties to transpilation (without it, resets would grow linearly with p).
- [P] No error mitigation was applied to the experimental results of Fig. 2.
- [P] Results (experiment / matrix product state / statistical mechanics model):

  | Quantity | Experiment | MPS (L = 10 to 40) | Stat mech |
  |----------|------------|--------------------|-----------|
  | magnetization p_c | 0.495(1) | 0.4947(5) caption, 0.4950(5) text | 0.4970(5) |
  | magnetization nu | 1.05(2) | 1.05(2) | 1.0000(2) |
  | quantum-fluctuation variance p_c | 0.509(2) | 0.500(6) | 0.5010(1) |

- [P] Quantumness test: the distribution of shot-to-shot fluctuations (Delta M_z)^2 for fixed circuits agrees better with the fully quantum MPS result than with a classical fully dephased Markov process (rho -> diag(rho)), measured by Kullback-Leibler divergence, for p below 0.5. For p above 0.5 readout noise dominates and no model fits; the zero-variance peak is absent in the data.
- [P] Noise robustness (supplement): in a statistical-mechanics model, depolarizing noise of 0.01 (single-qubit) and 0.001 (two-qubit) shifts p_c to 0.498(1) with nu = 1.000(2); 0.3 and 0.03 shift it only to 0.504(3). The exponents survive the noise.
- [P] Classical cost: MPS cost scales as O(L^3.15); the bond dimension hits the 512 cap in the volume-law phase and for individual realizations in the area-law phase. One trajectory to 2 L^2 steps at p = 0.5 takes 1.7 s (L = 10) to 103 s (L = 40) on an AMD EPYC 9654.

## Methods

[P] The scrambler is a shallow random circuit, and every observable comes from raw shots.

- [P] Scrambling gate: B = [U1 x U2] CZ [U3 x U4] with U(theta, vartheta, Theta) = R_x(theta) R_z(vartheta) R_x(Theta), angles uniform on [0, 2 pi) (Eq. 2-3). Frame potential about 2.14 against 2 for Haar. The CZ is omitted at the chain end.
- [P] Control: mid-circuit measurement followed by a conditional X (reset).
- [P] Steady state reached after L^2/2 steps in the main text and L^2 steps in the supplement (listed under inconsistencies).
- [P] Observables: magnetization M_z = (1/L) sum Z_i averaged over initial states, measurement records and circuits; quantum fluctuation var_Q[M_z] = var over circuits of the within-circuit shot variance (Eq. 4-5), with the total variance split into circuit and quantum parts.
- [P] Finite-size scaling collapse O ~ L^(-beta/nu) f[(p - p_c) L^(1/nu)], fit by Levenberg-Marquardt (lmfit) with reduced chi-squared loss, fit range p in [0.35, 0.65], L in [10, 100] for the magnetization.
- [P] Shots: 10^4 per realization for Fig. 2 and 1000 per realization for the Fig. 4 fluctuation distributions in the main text; 1000 per realization in the supplement.
- [P] Comparators: noiseless MPS with ITensors.jl using a folded layout for periodic coupling, truncation error 1e-10 (1e-15 for bond-dimension studies); a replica-trick statistical-mechanics model for first and second moments; the dephasing Markov model.
- [P] Kullback-Leibler divergence is computed with a mixture of a delta function at zero and a kernel density estimate, error bars by bootstrap (100 resamples).

## Relevance

(a) Calibration-aware or noise-adaptive compilation on IBM hardware: medium. [I]
- [P] Qubit selection used T1, T2, readout error, single-qubit error and two-qubit error of the 156-qubit device to pick a 100-qubit chain. The supplement shows the chosen chain on the device map beside per-qubit histograms.
- [P] Fig. 2 uses no error mitigation on ibm_fez at about 1e4 operations over 100 qubits.
- [I] Against the MPS values in the results table, the experimental critical points and exponents differ by 0% to 4%, the largest gaps being beta 1.05 against 1.01 and z 2.08 against 2.001.
- [I] The selection formula is not given, so the technique transfers as a principle and not as code. The dephasing-null comparison transfers as a method for certifying that a hardware signal is quantum.

(b) Cayley-Dickson, octonion, zero-divisor, nonassociative structure: none. [I]
- [P] No occurrence of the terms in the text (grep).

(c) Shor's algorithm and factoring resource estimates: low. [I]
- [P] The paper presents mid-circuit measurement with feedback at 100 qubits as a step toward fault tolerance.
- [I] Against the assumptions of note 2505.15917: readout takes 1.6 us against an assumed 1 us surface-code cycle; the mean two-qubit error 1.8e-2 is a mean over the chosen chain with a heavy tail (the Fig. 5 two-qubit error color scale runs from 5e-3 to 2e-1) and sits beside the 1.95e-3 to 3.37e-3 medians of note 2507.08088; the largest circuit holds about 1e4 operations against 6.5e9 Toffolis, a factor of about 650,000. The paper reports no feedforward latency, so the 10 us reaction-time assumption cannot be compared.

(d) Concrete action items for gororoba-quantum:

## In-paper inconsistencies and gaps

- [P] Shots per realization: main text says 10^4 for Fig. 2 and 1000 for Fig. 4; the supplement says 1000 for every realization.
- [P] Steady-state time: L^2/2 steps in the main text, L^2 steps in the supplement.
- [P] The matrix-product-state magnetization critical point reads 0.4947(5) in the Fig. 2 caption and 0.4950(5) in the text.
- [P] The abstract quotes nearly 5000 entangling gates; the supplement gives a maximum near 8000 at p = 0.2. The 5000 figure belongs to p = 0.5.
- [P] The two-qubit error of 1.8e-2 is a mean over the selected chain on the data days; note 2507.08088 reports medians on other days and devices. The statistics differ and cannot be compared one-to-one.
- [P] The qubit-selection formula and the transpiler options (optimization level, twirling, dynamical decoupling) are not stated.
