## Main claims and evidence

- [P] The light-shining-through-a-wall campaign (February to May 2024) found no evidence for axion-like particles.
- [P] For pseudoscalars below about 0.1 meV the di-photon coupling limit is 1.5e-9 1/GeV at 95% confidence, more than a factor 20 beyond earlier similar experiments.
- [P] Limits are also given for scalar, vector and tensor bosons, and the campaign demonstrated stable operation and calibration.
