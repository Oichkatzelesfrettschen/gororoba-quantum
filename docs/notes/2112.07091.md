## Mechanism and evidence

- [P] `palloq` combines circuit selection and calibration-aware physical placement
  for several programs on one processor (Sections I and IV). The source points
  to https://github.com/rum-yasuhiro/palloq; repository contents were not audited.
- [P] Section III-B introduces a physical buffer: idle qubits separating the
  occupied regions of simultaneous circuits. The Toffoli study varies buffer
  distance and circuit length. The authors report a loss for adjacent circuits
  and recovery with separation in that experiment.
- [P] Section IV constructs hardware and program graphs weighted by two-qubit
  reliability and gate count respectively. Placement greedily matches important
  program edges to reliable hardware edges, then reserves surrounding qubits.
  Algorithm 1 sorts the circuit queue by CX depth and removes the occupied
  qubits and their distance-d neighbors from further allocation.
- [P] Section III-C compares individual and simultaneous randomized benchmarking
  to summarize changes in gate-error means and variation across the processor.
  The proposed aggregate metric concerns suitability for multiprogramming;
  Section III explicitly sets aside localization of each crosstalk source.
- [P] Section V runs a queue of 100 small benchmark circuits with buffer values
  zero through three on three 27-qubit and two 65-qubit IBM devices. The paper
  evaluates successful-trial probability, circuit duration, and hardware usage.
- [P] Section V-B reports approximately 80 percent utilization at buffer zero,
  roughly half that utilization at buffer two or three, and a twofold increase
  in total circuit duration from the densest to sparsest placements. The same
  paragraph contains an inconsistent earlier sentence saying duration reduces;
  the directional claim here follows its explicit final comparison.
- [I] The duration measure sums circuit durations. The paper supplies neither
  present-day Runtime billing evidence nor a guarantee about charged seconds.
  Its device generation and CX workloads also differ from Heron CZ diagnostics.

[I] Diagnostic applicability is high: companion placement and schedule controls
can support phase-estimation diagnostics and acquisition of defined Walsh
endpoints. That experimental use is separate from the paper's absent direct
Cayley-Dickson contribution and low relevance to factoring resource estimates.

- [I] `packing.candidate_replicas` enumerates embeddings of an already compiled
  pattern; `live_run.allocate_fusion_replicas` searches joint placements for
  compatible groups. palloq supplies prior art for workload-aware placement and
  physical buffers. The compiled-pattern constraint and the particular joint
  search remain implementation choices to evaluate, rather than proof of novelty.
- [I] The repository cross-group physical-CZ buffer implements a form of the
  paper's spacing mechanism. Generic nonadjacency and idle-qubit buffers belong
  to prior art. Treat graph choice, faulty-edge handling, policy recording, and
  correctness of the repository implementation as specific engineering surfaces.
- [I] `execution_pubs` composes corresponding entries into one circuit with
  identifiable registers. Several separately submitted PUBs establish a work
  queue; physical concurrence requires composed circuits or an independently
  verified provider scheduling mechanism.
- [I] A fixed diagnostic with varied companion activity can turn the packing
  problem into an intervention study. palloq alone does not isolate whether a
  changed endpoint comes from patch quality, coherent interference, readout,
  scheduling, or drift. See the [joint design synthesis](quantum_experiment_parallelism.md).

## Prior art and action gates

1. Cite palloq alongside [QuMC](2102.05321.md) for multiprogramming, calibration
   placement, and physical separation. Cite [CQCS](2109.01714.md) for replicas.
2. Evaluate occupied qubits, scheduled duration, charged usage, and endpoint
   uncertainty separately. An allocation wins when a declared scientific target
   is achieved at lower measured cost, subject to its fidelity constraint.
3. Keep the target diagnostic on a fixed patch while changing companion activity
   for the first causal comparison. Balance later patch rotations and execution
   periods; retain arm-by-patch interactions and recalculated timing controls.
4. Preserve shot order, register attribution, calibration identity, and actual
   schedule information. A physical buffer reduces a proposed mechanism; the
   buffer alone does not certify independence or remove shared-controller effects.
5. Require a submit-free compiled plan and the existing written QPU approval case
   before hardware execution. Reusing the paper's benchmarking protocol is an
   option to justify against the budget, rather than a mandatory extra workload.

[I] Candidate contribution: a controlled, auditable reduction in charged time
for specified diagnostic endpoints, or a supported explanation of their
model-hardware residuals. Basic concurrent execution already has prior art.
