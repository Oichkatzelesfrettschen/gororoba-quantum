## Relevance

[P] The paper develops emanation tables and higher-dimensional zero-product
patterns, proposing fractal-like organization of box-kite structures. [I]
The emanation-table formalism is the historical counterpart to the
32-dimensional assessor census (emanation-table cell counts vs. the
verified 1260-edge census); the two enumerate different vertex universes
and are not competing answers. [I] The fractal and meta-fractal claims are
hypotheses requiring independent models, not established results.
