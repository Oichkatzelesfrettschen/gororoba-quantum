## Main claims and evidence

- [P] eQASM specifies timing explicitly: waiting instructions (QWAIT, QWAITR) generate timing points on a timeline and operations attach to those points.
- [P] The architecture supports comprehensive flow control with feedback and is executed on a quantum control microarchitecture.
- [P] The paper motivates deterministic timing for repeatability of quantum experiments.

## Limits

- [P/I] An instruction-set paper; it states no repetition period, shot scheduling or host-side latency for any deployed machine.
- [P/I] Whether Quantum Inspire hardware runs eQASM on this controller generation is not stated here; the cQASM-to-hardware path is documented in the Quantum Inspire pages the repository retains.
