## Relevance

[P] The paper gives bit-string construction rules for higher-dimensional
zero-divisor configurations and their recursive organization. [I] These
recursive XOR rules suggest which discrete invariants might persist through
successive Cayley-Dickson doublings, informing the higher-dimensional
operator-span rank investigation. [I] They do not establish the observed
ranks (1, 22, 127, 592, 2545) or the R(D) formula, which concern a different
operator family and remain conjectural.
