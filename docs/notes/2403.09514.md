## Main claims and evidence

- [P] A measured-QFT replaces O(n^2) two-qubit gates with O(n) mid-circuit measurements and classical feed-forward, without connectivity constraints.
- [P] IBM hardware reaches certified process fidelity above 50 percent on up to 16 qubits and above 1 percent on up to 37 qubits.
- [P] Feed-forward-compensated dynamical decoupling (FC-DD) suppresses errors on idle qubits during mid-circuit measurement and feed-forward.

## Mapping to this repository

- [I] The N=21 orbit window is the measurement and feed-forward interval FC-DD targets; the window_echo arm is a hand-placed echo of that kind, since IBM rejects automatic DD on dynamic circuits.
- [I] Echo placement must respect the measurement and dispatch boundaries of the returned schedule, which the patch characterization records.
- [I] The staged plan ([patch_window_staged_plan.md](patch_window_staged_plan.md)) records where the source enters the design.
