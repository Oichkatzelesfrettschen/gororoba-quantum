## 1. Findings, ranked by consequence

### F1. A5-542: the 11-block distribution has an unverified per-block honesty assumption (hypothesis)

The counting formula `minimal_new = Q1 - rankE` per bidegree block (deg1.tmpl, emitted by gen_blocks.py) is dimension-theoretically sound: the box total `sum(Q1) - sum(rankE) = dim K/(M+mK)` is a rank identity and is ordering-independent. But the *per-bidegree split* relies on the standard count Q1 against the module `s1M = std(M + m*K)` being compatible with the (tw,t3) bigrading, and the Singular ordering `wp` sees only tw. The 90 `Kg` generators from `modulo(H1,H2)` are tw-homogeneous but nothing retained checks t3-homogeneity; a t3-smeared initial ideal would misattribute counts between blocks at fixed tw while leaving the total 32 intact.

Counter-evidence (computed, from the 8 retained deg1 logs): the analogous property for `sK` is cross-checked per block (`badk = QK - rankE` counters read 0 in all 8 units), and the identical distribution across 2 primes × 4 s2-regimes would require a structural smear.
Decisive cheap test: log the (tw,t3) bidegrees of the 90 Kg generators (one-line addition to deg1.tmpl); a t3-inhomogeneous generator would expose the smear.

Consequence if it fires: the *distribution* 2,1,6,6,1,3,5,3,2,2,1 moves; the total 32 stays.
