# Tuna randomized-block draft: independent feasibility feedback

A read-only builder review assessed the initial randomized-block draft and returned a
hold for a concrete acquisition case. The review was an early design check, not the
requested final high-effort Codex review of a frozen case. The review did not submit
jobs, contact a provider, or modify the worktree. The initial candidate snapshot was
not separately retained or hash-bound, so this report records feedback and disposition
rather than claiming a review receipt for the revised bytes.

1. Eight blocks at a two-sided 1% threshold reject only when all eight nonzero contrasts
   agree in sign. The revised [draft](../../design/tuna-destination-randomized-blocks.md)
   states the condition, illustrates its power consequences and compares additional blocks.
2. Prior-source carryover can produce eight positive contrasts without any direct effect
   of the current source. The draft gives the explicit two-position construction and keeps
   washout, independently measured history and attribution as unresolved requirements.
3. The randomized test addresses a sharp null, rather than identifying a marginal
   distribution difference or an average causal effect. The draft now states that scope.
4. Independent unbiased order bits are assumptions requiring provenance and justification;
   finite source tests do not prove them. The draft gives biased and correlated-bit
   counterexamples and retains an assignment-law qualification requirement.
5. The sixteen-job timeline can exceed ten hours under an explicit scenario using the
   historical maximum observed wait and execution ceilings. The draft records the arithmetic
   and the missing queue model, horizon and operational controls.
6. Conditioning on complete execution can select assignment sequences and change the
   conditional error rate. The draft makes incomplete acquisition inconclusive and forbids
   selective replacement or retry under the same approval. Exact veto rules remain open.
7. The private provider trace remains the direct route to checking reconstruction; source
   equality and the randomized sequence test leave served schedules and physical causes
   unidentified. The existing private request remains unsent.

The review findings establish that the draft requires substantive statistical and
operational work. The final candidate still requires the separately requested high-effort
review bound to its exact sources, analyzer, assignment and admission records.

A subsequent read-only builder check evaluated the added four-job history-control
alternative. The Gram-matrix calculation holds under its additive model, but two-job
memory generates spurious direct and one-job coefficients, and a two- plus three-job
memory construction passes the proposed model residual while producing those coefficients.
The draft now states both counterexamples. The check also established that conditioning
on prior blocks gives a realized-history estimand and that a global sign-flip analysis
cannot hold later outcomes fixed while changing earlier assignments when history carries
over. A qualified sequential analysis or a separately justified no-interference model
remains open. The follow-up was also an advisory read of an evolving draft, not an
exact-byte approval of a final candidate.
The follow-up also gave a constant-response example whose eight-block coefficient
estimator has randomization standard deviation approximately 0.0884. The draft records
that example as a precision warning rather than as a device noise estimate.

A third read-only builder check assessed the [sequential job-assignment alternative](../../design/tuna-sequential-destination-uncertainty.md).
The reviewer verified its conditional-expectation identity, Hoeffding factor, radii,
queue arithmetic and unconditional separation statement. The check identified the need
for both complete potential outcomes, assignment concealment and a qualified fairness
law, and warned that source-dependent scheduling remains part of the estimand. The
sequential note now states those limitations and gives a biased-assignment sensitivity
calculation. The check examined an evolving draft; it is not an exact-byte final-case
review or a provider admission.

A fourth read-only builder check examined the working two-response e-process case.
It verified the four-threshold union bound, source hashes, decoded q6 counts, toy-model
enumeration and queue arithmetic. It identified assignment concealment, an irrevocable
failure-observation boundary, a separate reduced QX conformance case, and fresh
operational checks as requirements before freezing. The working case now states those
requirements, including the fact that late data cannot replace a neutral inferential
score. A prospective implementation, exact statistical contract, QX case and their
final source-bound high-effort review remain open. This advisory review did not bind an
immutable candidate snapshot and did not perform provider operations.
