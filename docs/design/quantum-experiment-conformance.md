# Quantum experiment conformance on Tuna-17, QX and Ascella

The multidimensional-spacetime blueprint combines valid finite algebra with claims that its circuits do not
establish. This design gives every proposal a bounded outcome: executable mathematical references and device
conformance suites for supported constructions, and reproducible counterexamples for unsupported inferences.
The first acquisition question separates compilation error from residual device error in six-dimensional
transition probabilities. Software admission and acquisition completion have separate records.

## Mathematical and physical boundaries

The encoding `a -> L_a` is linear. Operator composition remains associative and differs from multiplication
in a non-associative algebra. A zero-product herald has zero probability while the complete normalized state
retains unit norm. Measure its unconditional probability and the declared controls; conditional estimates
require an observed, explicitly reported denominator.

The `open_gororoba` sedenion geodesic module describes its geodesic equivalence as a postulate. A quantum
circuit implementing finite signed permutations supplies neither a spacetime metric nor a stress-energy
tensor. A future physical bridge needs a metric/operator derivation with units and observables, conservation
laws, Schwarzschild and flat limits, null constraints and step-size convergence. The blueprint's Wigner
negativity and cancellation arguments supply those quantities with no derivation. The implementation in
this repository makes finite algebra and device claims only.

## Dimension-six transition contract

Use `U_r=A_r tensor B_r` from the source note, for `r=0,1,2`, with `omega=exp(2*pi*i/3)`.
The source-defined cubic Tao matrix is a fourth, fully specified offline candidate. Exact arithmetic checks
orthonormality, all reference cross-overlaps and the candidate's actual residuals.

Embed `(a,b)` at computational index `4*a+b`. Logical labels `0..5` correspond to qubit indices
`0,1,2,4,5,6`; qubit indices `3,7` are leakage labels. Extend every six-dimensional unitary by identity
on the leakage subspace. For each ordered preparation/measurement basis pair and each of six inputs,
implement `U_s^dagger U_r`, including input preparation. The three bases produce 54 settings.
Same-basis settings are compiled identity-transition controls. Independently compiled transition matrices
do not identify reusable physical preparation and measurement instruments.

Tuna-17 lowering records the requested operator, routed unrounded operator, rounded native operator,
rotation grid, layout, measurement destinations and compiler identity. Reset-free QX twins reproduce the
rounded payload with a compact physical-to-logical map. QX measurement registers and Tuna classical-bit
registers require their respective recorded decoding rules.

Ascella uses a single connected source and six logical modes. Input selection belongs to the complete
requested unitary. Reconstruct the full optical operator to the existing `1e-9` admission tolerance.
Record logical output labels and every returned outside-subspace category if compilation expands the mesh.
Detected-event conditional probabilities and incident-shot probabilities have distinct denominators.
Infer photon loss only when the result contract provides the incident-shot denominator.

For each setting retain three probability laws: mathematical target, compiled prediction and device
observation. Report ideal-to-compiled, measured-to-compiled and measured-to-ideal residuals separately.
The default precision target is absolute probability error 0.05 with simultaneous coverage 0.95 across
the frozen endpoint family. A deterministic binomial calculation sizes the case under an explicitly
stated independent-shot model. Batch-level results and shot order expose violations of that model;
intervals carry the model qualification. Insufficient counts, incomplete batches or failed readout
controls produce an inconclusive acquisition result rather than a successful conformance verdict.

## Cancellation and amplification contracts

The sedenion suite reuses certified LCU construction, bright and sign-mutation arms. Full data encoding
uses four data qubits and a selector. A reduced optical representation needs an explicit support isometry,
restricted block, dilation normalization and port map; its name identifies the restriction. Preserve
executed `+, -, -, +` order evidence separately from requested order. A new acquisition asks a device
question about compilation, leakage or residual error; exact cancellation itself is settled by arithmetic.

The amplification suite prepares `V=H tensor H` on two qubits, marks `|11>` with its phase oracle and
reflects about `V|00>`. Retain the preparation, oracle and reflection stages in the emitted circuit.
The unamplified, omitted-oracle, omitted-reflection and direct `X tensor X` target-state controls distinguish
the intended mechanism from target preparation alone. Check full target-state equivalence up to global
phase. Report the actual gate resources and empirical device performance; ideal success one is a matrix
identity with a different evidence boundary from measured success.

## Investigation closure and reopening

The Fano investigation completes when exact incidence, rank, commutation, eigenspace dimension and a
signed-cancellation counterexample reproduce. A future protection experiment requires a code-space
isometry, logical operators, disjoint register assignment, named channel or Hamiltonian and a falsifiable
invariant-subspace or lifetime prediction.

The spectral investigation completes when exact examples distinguish `<v|P|v>`, its squared modulus and
`||P v||^2/alpha^2`, including a non-projector filter. Acquisition reopens only after the source operator,
group-word convention, boundary rule, block-encoding normalization, resource count and truncation error
are specified. A finite filtering benchmark would receive a separate name and proposal.

## Execution and retained evidence

The [acquisition review procedure](../runbooks/quantum-experiment-acquisition.md) states the questions,
controls and pending admission requirements. The [evidence bundle](../evidence/quantum-experiment-conformance/README.md) holds deterministic references,
generated cases and replay instructions. The [planning entrance](../state/plans.md) owns implementation
obligations. Provider approval and the research registry keep their existing authority.

Local validation uses exact arithmetic, complete operator comparisons, compiler checks and count fixtures.
Every remote QX, simulator and QPU execution waits for approval of its completed written case. Cases bind
payloads, source revision, encoding, seed provenance, endpoint family, shot count, execution and credit
ceilings, controls and stop conditions. Provider reads can refresh constraints; a catalogue read alone
establishes neither instrument readiness nor a hardware result.

Use existing guarded submission and lossless collection paths. Tuna batches obey the three-circuit limit,
the provider pause policy and account-wide queue ownership. QX twins fit the retained ten-qubit and
shot limits. Ascella cases admit connected sources, native mode expansion, duration and credit bounds.
Derive ceilings from validated payloads and current planning data rather than copying blueprint timings.

Raw results remain immutable. Analyses bind raw hashes, compiler and payload identities, actual execution
metadata, endpoint definitions and analysis version. Mock counts enter fixture checks only. A retained
acquisition receives its provider registry entry and ledger updates in the change that retains it.
