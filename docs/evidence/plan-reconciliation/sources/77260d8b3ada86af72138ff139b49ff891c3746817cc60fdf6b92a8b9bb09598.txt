## Simulator choice

Aer cannot be rebuilt offline: noise_model_calibration is a timestamp only, placed_qubit_calibration holds T1, T2 and readout without gate errors, and collect.json, raw metadata and job_metrics carry no calibration. CompiledLaw evaluates the archived ISA exactly as a density matrix on its active wires (4 here): fixed gates fused between insertions, exact branches through measure, reset and if_else, rz(rate x duration) inserted after the pulse count _probed uses, round1 depolarizing every active wire at the first measurement and round2 at the second, the mid assignment flip on the recorded bit before the if_else, end rates on later measurements.

Validation on the 8 members of 083417: zero rates and nuisances reproduce ideal_compiled_probabilities (CompileLaw enforces it on every compile); a single rotation at each single-gap start equals _probed to 5e-16; against distribution() at round1 = 0.1 the laws differ by up to 0.0085 (nominal, window_delay), 0.014 and 0.025 (extra_readout) and 0 (window_echo), from nuisance placement, so the block never compares against joint_quasistatic_fit.

## Deviations from the plan

1. dt offline: dt_source reads family_configuration.dt_seconds, then docs/evidence/heron_platform/targets.json, then the live Target, and records the source; dt_seconds wraps it.
2. Window: from the control's first measure play to its first later play that is neither measure nor reset; an overlapping interval enters whole, and the single gap's intervals always enter.
3. No per-interval logical state: the exact evaluator makes it unnecessary.
4. window_segments accepts measure as a one-pulse instruction on non-control wires (synthetic schedule only).
5. Coupling probe at a point with every rate nonzero (a probe at zero misses 143, which acts only with 136 in feedback members).
6. Search: grid at no more than a quarter period per qubit, odd counts, cap 32768; grid evaluated at the no-detuning nuisances; L-BFGS-B from 0 plus the 12 lowest grid points of each model. Half-period spacing with 6 seeds gave 75.4 and 56.3.
7. Schema: top-level schema_version "n21-orbit-rca-mechanism-fit-v2" beside the piecewise block.
8. ci/gates.json not edited.
