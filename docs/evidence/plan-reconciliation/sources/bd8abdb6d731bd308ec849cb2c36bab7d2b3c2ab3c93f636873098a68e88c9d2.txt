# Packed Pauli response under current provider balances

This case asks whether increasing simultaneous state-preparation load changes the operational dark/bright
contrast of a fixed four-qubit measurement. The question matters because a nominal increase in replica count
can destroy the response or correlate its errors; more qubits alone establish neither more information nor
safe packing. The case uses the [provider audit](../evidence/provider-api-audit/README.md) and the exact
[Pauli-effect identity](../notes/topics/bdsc-pauli-readout.md). The user limits design to current independent
balances: 30 IQM credits and 25 Open Quantum Spark credits. Every program is unsubmitted and native-unadmitted.

## Claim, null and possible decisions

The target is `E0 = I/2 - YIXY/4 + YZXY/4`. Its kernel, spectrum and basis convention already have exact
certificates. Hardware tests the implemented preparation/measurement channel, rather than proving that identity.

Four fixed sentinel cells prepare `(e7+e12)/sqrt(2)` and `(e7-e12)/sqrt(2)` in two fixed quadratures.
Cells 0/1 read `X3 X1 Y0`; cells 2/3 read `Y3 X1 Y0`, with the sector bit q2 read in Z. Each cell carries
algebra bit b on logical qubit b. The Y readout gives the bounded score `g` in `{0,1/2,1}` and the kernel event.
The ideal effect means are 0 and 1. The X quadrature has ideal parity zero; it remains a phase-sensitive diagnostic.

The sparse arm prepares the four sentinel cells; the dense arm also prepares the remaining load cells.
Both arms measure the same complete register and apply the same basis rotations. Idle load cells therefore
still experience readout-basis pulses. The intervention changes GHZ-type preparation load and measurement
context, not drive activity alone. A global source barrier separates preparation and readout; only native
compilation and served scheduling can establish its physical implementation.

The primary response per acquisition is the joint-shot mean `B_Y-K_Y`, using the two sentinel cells' raw g
scores. The paired difference is dense response minus sparse response. The sharp operational null says the
density assignment changes none of those paired outcomes. A two-sided exact assignment p-value at most 0.05
rejects that null under the assumptions below. The ideal null is zero; a common unchanged affine response
also predicts zero. A rejection identifies a response to the compound density intervention, not a unique
crosstalk, leakage, readout or coherent-error channel. A compatible test establishes neither equivalence nor
permission to multiply cells into independent trials. Equivalence needs its own justified margin and design.

Secondary, explicitly exploratory outputs are X/Y quadratures, kernel-event fractions, product-reference
assignment errors, per-cell response and drift. A phase estimate is withheld when the quadrature amplitude
is unresolved. Readout-inverted scores, if later added, are conditional sensitivity analyses, never primary data.

`1/32768`. Two ion pairs have minimum 1/2, so the ion case cannot confirm the primary density result at 5%,
even with an extreme outcome. Its valid question is coarse raw implementation/packing qualification on that
device and calibration. More simultaneous cells do not repair that temporal-replication denominator.

## Randomization, covariance and uncertainty

The generator freezes seed 20261022. Each IQM/Rigetti pair independently randomizes sparse/dense order;
the four computational references are all-zero/all-one at both ends. The ion sequence counterbalances
two pairs in opposite orders and remains descriptive. The native compilation must retain the same sentinel
physical placement, state-preparation macro and measurement map in both arms. Missing or unverifiable maps
stop admission; a software map is not evidence that the provider applied it.

The primary test conditions on all completed, prespecified pairs and uses their integer joint-score
numerator differences. `packed_pauli_analysis.paired_pvalue` enumerates every sign assignment in bounded
integer batches, retains ties and returns an exact Fraction. The test requires actual randomized assignment,
preserved acquisition ordering/identity, the sharp no-effect null and absence of carryover/interference
between acquisitions. It does not require treating the simultaneous cells as independent. Those assumptions
remain explicit admission obligations, not properties inferred from a job's circuit array.

Ordered shots, when supplied, support block/lag diagnostics. Counts-only data leave serial dependence
unresolved; any shot-IID interval is labeled conditional and secondary. Use joint per-shot sentinel scores
and full shared-shot covariance. Prohibit optional stopping, dropping inconvenient pairs, or refitting a
favorable placement after outcomes. A failed/short acquisition, calibration transition that invalidates
the compiled case, changed layout, missing raw joint outcomes or violated credit ceiling stops the case;
an interrupted case reports diagnostics, not the complete preregistered test.

## Throughput and cost optimization

The graph witnesses attain the connected-template width ceilings. They optimize count, not joint fidelity,
usable independent information or pulse concurrency. Native layout qualification must compare valid
measurement/calibration metrics and retain weak or missing metrics explicitly before selecting sentinel roles.

IQM accepts 100 circuits/job, 20000 shots/circuit and 2000000 executions/job on these QPUs. The 36-circuit
design fits one circuit batch, but separate circuits remain separate acquisitions only if the served ordering
and identity support the randomization contract. At 20000 shots the same design reaches 720000 executions;
that is a structural capacity, not an affordable timing prediction. Increase shots only after the compiled
repetition/reset schedule and a defensible server-compilation bound fit the total ceiling with reserve.

IQM charges `rate * ceil(compilation_seconds + execution_seconds)` per job. The 24-credit science ceiling
permits 32 billed seconds on Emerald or 48 on Garnet, including compilation. Native transpilation on the
client does not establish zero server compilation. A smaller circuit batch can reduce failure exposure but
adds compilation and rounding overhead. No speculative timing estimate authorizes a submission.

The live broker tariff gives Rigetti `ceil(0.18+3215*0.000255)=1` credit/program and IonQ
`ceil(0.18+100*0.048)=5`. Each job contains one full packed QASM program. The API's advertised 1024 circuits
is not used to assume free multi-circuit multiplexing. The Rigetti case fits the existing one-credit MCP cap;
the ion case requires a separately approved five-credit cap change and remains blocked under the current cap.
Keep Standard priority. Public upload is itself disclosure under the provider's terms and requires approval.

Do not queue the whole experiment merely to increase activity. The execution plan must preserve pair identity
and chronology, bound active jobs, retain actual start/finish/calibration records, and recheck account occupancy
and remaining credits. API request-rate units and compute credits remain separate accounting surfaces.
