# tuna-boxkite-stages: box-kite Tuna-17 T0 depth ladder, then T1, coherence and walks

Owner session: boxkite-quantum-main. Branch: research/box-kite-case-generator
(case dirs box_kite_tuna_t0, _t1, _coherence, _walk, _twins_qx).
Device: Tuna-17, 10 batches, 30 jobs, about 3 min execution, about 45 min
wall. Approval: not requested (a relayed approval from another session does
not count; the owner answers here).

## Finding

The census settles the algebra; the hardware measures how much predicted
contrast survives on a stated layout. The previous plan (44 batches) spent
most batches on questions the census already answers.

## Reachability

experiment-program.md 4(g) as rewritten by boxkite-generator; cases go
through `qi_platform.py submit` (3 circuits per batch, one queued batch
account-wide, 120 s pause, QX dry run first).

## Design

Layout: exclusions {0, 5, 6, 7, 9}, q15 avoided, pairs M = {(1,4), (10,13),
(12,14)} (maximal induced matching, confirmed by ibmquantum-12), selectors
q1, q10, q12; walks on star 11 {8, 13, 14} and path 8-11-13. Each batch:
witness + readout-0 + readout-1.

| stage | batches | shots | content |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | 3 | 16384 | reduced LCU of edge (1,10)(4,15), depths 1/5/9 CZ, 3x3 Latin square depth x pair, both y_sign values present at every depth |
| T1 | 2 | 4096 | opposite edge (1,10)(4,15), its twist (4,10)(1,15) (census twists check before T1), strut (1,10)(2,9), each at y_sign +-1 |
| coherence | 2 | 4096 | X and Y on d for a bright edge and a strut, plus reference |
| walks | 3 | 4096 | (2,11,3) 6 CNOT star 11; (2,3,10) 4 CNOT path 8-11-13; omit-last-block control; each plus reference |

Per batch: identical bytes on local qxelarator, relabeled twin on remote
QX (witness circuits only), submit, collect, checks (16384/4096 shots,
readout-0 P1 <= 0.10 and readout-1 P1 >= 0.80 on used qubits, reference
P(selector = 0) >= 0.7, digests equal cases.json, queue empty), 120 s
pause.

## Acceptance and falsifier

T0: pooled depth-1 contrast with its 99% upper bound below 0.5 ends the
Tuna program; fitted r outside [0.87, 0.97] at 99% resizes T1-walks before
they run. T1: D > +0.4 same, < -0.4 opposite, |D| < 0.2 strut; any
misclassified item refutes the layout's separation estimate. Walks:
sign(<Z_c>) opposite omega at |z| > 3, or control |<Z_c>| > 0.1 + 3 sigma.

## Out of scope

Full Design B and full-encoding coherence on Tuna (below the 0.10 floor).

T0 as generated: y_sign -1 on the pair whose index equals the batch, so
every depth, pair and batch holds one dark and two bright replicas; C(n) =
mean bright corrected P minus mean dark corrected P (cancels a common
additive shift); accepted confound: a pair x depth interaction absorbed
into r. T1 twist pair confirmed from census kites[2].twists[0]. T1 caveat:
D compares one pair across two batches, so batch drift enters D and only
the strut detects it. Witness qubits observed: T0/T1/coherence
{1,4,10,12,13,14}, walks {1,4,8,11,13,14}; gates H, S, Sdag, Z, X, CZ only.
