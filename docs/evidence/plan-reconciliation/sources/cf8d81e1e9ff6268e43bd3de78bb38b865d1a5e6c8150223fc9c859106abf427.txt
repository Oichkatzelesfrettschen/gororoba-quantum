   scripts/n21_orbit_rca.py:2538 grows `CONTRACTS` from 3 to 5 for every
   non-split record, and `admissibility` (default `ORBIT_VARIANTS`, which
   now holds `window_delay_long`) lists the missing pair, so `--between-arm`
   over a retained pre-sweep n21-orbit-rca record (results/live/2026-10-18T062500,
   2026-10-18T163059, 2026-10-18T211254: family_configuration without
   `window_sweep`) reports every contract not_admissible at alpha 0.01
   instead of the three contracts at 0.05/3. `contract_names` should key on
   the configuration (sweep / split / plain, e.g. read from members as
   `overlap_split` does) and pass the matching variants to `admissibility`.
   No retained between-arm.json covers those records, so no gate fails yet.
2. `magnitude_mde` (n21_orbit_rca.py:3458) and `herald_bound` through it
   use `CONTRACT_ALPHA` (0.01) for split records, whose contracts run at
   `contract_alpha(True)` = 0.0125. The herald admission bound for a split
   is therefore computed at a different alpha than its contracts; it errs
   conservative, but the recorded MDE does not describe the split's test.

## Note

3. Approval binding holds: `qpu_planning.settings` (qpu_planning.py:277-287)
   puts `overlap_split` and `pin_layout` in `policy.design`, so a submit
   that drops or changes either re-plans to a differing design and refuses;
   `pinned_orbit_layout` refuses another backend, an out-of-device or a
   calibration-excluded pinned qubit; `pinned_roles_violations` refuses a
   routed arm that leaves the pin. `overlap_arm` is a constant, so a legacy
   ten-member record re-planned now yields twelve members and refuses by
   digest. qpu_cases.py:321-334 reproduces `--overlap-split` and
   `--pin-layout BACKEND:Q,...` from the design; argparse order is free.
