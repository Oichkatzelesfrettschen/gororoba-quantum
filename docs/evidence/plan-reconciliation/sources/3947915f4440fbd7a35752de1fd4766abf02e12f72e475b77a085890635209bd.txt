# Provider API, billing and research-design implementation plan

> Execute the approved audit in the current session with test-first changes and independent final review.

**Goal:** Admit provider facts and repair API/MCP information loss before designing a discriminating hardware acquisition.

**Architecture:** Keep IQM's existing client and source snapshots, resolve calibration identities before related reads,
and expose observation provenance without rewriting pinned historical sources. Put Open Quantum adaptations in a
repository-owned wrapper around the pinned vendor tools, rather than editing installed packages. Keep Quandela's
concurrent MCP repair under its existing owner and link the audit's residuals to that work.

**Tech stack:** Pinned repository Python/CI tools, the isolated Open Quantum stack, IQM REST v1/GraphQL,
MCP stdio, exact algebra certificates and local circuit checks.

**Specification:** The user's approved audit scope and the claim boundaries in
[hypercomplex-operator-evidence.md](hypercomplex-operator-evidence.md),
[matched-response case](tuna17-zero-divisor-matched-response.md), and
[BDSC Pauli readout](../notes/topics/bdsc-pauli-readout.md).

## Constraints and review focus

- Provider job submission, circuit upload, slots, QRNG and notebooks remain separately approved operations.
- A price estimate without circuit upload is a read-only diagnostic; preserve its exact scope.
- Existing ledger-bound source bytes stay immutable; corrections use successor records.
- Credits from different providers are independent budgets, rather than convertible currency units.
- The user limits the pilot design to current balances: IQM 30 credits and Open Quantum 25 Spark credits.
  A larger pilot must fit those balances; purchases, top-ups and a purchased-credit fallback stay excluded.
- A local algebra theorem or simulator result supplies no device-noise evidence or fundamental scalar-field test.
- Missing data, mixed calibration identities, stale prices, error-shaped successful MCP responses and filtered
  pagination must each have a regression before a claimed repair.

## Acceptance cases

IQM tests must force a default-calibration transition between calls and establish that related reads use the
resolved immutable identity. Observation intervals must retain fractional UTC seconds and errors must reach MCP
as errors. Existing tool signatures and the single cancellation mutation retain their boundaries.

Open Quantum tests must read `n_qubits` separately from `limits.max_qubits_per_job`, preserve unknown values,
filter across multiple pages, reject upstream error payloads, and delegate spending without changing caps,
approval requirements or publication semantics. Live checks use catalogue/constraint reads and circuit-free quotes.

The run design must identify at least one outcome that changes a named open operational claim. It must explain
why another backend is informative and why the result cannot settle a theorem, a fundamental hypercomplex scalar
field, or a noise mechanism the interventions leave confounded. A reserve, actual quote and reviewed analysis
remain admission requirements rather than assumed execution authority.
