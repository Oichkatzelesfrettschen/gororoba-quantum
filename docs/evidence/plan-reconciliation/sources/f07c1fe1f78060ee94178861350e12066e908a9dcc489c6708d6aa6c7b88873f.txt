## Fix before submit

1. The unbracketed pair is a different gate program, not the nominal program
   without barriers. isa.qpy `count_ops`: PUBs 1/2 (nominal) hold 44 CZ,
   79 sx, 61/60 rz; PUBs 3/4 (unbracketed) hold 43 CZ, 77 sx, 57/56 rz.
   With the window barriers gone the transpiler resynthesized the 2q block
   around the window: the nominal tail `cz(136,143); sx 142; sx 143 |
   barrier | sx 136; sx 142; sx 143; cz(136,143)` becomes `sx 142; measure;
   reset; sx 142; sx 143; cz(136,143)` with one CZ removed. window_overlap_loss
   (magnitude_decline, scripts/n21_orbit_rca.py:2768) then compares two arms
   that differ in CZ count as well as in readout overlap. The confound runs
   against the hypothesis (one fewer CZ raises the unbracketed contrast), so
   a pass still supports overlap loss, but a fail cannot be read as "overlap
   costs nothing". Fix: build the unbracketed member from the bracketed ISA
   (transpile with barriers, then strip the window barriers post-routing,
   or compile both at optimization_level that preserves the block), and check
   equal count_ops except barriers; or state the confound and its direction
   in the case so a fail is reported as unresolved.

## Notes

2. Overlap is possible but ALAP-dependent. In PUB 3 the first post-window
   work ops (`sx 143; cz(136,143)`) depend only on `cz(142,143)` before the
   control's measure, so the circuit order allows them during the readout
   and reset. Under ALAP the 143/136 chain up to the next `cz(142,143)` (two
   CZs plus sx) is pushed to the end of the 836 dt measure+reset span, i.e.
   it overlaps the reset's readout, not the first measurement. isa.qpy holds
   no schedule, so the exact overlap is not verified; the case should say
   "overlaps the control's measure-plus-reset span under the backend
   scheduler" rather than "the readout window", and --collect's
   scheduler_timing should be read to confirm overlap before the verdict is
   interpreted.

3. Spectator timing and ordering check out. idle (PUB 7) is `delay 836 dt`
   on 142 = 415 + reset_dt 421 (family_configuration.reset_dt); reset_only
   (PUB 9) is `delay 415 dt; reset` inside the same barrier pair, equal when
   the backend reset lasts reset_dt. In idle/immediate the terminal `reset
   142` follows a barrier after `measure 143` (qpe_diagnostics.py:439-444),
   so it cannot perturb qubit 2's readout. Laws (qpe_diagnostics.py:449)
   and the clbit map (control clbit 0 unwritten = 0, qubit 2 on clbit 1,
   herald clbits 2..5) match the compiled circuits.

4. spectator_idle_dephasing does not isolate intrinsic T2. In idle and
   reset_only the control sits in |+> across the window and its ZZ with 143
   is never recorded, so idle-vs-immediate measures T2-like loss plus the
   unresolved ZZ(142,143) dephasing; in 151959 the branch turn was 0.114 rad
   on a different control (144) and cost under 1 percent, but 142-143 is a
   different pair. The case should name the passing quantity "loss across an
   unrecorded-control idle", not intrinsic dephasing.

5. Power. Magnitude SE ~0.011 per arm at 8192 shots (0.008 at 16384),
   difference SE ~0.016; at alpha 0.0167 (z 2.13) plus herald margin ~0.013
   the overlap pass needs a loss above ~0.047 on a ~0.15-0.17 magnitude
   (modeled alternatives 0.169/0.174), i.e. above ~30 percent. The 45 percent
   premise (~0.07) clears it with z ~4.4; a loss of 20 percent fails by
   power. Visibility: per-quadrature SE ~0.009 at v 0.59, ~0.004 at v 0.95,
   difference SE ~0.010-0.013; with z 2.13 and spectator_margin (twice the
   qubit-2 herald limit, ~0.022) a pass needs a gap above ~0.05. A T2-only
   idle loss (exp(-3.34 us / 111 us) gives 0.97, gap ~0.03) fails by power,
   so a fail of contract (3) is uninformative unless the gap reads large;
   the 0.59-vs-0.99 effect of 151959 is well inside power.

6. Alpha schemes differ. record.json observable_contracts carry alpha
   0.00625 (0.05/8, the planner's per-endpoint split over 2 pair contrasts
   and 6 spectator members), while the analyzer's three overlap contracts
   use FAMILY_ALPHA/3 = 0.0167 (contract_alpha(True), n21_orbit_rca.py:2247).
   Both are family-wise 0.05 for their own family; the case should state
   that the preregistered verdicts are the analyzer's three at 0.0167.

7. Herald margin direction is correct: magnitude_decline passes only when
   upper + margin < 0, so the margin works against a pass.

8. Case overstatement. Fixing control at 142 recovers 083417's roles, but
   the calibration week still differs, and the 45 percent loss of 083417 was
   the window_delay arm against nominal, not a barrier comparison
   (N21_ORBIT_RCA.md, contract forecast table). A pass of (1) shows the
   unbracketed program loses magnitude on this calibration (subject to
   item 1); it does not show that 083417's loss was the overlap. A fail of
   (1) together with a nominal magnitude near 151959's 0.15 points to the
   role/calibration change instead.
