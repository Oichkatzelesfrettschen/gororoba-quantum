## Herald

- Opt-in: a herald flag on the n21-orbit-rca family that the policy records
  (so the approved record binds it). Default off, so every retained
  n21-orbit-rca record, core-446 (window bracket) and the planning contracts
  replay unchanged.
- Circuit (gororoba_quantum/qpe_diagnostics.py `n21_orbit_diagnostic`, built
  through `diagnostic_experiments.n21_orbit_rca_group`): with the herald on,
  the first instructions measure every qubit of the circuit (control and the
  three work qubits) into a separate ClassicalRegister named `herald`, then a
  barrier over all qubits, then the unchanged program. The `phase` register
  and the labeled law over it are unchanged; the herald bits are ideally 0.
  Check that ideal_compiled_probabilities / the labeled-law and exact-law
  paths marginalize to the `phase` register (or otherwise keep the 4-entry
  law), and that the planner's admission and the window_bracket contract
  accept the heralded circuits.
- `qpe_diagnostics.window_bracket_violations`: the window opens at the first
  measurement whose clbit is in the `phase` register (not the herald), so a
  heralded circuit reads the same violations as its unheralded twin.
- `scripts/n21_orbit_rca.py window_roles`:
  - writers: take measurements into the `phase` register only;
  - opening: the control's first measure play after the herald (plays carry
    no clbit; drop the herald prefix, every pulse up to and including the
    herald measurements and their barrier, from the pulse list, and drop the
    herald measure instructions from the instruction list, consistently, so
    `positions` and the one-to-one sx/x/cz check are unchanged);
  - a heralded member must yield exactly the couplings its unheralded twin
    yields on the same schedule minus the prefix. Add a contract for that
    (synthetic or retained-schedule based).
- Analysis: the herald is an admission bound on the member-differential
  initial excitation, never a post-selection. Herald P(1) per qubit equals
  initial excitation plus readout p(1|0); the readout term is common to the
  members (same qubits, same readout), so the identifiable quantity is the
  spread across members of herald P(1) on each qubit. Preregister: the run is
  herald-admissible when, on every placed qubit, the max minus min over the 8
  members of herald P(1) is at most a bound B, tested at a stated alpha with
  the 27,648-shot binomial resolution. Derive B from the contracts: the bound
  is the excitation spread that moves the largest between-arm contract by at
  most a stated small fraction (say one quarter) of its minimum detectable
  deficit at 27,648 shots (about 0.018); write the derivation into the
  analysis module docstring and the README/notes. Statistics, levels and
  shot counts of the three between-arm contracts are otherwise unchanged.
  `--between-arm` reports the herald verdict beside admissibility.
- No new behavior for members without the herald.
