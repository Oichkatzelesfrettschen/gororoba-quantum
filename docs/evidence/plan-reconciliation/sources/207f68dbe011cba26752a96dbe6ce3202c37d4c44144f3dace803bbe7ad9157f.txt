Owner session: boxkite-quantum-main. Covers tuna-walsh (#116), the ladder
open questions of tuna-17-run-design.md, and F4/R1 of research-ibm-parked

## Finding

- #116: `scripts/associator_walsh_classes.py` EXPECTED_CUBIC_CLASSES holds
  the two dimension-32 cubic classes: |W| multiset {0:24, 8:7, 24:1}
  (K = 11/32, 234 pairs, (1,16) among them) and {0:22, 8:8, 16:2}
  (K = 5/32, 126 pairs, (9,18)). The draft design doc on
  tuna/bipartite-walsh-and-lcu-packing (6fc0af4, another session, old main)
  gives blocks X = (1|2,4,8,12), Y = (10,13,11,14) | (13,11,14,16) and 5 vs
  6 CZ from an uncommitted search (unverified).
- Both Y blocks contain q13, whose e10 the ladder showed to depend on its
  neighbors' context (0.088 all-excited, 0.042 isolated). In a Walsh circuit
  q10 and q11 are in block Y and vary shot by shot, so a single bracket
  cannot correct q13; #116 depends on the readout-context follow-up.
- Ladder open questions (tuna-17-run-design.md "Open questions"): X X
  neighbor control and one-neighbor-at-a-time context readouts (new runs);
  rho in line-phase bins and the larger-of-1f/2f line test (analysis of the
  retained raw shots, no new run); H vs Y90-X pricing and wait ladders
  (new runs, lower priority).
- #97: docs/notes/rank_locus_two_dimension_gap.md designs three exact
  computations. Nobody else holds the rank-locus code (last commits
  211001c, 566ff07 from this program; no other plan row).
- #51: items 3-8 of RANK_STRATUM_TOPOLOGY.md; item 3's first deliverable

## Design

Order (each step its own branch and PR from main):

1. analysis/ladder-line-phase-bins -- rho per line-phase bin (8 bins,
   stationary bins at the modulation extrema) and the larger-of-1f/2f line
   statistic over the retained ladder raw shots. A second analysis record
   beside box_kite_tuna_17_ladder_analysis.json, its own digest; the ladder
   record is not rewritten. The 2f rule on already-seen data is exploratory
   and labeled so; it binds only a fresh run. Gate core-433 (--check) and
   core-550 (--self-test). Sonnet agent.
2. algebra/rank-locus-gap-directions -- #97 computation 1: complement of T_F
   in T_1(x) at every certified non-zero-divisor witness, its projection
   onto span{e8, e24}; prediction rank 2 at every witness, falsifier rank
   below 2. parallel.py over witnesses. Gates core-551/552. Sonnet agent,
   concurrent with step 1.
3. tuna/walsh-readout-context-series -- generator
   `scripts/qi_walsh_exemplar_cases.py`:
   - phases from predict.associator_phase; relabeled circuits asserted to
     reproduce the census |W| signatures above (never hand spectra);
   - CCZ / CCZ+CZ on a 4-path by an exhaustive gated search with a free end
     map, T/Tdag on the pi/36 grid; relabel map y = (A_fin A)^T z + c;
   - layout checked against the exclusion set {0,3,5,6,7,9,15} and the
     coupler graph; draft doc ported onto the fresh branch;
   - readout-context circuits: readout-xx (q13 X, X X on q10 and q11),
     readout-q10 (q13 and q10 excited), readout-q11.
   Series (each batch 3 circuits, 131072 shots, about 72 s per job):
   batch-00 readout-0, readout-1, readout-xx; batch-01 C1 (high on X, low
   on Y), C2 (swapped), readout-q10; batch-02 C1 and C2 replicates,
   readout-q11. About 650 s execution, about 25 min wall with pauses.
   q13 correction preregistered three ways: raw, isolated bracket, and
   context-resolved (conditioned on the measured q10/q11 bits of each shot).
   Predictions and falsifiers: forbidden-outcome weight, spectral shape
   (weight moved among allowed coefficients = coherent error), class-by-block
   asymmetry from the C1/C2 swap, K per line-phase bin. No depolarizing-K
   falsifier: rho above 1 already excludes global depolarizing. Gates
   core-428 (--check), core-429 (--self-test), core-431 (analysis replay),
   core-432 (QX conformance). Built by this session.
4. Written case against the QX dry run, then the owner's approval in this
   session, then Tuna under the 3-circuit / one-queued / 120 s limits.
5. algebra/rank-locus-second-order -- #97 computation 2: centered Schur
   construction of nullity_twelve_locus.py at the {a, 0} witnesses, then
   nullity 8 and 4; full T(x), every variable kept, sum-of-squares
   splitting of V against T_F. Outcome: V in T_F refutes the conjecture at
   that nullity; a transverse real solution names a candidate only.
   Gates core-553/554. Opus-level work, this session.
6. #51 item 3 first deliverable: D_(5,12) pieces (B17, A15, {a,0},
   Proposition 8.2 points) with the {a,0} dimension from step 5. Gates
   core-555/556. Starts only after step 5 settles.
