# tuna-pre-t0: Tuna-17 readout refresh and Q0/Q6 double-X discriminator, one batch

Owner session: boxkite-quantum-main. Branch (planned): in
research/box-kite-case-generator as results/quantum_inspire/cases/box_kite_tuna_pre/.
Device: Tuna-17, 1 batch of 3 circuits. Approval: not requested.

## Finding

- The exclusion set {0, 5, 6, 7, 9} and the readout factors behind the
  box-kite separation model come from batches of 04:18-04:36Z
  (experiment-program.md 4(g)); they are hours old, and q13 (0.808) and
  q14 (0.800) sit on the program's readout-1 >= 0.80 stop rule.
- PR #192 (ibmquantum-e5) leaves one Q0/Q6 question open: under-rotated X
  versus |1> misassignment. Ramsey data cannot separate them; a double X
  can. Claim proposed-tuna17-q0-q6-double-x-discriminator predicts P1 after
  X X of 0.98 (Q0) and 0.52 (Q6) under under-rotation against 0.038 and
  0.013 under misassignment, from the 05:26-05:33Z state. #192 (316dc27)
  adds that X and X90 are separate native operations: the retained Ramsey
  already excludes a fault confined to native X on Q6 (raw quadrature mean
  0.064 against a floor of 0.148) but not on Q0 (0.287 against 0.175).
  X X separates either kind of X under-rotation from misassignment, so the
  batch design holds; the Q6 reading mainly tests misassignment against an
  X90-coupled rotation error.

## Design

One batch, 3 circuits, qubit[17], q0 rightmost: readout-0 (all 17 idle,
measure), readout-1 (X on all 17, measure), double-X (X X on all 17, then
measure). Shots 4096 per circuit (P1 standard error below 0.008 at
p = 0.5). QX twin of the double-X circuit only. Analysis in the generator's
analyze: per-qubit P(1|0), P(1|1), P(1|XX), the re-admission of q5/q6/q7/q9
by readout, and the discriminator verdict.

## Acceptance and falsifier

Precondition (from #192): the batch's own readout pair still shows the
fault, Q0 P(1|1) < 0.7 and Q6 P(1|1) < 0.3; otherwise the double-X result
is reported as fault-recovered and not read against the predictions.
Decision fixed now: Q0 P(1|XX) above 0.5 and Q6 above 0.25 favor
under-rotation; both below 0.1 favor misassignment; anything else is
unresolved. Stop rule for T0: any qubit of M or of the walk star with
readout-1 below 0.80 or readout-0 above 0.10 re-plans the layout first.
