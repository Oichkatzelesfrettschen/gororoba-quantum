# boxkite-kaggle-sweep: exhaustive family (a) on a Kaggle CPU kernel with qxelarator

Owner session: boxkite-quantum-main. Branch: research/box-kite-case-generator.

## Finding

The exhaustive 848-circuit (1010 P = 1/2 replicas, 2048 shots) check of
family (a) leaves remote QX and needs a home: AGENTS.md routes simulations
of 14 qubits or fewer to Kaggle, and a Kaggle result enters research state
only through a CPU replay gate.

## Reachability

`kaggle_run.py push` (CPU machine, default) of the generator's sweep entry;
the replay gate in CI.

## Design

- Sweep entry (built by the boxkite-generator agent): regenerate all 848
  circuits in-process, run each cQASM text through qxelarator 0.10.0 at
  2048 shots (seeded if qxelarator exposes a seed), write one record with
  per-circuit sha256, census predictions per replica, counts and verdict
  under the program's falsifier (any shot violating a deterministic row;
  P = 1/2 rows within |z| < 4.5, Bonferroni over about 1010 rows at family
  error 0.01).
- kaggle_run.py gains a pin for qxelarator==0.10.0 for this entry.
- Orchestrator pushes one CPU kernel, collects into results/kaggle/, and
  adds the replay gate (stdlib + repo, no qxelarator) as core-426/core-427
  with a self-test that rejects mutated counts.

## Acceptance and falsifier

Prediction: zero deterministic violations and every P = 1/2 row inside the
bound (QX is exact). Falsifier: any violation refutes that circuit's
emission, stops the family and blocks the QX smoke submission until
explained.

## Shared files and dependencies

Needs boxkite-generator's sweep entry. results/kaggle/ retention; Kaggle CPU
quota (one of five concurrent CPU sessions).

## Effort and agents

Small after the entry exists; orchestrator only.

## Out of scope

(d)/(e) sweeps until the generator builds them.

Entry built: scripts/box_kite_qx_sweep.py (families a: 848 circuits, and
de: 149 circuits). Push (CPU machine, no --ci-env: qxelarator wheels are
cp313 only):

    "$PYTHON" scripts/kaggle_run.py push --slug box-kite-qx-sweep --with qx-emulator \
        --include docs/evidence/box_kite_census/census.json \
        --include docs/evidence/quantum_inspire/catalogue.json \
        --include results/quantum_inspire/coupler_zz_tuna_17_analysis.json \
        scripts/box_kite_qx_sweep.py -- --run docs/evidence/box_kite_qx_sweep

About 1 h (69 dense d2/d3 circuits at about 215 s each). The replay gate
re-derives every verdict from retained counts and re-checks predictions by
state vector (`--bind`); it does not resample, which is the owner's call
for research-state entry (CI cannot install qxelarator).
