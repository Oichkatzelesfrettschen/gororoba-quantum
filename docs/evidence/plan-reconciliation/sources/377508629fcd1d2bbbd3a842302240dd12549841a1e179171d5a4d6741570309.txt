# Hypercomplex operators, falsifiers and device evidence

The programme tests finite algebra and faithful circuit encodings before spending provider resources. Ordinary complex hardware measures implementation conformance and noise. A complex encoding of a quaternion operator reproduces its probabilities and therefore supplies no independent test of a fundamental quaternion scalar field.

## Claims and finite falsifiers

| Symbol or path | Invariant | Consequence | Falsifier and result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `quaternion_matrix` | Products and adjoints agree with quaternion multiplication | Two complex channels encode each quaternion coefficient | The `ij=k`, `ji=-k` tests pass; a component projection changes the experiment |
| Right quaternion module | `L_a(psi*q)=(L_a psi)*q` by associativity | Left multiplication is right-linear | The draft's contrary statement fails directly |
| Anti-Hermitian generator | A fixed chosen scalar imaginary unit need not commute with the generator | Fixed-scalar factorization and an operator-dependent complex structure are distinct questions | `L_j` fails commutation with `L_i`; the symplectic antiunitary condition differs from Hamiltonian time-reversal symmetry |
| `order_interferometer` | Balanced paths retain total norm one | Dark-port probability is `sin(theta)^2 sin(phi)^2` | Two distinct noncommuting phases in an ordered comparison suffice; the draft's three-element minimum fails |
| Paired-channel measurement | Sum adjacent complex probabilities | Global right-quaternion representatives have equal site laws | Resolved channels alone can change under a right-j representative and supply a false anomaly |
| Octonion left operators | Operator composition is associative | The envelope spans `M8(C)` | `L_e1 L_e2 != L_(e1 e2)`; the encoding is linear rather than a product homomorphism |

The [source-bound certificate](../evidence/hypercomplex-operators/README.md) records the executable subset. Soler-type field-choice results require their hypotheses; they do not classify every finite operational theory. Kramers symmetry concerns an antiunitary commuting with a Hamiltonian, while quaternionic linear dynamics commute with their quaternionic structure at the unitary level. The distinction matters when multiplication by the chosen complex imaginary unit changes sign under an antiunitary.

The [eight-generator sedenion envelope](../evidence/sedenion-clifford-envelope/README.md) supplies a complementary positive result: `L_e1` through `L_e8` satisfy `Cl(0,8)` and their 256 words span `M16(R)`. The full fifteen-generator failure does not exclude this subset. The operator result supports finite encoding controls while retaining the coefficient algebra limitations.

## Literature corrections

The [source index](../INDEX.md#hypercomplex-operator-audit-sources) binds eight exact versions and their reading scopes. Furey's 2018 EPJC article is **1806.00612**, not 1702.04336. DOI 10.1038/s41598-021-01814-1 names **N. Masi**, rather than Gresnigt. The sister Rust repository repairs those canonical bibliography records and generated mirrors.

Furey and Hughes 2409.17948 proposes a triality-based three-generation construction. Furey 2607.18450v3 explicitly includes a sedenionic embedding; 2505.07923v4 describes a Jordan superalgebra while identifying missing top-quark representations. Gresnigt 2604.24795 introduces Higgs and Yukawa structures with six Higgs doublets. These sources refute blanket claims that later programmes never address generations, dynamics-related structure or sedenions. They leave predictive uniqueness and empirical discrimination as separate obligations.

Fernandez and Schneeberger's ordered-circuit simulation theorem explains the extra complex channel. Moretti and Oppio's reduction requires its relativistic symmetry assumptions. The recent operational-foundations preprint supplies additional claims to check, rather than a proof certificate admitted by retrieval alone.

The [foundational citation review](../notes/papers/quaternionic-foundational-source-review.md) distinguishes verified identities from pending theorem and historical-experiment body readings. The [exact-version optical addendum](../notes/papers/quaternionic-optical-bound-reading.md) records the reported `0.03 degrees` effective-phase bound, Gaussian propagation, an insensitive noncommuting case and the scattering assumptions disputed by Adler and the authors. The Procopio interferometry result constrains a specified optical model. Its reported phase bound cannot become a universal quaternionic coupling bound without the model map and uncertainty treatment. Renou's 2021 Nature article is an independent-source real-versus-complex theoretical discrimination result; experimental realizations require their own citations. Conventional controlled internal operators can produce order sensitivity in complex quantum mechanics, so order sensitivity alone fails as scalar-field discrimination.

## Numerical and transform contracts

`periodic_generator` discretizes `A=L_i*d_xx + g*L_j*cos(x) + (g/2)*L_k*sin(x)` on a periodic grid. The generator is anti-Hermitian. `split_evolve` uses symmetric Fourier/potential splitting without renormalizing numerical errors away. A four-site grid resolves `sin(x)`; `sin(2x)` aliases to zero. The tests compare propagation against a matrix exponential and check norm preservation.

The left-i Fourier transform has two conjugate complex channels. The right-j transform is real-orthogonal on four real coefficients per site; it fails complex linearity relative to right-i. Device cases implement the complex-linear left transform. Identity references are labeled as identities; real and imaginary superposition inputs expose phase-sensitive output laws. A synthesized identity is not evidence of a Fourier round trip.

`dephase_evolve` implements a site-projector GKSL channel and checks Hermiticity, positivity and trace. `shot_diagnostics` reports block variability and lag-one dependence. Neither a finite linear unitary system nor a noisy shot sequence establishes chaos. Lyapunov claims require a defined nonlinear/state-space model, perturbations, scaling ranges and independent controls. The implementation makes no chaos or computational-advantage claim.

### Executable boundaries

The exact certificate covers the following bounded objects:

- Every imaginary basis left operator in dimensions 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 is skew. For real coefficients,
  `L_x = x_0 I + A`, `A^T = -A`, implies normality. The general adjoint identity belongs to
  [BCDI Lemma 2.3](../notes/0905.2987.md); the finite check carries its own denominator.
- The dimension-16 `e1+e10` Gram operator has projectors at eigenvalues 0, 2 and 4 with ranks 4, 8 and 4.
  The dark vector `e4-e15` has zero product, while changing its relative sign gives squared product norm 8 before
  input normalization. The [singular-sector derivation](../notes/singular_sector_dynamics.md) owns the general law.
- A contraction `diag(1,1/2)` maps an equal input mixture to conditional populations 4/5 and 1/5 with success 5/8.
  Its positive failure operator completes the instrument. A dark branch has probability zero and an undefined
  conditional state; the report writes JSON null for that state.
- The X/Z controlled-order witness reads -1; its incoherent control reads 0. The octonion associator encoding
  at basis inputs 1, 2 and 4 reads -1, while the identity-input control reads +1. Matrix reparenthesization leaves
  the operator product unchanged. These statements describe different observables.
- Bell-pair conditional Z or X ensembles both average to the remote density `I/2`. In the certified dark/bright
  logical basis the selector effect is `diag(0,1)`: Z selection gives conditional success 0 or 1, while X selection
  gives 1/2 in both arms. Summing the complete instrument leaves unconditional success 1/2 for either choice.
  A global phase cancels in a density matrix; a phase on one reference branch produces `cos(phi)` in the control X observable.
- `X+iZ` is a nonzero rank-one matrix with square zero and parity-time invariance under X and conjugation.
  It is an explicit standard-complex exceptional-point control, separate from normal real left multiplication.
- Exact rotation/dephasing instruments preserve total probability. The certificate separates fixed per-step
  rotation from fixed total rotation, so conditioning and changed exposure cannot masquerade as a Zeno result.
- All lower-algebra basis products agree under embeddings 8 into 16 and 16 into 32. An outside-support operand
  changes the product. Padding alone changes neither those products nor physical register dimension.
- The three-qubit repetition encoding satisfies the Knill-Laflamme scalar-compression condition for the identity
  and single-X errors and fails the single-Z condition. This reference tests error-set specificity, rather than
  claiming a sedenion code. The [star-lattice code](../notes/topics/cd-star-lattice-tower.md) is `[[9,1,3]]`:
  one logical qubit. An arbitrary coherent sixteen-label register needs four logical qubits.

The exact models are local derivations, not claims of publication priority. The
[instrument source note](../notes/papers/operational-instrument-sources.md) verifies primary arXiv identities and
abstracts for parity-time dynamics, measurement constraints, error correction, Gaussian information and anyons.
The draft's arXiv 2203.01234 citation names a gravity paper, and the reported nitrogen-vacancy experiment contradicts
the blanket claim that parity-time dynamics on quantum hardware are untested. Abstract verification establishes
neither paper-body validation nor a replay of those experiments. The finite equations above are checked independently.

  is approximately -0.00561 with standard error 0.00580, an unresolved post-hoc contrast. The pi/12 acquisition has
  an order deviation and lacks a matched bright arm.
- Garnet resolves its registered kernel responses under its own compile, heralding, angles and calibration.
  Its bright control was acquired at 0.1 only. A pooled cross-provider slope would conflate those differences.
- The common-affine response analysis retains same-shot covariance and shared readout uncertainty. Its fixture
  depth and regional selector differences remain confounds. Failure to reject does not establish the channel.
- Kingston preparation controls depend on history, repetition delay, scheduler chunks and calibration. Their
  producer's parameter bounds remain authoritative; a date-only regression cannot identify drift from those controls.
- Ring-down and atlas claims retain their existing producer gates. Physical wait duration, simulator wall time,
  provider execution duration and billed time remain distinct quantities.

Scientific rejection is an accepted output. Historical preregistered decisions retain their original rules. New
retrospective summaries remain exploratory and generate no fresh p-values from already aggregated records. A new
finite test family uses Holm correction at family-wise alpha 0.05, with effect estimates and intervals. A false
Holm decision means unresolved, rather than supported. Future equivalence claims require a frozen meaningful margin.

For a four-arm odd double difference with n independent shots per arm, each Z variance is at most 1, so contrast
variance is at most `1/n`. A planning-only absolute effect of 0.05 at 80% power and a four-comparison, two-sided
Bonferroni allocation requires `ceil((Phi^-1(1-0.05/8)+Phi^-1(0.8))^2/0.05^2)` shots per arm under that IID model.
Bonferroni supplies a conservative power allocation for the Holm family. The final case must add measured readout
and between-block variance before choosing shots; raw shot count cannot substitute for independent acquisition blocks.
At least four independently timed balanced blocks are required to estimate a block component, and the case must
justify the block model and its resulting power. A calibration change creates a new stratum rather than an assumed
independent replicate. These unresolved device quantities keep the contracts at design status.

The retained timing checker applies the 50 ns proposal against every backend's minimum recorded `measure_2` duration
and dt. All three fail that necessary bound. A passing future bound would still require live gate, reset, feedback
and alignment checks. The plan makes no 10-50 ns hardware measurement claim.

Squeezing remains capability-gated pending bosonic preparation and quadrature readout, energy and truncation bounds.
Torus protection remains definition-gated pending a transport invariant and perturbation model. Error-correction
benefit requires adequate logical capacity, a named error set, syndrome schedule, decoder and resource-matched baseline.
The [zero-divisor code falsification](../notes/topics/c5-zero-divisor-code-falsification.md) concerns a classical
annihilator decoder; it neither proves nor refutes a separately specified quantum code.
