# ISSUE-131: audit dynamic members' ideal measured law against the CZ they execute

Owner session: ibmquantum-e5. Branch (planned): conformance/dynamic-member-branch-law-cz-audit.

## Finding

`scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py` classifies a member through the
unitary of its bound circuit. `unitary_part` (line 186) returns the reason
string "mid-circuit measurement, reset or control flow" for any member with a
`measure`, `reset` or block-carrying instruction after final measurements are
stripped, `classify` (line 242) passes it through, and `census` (line 295-304)
files the name under `unchecked`. No CZ count, no declaration check.

Observed (record `docs/evidence/walk-control-scaffolding/local_member_conformance.json`,
read with a JSON load): 1452 distinct members, 950 entangling, 438 checked
rows, and one `unchecked` reason holding 64 names; no member is "wider than 10
qubits". The issue's 62 is the count at filing; the record holds 64 (38 name
families once digits collapse).

Observed (`probe.py`, `probe2.py` in the session scratchpad, which import
`local_member_cz_conformance` and `qpe_diagnostics.ideal_branch_probabilities`):

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| dynamic members, width <= 10 | 64 (widths 4: 10, 5: 23, 6: 27, 7: 4) |
| measure + reset ops per member | 2: 4, 3: 41, 4: 14, 5: 5 members, so at most 2^5 = 32 branches |
| control flow | 25 `if_else`, every condition a single `Clbit`; no switch, loop or register condition |
| classical registers per member | 1 for all 64 |
| ISA CZ at level 3, seed 11, no coupling map | 47 members execute CZ, 17 execute none |
| CZ-bearing, ideal law a point mass | 19 |
| CZ-bearing, law a product over classical bits | 24 (the 19 plus 5) |
| CZ-bearing, law correlated across bits | 23 (all `n21_*`, `orbit_*`, `coherence_measure_reset_{xx,yy,zz}`) |
| exact enumeration plus transpile of all 64 | 1.4 s serial |

The point-mass test alone over-reaches: `folded_qpe_s0..s3` are point masses
by design and execute 41 CZ because the controlled rotation transfers the
eigenphase onto the control (`order_finding.folded_qpe`, `controlled_rotate`).
The issue's rule "point mass or product" therefore declares them, and the
declaration is the honest record: the one-hot law is the pass criterion and the
CZ is the probe. The sharper statement is that the audit cannot prove a CZ
unnecessary for a dynamic member; it proves the law has no cross-bit
correlation, which is the condition under which the static rule's
"product output" family already requires a declaration.

Inferred, then confirmed by the probe: "product over registers" is vacuous.
Every dynamic member has one classical register, so a register-factorization
test passes 47 of 47 CZ-bearing members. The test must factor over classical
bits (24 of 47); no member factors over bits and not over registers, so the
bit test is strictly the register test refined.

`folded_qpe_null` (`experiments._folded_qpe_mod15`, line 279): `folded_qpe(1,
identity=True)` runs `eigenstate_prep` on work qubits 1-4, then H, measure,
reset, H, conditional P(-pi/2), H, measure on the control with no controlled
rotation. The control returns to |0> through H-H, so the law is one-hot at 0
(`one_hot(0)`). Observed: ideal law point mass, ISA CZ 6 (all from the
preparation; the measured control never interacts with the work register).
Currently no rule sees it.

## Reachability

`"$PYTHON" -B -W error scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --check`, gate
core-289 (full tier, 120 s), through the default flow: `planned_groups`
enumerates every registered group and every family group rebuilt from retained

## Design

All changes sit in `scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py`; its imports
already add `gororoba_quantum/` to `sys.path`.

1. Branch enumeration reuses `qpe_diagnostics.ideal_branch_probabilities`
   (`_branch_instructions`, line 296): a statevector per branch, `measure` and
   `reset` split by projection (`_project`, probability floor 1e-14), `reset`
   applying X on outcome 1, `if_else` on a single `Clbit` selecting the block by
   the stored bit, `barrier` and `delay` skipped, little-endian classical index.
   It returns the 2^num_clbits joint law. It is the exact helper
   `qpe_diagnostics._verify` already checks against the Fraction law
   `n21_labeled_law` (feed-forward and no-feed-forward) and against padded
   compiled circuits, so no second enumerator is written. It raises
   `ValueError` for any control flow other than `if_else` or a non-`Clbit`
   condition; the audit catches exactly that and lists the member as
   unchecked with reason "unsupported control flow: <ValueError text>", so a
   future switch member fails to classify visibly and never silently passes.
2. Cost guard. A new constant `MAX_BRANCH_OPS = 12` bounds measure plus reset
   operations (2^12 branches of at most 2^10 amplitudes is 64 MB of live
   states); a member above it lists as unchecked "more than 12 mid-circuit
   measurements or resets" beside the existing width reason. The current
   maximum is 5.
3. `classify_dynamic(member) -> tuple[str, str]` at module level (picklable for
   `parallel.ordered_map`): binds the circuit as `unitary_part` does
   (`assign_parameters(member.binding)`), enumerates the law, and returns
   `law` in {`point_mass`, `bit_product`, `correlated`} with a ISA CZ count from
   `transpile(member.circuit, basis_gates=BASIS, optimization_level=OPT_LEVEL,
   seed_transpiler=SEED)` (total CZ; a dynamic circuit has no local-component
   split, so the static product-output rule applies: any CZ counts). Tests:
   point mass is `max(p) > 1 - TOLERANCE`; bit product compares each of the
   2^n joint entries with the product of single-bit marginals at TOLERANCE
   (1e-9), n <= 12 clbits here. Both are exact to float64 and independent of
   BLAS reduction order at this tolerance; the record stores only the label,
   the point-mass outcome as a bit string, and the CZ integer, never a float,
   so `--check` equality is byte stable and holds in normal and `-O` runs
   (core-289 runs both).
4. `census()` runs the dynamic classification in one
   `parallel.ordered_map(classify_dynamic, members, min_items=2)` over the
   dynamic members only, after the existing unitary-part pass identifies them,
   and keeps the serial order; the transpile moves into the worker so the 64
   transpiles are parallel too. The existing loop over static members is
   unchanged. `unchecked` shrinks to width, branch-budget and unsupported
   control-flow reasons (all empty on the current tree).
5. Record schema `local-member-cz-conformance-v3`: new row `kind:
   "dynamic_law"` with `law`, `outcome` (point mass only, else null), `cz`,
   `templated`, `declared`. `needs_declaration` returns `row["cz"] > 0` and
   `law != "correlated"` for dynamic rows. `enforce` adds the dynamic
   patterns to its "every declaration matches a CZ-executing member" loop, so a
   stale pattern fails.
6. New `DYNAMIC_LAW_PROBES: dict[str, str]`, each pattern with the measurement
   that needs the CZ. Required patterns (24 members):
   - `folded_qpe_s\d`, `qpe_archived_s\d`, `qpe_delay_s\d`,
     `qpe_no_feed_forward_s[02]`, `correction_known_s[13]`,
     `correction_measured_s[13]`: the one-hot eigenphase readout; the
     controlled rotation (`order_finding.controlled_rotate`) kicks the
     eigenphase onto the control, so the CZ is the measured phase transfer
     (`qpe_no_feed_forward_s[13]` joins as a product with spread, the
     feed-forward-off control of the same circuit).
   - `folded_qpe_null`: identity-multiplier null; it keeps the eigenstate
     preparation so every folded member carries the same preparation cost and
     the contrast with `folded_qpe_s\d` isolates the controlled rotation
     (`experiments._folded_qpe_mod15`). The declaration names that mechanism.
   - `hybrid_shor_N15_a2`: bit-product law over the order-finding phase bits
     with 35 CZ; the declaration names the modular-multiplier chain the bits
     read. Written against `order_finding.py` after reading it.
   - `coherence_measure_reset_x[y]|y[x]`: orthogonal-basis arms whose law is a
     product while `_xx`, `_yy`, `_zz` correlate; the CZ is the shared Bell arm
     (`diagnostic_controls`, conditional-Ramsey Bell arm).
   Patterns are refined against the member list at implementation time; each
   regex must match at least one CZ-executing member.
7. Docs: one paragraph in the module docstring, one in
   `docs/evidence/walk-control-scaffolding/README.md` (line 101 region)
   replacing the sentence that dynamic members are unchecked, and the
   `--write` regeneration of the record.
8. Gates. core-289 keeps its command and its full tier. Added (names from the
   session range):
   - core-406: `scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --self-test`, synthetic
     circuits: Bell pair measured together (correlated), two independent H
     measurements (bit product), X-then-measure with a spectator CZ pair
     (point mass), reset-after-measure, `if_else` feed-forward whose law is
     point mass, and a `switch_case` circuit that must list as unsupported.
   - core-407: falsifier gate, `--self-test-undeclared`: drops
     `folded_qpe_null` from the dynamic declarations in memory and requires
     `enforce` to fail naming it; also requires a stale pattern to fail.
   - core-408: enumerator cross-check, `ideal_branch_probabilities` against
     `qpe_diagnostics.n21_labeled_law` for the dynamic members the audit
     classifies as `correlated` (n21 feedback and no-feedback), reusing
     `qpe_diagnostics._verify`'s Fraction law so the audit's input is the
     proven one. Fast (<2 s); pr tier.
   - core-409: reserved, unused unless the review asks for a separate -O run.
   The lint and type gates cover `scripts/` whole; no list edit.
   Parallel rule: the one loop over independent items goes through
   `parallel.ordered_map`; no serial loop over members remains in the dynamic
   path.

## Acceptance and falsifier

Prediction, stated from reading `folded_qpe` before the probe and confirmed by
it: `folded_qpe_null` classifies `point_mass` with outcome `00`, executes 6
CZ, and appears in the record with a declaration; deleting its pattern makes
`--check` fail with "members executing CZ their law does not need,
undeclared: ['folded_qpe_null']". Predicted counts after the change: 64 dynamic
rows, 17 with zero CZ (no declaration), 47 with CZ, 24 requiring a
declaration, 23 correlated and exempt, `unchecked` empty.

Commands (all from the repository root, `PYTHON=${PYTHON:-python3}`):

```sh
"$PYTHON" -B -W error scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --write
"$PYTHON" -B -W error scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --check
"$PYTHON" -B -O -W error scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --check
"$PYTHON" -B -W error scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --self-test
GOROROBA_WORKERS=1 "$PYTHON" -B scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py --check
ruff check scripts/ ; ruff format --check scripts/ ; mypy scripts/local_member_cz_conformance.py
```

The serial and pooled runs must produce the same bytes. CI is the merge
authority for core-289 timing; replicate the shard on Kaggle CPU only at
session close-out (memory: Kaggle shards once per session).

Falsifiers: (a) `folded_qpe_null` classifies `correlated` or shows 0 CZ, which
would put the premise (point mass plus preparation CZ) wrong; (b) the counts
differ from the table above, which would mean the enumerator and the probe
disagree and the cross-check core-408 localizes which; (c) pooled and serial
records differ; (d) core-289 exceeds its measured seconds by more than the
parallel dynamic pass (about 1.4 s serial) explains.

Runtime: the dynamic pass adds about 1.4 s serial (64 members, 1.4 s measured
for enumeration plus transpile), less when pooled; the census setup
(`planned_groups`, 1452 members digested) dominates and is unchanged, 76 s
wall in the probe on the build host. core-289 stays at 120 s and stays in the
full tier (the pr tier admits gates under two minutes). core-406, 407 and 408
are each a few seconds and join the pr tier after their `seconds` are measured.

## Effort and agents

One session day, one file of about 150 changed lines plus three gates and the
regenerated record. No subagent required; if parallel review is wanted, a
sonnet agent on core-406 synthetic cases only, under the orchestrator's review
of the diff.

## Out of scope

- Proving a dynamic member's CZ unnecessary (a CZ-free circuit with the same
  branch law). That needs a synthesis or light-cone argument per family; the
  audit declares and names the measurement instead.
- Noisy-law or hardware-law comparison: the audit reads the ideal law only.
- Dynamic members wider than 10 qubits, `switch_case`, loops and register
  conditions: listed as unchecked with a reason until a member uses them.
- Correlated dynamic members (23): no declaration required; whether their
  entanglement is needed is a design question for their own plans.
