# The W8 closure ideal restricted to the embedded A_4 coordinates

The restriction of the A_5 piece W8 to the embedded sedenion coordinates is the reduced zero-divisor variety of A_4,
and it differs from the Pfaffian ideal of the nullity >= 4 locus in its scheme structure. Over F_32009 the restricted
ideal is J = (y_0, y_8, U - V, W), a prime complete intersection of dimension 12 and degree 4. The A_4 ideal that the
strata records' construction gives (the realified order-6 Pfaffians of the doubly pure encoding, PB below) has radical
J, the single minimal prime J with multiplicity 1, and degree 4, so radicals and component multiplicities agree. PB is
a proper subideal of J: U - V and W are not in PB, the Hilbert series differ from degree 2 on, and PB carries embedded
components over a locus of dimension 6. The hypothesis of equal ideals, equal Hilbert series and equal multiplicities
is therefore refuted at the ideal level and survives for the radical and the unmixed part (ledger row A5-222).

`a5_w8_a4_restriction.py` builds every ideal with exact integer polynomial arithmetic and checks them against cd_core;
`compare.sing` and `run.sh` run the Singular comparisons; `result.json` holds the exact checks and the SHA-256 of
every Singular input; `log_*.txt` hold the Singular outputs with the `/usr/bin/time -v` accounting.

## Conventions

The product is the cd_core doubling, e_p e_q = cd_sign(dim, p, q) e_(p XOR q), and L_y is
`cd_core.left_mult_matrix_exact(dim, y)`. The A_5 coordinates are x_0..x_31, the embedded A_4 is x_16..x_31 = 0 with
coordinates y_0..y_15. A doubly pure y of A_5 has x_0 = x_16 = 0 and z_k = x_k + i x_(16+k), k = 1..15; W8 is
mu = 0, U = V, W = 0 with u = z_1..z_7, mu = z_8, v = z_9..z_15, U = u.u, V = v.v, W = u.v (the "Pieces" table of the
[strata record](../a5-zero-divisor-strata/README.md#conventions), the same equations as the
[equations record](../a5-rank-strata-equations/README.md)). Q = y_1^2 + ... + y_15^2.

## Method

**The restricted W8 ideal.** The 32-coordinate ideal holds x_0, x_16 and the real and imaginary part of mu, U - V and W
(8 generators), built from the record's own `GP` arithmetic and `gdot` and compared with the record's
`piece_ideals` W8 generators at 40 integer split points (`w8_crosscheck`: 120 comparisons agree up to a unit). Setting
x_16..x_31 = 0 maps the ideal onto the ideal generated by the images, because the quotient map is a ring
homomorphism, and leaves J = (y_0, y_8, U - V, W): the imaginary parts and x_16 vanish and the real parts of the
generators are rational. J is a complete intersection of two quadrics in the variables u + i v and u - i v, which
carry U - V + 2 i W and U - V - 2 i W; Singular confirms J = radical J.

**The A_4 zero-divisor ideals.** Three constructions are compared, each a Pfaffian ideal of left multiplication.

- PB follows the strata records. The doubly pure encoding w_k = y_k + i y_(8+k), k = 1..7, carries the 7 x 7 Im-block
  C_w of left multiplication by w on the complexified octonions, entry (a, b) = cd_sign(8, a XOR b, b) w_(a XOR b). A
  skew matrix has rank at most 2r exactly when its principal Pfaffians of order 2r + 2 vanish, and the transport
  nullity L_y = 12 - 2 rank C_w gives nullity >= 4 as rank C_w <= 4, the seven order-6 Pfaffians. Their real and
  imaginary parts, with y_0 and y_8, are the 16 generators of PB. The doubly pure identities hold at dimension 16 (J =
  L_(e_8) squares to -I, anticommutes with L_(e_k), k not in {0, 8}, and every L_(e_k) is skew), the transport holds at
  126 points (`transport`: nullity 0 with rank 6 and nullity 4 with rank 4 only), and exact expansion gives Pf_j =
  +-(w.w) w_j for all seven (`pb_structure`).
- PA takes the 16 x 16 skew matrix L_y at y_0 = 0 and its order-14 principal Pfaffians (106 distinct nonzero), with
  y_0, as one reads the A_4 nullity >= 4 locus off L_a directly.
- PAp takes the order-12 principal Pfaffians of the 15 x 15 Im-block of L_y at y_0 = 0 (455), with y_0. This is the
  A_5 order-12 Pfaffian ideal of R_8 evaluated at the real vectors z = y, because nullity L_(a,0) = 2 nullity L_a.

PB is the construction the records use: it works in the complex coordinate w and passes to the real coordinates by
real and imaginary parts, which describes the Zariski closure of the real zero-divisor set. PA and PAp ask for rank
conditions on a complex vector y, a larger set.

**Comparisons.** At p = 32009 in the global degree-reverse-lexicographic order, `compare.sing` computes standard bases
with `std` (`option(redSB)`), dimension, degree (`mult`), the first and second Hilbert series (`hilb`), containments by
`reduce`, `radical`, the ideal quotient and saturation, and `primdecGTZ`. Each result prints as a `RESULT` line.

## Results

Hilbert series are the first-series numerators over (1 - t)^16 as `hilb(std, 1)` prints them. J gives
1, -2, -1, 4, -1, -2, 1, which is (1 - t)^2 (1 - t^2)^2, so the Hilbert series of F_p[y]/J is (1 + t)^2 / (1 - t)^12.

| PB, order-6 Pfaffians of C_w, realified | 16 | 12 | 4 | PB in J; J not in PB (y_0, y_8 in PB, U - V and W not) | radical PB = J, mutual containment |

**PB against J.**

- Radicals: `radP_in_J` and `J_in_radP` both 1, and the Hilbert series of radical PB and J agree.
- Hilbert series: PB gives 1, -2, 1, -14, 70, -168, 301, -644, 1736, -4188, 7858, -11370, 12852, -11438, 8008, -4368,
  1820, -560, 120, -16, 1; the coefficient of t^2 is 1 against -1 of J, because J holds the quadrics U - V and W
  that PB lacks.
- Multiplicities: PB has dimension 12 and degree 4 and its radical is the prime J of degree 4, so J is the only
  minimal prime of PB and the length of PB along J is 4 / 4 = 1.
- Embedded part: the ideal quotient PB : J has dimension 6 and two minimal primes, each of dimension 6 and degree 2 and
  each containing J, and the saturation of PB by PB : J equals J. PB is therefore J intersected with components
  supported on a locus of dimension 6. Over C the exact identity Pf_j = +-(w.w) w_j gives PB = (q m_w) + (q-bar
  m_w-bar) with q = w.w, whose embedded locus is {w = 0} x {q-bar = 0} and {q = 0} x {w-bar = 0}, two cones of
  dimension 6 and degree 2 that F_32009 separates because 32009 = 1 mod 4.
- Primary decomposition: stage 4 (`primdecGTZ`) stalled at its 3600 s cap with no result (`log_PB_stage4.txt`); the
  embedded structure above rests on stage 3 and on the exact identity.

**PA against J.** `std` gives dimension 14 and degree 2. The ideal (y_0, Q) is prime (`minAssGTZ` returns itself),
has dimension 14 and degree 2, and PA lies in (y_0, Q) intersect J. The products Q y_8, Q (U - V) and Q W each have
the third power in PA, so radical PA contains radical of (y_0, Q) J, which is the radical of the intersection; hence
radical PA = (y_0, Q) intersect J. The isotropic quadric Q = 0 carries no real point but the origin. At the point
e_1 + s e_9 with s^2 = -1 mod p, Q = 0, L_y has rank 8 mod p and U - V = 2: it lies in V(PA) and V(PAp) and outside
V(J) and V(PB) (`isotropic_witness`). An isotropic point with generic coordinates (y_1..y_14 from a quadratic polynomial in the index, y_15
  solving Q = 0) also has rank 12 mod p, so the quadric Q = 0 carries nullity-4 points of no real origin.

**PAp against J.** The standard basis of the 456 degree-6 generators (stage 1) stalled at its 7200 s cap with no
result, so the dimension, degree and Hilbert series of PAp are not computed; a `slimgb` portfolio probe of the same
ideal (scratch, 12 GiB cap, not committed) also ended without output. The containment PAp in J
(every generator reduces to 0 modulo std(J)) holds, so V(J) lies in V(PAp), and the witness above lies in V(PAp)
outside V(J), so J is not in the radical of PAp. By Theorem B of the equations record, V(PAp) = W8 union Ehat, with
Ehat = E8-bar of dimension 11 and no real point but 0; this record does not re-verify that union in these coordinates.

## Controls

- **Pointwise** (`pointwise`). All 84 two-term zero divisors e_i + t e_j (the left and right factors of the corpus
  `sedenion_zero_divisor_corpus`) have nullity 4 for L_a and nullity 8 for L_(a,0), lie in V(J), V(PB), V(PA) and
  V(PAp) and in the 32-coordinate W8 at the embedded point (a, 0). 40 deterministic integer points of y_0 = y_8 = 0
  with nullity 0 lie outside all four, a generator of each ideal being nonzero. No random draw enters; each point is an
  explicit integer vector.
- **Naive construction rejected** (`naive_construction_control`). a = e_0 + s e_1 with s^2 = -1 mod 32009 has nullity 8
  over F_32009 at y_0 = 1, so the 13 x 13 minors of the real 16 x 16 matrix in y_0..y_15 describe a locus that no
  ideal containing y_0 can match. The record builds no minor ideal for that reason.
- **Mutations** (`--self-test`, normal and `-O` modes). A flipped entry of J = L_(e_8) fails the doubly pure
  identities; U + V in place of U - V does not vanish on the zero divisors; W8 without x_0 = x_16 = 0 contains the
  invertible point e_0; order-4 in place of order-6 Pfaffians of C_w do not vanish on the zero divisors.
- **Singular mutations** (`controls.sing`). The restriction with U + V has dimension 12 and degree 4 and neither
  contains nor lies in radical PB; the restriction without y_0 has dimension 13 and lies in radical PB without containing
  it. Each differs from radical PB = J.

(Singular 4.4.1, built 12 October 2026), Python 3.14.7, python-flint 0.9.0. Checklist of

   for the later stages) with `MemorySwapMax=0`; no address-space cap. `run.sh` falls back to `prlimit --as` (each
   process's virtual address space, not resident memory) where no user bus answers, and each log names the limiter.
2. Wall time: `timeout -k 10` of `LIMIT` seconds, 7200 by default.
3. Threads: one per Singular process, at most five processes at once on 10 CPUs, no other job on the host.
4. Flags: `-q --no-warn`, `option(redSB)`, global order `dp`, default `radical`, `primdecGTZ`, `sat`, `quotient`;
   `LIB "primdec.lib"` and `"elim.lib"`. Every launch takes stdin from `/dev/null`.
5. Environment: `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`; Python with `GLIBC_TUNABLES=glibc.rtld.optional_static_tls=4096`.
6. Exactness: exact arithmetic over F_32009 and Z; `radical` and `primdecGTZ` may use internal coordinate changes
   whose results the stages recheck by containment; no seed of this repository enters.
7. Progress: `RESULT` lines and the `/usr/bin/time -v` block per stage log.
8. Cap falsifier: exit status 137 or 143 with a "terminated by signal" line in the log marks a cap, an "out of memory"

9. Measured-combination row: none exists for these ideals.
10. Checkpoints: one log per family and stage; ideals regenerate from the script (`ideal_PA.sing` 6.6 MB and
    `ideal_PAp.sing` 7.8 MB are written by `--work DIR` and stay out of the commit, their hashes in `result.json`).
11. End state: local host, nothing to deallocate.
12. Run-rulebook entry: not written; three stages ran over 30 minutes and the rulebook lies outside this record's file
    list.

## Falsifier and scope

- The ideal-level verdict depends on PB as the definition of the A_4 zero-divisor ideal. A generating set whose ideal
  contains U - V and W (for example the 13 x 13 minors of the realified 16 x 16 matrix, which this record does not
  compute) and whose Hilbert series equals that of J restores equal ideals; the radical, degree and multiplicity
  statements stay.
- The nullity transport L_y = 12 - 2 rank C_w is checked at 126 integer points and at the 84 zero divisors, and the
  identity Pf_j = +-(w.w) w_j is exact; the proof of the transport for A_4 follows the A_5 route of the strata record
  and is not repeated.
- A point of V(J) with nullity below 4, or a nullity-4 point with y_0 = y_8 = 0 outside V(J), falsifies V(PB) = V(J);
  none appears among 84 zero divisors and 40 invertible points.
- Only p = 32009 is computed; characteristic 0 is not run.
