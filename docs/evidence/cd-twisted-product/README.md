# Twisted product of the W8 closure ideal

[CdTwistedProduct.v](../../../proofs/CdTwistedProduct.v) proves, as polynomial
identities over a commutative ring that holds i with i i = -1 and the inverse
of 2, that the 32-coordinate W8 equations of A_5 are the real and imaginary
parts of the sedenion zero-divisor equations of the twisted point z_k =
x_k + i x_(16+k), and that the same equations split off z'_k = x_k - i
x_(16+k). The ring is the functor argument `TwistedRing`; the polynomial ring
of the 32 coordinates is an instance, so each identity is an identity of
polynomials. `Zmod5` instantiates the signature at F_5 (i = 2, 1/2 = 3) as
the closed instance that the receipts print.

For each equation f, with u = (z_1, ..., z_7), v = (z_9, ..., z_15), U = u.u,
V = v.v and W = u.v (`eq_uv` for U - V, `eq_w` for W):

| Theorem | Identity in the coordinates x_k |
| --- | --- |
| `eq_uv_twisted`, `eq_w_twisted` | f(z) = Re f + i Im f |
| `eq_uv_twisted_conjugate`, `eq_w_twisted_conjugate` | f(z') = Re f - i Im f |
| `eq_uv_real_part`, `eq_w_real_part` | Re f = (f(z) + f(z'))/2 |
| `eq_uv_imaginary_part`, `eq_w_imaginary_part` | Im f = (f(z) - f(z'))/(2 i) |
| `eq_uv_re_expanded`, `eq_uv_im_expanded`, `eq_w_re_expanded`, `eq_w_im_expanded` | Re f and Im f equal the polynomials of [ideal_W8.sing](../a5-w8-twisted-product/ideal_W8.sing), term by term |
| `W8_ideal_exchange` | for every ideal I of the ring, I contains the eight forms x_0 +- i x_16, x_8 +- i x_24, (U - V)(z), (U - V)(z'), W(z), W(z') exactly when it contains the eight real and imaginary parts x_0, x_16, x_8, x_24, Re(U - V), Im(U - V), Re W, Im W |

Each identity uses i i = -1 and 2 (1/2) = 1 only through
`pair_product`, `pair_product_conjugate` and the two part lemmas, and no
theorem concerns dimension or degree. `W8_ideal_exchange` is the statement
that the ideal of the W8 equations equals the ideal generated by J(z) and
J(z') for the zero-divisor generators J = (y_0, y_8, U - V, W) of the
sedenions.

## Remaining computed evidence

Ledger row A5-223 of
[ledger.tsv](../a5-exceptional-fibers/ledger.tsv) keeps what stays computed:
that J(z) and J(z') involve disjoint coordinate sets after the change of
variables, so the variety is the product V(J) x V(J), its dimension 24, its
degree 16 and its first Hilbert numerator N_J(t)^2. The records are in
[a5-w8-twisted-product](../a5-w8-twisted-product/README.md).

## Assumptions

[assumptions.txt](assumptions.txt) holds twelve `Print Assumptions` queries on
the `Zmod5` instance; each reads "Closed under the global context".
[kernel.txt](kernel.txt) lists no axiom. No proof introduces an axiom or an
`Admitted`.

## Replay

```sh
ROCQ="${ROCQ:-rocq}" sh docs/evidence/cd-twisted-product/replay_rocq.sh
sha256sum -c docs/evidence/cd-twisted-product/source_hashes.sha256
```

`replay_rocq.sh` requires the banner of [toolchain.txt](toolchain.txt) (Rocq
9.3.0 on OCaml 5.5.1, the switch of `ci/rocq_toolchain.pin`), compiles the
proof and [assumptions.v](assumptions.v) in temporary storage with `-w @all`,
fails on any warning or compiler output, runs `rocq check`, and compares the
outputs byte for byte with the two receipts. `scripts/rocq_kaggle_replay.py
--only cd-twisted-product` selects the replay and `source_hashes.sha256`
binds the source and the receipts.

The replay takes about one minute.
