# Residual structure in retained Tuna-17 and Quandela records: first exploratory pass

Scope: offline pass over retained Quantum Inspire Tuna-17 and Quandela (Belenos, Ascella) records of `gororoba-quantum`, reusing `scripts/qi_shot_record_line.py`, `gororoba_quantum/qi_records.py`, `gororoba_quantum/qd_records.py` and `scripts/qd_belenos_mesh.py` (`propagate`, `fit_efficiency`). Each analysis is a script in the output directory with a JSON beside it; the falsifier is the script docstring and the `falsifier` key of the JSON. `ledger_all.json` pools every test. Reproduce with `run.sh SCRIPT.py` (4 workers, 8 GiB cap), then `combine_ledger.py`.

Multiple testing: 90 tests in the pooled ledger (analysis 1: 9, 2: 7, 3: 61, 4: 13). BH at q = 0.05 over all 90 keeps 65 (7, 4, 54, 0 by analysis); Bonferroni (alpha 5.6e-4) keeps 58. Of the 54 analysis-3 survivors, 30 are all-mode variants (15 CMH, 15 residual) that re-detect the known stray-mode effect, 23 are stray-excluded rows, 1 is the plan-time relation. The inherited scan threshold (band-corrected p < 1e-6 per job) is stricter than any corrected alpha here. Observed means a number read from a record; inferred is marked.

## Ranked list of what survived correction

1. Belenos per-mode throughput moved between 2026-10-23 08:13 and 19:50 UTC, more than the plan-time record says (analysis 3). Between runs of byte-identical compiled circuits, the generalized CMH statistic of run against mode (24 settings as strata, stray modes 0, 1, 5 excluded) is 1369 (08:13 to 19:50) and 2156 (07:33 to 20:22) on 20 degrees of freedom (log10 p -277 and -447). The log odds ratio of modes 16 to 23 against the rest is -0.64 to -3.94 (factors 0.53 to 0.02; mode 20: -3.20 and -3.94); mode 5 gains +2.64 and +3.70 (factors 14 and 40). The plan-time `t_out` of modes 16 to 23 moved by -0.01 to -0.29 in ln, so the plan record understates the change by about an order of magnitude. The rank correlation of the shift with the `t_out` change is 0.81 (p 9e-5, 17 modes) for the first pair and 0.51 (p 0.02, 20 modes) for the second. The retained `results/quandela/bdsc_stationarity_calibration_contrast.json` covers the calibration between 19:50 and 20:18 only (stray share 0.093 to 0.255, class means, no per-mode shares); the 08:13 to 19:50 interval and the per-mode shares of modes 16 to 23 are not recorded there, so this extends the retained contrast.
2. Tuna-17 readout error fluctuates far beyond counting noise and not along one drift (analysis 2). Groups are keyed by qubit, prepared state and full prepared X-set; within clusters of readout jobs at most 6 h apart, e0 (prepared |0>) Pearson Q/dof is 21.9 (Q 15995, dof 731) and e1 (prepared |1>) 273 (Q 101150, dof 370); the sum of squared per-group slope z is 12.5 and 30.0 per group (null 1). Slopes are not one-signed (negative in 90 of 182 e0 groups, 64 of 143 e1 groups; sign-test p 0.94 and 0.24), and the first-to-last bracket chi-square per qubit does not grow with elapsed time (Spearman 0.06, 20 pairs, p 0.79). Largest step: 0.170 in one qubit's flip probability between two all-ones readout jobs 3.2 minutes apart (cluster of 2026-11-06). The analysis-1 block statistic shows non-stationarity inside single readout jobs too (14 of 66 readout jobs at adjusted p < 0.01, 8 below 1e-10, minimum 1e-145), so part of the excess is drift within a job and not only change between brackets.
3. The Tuna-17 shot-order line persists after 2026-10-18, rarer and mostly weaker (analysis 1). 21 of 165 later Tuna-17 jobs cross p < 1e-6 (12.7 %, Wilson 8.5 to 18.7 %); 31 cross Bonferroni at 0.05 over 165 jobs. Against the earlier 72 of 159 (45 %): Fisher p 1e-10 (all jobs); payload jobs 21 of 99 against 72 of 108 (p 3e-11). Peak frequency does not move (0.020019 to 0.020227 cycles per shot, a 400.4 to 404.5 us period at 50 Hz; earlier 0.020020 to 0.020353). Detection differs by family (chi-square p 2e-7): DeMarrais 9 of 9 (log10 p -2500 to -4400), one-CZ null 1 of 56.
4. A line in the prepared-|1> readout circuits, outside the primary band (analysis 1, exploratory). 15 of 32 later readout-1 jobs carry a narrow line at 0.02126 to 0.02138 cycles per shot (log10 p -6 to -29 each), 1.062 times the payload line of the same series; 5 of 22 earlier readout-1 jobs on rescan; 0 of 224 QX jobs. It sits on bit 6 (14 of 15 detected jobs), bit 9 (6) and bit 4 (4); qubit 6 is documented to have a collapsed |1> readout (`docs/notes/quantum_inspire/tuna-17.md`). Wall-clock frequency is not established for these jobs (no usable per-shot timing), so 50 Hz is not claimed. Readout-0 jobs read 0 of 32, but that control is power-limited: 24 scan 10 to 13 bits and 8 two-qubit jobs scan one. The earlier "absent from all 51 readout circuits" rests on the 0.019 to 0.021 band. The 0.010 to 0.040 band was declared after the primary scan; it needs a preregistered confirmation.
5. Stray-mode clicks (modes 0, 1, 5) carry the run-to-run change inside one plan record (analysis 3), a known effect. For two runs of identical circuits 1 h apart on one plan record, CMH over all 24 modes is 400230 on 23 dof and falls to 15.9 on 20 dof without modes 0, 1, 5 (p 0.73). Mode 5 log odds ratios over the 15 pairs have median size 1.06 (range -1.81 to +3.70). This reproduces the retained off-cone-click finding.

Not found: a monotone photon-number trend inside a job (analysis 4), growth of the Tuna-17 bracket difference with elapsed time, a change of the line frequency.

## Analysis 1: Tuna-17 shot-order line past the 2026-10-18 cutoff

Falsifier. No later job crosses the corpus threshold; or the later fraction equals the earlier 72 of 159; or later amplitude and frequency equal the earlier ones; or the QX twins or readout circuits show the line (scanner miscalibrated).

Data. Corpus rule of `qi_shot_record_line.inventory` (stamp after `CORPUS_THROUGH = 2026-10-18T165957Z`, backend type 7): 46 stamped run directories, 135 raw files. The earlier inventory counted 49 directories and the same 135 files. Added: 14 unstamped or nested directories (8 `tuna17-quaternion-order-noise/order-batch-*`, 6 `*-destination-*/prepared-*`), 30 files; 165 Tuna-17 files in all. Controls: 111 later QX files (36 directories). All 96 directories pass `qi_records.read_run`; the listing is `data.directory_list` in the JSON. The scanner's `--self-test` passes (fixed repository seeds).

Result.
- Primary band 0.019 to 0.021, threshold 1e-6, the whitened zoom-FFT unchanged: 21 of 165 (stamped set alone 20 of 135). Controls: QX 0 of 111 in both bands; Tuna-17 readout circuits 0 of 66 in the primary band.
- Families (detected of jobs): DeMarrais 9 of 9, kernel cancellation 4 of 18, BDSC Pauli 2 of 27, pathion coset block 2 of 12, pathion coset coin 2 of 10, one-CZ null 1 of 56, quaternion-order noise 1 of 24, projector and destination runs 0 of 9.
- Per-job BH at 0.05 over 165 jobs: 44 jobs; Bonferroni: 31. Jobs between 1e-6 and 3e-4 are corrected-significant but below the inherited threshold.
- Amplitude (maximum first-harmonic amplitude over bits, detected jobs): later median 0.055 (0.017 to 0.290, 20 jobs), earlier 0.146 (0.013 to 0.428, 72 jobs); Mann-Whitney p 0.08. By family the median is 0.265 (DeMarrais) against 0.017 to 0.055 elsewhere. Circuits differ between corpora, so this is a class-level reading (inferred); the readout brackets are the only circuits shared across the cutoff.
- Frequency: peak 0.020019 to 0.020227 cycles per shot, family-specific (DeMarrais 0.020064 to 0.020081, kernel cancellation 0.020210 to 0.020227); Mann-Whitney of later against earlier medians p 0.29.
- Secondary band 0.010 to 0.040 (declared after the primary result, same statistic and threshold): 19 more Tuna-17 jobs, 15 of them readout-1 circuits (item 4) and 4 payload jobs (BDSC witness circuits near 0.0317). Rescan of the earlier corpus in this band: Tuna-17 81 of 159 (primary 72), QX 0 of 113.

Verdict: structure found. A1.1 to A1.3 and A1.6 to A1.9 survive BH and Bonferroni (9 tests, within-analysis alpha 5.6e-3); the amplitude (A1.4) and frequency (A1.5) shifts do not.

## Analysis 2: Tuna-17 readout-bracket drift against wall clock

Falsifier. Within a cluster of readout jobs (consecutive jobs at most 6 h apart) each qubit's flip probability, in one prepared X-set context, is constant; the pooled slope per hour has an interval containing zero for e0 and for e1; a 24 h harmonic of the residuals is absent.

Data. 100 pure readout circuits (X gates plus full measurement) from 62 Tuna-17 run directories, 2026-10-16 22:12 to 2026-11-08 11:08 UTC, all through `qi_records.read_run` (0 failures). Time is the job midpoint (finished minus half the execution time). Nine clusters: one spans 14 h, one 5.4 h, one 3.8 h, the rest under 2.1 h. Brackets are the first and last readout of the same prepared state in a cluster.

Result.
- Homogeneity (binomial null): e0 Q = 15995 on 731 dof; e1 Q = 101150 on 370 dof (log10 p -2828 and -21437).
- Any linear drift: sum z^2 = 2270 over 182 e0 groups and 4290 over 143 e1 groups (log10 p -356 and -798).
- Direction: negative slope in 90 of 182 (Wilson 0.42 to 0.57) and 64 of 143 (0.37 to 0.53) groups; median relative slope per hour +7e-4 and +4.6e-3 (interquartile range -0.057 to +0.054 and -0.12 to +0.18). No common direction (sign-test p 0.94 and 0.24).
- First-to-last bracket pairs (20): median absolute flip-probability difference 0.0009 to 0.028, maximum 0.170; chi-square per qubit against elapsed hours Spearman 0.063 (p 0.79). The bound is weak at 20 pairs, and clusters are confounded with series.
- Largest groups: qubit 11 prepared |1> with X on qubits 2, 4, 8, 11, 12 in the 14 h one-CZ-null cluster (mean flip 0.236, 12 jobs, Q 93070); qubit 1 prepared |1> in the quaternion-order cluster (mean 0.102, Q 4265); qubit 11 prepared |0> with no X gates (mean 0.037, Q 7758).
- Time of day: not run. Only one cluster spans more than 6 h, so a 24 h harmonic is collinear with elapsed time and series, and documented calibration steps (q9, q0, q6 in `docs/notes/quantum_inspire/tuna-17.md`) separate the clusters. The harmonic regression is in the JSON as exploratory with its binomial p (not interpretable).

Verdict: structure found for fluctuation beyond counting noise (A2.1 to A2.4 survive BH and Bonferroni); `null (bound: no monotone growth with elapsed time, 20 bracket pairs; no common slope sign)`; time of day `not run`. Inferred: qubit-specific fluctuation of readout assignment or preparation, partly inside single jobs; not a platform-wide ramp.

## Analysis 3: Belenos per-mode efficiency across calibration sessions

Falsifier. Between runs returning byte-identical compiled circuits, per-mode click shares differ only by multinomial noise (CMH null); across plan-time sessions the model-based log efficiencies agree within Fisher intervals widened by the jackknife and overdispersion; default and none mitigation runs agree.

Data. 32 completed single-photon `qpu:belenos` runs (belenos mesh 10-18, BDSC qualification, diagnostic, stationarity, drift series; 10-18 to 10-24), 20 distinct plan-time records (`perfs_at_plan` t_out plus dark counts; equal records define a session, as `bdsc_drift_series.py` does). All 68 retained Belenos runs share one coupler set, so the coupler-based session rule splits nothing. Multiphoton runs (2, 3, 6 photons) not examined: their one-click marginals need permanents. Replay check: `fit_efficiency` on the retained four-run session of 2026-10-18 reproduces `efficiency_under_ideal` to 6e-7 relative.

What counts identify. A factor per output mode common to all settings, relative to the other modes only. Not identified: absolute efficiency (every setting has a free scale), static mesh routing offsets, dark and stray clicks (modes 0, 1, 5), which alias into the factor. Counts passed the default DetectorBalancing correction, so a fitted factor is what remains after the service's own correction. The identical-circuit design separates an efficiency-type change (one factor per mode for all settings: the CMH part) from a phase or mixing change (setting-specific: the residual).

Result (3 identical-circuit sets, 15 consecutive pairs: 3 within one plan record, 12 across records).
- Stray modes included: CMH 2.6e3 to 4.0e5 on 23 dof in every pair; within-record pairs (4.0e5, 3.1e5) are as large as across-record pairs.
- Stray modes excluded: within-record pairs 15.9 and 25.7 on 20 dof (p 0.73, 0.18; null), and 144 for the 35-minute pair of belenos-mesh repeats. Across-record pairs 18.7 to 2156: the 08:13 to 19:50 and 07:33 to 20:22 intervals 1369 and 2156, the 19:50 to 20:18 calibration pair 71.7, other significant pairs 40 to 221 (q/dof 2 to 11), non-significant 18.7 to 26.2. RMS centered log odds ratio of non-stray modes: 0.12 within record, 0.30 across (binomial expectation 0.07). Modes beyond three within-record RMS (0.36) in across-record pairs: 16 to 19 (2 to 4 pairs each), then 13, 20, 21, 22, 23.
- Setting-specific part: Pearson residual over dof 1.1 to 2.2 (within-record 1.23 and 1.31; belenos-mesh repeat 6.9): modest, approximate (difference of Pearson statistics).
- Model-based fits (`fit_efficiency` on the ideal mesh of each returned circuit): overdispersion Pearson/dof 19 to 1e5, so the ideal mesh model fails and these intervals are quasi-likelihood; kept in the JSON, out of the ledger. The mitigation contrast (mm-def-e1 against mm-none-e1, one plan record) is not identified: the two runs carry different returned meshes (chi-square 29350 on 23 dof reflects model misfit). `not run` as a mitigation test; no identical-circuit default-versus-none pair exists.
- Plan-time relation: rank correlation of centered shift with ln `t_out` ratio is 0.81 and 0.51 across the 10-23 intervals, near 0 in the other between-record pairs (Stouffer z 2.80 over 12 pairs; consecutive pairs share runs, so p 5e-3 is optimistic).

Verdict: structure found. Per-mode throughput of modes 16 to 23 and the stray-mode 5 share changed across 2026-10-23 by factors the plan record misses; other between-record changes in non-stray modes sit 2 to 50 times above the within-record level. 54 of 61 analysis-3 rows survive BH, 47 Bonferroni (analysis-local alpha 8.2e-4); the within-record stray-excluded CMH rows (15.9, 25.7) do not. Inferred: a detector or output-loss change; the counts cannot separate it from a static mesh offset.

## Analysis 4: Within-job photon-number trend, Belenos and Ascella

Falsifier. In every job that repeats an identical circuit and input, the setting-stratified weighted slope of the mean detected photon number per sample against iteration index has an interval containing zero after BH; sim jobs give uniform p-values. A positive result is a monotone trend: slope interval excluding zero and the same sign in at least three quarters of the repeated groups.

Data and limits (observed). 99 retained `qpu_*` records: 12 tested, 8 underpowered (fewer than 30 off-mode samples), 1 confounded, 52 with no repeated circuit inside the job (position aliased with setting; not identifiable), 26 without per-iteration counts or variance. Quandela returns counts per iteration and no iteration timestamp, so the index is the only clock. `nb_shots_used` equals the sample budget in nearly all jobs, so loss per optical trial is identifiable only where it exceeds the samples: Belenos teleportation (up to 2.5e6 trials per 1200 samples), associator visibility, suppression law and Ascella CZ tomography, none of which repeats a circuit inside a job. `physical_perf` is a model value and is not trended. Samples passed DetectorBalancing. For single-photon inputs the statistic reads multi-click share (g2 and coincidences), not loss.

Result.
- 12 trend tests (Belenos mesh job-16, mode-swap job-00, ten Ascella jobs): smallest p 0.09. Slope intervals for the mean detected photon number per iteration: mesh job-16 [-1.5e-5, +1.1e-5] (24 iterations); mode-swap job-00 [-3.6e-3, +0.8e-3] (6 usable iterations, about 1.1 detected photons per sample); Ascella jobs about ±1e-6 to ±1e-5.
- Yield per optical trial: one identifiable job (Ascella two-photon associator, 8 iterations, 3 repeated circuits): logit slope -0.0073 [-0.021, +0.0064] per iteration.
- Mesh job-12 reads z = 9.9 (relative +7.5e-4 per iteration) and is excluded: it is the dwell ladder, where the sample budget (1500 to 60000) rises with the index inside each repeated group, so index and budget alias.
- Type-I control: only 2 of 37 sim jobs reach 30 off-mode samples, so p-value uniformity cannot be read; the control is effectively not run and the null rests on the slope's asymptotics.

Verdict: `null (bound: relative slope per iteration within about ±1e-5 for single-photon jobs with 24 iterations, ±0.3 percent per iteration for the 6-iteration six-photon job)`. A loss-trend identification for the Belenos multiphoton jobs: `not run` (position aliased with setting).

## Checks that did not run, and residual risk

- Time of day on Tuna-17 (analysis 2) and the default-versus-none mitigation contrast on Belenos (analysis 3): not identified by the retained design.
- Multiphoton Belenos efficiency fit, Ascella per-mode efficiency (6 of 51 Ascella jobs carry per-mode transmission): not run.
- The secondary scan band, the 19 secondary-only Tuna-17 jobs and the stray-mode-excluded CMH choice were set after seeing the primary results; each needs a preregistered repeat before it enters a claim.
- The plan-time record is what the service published and can lag the chip (`docs/notes/quandela/calibration.md`); within-record variation in analysis 3 may be real chip change.
- Pearson residuals in analysis 3 and the Cochran pooling are approximate; the CMH part is a score test with exact conditional moments.
- Qubits of one Tuna-17 job share shots, so group-wise statistics in analysis 2 are not strictly independent; the Q/dof values of 22 and 273 survive any plausible correction.
