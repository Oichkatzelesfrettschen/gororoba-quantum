# Components of the E8-bar -> VB quadric scheme

The quadric ideal K2 of the fiber cone of E8-bar -> VB (21 quadrics in y1..y10, row A5-307) has two minimal primes,
of degrees 3 and 4 (A5-034, A5-307, A5-428). This directory identifies both components as rational normal scrolls and
records how they meet (row A5-611).

Mechanism. `vbc-PRIME.sing` holds the 21 retained quadrics at one prime and runs, in Singular 4.4.1 over F_PRIME:
`minAssGTZ` for the minimal primes; `minbase` for the count of linear and quadric generators of each prime;
`mres` for the Betti tables; `slocus` (sing.lib) for the singular locus of each affine cone; the rank of the Jacobian
matrix of the degree-3 prime at the point of its singular locus; and the radical of the sum of the two primes against
the ideal of the linear forms of both, which tests whether the components meet exactly in the intersection of their
linear spans.

Inputs (each `.sing` embeds the quadrics verbatim, with a closing semicolon added):

| Prime | Quadrics |
|---|---|
| 32009 | `working/build/logs/part-b/vb/relations-d2.rel` |
| 32029 | `working/distribute/out/a5-p2-small/vb_p2/d2/relations-d2.rel` (the input of the K2 cofactor certificate) |
| 32057 | `third-prime/relations/e8-vb/d2/relations-d2.rel` |

Result, identical at the three primes:

- K2 equals its radical, has dimension 4 and multiplicity 7, and has projective dimension 6 with Betti numbers 21, 70,
  105, 84, 35, 6 in one row, so k[y]/K2 is Cohen-Macaulay with a 2-linear resolution.
- The prime of degree 4 is generated by 3 linear forms and 6 quadrics, and its affine cone is singular only at the
  origin. It is a smooth threefold of minimal degree spanning a P^6, so it is the rational normal scroll S(1,1,2).
- The prime of degree 3 is generated by 4 linear forms and 3 quadrics, and the singular locus of its affine cone is a
  line, one projective point, where the Jacobian rank of the prime falls to 4 below its codimension 6. It is a
  threefold of minimal degree in a P^5 with an isolated singular point, so it is the cone S(0,1,2) over the cubic
  scroll S(1,2) of a P^4. The Segre threefold P^1 x P^2 = S(1,1,1), which is smooth, is excluded.
- The radical of the sum of the primes has dimension 3 and degree 1 and equals the ideal of the linear forms of both
  primes: the two components meet in a projective plane equal to the intersection of their linear spans, so V_+(K2)
  is a linearly joined union of two varieties of minimal degree. The vertex of the cone lies on that plane.

Classification used: a variety of minimal degree is a cone over a smooth one, and a smooth one of codimension above 1
is a rational normal scroll or the Veronese surface (Eisenbud and Harris, On varieties of minimal degree, 1987,
Theorem 1, valid in every characteristic). The smooth threefold scrolls of degree 3 and 4 are S(1,1,1) and S(1,1,2);
the cubic scroll S(1,2) is the only smooth surface of minimal degree in a P^4. Eisenbud, Green, Hulek and Popescu
(Small schemes and varieties of minimal degree, 2006, Theorem 0.4) state that a reduced 2-regular scheme is such a
linearly joined union, which matches the linear resolution above.

Run: one aarch64 host, Singular 4.4.1, 0.2 s per prime, exit 0 (`timeout 1800 Singular -q vbc-PRIME.sing`).

Second method, over Q(i) reduced at small primes (`point-counts/`, run by the RIEMANN session on the gate workstation):
`vb-qi-dump.sing` is the Q(i) arc script of A5-597 with one print line added, and `vb-qi-dump.log` holds the
generators of K2 and of the two primes over Q(i); `count_points.py LOG p...` reduces them with i -> sqrt(-1) mod p and
counts F_p points of each component inside its linear span. At p = 13: 2549 = q(q+1)^2 + 1 points on the degree-3
component (the cone S(0,1,2)), 2562 = (q+1)(q^2+q+1) on the degree-4 component (S(1,1,2)), 183 on their intersection
(a plane) and 4928 = 2q^3 + 3q^2 + 2q + 1 on the union; at p = 17: 5509, 5526, 307 and 10728, the same closed forms. The
P^1 x P^2 alternative predicts 4941 and 10745. `slocus.sing` repeats the singular-locus test over Q(i): affine
dimension 1 for the degree-3 prime, 0 for the degree-4 prime. `count_points.py` is retained in the repository's lint form (ruff format and check, mypy); the run that produced `count.log` used the unformatted original, and this form, run again on the same log, prints both RESULT lines unchanged (75 s, 5.7 GB). `timings.txt` holds the GNU time lines (count 116 s,
5.7 GB; dump 241 s, 1.4 GB).

Not covered: a full characteristic-0 identification (the Q(i) generators are reduced at 13 and 17 only); the analogous scroll S(1,1,2) of E8-bar -> N+- is A5-502 and A5-508.
