# Arcs into the fibers of Iso -> N- and W8 -> N-, and the one-chart test

The exceptional fiber over the origin is E0 = Proj F(I) (reduced), with F(I) = k[y]/L. Every relation f in L of
degree d satisfies f(G) in m I^d, so f vanishes at the limit vector F of every arc x(s) through the origin of V(J)
(the coefficient vector of the least power s^nu in G(x(s))). K2 lies in L (degree-2 receipts), so K2 vanishes on
every limit vector; this is the falsifier every family below must pass. Everything here holds over F_32009 and
F_32029 only.

## Proven: V(P) lies in E0 for W8 -> N- at both primes, so the analytic spread of I is 6

`cert-w8-P-p32009.sing` and `cert-w8-P-p32029.sing` (generated by `arcs_scan.py`, logs beside them) take the linear
family a = s, c = C s, d = D s, t7 = T s, t1 = U s, t9 = V s in the split coordinates a = t2 + i t4,
b = t2 - i t4, c = t5 + i t6, d = t5 - i t6, with b = (g + c d - t7^2)/a, and compute in Singular, symbolically in the
parameters: J vanishes on the arc identically; nu = 2 (every coefficient of a lower power vanishes identically); K2
vanishes on the limit F identically; at 32009 every generator of P vanishes on F identically, and at 32029 the nine
linear forms Lambda vanish on F identically; the rank of [dF | F] is 6 at two parameter points.

A rank at a point bounds from below the dimension of the cone of limits, so the closure Y of the limits is an
irreducible cone of dimension at least 6 inside V(K2) (dimension 6). At 32009 Y lies in V(P), which is geometrically
irreducible of affine dimension 6 (`../w8-kp/README.md`: V(P) = C20, rad P = I(C20)), so Y = V(P). At 32029 Y lies in V(K2 + Lambda), whose 6-dimensional part is the
degree-20 component (`../w8-kp/README.md`), so Y is that component. The 32029 step depends on the decomposition at 32029 in the same README. Hence L lies in
I(C20) = rad P (at 32029, in the degree-20 prime), h_d >= HF(k[y]/I(C20), d) for every d, and dim F(I) = 6 = dim k[y]/K2: the analytic spread of I is 6. With the
findings-note criterion (K2 radical and Cohen-Macaulay), F(I) = k[y]/K2 in every degree now hinges on the
multiplicity alone: e(F(I)) = 50 exactly when V(K2 : P) also lies in E0, and 20 otherwise. The numeric engine
`arcs_num.py` (exact truncated series over F_p with dual parts, a code path separate from Singular) reproduces this
family (`num-w8-p32009-basic.txt`: nu 2, rank 6, K2 vanishing, Lambda vanishing, a generator of K2 : P nonzero).

Structure that explains the ranks (`l0-FIBER-pP.sing` and `.log`, both fibers at both primes): on both slices G
vanishes on the linear 3-space L0 = {t2 = 0, t4 = t6, t5 = t7} (W8: also t9 = 0), V(J + G) near the origin is L0, so I is not
m-primary; the degree-2 parts of G cut the tangent cone of V(J) in L0 doubled. Arcs in a generic direction reach the
values of the quadratic parts (rank 4 on Iso: a projective 3-fold), and arcs tangent to L0 reach the 1-dimensional
fibers over L0 (rank 2). A failed search does not bound the analytic spread: a Rees valuation centered at the origin
need not be monomial in either coordinate system used here.

Falsifiers that fired and the defects they caught: without the sign map eps (the K2 runs of Iso use generators 6-15
of the opposite sign to the header, `RESULT signs` lines) K2 failed on every limit; families with b(0) != 0 (numerator
of order equal to the order of a) gave nu = 0 and failed K2, and the weight rule now requires the numerator order to
exceed it; a series division that assumed a of order 1 broke J at order 4 and K2, and the engine now divides by
s^ord(a). A symbolic Singular version of the L0 families grew to 17 GB and was stopped; its record stays in the
local failed-runs archive.

## The deciding test: one blowup chart

`chart_test.py` writes `chart-FIBER-pP.sing`: for a0 = l0(G) with QRNG coefficients (draw
`docs/evidence/anu_qrng/draw-2026-11-05T235924Z.json`, indices in the script header), the chart of Bl_I V(J) where a0
is a unit is A = k[t, z]/((J, a0 z_i - G_i) : a0^infinity), and A/(t) is an affine piece of E0. For Iso, K2 is prime
with no linear form (h_1 = 17), so V(K2) meets every chart densely: dim A/(t) = 4 gives E0 = V(K2) and F(I) = k[y]/K2
in every degree; dim A/(t) <= 3 gives dim E0 <= 3, an analytic spread of at most 4, and a relation of L outside K2 in
some degree of at least 6 (L agrees with K2 through degree 5). No chart saturation has finished, so the test has not
decided the case. Runs, all with the same QRNG form (`chart-iso-pP-coefficients.json`), logs under `chart-runs/`:
