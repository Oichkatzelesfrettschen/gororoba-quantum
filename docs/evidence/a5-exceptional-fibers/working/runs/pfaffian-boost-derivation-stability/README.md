# Boost-derivation stability of the ten-Pfaffian ideal on the source closures

The kernel-coordinate ideal that the fiber-cone records blow up is generated by the 3,003 ten-Pfaffians of the
15 x 15 skew matrix C(z), which is linear in the split coordinates z = (u, mu, v). The boost u -> c u + s v,
v -> s u + c v with c = (w^2 + 1) / (2 w) and s = (w^2 - 1) / (2 w) is the flow exp(t D) of the linear vector
field D: u' = v, v' = u, with w = e^t. A5-414 proves that the boost has no congruence lift to C, and A5-600
samples the fiber of E8-bar -> VB at twelve values of w. This record asks a question that has no w in it: is
the ideal stable under D?

## Result

Modulo 32009 (i = 10754) and over Q(i), with the same RESULT lines (`stab-p32009.log` and `stab-qi.log` have
one sha256):

| Test | Boost derivatives outside | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| the Pfaffian span alone (dimension 2,647 in degree 5) | 1,960 of 3,003; joint span 3,307 | identity and three G_2 derivations: 0; rotation u' = v, v' = -u: 3,003 |
| modulo the W8 closure ideal (3 generators, dimension 12, multiplicity 4) | 0 | rotation 1,960; G_2 0 |
| modulo the Iso closure ideal (4 generators, 11, 8) | 0 | rotation 1,470; G_2 0 |
| modulo the X+ and X- closure ideals (39 generators, 10, 12) | 0 | rotation 2,002; G_2 0 |
| modulo the E8-bar closure ideal (37 generators, 11, 24) | 1,470 | rotation 3,003; G_2 0 |

D maps every generator of every one of the five closure ideals into that ideal (`boost_moves 0`). The closure
equations are those of `weight_slice_fiber_relations.source_equations`.

## Consequence for W8, Iso, X+ and X-

Let I(Y) be one of the four closure ideals and J = (P) + I(Y), with P the ten-Pfaffians. D is a derivation, D of a
generator of I(Y) lies in I(Y), and D of a Pfaffian lies in the span of the Pfaffians plus I(Y) in degree 5, so
D J lies in J by the Leibniz rule. D preserves degree and each graded piece of J has finite dimension, so
exp(t D) maps J_d onto J_d and I(Y)_d onto I(Y)_d for every complex t. The boost g_w is therefore an automorphism
of the scheme Y that carries the ideal sheaf J / I(Y) to itself, and g_w^* is an isomorphism of the local ring at
g_w z0 onto the local ring at z0 that carries J to J and the maximal ideal to the maximal ideal. The Rees algebra
and the fiber cone of J at g_w z0 are then isomorphic, as graded algebras, to those at z0: in every degree, for
every w other than 0, at every point z0 of Y. The four incidences of these sources into VB that A5-419 samples,
and their incidences into V, carry one fiber cone along the boost. This step is an argument on top of the
computation; it is not machine-checked.

## E8-bar

The same test fails for the 37-generator E8-bar closure ideal: 1,470 boost derivatives stay outside the span.
The argument above gives no statement there, in either direction. A5-600 reads one normal form of the quadric
ideal at twelve values of w, so the fiber may still be constant along the boost by a mechanism other than a
linear symmetry of this pair. Two readings remain open: the 37 generators may define a scheme whose radical or
equidimensional hull is larger (the check of that, `e8rad-p32009.log`, reached its 1 h limit at 4.3 GiB after
printing dimension 11 and multiplicity 24), and a vector field other than D may stabilize the pair and move the
basepoint in the same direction.

## Stabilizer algebras modulo 32009

`derivation_stability.py ... alg` solves, for a generic linear vector field sum c_ab z_b d/dz_a, the linear
conditions on the 225 coefficients under which the field maps an ideal into itself in the degrees present.

| Ideal | Dimension | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| the Pfaffian span alone | 15 | the 14 G_2 derivations and the scaling, the algebra A5-414 finds from congruences |
| the W8 closure ideal | 59 | 15 fields mu d/dz_a that vanish on mu = 0, and 44 = 2 x (21 + 1): so(7) and a scaling on each of u + v and u - v |
| the Iso closure ideal | 40 | the same 15, and 25 = 21 + 4: so(7) on the pair (u, v) with gl(2) mixing u and v |
| the pair (W8 closure, Pfaffians) | 31 | the same 15, G_2, the scaling and the boost: 15 + 14 + 1 + 1 |
| the pair (Iso closure, Pfaffians) | 31 | the same decomposition |

The closure stabilizers use every generator and are exact modulo 32009. The Pfaffian conditions use every 13th
Pfaffian (231 of 3,003), which gives upper bounds 15 and 31; the lower bounds come from the fields named in the
right column, each of which stabilizes the full ideal (G_2 and the scaling by A5-414, the boost by the table
above, mu d/dz_a because mu lies in the closure ideal). On W8 the boost scales u + v by w and u - v by 1 / w.
The run reached its 2 h limit (2.0 GiB) after W8 and Iso; E8-bar, X+ and X- carry no stabilizer line.

## Cost

One core of an x86-64 workstation, Singular 4.4.1: 3 min 45 s at 0.52 GiB modulo 32009 and 9 min 22 s at
0.74 GiB over Q(i) for the stability run; the stabilizer run stopped at 2 h. The standard basis of the same
question over the function field F_32029(w) reached a 4 h limit at 1.5 GiB without a basis (A5-600).

## Scope

- Degree 5 is the degree of the generators; the argument extends it to the whole ideal.
- The fiber cone statement is for the scheme of the retained closure equations and the ideal generated by all
  ten-Pfaffians. A record that uses another local generating set relies on its own identity with this ideal
  (the Schur form of A5-593 at VB).
- Three of the 14 G_2 derivations are run here as controls.
