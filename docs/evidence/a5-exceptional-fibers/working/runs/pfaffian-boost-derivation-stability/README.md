# Boost-derivation stability of the ten-Pfaffian ideal on the source closures

The kernel-coordinate ideal that the fiber-cone records blow up is generated by the 3,003 ten-Pfaffians of the
15 x 15 skew matrix C(z), which is linear in the split coordinates z = (u, mu, v). The boost u -> c u + s v,
v -> s u + c v with c = (w^2 + 1) / (2 w) and s = (w^2 - 1) / (2 w) is the flow exp(t D) of the linear vector
field D: u' = v, v' = u, with w = e^t. A5-414 proves that the boost has no congruence lift to C, and A5-600
samples the fiber of E8-bar -> VB at twelve values of w. This record asks a question that has no w in it: is
the ideal stable under D?

## Result

Modulo 32009 (i = 10754) and over Q(i), with the same RESULT lines (`stab-p32009.log` and `stab-qi.log` have
one sha256):

| Test | Boost derivatives outside | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| the Pfaffian span alone (dimension 2,647 in degree 5) | 1,960 of 3,003; joint span 3,307 | identity and three G_2 derivations: 0; rotation u' = v, v' = -u: 3,003 |
| modulo the W8 closure ideal (3 generators, dimension 12, multiplicity 4) | 0 | rotation 1,960; G_2 0 |
| modulo the Iso closure ideal (4 generators, 11, 8) | 0 | rotation 1,470; G_2 0 |
| modulo the X+ and X- closure ideals (39 generators, 10, 12) | 0 | rotation 2,002; G_2 0 |
| modulo the E8-bar closure ideal (37 generators, 11, 24) | 1,470 | rotation 3,003; G_2 0 |

D maps every generator of every one of the five closure ideals into that ideal (`boost_moves 0`). The closure
equations are the ambient homogeneous cone equations, member by member the table that
`weight_slice_fiber_relations.source_equations` pulls back to a slice; the test runs on the cones, not on slice
germs. Every ideal in the test is homogeneous, so the reduction against a standard basis with degree bound 5
decides membership in degree 5 exactly.

The identity control is vacuous: the identity field is the Euler operator, which multiplies a quintic by 5. The
rotation is the discriminating control. It also maps the Iso closure ideal into itself and moves two of the three
W8 generators (`rotation_moves 0` and `2`), which is what the stabilizer algebras below predict: the rotation
lies in the gl(2) that mixes u and v and exchanges the two eigenspaces u + v and u - v of the boost.

## Consequence for W8, Iso, X+ and X-

Let I(Y) be one of the four closure ideals and J = (P) + I(Y), with P the ten-Pfaffians. D is a derivation, D of a
generator of I(Y) lies in I(Y), and D of a Pfaffian lies in the span of the Pfaffians plus I(Y) in degree 5, so
D J lies in J by the Leibniz rule. D preserves degree and each graded piece of J has finite dimension, so
exp(t D) maps J_d onto J_d and I(Y)_d onto I(Y)_d for every complex t. The boost g_w is therefore an automorphism
of the scheme Y that carries the ideal sheaf J / I(Y) to itself, and g_w^* is an isomorphism of the local ring at
g_w z0 onto the local ring at z0 that carries J to J and the maximal ideal to the maximal ideal. The Rees algebra
and the fiber cone of J at g_w z0 are then isomorphic, as graded algebras, to those at z0: in every degree, for
every w other than 0, at every point z0 of Y. The four incidences of these sources into VB carry one fiber cone
along the boost. The sources have no incidence into V; E8-bar -> V and E8-bar -> VB are the other two incidences
on which G_2 and the scaling miss a direction.

The retained fiber cones are computed on a slice germ. At a basepoint of VB the tangent span of G_2, the scaling
and the boost has the dimension 7 of the orbit and rank 15 together with the retained slice (A5-419). Each of the
three preserves the pair, so (g, s) -> g s is a local biholomorphism under which the pair is the product of the
orbit germ with the slice pair, and the slice fiber cone is the ambient one. A5-419 is the retained test: at the
retained basepoint and at the boosts (5/4, 3/4), (5/3, 4/3) and (13/12, -5/12) it reads the whole projective
space of the generators (P^3 for Iso, P^4 for W8, P^2 for X+ and X-) over Q(i) and modulo 32003. The statement
is about fiber cones. Limiting kernel planes and real limit sets need a lift of the boost to the kernel bundle,
which A5-414 excludes for congruences. The automorphism carries the radical of the
closure ideal to itself as well, so the statement holds for the reduced germs that the retained records use.
This step is an argument on top of the computation; it is not machine-checked.

## E8-bar

The same test fails for the 37-generator E8-bar closure ideal: 1,470 boost derivatives stay outside the span.
The argument above gives no statement there, in either direction. A5-600 reads one normal form of the quadric
ideal at twelve values of w, so the fiber may still be constant along the boost. Four readings remain open, each
with a test:

- Radical. The retained fiber-cone records replace the source germ by the intersection of its minimal primes
  through the basepoint, and the instability here is for the 37 generators as given. D maps that ideal into
  itself, hence its radical too, and the Pfaffians may be D-stable modulo the radical. The check of the radical
  (`e8rad-p32009.log`) reached its 1 h limit at 4.3 GiB after printing dimension 11 and multiplicity 24.
- Local against global. The 1,470 derivatives lie outside the homogeneous ideal; the fiber cone at a basepoint
  sees the localization there, which can be larger. A standard basis in a local ordering at the retained VB
  basepoint decides D-stability of the local pair. The failure locus is closed and invariant under G_2 and the
  scaling, which commute with D, so on the boost orbit of VB it is a finite set of values of w or the whole orbit.
- The chart test below reads 18 of 21 outside at k = 1, where the G_2 control passes; the k = 2 run reached its
  3 h limit without a verdict.
- Another linear field. Excluded: the stabilizer algebra of the E8-bar pair is G_2 and the scaling (A5-604).
- An isomorphism of the fibers that no symmetry of the pair induces. Degree-3 relation counts at further values of
  w test it (A5-428 holds one count at one boost).

## Test in the chart of a pivot Pfaffian (modulo 32009)

At the point z0 = (1, 0, 1, 0, ...) of VB the matrix C(z0) has rank 8, and where the Pfaffian pB of a principal
8 x 8 pivot is invertible the ten-Pfaffian ideal is generated by the 21 entries F of the Schur form on that pivot.
`pivot_stability.py` keeps everything homogeneous: it reduces pB^k D(F_j) against a degree-bounded standard basis
of (F) + I(Y), so a zero count places D(F_j) in the pair ideal after inverting pB, which holds in the local ring
at z0. A nonzero count at one k does not exclude a larger k.

| Pair | k | boost | G_2 derivation | rotation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W8 | 0 | 15 of 21 outside | 21 | 21 |
| W8 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 21 |
| E8-bar | 0 | 21 | 21 | 21 |
| E8-bar | 1 | 18 | 0 | 21 |

The fixed pivot breaks G_2, so k = 0 fails for every field; at k = 1 the G_2 derivation passes on both pairs and
the rotation fails on both, which calibrates the level. The boost passes on W8, as A5-602 requires, and leaves 18
of the 21 entries outside on E8-bar. The W8 unit took 2 min 8 s at 0.7 GiB and the E8-bar unit 8 min 32 s at
1.0 GiB on one core of an aarch64 host, Singular 4.4.1. A first design, a standard basis in a local ordering in
all 15 variables, reached 40 min and 9.1 GiB on the W8 calibration without a verdict and is discarded.

Level k = 2 for E8-bar is undecided. `pivot_stability_level2.py` emits the script `piv2-e8.sing` (retained as run;
the producer was not rerun against it), which needs the standard basis of (F) + I(E8-bar) to degree 13. The run
printed the k = 0 counts and reached its 3 h limit at 1.5 GiB on one core (`piv2-e8.log`, `piv2-e8.time`, exit
124). The test that decides every level at once is membership of D(F_j) in the saturation by pB, that is in
(F) + I(E8-bar) + (1 - t pB) with a new variable t; it is not run.

## Stabilizer algebras modulo 32009

`derivation_stability.py ... alg` solves, for a generic linear vector field sum c_ab z_b d/dz_a, the linear
conditions on the 225 coefficients under which the field maps an ideal into itself in the degrees present.

| Ideal | Dimension | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| the Pfaffian span alone | 15 | the 14 G_2 derivations and the scaling, the algebra A5-414 finds from congruences |
| the W8 closure ideal | 59 | 15 fields mu d/dz_a that vanish on mu = 0, and 44 = 2 x (21 + 1): so(7) and a scaling on each of u + v and u - v |
| the Iso closure ideal | 40 | the same 15, and 25 = 21 + 4: so(7) on the pair (u, v) with gl(2) mixing u and v |
| the pair (W8 closure, Pfaffians) | 31 | the same 15, G_2, the scaling and the boost: 15 + 14 + 1 + 1 |
| the pair (Iso closure, Pfaffians) | 31 | the same decomposition |
| the E8-bar closure ideal | 16 | G_2, the scaling and the boost; no field vanishes on the closure |
| the pair (E8-bar closure, Pfaffians) | 15 | G_2 and the scaling; the boost is outside |

The closure stabilizers use every generator and are exact modulo 32009. The Pfaffian conditions use every 13th
Pfaffian (231 of 3,003), which gives upper bounds 15 and 31; the lower bounds come from the fields named in the
right column, each of which stabilizes the full ideal (G_2 and the scaling on the Pfaffians by A5-414, the 14
derivations on every closure ideal by the symmetry computation of `verify_regularity_independent.py`, the boost by
the table above, mu d/dz_a because mu lies in the closure ideal). On W8 the boost scales u + v by w and u - v by 1 / w.
The run reached its 2 h limit (2.0 GiB) after W8 and Iso. A second run for E8-bar alone (`alg-e8-p32009.log`,
2 h 6 min, 2.0 GiB) gives the last two lines: no linear vector field beyond G_2 and the scaling preserves the
kernel-coordinate ideal on E8-bar. X+ and X- carry no stabilizer line.

## Cost

One core of an x86-64 workstation, Singular 4.4.1: 3 min 45 s at 0.52 GiB modulo 32009 and 9 min 22 s at
0.74 GiB over Q(i) for the stability run; the stabilizer run stopped at 2 h. The standard basis of the same
question over the function field F_32029(w) reached a 4 h limit at 1.5 GiB without a basis (A5-600).

## Scope

- Degree 5 is the degree of the generators; the argument extends it to the whole ideal.
- The fiber cone statement is for the scheme of the retained closure equations and the ideal generated by all
  ten-Pfaffians. A record that uses another local generating set relies on its own identity with this ideal
  (the Schur form of A5-593 at VB).
- Three of the 14 G_2 derivations are run here as controls.
