# Invariants of the E8-bar and Iso quadric ideals in a second computer-algebra system

Every ideal-theoretic statement of the exceptional-fiber record rests on Singular 4.4.1. This directory recomputes
the invariants of five retained quadric ideals modulo 32009 in Macaulay2 1.25.11, whose standard-basis engine,
minimal-prime routine and primary decomposition (Shimoyama-Yokoyama) share no code with Singular's `std`,
`minAssGTZ` and `primdecGTZ` (ledger row A5-599).

## Result

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `KA-e8-nm.sing`, `KA-e8-np.sing` (weight slice) | 148, 5, 11 | 1 + 16 T - 12 T^2 + 10 T^3 - 5 T^4 + T^5 | (4, 4, 14), (5, 11, 9) | (4, 4; 4, 2), (4, 4; 4, 4), (5, 11; 5, 11) | A = K : rad K has 17 generators, dimension 4, degree 2, numerator 1 + T, and is prime; K : A = rad K; (rad K)^2 lies in K; 5 linear forms in rad K |

| `KA-iso-nm.sing` | 68, 5, 23 | 1 + 12 T + 10 T^2 | (5, 23, 0) | (5, 23; 5, 23) | radical; prime |

- The numbers are those of A5-451, A5-454, A5-457 and A5-547 for E8-bar (three associated primes of dimension and
  degree (5, 11), (4, 4) and (4, 2), a nilradical of square zero generated by five linear forms, K : A = rad K) and of
  A5-446 for Iso (prime of dimension 5 and degree 23), and the reduced numerators are those of A5-595.
- In each case the intersection of the primary components equals the ideal.
- Control: the ideal with y_1^2 added has dimension 4 and degree 28 (weight slice), 22 (header slice) or 46 (Iso).

## Scope

- One prime, 32009, with the root 10754 built into the retained files. The Q(i) statements are not recomputed.
- Five ideals. The W8 quadric ideal has no retained file in this form and is not run.
- `associatedPrimes` of Macaulay2 (the Ext-module method) exceeded 11 GiB on the first ideal and was stopped; the
  associated primes here are the radicals of the Shimoyama-Yokoyama components.
- The relation computations that produce the ideals (standard bases of J + m I^2) are not repeated in Macaulay2:
  this directory tests the invariants of the retained ideals, not their derivation.

## Replay

    sh docs/evidence/a5-exceptional-fibers/working/runs/quadric-ideals-macaulay2-p32009/run_m2.sh

from the repository root with `M2` on PATH. The driver compares the RESULT lines with `expected-results.txt` and
exits nonzero on a difference. The retained run took 1 min 49 s at 234 MiB on an aarch64 Linux host, with Macaulay2
1.25.11 of the Ubuntu 26.04 package `macaulay2` unpacked in a user directory.
