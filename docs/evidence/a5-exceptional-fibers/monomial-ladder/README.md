# Truncated ranks of the fiber cone by monomial linear algebra

The fiber cone F(I) = ⊕ I^d / m_R I^d of a slice ring R that sits inside a power series ring P has
truncated ranks that need no standard basis. With n the maximal ideal of P, A_d the image of I^d and B_d
the image of m_R I^d in P / n^N,

    r_N(d) = dim A_d − dim B_d ≤ h_d = dim I^d / m_R I^d,

with equality once N is large, and h_d ≤ HF(k[y]/K2, d) because the defining ideal of F(I) contains K2.
A cell is exact when r_N(d) meets HF(k[y]/K2, d).

## Monomial structure of the same-sign X slice: what is computed and what is open

Notation: P = k[[a, ρ, σ, w]], R = k[[t]] + (a, ρ, σ) P inside P, I = (G_1, ..., G_15) R, and
M = w² (a, ρ, σ) + w (ρ, σ) (a, ρ, σ)², a monomial ideal of P. The extension of m_R to P is
q = (w², a, ρ, σ) P.

Computed. `monomial_probe.py` reduces spanning sets of the k-vector spaces I and m_R I modulo n^N; every row of
the reduced echelon form has one nonzero entry, so both truncated spaces are spans of monomials of P:

| Incidence | p | N | rank of I | rank of m_R I |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X− → N− | 32009 | 12 | 973 | 958 |
| X− → N− | 32029 | 12 | 973 | 958 |
| X+ → N+ | 32009 | 12 | 973 | 958 |
| X+ → N+ | 32029 | 12 | 973 | 958 |
| X− → N− | 32009 | 14 | 1,788 | 1,773 |

The model. `monomial_model.py` computes the Hilbert function of the graded algebra
F_q(M) = ⊕_d M^d / q M^d, the fiber cone of the monomial ideal M of P taken with respect to q. It prints
15, 51, 122, 240, 417, 665, 996, 1422, 1955, 2607 for d = 1 to 10. `records/x-same-k2-hilbert-d10.jsonl` holds
HF(k[y]/K2, d) for d = 1 to 10 (X− at both primes, X+ at p = 32029, from `../k2_hilbert.py`): the same ten
numbers.

The map. If I P = M, each I^d lies in M^d and m_R I^d lies in q M^d, which gives a homomorphism of graded
k-algebras φ: F(I) → F_q(M). Nothing here certifies φ: neither I P = M to every order, nor that φ is injective
or surjective in any degree. The agreement above is an agreement of Hilbert functions: of the ladder with the
model through degree 8, and of k[y]/K2 with the model through degree 10.

What the model does not give. F_q(M) is a quotient-by-monomials construction and may be nonreduced; F(I) is
nonreduced (y1, y5, y6 are nonzero nilpotents, ledger A5-303 once it lands), so the model is not a toric variety
and no reducedness or normality passes through it. If I and m_R I are spans of monomials to every order, then so
are I^d and m_R I^d, F(I) has a basis of monomials of P in each degree, and its defining ideal L is generated by
binomials y^α − y^β and monomials y^α; that statement, too, rests on the unproved "every order".

What would close it, in order:

1. an exact identity of R-modules between I and the span of a finite set of monomials, with the transition
   matrices written over the polynomial subring that R completes (coefficients rational in w), which replaces
   "through order 14" by a certificate;
2. from it, the presentation of F(I) by the finite set of monomial generators, with a membership certificate
   for each relation;
3. a finite completeness criterion: a Gröbner basis of the resulting binomial and monomial ideal, compared with
   K2, which decides L = K2 in every degree;
4. the same over Q, since every statement here is at p = 32009 or p = 32029.
